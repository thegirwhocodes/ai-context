## How Cold Turkey Blocker Works

Cold Turkey Blocker is a desktop-only distraction blocker for Windows and macOS that operates at the OS level—not just inside your browser. It can block websites, desktop applications, and in its most extreme mode, your entire computer.

When you activate a block, it enforces at the operating system level: blocked websites show a Cold Turkey page in any browser, and blocked applications get force-closed the instant you try to open them.

## Architecture & Technical Design

It operates at the OS level, requiring deep system integration that Apple's mobile operating system explicitly prohibits for third-party apps. This OS-level approach is what distinguishes it from browser-based blockers—it has network-level blocking capabilities that can work alongside DNS filtering services.

## Frozen Mode & Locking

The tool locks the entire computer for a chosen duration, optionally logging you off or shutting down for forced breaks. Optional locks prevent you from disabling, uninstalling, or editing blocks until the timer runs out.

## Platform Support

It is available for Windows and macOS. All of your settings and statistics are stored locally on your computer, and anything you block is kept private as Cold Turkey does not collect your data.
