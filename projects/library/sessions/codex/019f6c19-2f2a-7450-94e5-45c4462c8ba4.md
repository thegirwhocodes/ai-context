## Why there are multiple Library folders

- [/System/Library](/System/Library): core macOS files. Sealed and largely read-only.
- [/Library](/Library): computer-wide resources shared by all users. It is owned by `root`.
- [/Users/naomiivie/Library](/Users/naomiivie/Library), also written `~/Library`: Naomi’s private app settings, caches, mail data, containers, and preferences.

Apps may use all three. Folders with similar names, such as `Application Support` or `Preferences`, serve different scopes.

## Why OneDrive is blocked

`/Library` requires a fixed path, root ownership, permissions, executable helpers, system extensions, and live mount points. OneDrive is user-scoped and can make synchronized files online-only. Moving `/Library`—or replacing it with a OneDrive symlink—could break application launches, updates, developer tools, networking extensions, audio plug-ins, Python/R, and GarageBand.

Individual documents and projects can go in OneDrive. System and application-support folders should not.
