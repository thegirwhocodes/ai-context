## D.E. Shaw Connection

Jeff Bezos worked at D.E. Shaw and Company after graduating from Princeton, where he had a degree in electrical engineering and computer science. Bezos learned of Shel Kaphan's expertise through a connection at D.E. Shaw, and Kaphan became Amazon's first employee. Another early hire, Lovejoy, met Bezos while working at the hedge fund D.E. ## Hiring Practices from D.E. Shaw

Amazon's interview process involving tough brainteaser questions dates back to when CEO Jeff Bezos worked at investment-management agency D.E. Shaw, with certified candidates being flown for a day of "grueling" interviews. During interviews at D.E. Shaw, each interviewer would share their opinion using categories like "sturdy rent" or "sturdy no rent," and one "holdout" could determine whether someone was hired.

## The Bar Raiser System

While some have attributed the bar raiser innovation to Jeff Bezos, it emerged internally from the early leadership team and sought to codify a systemic way of hiring top talent that matched Amazon's culture. The bar raiser is an independent interviewer with veto power who ensures every new hire raises the average quality of the team. Bezos warned Amazon's early managers in 1998 that the company would live or die by the caliber of its hires, and this early doctrine was later formalized into Amazon's "bar-raiser" program.
