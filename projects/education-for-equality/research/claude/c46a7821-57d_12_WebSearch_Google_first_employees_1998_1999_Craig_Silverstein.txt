In 1998 Larry Page and Sergey Brin persuaded fellow Stanford PhD student Craig Silverstein to join the two-person garage startup for what early accounts put at a modest mid-$60k salary plus stock options. ## Urs Hölzle - Early Key Hire

By the time Hölzle joined Google in 1999, he was an experienced software developer, a contributor to DARPA's National Compiler Infrastructure Project, a co-founder of Anamorphic Systems, and an associate professor of computer science at UC Santa Barbara. Hölzle became Google's Vice President of Engineering.
