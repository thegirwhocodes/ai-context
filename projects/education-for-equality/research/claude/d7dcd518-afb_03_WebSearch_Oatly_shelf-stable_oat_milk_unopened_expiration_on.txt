## Unopened Shelf-Stable Oat Milk

Oatly shelf-stable oat milk lasts about a year from the production date, whether you store it in the fridge or at room temperature. You can store a shelf-stable oat milk carton at room temperature before opening it.

## Once Opened

Once opened, both shelf-stable and refrigerated oat milk packages stay fresh for five to seven days if you treat them appropriately. The best way to store an opened shelf stable or refrigerated oat milk is to keep it cold in the fridge.

## Storage Tips

For best results with opened cartons, make sure to tightly reseal the container and store it in the refrigerator. The recommended storage temperature should be at or below 40 degrees Fahrenheit, ideally in the back of the fridge on the lower shelf.
