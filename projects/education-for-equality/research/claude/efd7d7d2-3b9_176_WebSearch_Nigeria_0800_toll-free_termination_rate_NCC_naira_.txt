## 0800 Toll-Free Termination Rate

The charge for incoming calls to a Special Numbering Service (SNS) Provider using the 0800 numbering range is ₦15 per minute.

## Context on Special Numbering Services (0800)

The Special Numbering Service is a Value Added Service, introduced into the Nigerian market in 2006 to make communication easier for companies and individuals who would want customised and easily memorisable ways of reaching out to them such as 0700CALLNCC.

Amended rates for 0800 took effect November 1 (2022, based on the available documentation).
