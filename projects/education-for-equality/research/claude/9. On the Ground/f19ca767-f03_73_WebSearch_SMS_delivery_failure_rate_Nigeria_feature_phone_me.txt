## SMS Delivery Rates in Nigeria

Delivery success rates to Nigeria typically range from 85–95% depending on carrier relationships and routing quality. This means failure rates would generally be between 5-15%, depending on the SMS provider and service quality.

## Acceptable Failure Rate Standards

An acceptable failure rate is 5% or less. Below 90% delivery rate indicates significant issues that need investigation.

## Common Reasons for SMS Delivery Failures in Nigeria

Several factors contribute to SMS delivery failures:

- In Nigeria, delivery challenges are particularly pronounced in areas with poor network coverage, where message delivery can be inconsistent or delayed.

- Poor network coverage, switched-off mobile phones, full SMS inboxes, roaming phones, invalid phone numbers, networks that don't support status reports, or spam suspicion can cause delivery failures.

- Carrier policies or recipient preferences cause automatic message filtration or spam classification, with messages containing certain keywords, URLs, or non-verified sender IDs being more likely to be blocked.

While the search results don't specifically address feature phone failure rates, messages might not be delivered due to issues like full memory, coding errors, temporary network disconnection, or lack of support for certain message types or characters by the recipient's phone.
