# Foundational Literacy Course Overview

## Course Structure
This is a comprehensive reading program for ages 8-14, consisting of **8 units with 86 total lessons** and approximately **938 minutes of content**. The curriculum builds systematically from foundational skills to independent reading comprehension.

## Unit Breakdown

**Unit 1: Sound Awareness** (12 lessons, 10-12 min each)
Introduces phonological awareness through listening exercises, identifying beginning/ending sounds, rhyming activities, syllable manipulation, and sound pattern recognition.

**Unit 2: Vocabulary Building** (12 lessons)
Teaches contextual English vocabulary covering "body parts, family members, household items, market transactions, transportation, classroom objects, animals, plants, weather, and descriptive words."

**Unit 3: Listening Comprehension** (10 lessons, 12 min each)
Uses Nigerian-themed stories to develop comprehension skills including sequencing, inferencing, cause-and-effect understanding, and main idea identification.

**Unit 4: Oral English & Grammar** (10 lessons)
Covers sentence construction, subject-verb identification, verb tenses (past/present/future), question formation, adjectives, and comparative structures.

**Unit 5: Advanced Sounds** (10 lessons)
Focuses on phoneme segmentation, sound manipulation, alphabet knowledge, and letter-sound correspondences as preparation for phonics.

**Unit 6: Phonics** (12 lessons, 12 min each)
Uses Jolly Phonics ordering to teach letter sounds systematically, progressing from initial consonants and vowels through blending into words and tricky sight words.

**Unit 7: Blending & Decoding** (10 lessons)
Develops reading fluency using CVC words, consonant blends, digraphs (sh, ch, th), and increasingly complex sentence structures.

**Unit 8: Reading Comprehension** (10 lessons, 12 min each)
Applies all previous skills to independent reading with activities covering main ideas, detailed questioning, character analysis, sequencing, and summarization.

## Learning Progression
The curriculum follows a logical scope and sequence from auditory discrimination through decoding to fluent, comprehensible reading.
