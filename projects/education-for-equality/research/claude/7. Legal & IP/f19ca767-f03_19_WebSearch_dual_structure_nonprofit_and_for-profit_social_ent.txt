## Chan Zuckerberg Initiative - Dual Structure Model

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative is structured as a limited liability company (LLC) rather than a traditional charitable foundation, which allows it to invest in both for-profit startup companies and support nonprofit organizations, with more flexibility than tax-exempt foundations. CZI carries out its work through the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, LLC; the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Foundation (a 501(c)(3) private foundation); the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) at the Silicon Valley Community Foundation; and Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Advocacy, (a 501(c)(4) organization).

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative operates a hybrid investment approach supported by a hybrid organizational structure, embracing both nonprofit grants and for-profit impact investments to support high-impact social entrepreneurs. Any profit realized from venture investments will go back to the Initiative's philanthropic work.

## Bridge International Academies - For-Profit Social Enterprise Model

Bridge International Academies is a company which provides for-profit education to children in India, Kenya, Nigeria and Uganda, and calls itself a "social enterprise". As of March 2023, it is the world's largest for-profit primary education chain.

The company's mission is "Knowledge for all," targeting families living on US$2 per day or less with education services costing approximately US$5-6 per month per student, using an "Academy-in-a-Box" model that leverages technology and data to standardize education delivery through a vertically-integrated approach managing school construction, classroom instruction, curriculum development, teacher training, and financial management, employing tablets and smartphones with custom applications.
