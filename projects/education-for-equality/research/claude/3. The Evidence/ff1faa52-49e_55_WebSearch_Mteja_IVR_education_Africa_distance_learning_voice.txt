## Mteja Overview

Mteja.io is a cloud telephony service provider based in Nairobi, Kenya, that enables African businesses to streamline their business communication and take customer experience to the next level.

## IVR for Distance Learning in Rural Africa

Study material is shared via an agreed-upon technology such as email, Instant Messaging, WhatsApp, blog posts, or IVR which has proven to be the most effective technology for distance learning in rural Africa.

Organizations that aim to offer free training to underserved communities in rural Africa have opted for distance learning to offer training.

## Key Features for Education

Mteja's IVR solution offers several features specifically designed for educational use:

- Audio library: This feature allows you to create and manage audio files. You can either create the training material audio from text, record it on the web dashboard, or upload pre-recorded audio files.

- Schools can send daily lessons via bulk Interactive Voice Response (IVR) calls or SMS messages. This has the ability to assist learners in real-time. For example, during a lesson conducted via IVR calls, a learner can be prompted to "press 0 to speak to a live tutor" this allows a student to get assistance like they would if they were in a classroom.

## Real-World Application

Komaza, a forestry company that partners with smallholder farmers in Kenya to plant trees for sale as sustainable wood products, needed an IVR platform to help them empower and train farmers in the coastal region on good farming practices. Mteja's ready-made IVR solution enabled Komaza to reach out to thousands of farmers via scheduled, recorded broadcasts. The recordings were available in Swahili and Giriama, which are the most common local dialects in the coastal region.
