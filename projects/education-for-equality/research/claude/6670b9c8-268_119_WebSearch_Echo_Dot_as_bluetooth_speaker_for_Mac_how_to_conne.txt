## Basic Steps for Mac Connection

The Echo Dot features Bluetooth, so you can use it as a Bluetooth speaker for any compatible Bluetooth device.

### Method 1: Using the Alexa Web App

Start by opening alexa.amazon.com in your browser and logging in to your Alexa account. Once you do this, the left panel will display Settings. When you do, select Bluetooth by tapping the corresponding button. The Alexa App will search for available devices when you select "Pair a New Device".

Then, select "Apple menu" in the upper left corner, once the pop-up window appears, select "System Preferences" from the available options, go to the "Bluetooth" section, and in the "Device List", click on the "Connect" button next to your Echo Dot.

### Method 2: Using Voice Command

Put your Echo Dot in pairing mode by saying, "Alexa, pair Bluetooth." Then connect it through your Mac's Bluetooth settings.

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter connection issues, you can reboot your computer, turn your computer's Bluetooth off and back on, and move your Echo Dot closer to your computer.
