## AAA House

The Asian/Asian American House (AAA) is an open place for all members of the Wesleyan community to cultivate a continual awareness and interest of Asian and Asian American histories, literature, tradition, contemporary experiences, and concerns. AAA House is an option for any upper-class student interested in applying.

## Dinner Discussions for Asian American Identity Month

As part of Asian American Identity Month, AASC, Shakti, and other student groups facilitate a series of free dinner discussions.

The Asian American Student Collective (AASC) has hosted gatherings at AAA House (107 High Street) for various community events. Additionally, Shakti and the Asian American Student Collective have organized dinner and discussion events featuring student speakers and guest speakers.

In addition to academically focused events, there are cultural events and several dinner discussions, lectures, and student-led workshops. AASC and Shakti have held this identity month annually since 2015, when they organized Wesleyan's first-ever Asian American Heritage Month.
