## Unopened Body Lotion Shelf Life

Lotion expires within two years unopened, though most sources indicate a slightly longer timeframe. Most unopened commercial lotions can remain stable for 2-3 years from the manufacturing date, and when sealed properly, lotions can maintain their integrity for 2-3 years from the manufacture date.

More specifically:
- Unopened lotions can generally last up to three years if they have chemical components or non-organic while the organic ones like Eminence Skincare Lotion typically last up to 2 years.

## Storage Tips

To maximize shelf life, store lotion in a cool, dry location out of direct sunlight. Exposure to heat, direct sunlight, and humidity accelerates degradation. The bathroom, despite being a common storage location, is often too humid and warm for optimal preservation.

## Important Note

Expired lotion will lose efficacy and can grow bacteria and mold. If your lotion shows signs of degradation such as discoloration, separation, or an off smell, it's best to discard it.
