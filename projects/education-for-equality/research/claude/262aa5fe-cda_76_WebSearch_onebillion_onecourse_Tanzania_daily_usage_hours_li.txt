## Daily Usage Hours
onecourse delivers daily learning sessions of 30-40 minutes per day in math and literacy, exploratory and creative play, and access to an extensive digital library.

## Integrated Curriculum Structure
In onecourse, the child works through an ordered set of learning units covering reading, writing, and numeracy. The innovation is modular, giving the child a personalized and comprehensive learning experience, and the child learns at their own pace, building literacy and numeracy skills gradually.

## Tanzania Implementation
onetab is already being implemented at scale in Malawi, Tanzania, South Africa, Canada, Lebanon and the United States in both school and community settings. Additionally, a 15-month testing phase saw 2,000 children from 141 rural villages in Tanzania using the finalists' software until April 2019.

## Pedagogical Approach
The curriculum integrates literacy and numeracy within the same sessions. The numeracy work is built up very slowly and carefully by tackling concepts in stages and giving plenty of practice, basing early preparatory work on sorting and patterns, and ensuring children have done work with number lines and real-life objects before tackling addition and subtraction.
