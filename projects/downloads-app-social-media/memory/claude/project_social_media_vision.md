# Social Media Project Vision

## Who Naomi Is (Content Creator Context)
- College student at Wesleyan University, Nigerian background (Lagos to Connecticut)
- Spirit-filled Christian currently "basking in the love of God" — God has been telling her to post about Him
- Writes original worship songs (guitar + vocals) — first song: "King of all my life, I will sacrifice"
- Wants to share talking-head videos on what God has been teaching her
- Goals: build a personal brand, share faith authentically, create viral Christian content

## Content Strategy — 3 Modes
Naomi's content is organized into three distinct modes, each with its own editing style but sharing the same brand palette:

### Mode 1: Talking Head (Teaching / "What God's showing me")
- High-energy, viral-reach content
- Submagic-style word-by-word pop captions with yellow highlight on active word
- Jump cuts, zoom punches on key words, fast 2-4 second cuts
- Font: Poppins Bold, 65px, center of frame

### Mode 2: Worship / Music (Songs, guitar)
- Intimate, stop-and-feel content — captions should NOT compete with the music
- Gentle fade in/out captions, 2-3 word groups
- Long cuts (4-8 sec), cross dissolves, let it breathe
- Font: Cormorant Garamond Light, 45px, lower 20% of frame
- Color grade: warmer, softer, slight film grain, gentle vignette

### Mode 3: Lifestyle / Relatability (College life, Nigerian culture, daily walk)
- "She's just like me" content — bridges faith and mainstream
- Sentence pop captions (not word-by-word, more relaxed)
- Mix of jump cuts + natural pacing
- Font: Poppins SemiBold, 58px

## Brand Constants (Across All Modes)
- Color grade: always warm/golden, never cold or blue
- Palette: Warm Cream #FFF5E6, Honey Gold #D4A06A, Terracotta #C17852, Espresso #3D3532, Sage #A8B5A0
- Instagram story highlight covers: Warm Cream background + Terracotta icons
- Energy: warm, joyful, authentic
