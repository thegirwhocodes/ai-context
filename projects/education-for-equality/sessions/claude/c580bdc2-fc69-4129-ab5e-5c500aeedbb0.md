## What Cortex is
Personal AI agent — "your second brain." Three layers: **Ingestion → Interface → Action.** Ingests all your digital life (email, calendar, DMs, notes), builds a deep profile + memory + per-user LoRA, and acts for you with approval. Pitched now as one product at **$299/mo** (was $19/mo tool anchor), with Sage as the email agent-inside-Cortex and Sphere Energy as the calendar/energy-agent-inside-Cortex.
