## Tactiq Cloud Storage Support

Tactiq allows you to save transcripts and AI summaries to Google Drive for easy sharing in Google Docs or across your Google Workspace tools. You can also upload meeting notes to OneDrive so your content stays connected to Microsoft Teams and your Microsoft Office apps.

## Setting Up Integrations

To set up Google Drive integration, scroll down to the Storage section and find Google Drive then click Connect. Sign in with your Google account and click Continue and click Allow to grant Tactiq the necessary permissions.

## Export and Import Options

You can export Tactiq transcripts and upload them to Google Drive, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox for storage and sharing. You can download your transcript and upload it to any cloud storage provider you prefer.

## Automation Workflows

For automated workflows, when a meeting transcript is ready in Tactiq, the system will swiftly upload this important document to your Google Drive. This automation can be set up through integrations like Zapier.

The search results indicate that Tactiq supports native integrations with both Google Drive and OneDrive, allowing you to automatically save and store your meeting transcripts in these cloud services.
