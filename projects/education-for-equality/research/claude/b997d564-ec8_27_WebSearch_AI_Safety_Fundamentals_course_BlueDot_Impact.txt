## Overview

The AI Safety Fundamentals courses are one of the best ways to learn about AI safety and prepare to work in the field, and BlueDot Impact facilitates the courses several times per year.

## About BlueDot Impact

BlueDot Impact is a non-profit talent accelerator based in London and San Francisco that helps people build careers and organizations that positively impact the trajectory of AI through its courses, career support, events, and startup programs. Since 2022, they've trained over 5,000 professionals worldwide – from technical staff at frontier AI companies to government policymakers, with alumni working at organisations like Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and the UK's AI Security Institute.

## Course Tracks

The courses include multiple tracks:
- **Technical AI Safety**: This course gives a structured overview of key safety techniques and helps you understand what works, what fails, and where the gaps are.
- **AI Governance**: Covers why AI governance matters, who the players are, what technical factors shape choices, and the concrete tools governments can use to steer AI.

## Access and Cost

The courses are freely available and operate on a "pay-what-you-want" model.

## Community and Support

Every discussion is facilitated by an AI safety expert who can answer technical questions, challenge assumptions, and connect concepts to real work happening. BlueDot runs a talent pipeline, not just a course, actively scouting for high-potential participants and facilitating introductions to hiring managers and fellowship leads.
