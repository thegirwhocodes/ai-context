## Claude · 2026-04-04T19:18:24.480Z

**Rings** is a SwiftUI relationship management app (macOS + iOS) built around the concept of structured contact cycles.

**Core idea:** The "50-day cycle system" — friends are assigned to circles based on closeness, and the app automatically schedules when to reach out to each person:

| Circle | Contact Frequency |
|--------|------------------|
| Circle of 3 | Every 3 days |
| Circle of 12 | Every 12 days |
| Circle of 30 | Every 30 days |
| Circle of 50/100/200 | Every 50 days |

**Key features:**
- Rollover queue — missed contacts carry forward automatically
- Touchpoint logging (call, text, email, etc.)
- Local-first storage, with optional Supabase cloud sync across devices
- Biometric auth (based on the `BIOMETRIC_AUTH_ADDED` file)
- Daily notifications

**Stack:** Swift/SwiftUI, Supabase (optional, for sync/auth), targeting macOS 14+ and iOS 17+.
