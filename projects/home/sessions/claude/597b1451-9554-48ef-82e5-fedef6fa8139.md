**What are you building on Claude?**

> Sabi is a voice AI math tutor for children in Nigeria, built by Education for Equality. A child calls a phone number on any basic phone, with no smartphone or data plan needed. The tutor adapts to each child: it advances after consecutive correct answers and scaffolds after repeated mistakes. It remembers each child across sessions by phone number, including their module, level and accuracy history.
>
> Claude is the reasoning layer in the tutoring loop. It follows our pedagogy rules (one concept at a time, guided practice, then an independent check). It keeps replies to 1–3 sentences so they work on a phone call. It also carries each student's history into the next call. We chose Claude for instruction-following under tight constraints and for how well it handles a patient, encouraging teaching tone. Both matter when the learner is an eight-year-old on a phone line.
