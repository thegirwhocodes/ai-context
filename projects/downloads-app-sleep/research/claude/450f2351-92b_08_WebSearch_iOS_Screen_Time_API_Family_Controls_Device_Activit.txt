## How Apps Block Using Screen Time APIs

ManagedSettings is the enforcement framework that allows apps to define rules to shield apps and websites - preventing selected apps from being launched or shielding specific web domains in Safari.

The blocking process involves three key frameworks working together:

1. **FamilyControls & Authorization**: Family Controls prevents access to the Screen Time API without guardian approval, and once approved, the app cannot be removed without guardian approval.

2. **ManagedSettingsStore for Blocking**: Apps assign applicationTokens, categoryTokens, and webDomainTokens from FamilyActivitySelection to the shield's corresponding properties.

3. **DeviceActivity for Scheduling**: Defining rules in ManagedSettings doesn't automatically activate them - you need DeviceActivity to tell the system when to pay attention to the ManagedSettingsStore.

## Known Vulnerabilities

Notably, users can toggle off access to screen restrictions, and the issue persists that this toggle isn't behind the restrictions passcode. This has been identified as a significant flaw in apps like Freedom, where users can simply toggle off the access to screen restrictions without requiring the parent's approval.
