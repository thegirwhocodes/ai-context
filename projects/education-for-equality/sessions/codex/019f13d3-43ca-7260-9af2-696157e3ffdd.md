Yes. Glo has a business voice **Toll Free** service.

Published Glo pricing on the page:

- Security deposit: **₦5,000**
- Monthly rental: **₦1,500**
- Peak: **25 kobo/sec = ₦15/min**
- Off-peak: **20 kobo/sec = ₦12/min**

Source: [Glo Toll Free](https://www.gloworld.com/ng/business/sme/mobile-voice-data/toll-free)

So: **yes for Glo users**, but this is probably **not enough for Sabi by itself** if we need any child on **MTN, Airtel, Glo, or 9mobile** to call free. For that, we’d still need either a multi-network 0800/special-number provider, a VAS/special-number aggregator, or separate carrier arrangements. Glo also has **Toll Free Data**, but that is zero-rated data, not phone-call toll-free. Source: [Glo Toll Free Data](https://www.gloworld.com/ng/business/sme/mobile-voice-data/toll-free-data)
