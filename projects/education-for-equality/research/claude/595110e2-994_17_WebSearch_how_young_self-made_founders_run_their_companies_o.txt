## Core Operating System Frameworks

Operating cadence is a simple execution framework that connects strategy to weekly action through a system of meetings, dashboards, and metrics that creates accountability without bureaucracy, with weekly metrics, quarterly OKRs, and a CEO dashboard at its heart.

EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) is broader than a goal-setting framework, serving as a complete operating system covering six components: Vision, People, Data, Issues, Process, and Traction.

## Weekly Cadence and Metrics

The weekly check-in is the highest-return habit in the entire OKR process, with teams that have it completing 43% more OKRs than those without.

The spirit of the cadence follows: Weekly review of metrics, unblock issues, and make decisions; Monthly review of roadmap, forecast, and budget variances; Quarterly set OKRs, reset priorities, and recalibrate the operating plan; Annually align on strategy, capital plan, and hiring plan.

## CEO Dashboard and Metrics

The weekly metrics meeting runs like a flight check with no storytelling, just numbers, thresholds, and decisions. All metrics on the weekly dashboard must come from system-of-record data with no hand-edited slide decks or copy-paste errors, trusting the data or fixing the pipeline.

EOS advocates for a "Scorecard" with 5-15 numbers reviewed weekly, which differs from more complex metrics hierarchies, as the EOS approach to data is about creating basic accountability, not sophisticated analysis.

## OKRs Best Practices

Maximum three objectives per team and three KRs per objective, with the principle that when in doubt, remove and simplify, as focus is a feature, not a bug.

## Framework Comparison

OKRs work best for Series A–C companies with cross-functional teams and a bias for metrics that thrive in environments where speed and experimentation outweigh process, giving structure without suffocation for cultures that value autonomy.

EOS is best for founder-led businesses between $1M and $50M and companies drowning in operational chaos, providing instant structure when meetings are glorified complaint sessions and everyone is rowing in different directions.
