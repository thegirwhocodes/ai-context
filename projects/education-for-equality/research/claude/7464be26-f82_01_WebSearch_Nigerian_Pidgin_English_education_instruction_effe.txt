A 2025 study evaluated the implementation of Nigeria's National Language Policy (NLP) in secondary education, focusing on curriculum effectiveness and teacher readiness for 21st-century language classrooms across urban and rural contexts. ### Challenges in English Language Teaching

Research from 2024 notes that Nigeria is an African country with the largest number of English learners (ESL) in which Mother Tongue (MT) and Nigerian Pidgin (NP) make ESL instructor's job difficult, as Nigerian English has given entry to several pidgin words, phrases, and usages.
