## SCSP x AGI House Hackathon 2025 Grand Prize Winner

The grand prize winner of the SCSP x AGI House Hackathon in Alexandria, VA was SpectreRedTeaming, which won $25,000. The winning project demonstrated an agentic LLM jailbreaker using the Air Force's LLM, with the repository located at [JessSanChen/SpectreRedTeaming](https://github.com/JessSanChen/SpectreRedTeaming).

## Other Notable Submissions

There were other notable submissions to the hackathon as well:

1. **Port Sentinel Team**: This team did not win the hackathon but put in a winning effort. Their submission focused on combining port city vulnerability scanning with maritime port digital twin visualization to provide security insights for port infrastructure.

2. **Project Perception Team**: This team submitted to the SCSP and AGI House AI Expo Hackathon in Washington D.C. in June 2025.
