## Overview

Bakame AI is an artificial intelligence-powered education system that operates without internet access to bring the resource to all corners of the globe. The student-led venture earned international recognition, winning the Global Best M-Gov Award in Dubai.

## How It Works

Rather than relying on broadband infrastructure, the system operates through basic telephone networks. The user experience is straightforward: Users get the Bakame AI phone number, dial the toll-free number, and converse on the phone with an AI-driven chatbot. The caller converses with an AI chatbot focused on teaching the caller their desired language.

## Purpose and Technology

Bakame AI is described as an advanced AI-powered English learning platform. Bakame AI can be an information tool, with last-mile education being important because people don't necessarily know how to treat water, combat some diseases or know how to access some public services.

## Background

The system was founded by two TCU international students: Happy Niyorurema, a sophomore double-majoring in economics and finance who was raised in Rwanda, and Diarra Niang, a junior double-majoring in economics and business information systems, who was raised in Senegal.
