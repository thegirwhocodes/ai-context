## About IPA's Education Program

The Education Program Area at Innovations for Poverty Action aims to solve the gap in evidence of what works for education and to ensure that this evidence is used by local decision-makers.

## Education Research Focus

In partnership with academic leaders in the field, IPA's work has produced evidence on how to keep kids in school, such as through school-based deworming or sharing information on the benefits of schooling, and on how to make sure kids learn while there, such as in their teacher community assistant initiative in Ghana and through incentives, such as merit scholarships for students.

IPA's Education Sector works closely with ministries of education, NGOs, and other actors to support the use of research and data to improve outcomes for children and young people and their education systems and communities.

## Research Methodology and Scope

IPA conducts randomized controlled trials (RCTs), along with other types of quantitative research, to measure the impacts of development programs in sectors including education. The organization also works to improve methodological approaches—improving IPA's work in qualitative research is one of the methodological goals of IPA's Education Sector, with projects including responding to direct requests from ministries of education for targeted qualitative research.
