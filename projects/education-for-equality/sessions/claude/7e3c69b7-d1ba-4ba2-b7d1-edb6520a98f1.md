# Sabi Tester Guide — Nigerian Adult Volunteers

**Version 3.0 · Written 2026-07-30 · Naomi Ivie · nivie@wesleyan.edu · +1 860 436 7048**

---

## The short version

You'll make **6 short phone calls to Sabi over 2–3 weeks** and tell us what worked and what didn't. Phase 1 is 4 numeracy calls in week 1. Phase 2 is 2 literacy calls in week 2 or 3, after we've fixed what Phase 1 uncovered.

Total time: about 2 hours across the whole thing.

The most important thing you're testing is **whether Sabi can hear you**. She's built for Nigerian children on cheap phones in noisy homes, and her weakest spot is Nigerian accent — especially numbers. If she can't hear you, none of the other tests matter, so voice comes first on every call.

---

## How Sabi works

Dial **`+234 201 700 1459`**. After 2 seconds she hangs up. Wait 4–10 seconds and Sabi will call you back — she pays, not you. When you pick up you'll hear a warm female voice. She'll greet you, ask your name, and start a short lesson (numeracy in Phase 1, literacy in Phase 2). Answer her out loud like you would talk to a person. She'll ask questions, wait for you to reply, react to what you say, and end the call after 5–7 minutes. If the callback doesn't come within 60 seconds, dial again and stay on the line.

## The `*` (star) key — the most important button

**Anytime during the call, press `*` on your phone keypad.** Sabi stops mid-sentence, plays a short "okay, I'm listening" prompt, then a beep. Speak your feedback after the beep — anything at all, in whatever language feels natural. When you're done, she'll ask if you want to keep learning or stop for today.

You can also just *say* "I want to leave feedback" out loud at any point and she should stop the same way.

**This is your primary way of telling us what happened.** Please use it often.

---

## What you're testing for

### 1. Voice — can Sabi hear you? *(most important, every call)*

- Does she hear numbers right? Especially **30, 40, 45, 15, 50, 5** — say each one slow, natural, and fast on Call 4.
- Does she hear your **name** correctly? Use real Nigerian names — Oluremi, Gideon, Chinonso, Adaeze, Bunmi, Kemi, Tunde.
- After two unclear number tries, does she ask you to **press the digits + `#`** on the keypad? Does that work?
- When she half-hears a number, does she say something like *"I may not have heard the full amount, say it again slowly"* — instead of just marking you wrong?
- Does she ever **give you credit for an answer you didn't say**? Or **mark you wrong when you were right**?
- In noise (fan, TV, market, baby crying) — does she **hallucinate** words you never said? Watch the transcript.
- When you speak, does she **stop talking within about a second** (barge-in)?
