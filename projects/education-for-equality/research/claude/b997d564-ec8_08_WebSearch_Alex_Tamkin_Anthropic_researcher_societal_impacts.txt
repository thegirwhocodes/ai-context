## Background

Alex Tamkin is a researcher at Anthropic. He completed his PhD in Computer Science at Stanford, advised by Noah Goodman, where he was an Open Philanthropy AI Fellow.

## Research Focus

He's interested in how we can understand and improve the real-world impacts of AI systems. His research spans several key areas:

**Economic Impact Analysis**: Some of his recent projects include tracking the economic impacts of AI systems through the Anthropic Economic Index and several follow-up reports, and developing new interfaces for human-AI collaboration including Claude Artifacts.

**Privacy-Preserving Usage Analysis**: Tamkin developed Clio (Claude insights and observations), a privacy-preserving platform that uses AI assistants themselves to analyze and surface aggregated usage patterns across millions of conversations, without the need for human reviewers to read raw conversations.

**Task-Level AI Usage**: He analyzed over four million Claude.ai conversations through the lens of tasks and occupations in the U.S. Department of Labor's O*NET Database, and found that AI usage primarily concentrates in software development and writing tasks, which together account for nearly half of all total usage.
