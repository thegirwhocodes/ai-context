## Overview
Rori is an AI-powered math tutor accessible via WhatsApp, developed by Rising Academies. Rori is being developed across more than 50 of their schools in Ghana and Sierra Leone, and has already reached over 150,000 students across Ghana, Sierra Leone, Nigeria, Kenya and Rwanda.

## Cost Model
The cost model is particularly attractive for low-income settings. The intervention's low cost, estimated at $5 per student annually, makes it financially viable for scaling in contexts where traditional tutoring would be prohibitively expensive. Additionally, Rori is free and seamlessly integrates with highly available platforms.

## Technology & Accessibility
All you really need are a mobile internet connection and a $20 phone. The platform operates through WhatsApp, which is particularly suited to resource-limited environments. People typically purchase mobile data in small amounts—perhaps one gigabyte for a dollar, and text conversations use significantly less data than video, making this solution more cost-effective and accessible.
