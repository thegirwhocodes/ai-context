## Key Research Findings

Using the synthetic control method, research estimates that sanctions during the 2011-2014 period reduced Iran's real GDP by more than 17% with the largest drop occurring in 2012. Studies use a data-driven synthetic control unit constructed as a weighted average of the donor countries to estimate the negative effects of sanctions.

## Donor Pool Composition

The composition of donor pools varies across studies. One study's final donor pool consists of 18 countries, excluding those with missing observations or affected by major events such as war or sanctions, focusing on the MENA region, OPEC, and the OIC, comprising Algeria, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Congo, Egypt, Indonesia, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Malaysia, Malta, Morocco, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Tunisia, and the United Arab Emirates.

In another analysis, the counterfactual Iran is generated from five countries: Tunisia (41.2%), Qatar (21.7%), Malaysia (17.8%), Azerbaijan (12.4%), and Indonesia (6.9%).

## Oil Sector Focus

International sanctions imposed on Iran, targeting primarily Iran's key energy sector and its ability to access the international financial system, have harmed Iran's economic growth, specifically since 2011 through 2014.
