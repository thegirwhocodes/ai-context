## How This Research Would Help Spot Product Problems

This research would surface several critical risks:

- **Delivery channel mismatch**: If building a smartphone app when two-thirds of the target audience uses feature phones or 3G devices, the product misses its market. The GSMA data forces the question: voice calls and USSD, not apps.
- **Language of instruction trap**: The conflicting evidence on mother tongue vs. English means picking the wrong language could hurt learning outcomes. Pidgin complicates things further -- it is the lingua franca but has no standardized educational materials.
- **Cost viability**: At 8-10 cents/minute for ElevenLabs voice synthesis, a product serving millions of children faces unsustainable unit economics unless costs are driven down (hence the later research into YarnGPT, Chatterbox, and self-hosted TTS).
- **Literacy bootstrapping paradox**: If 50%+ of non-internet users cite literacy as the barrier, the product must teach literacy without assuming literacy -- pure voice/audio-first design is non-negotiable.
- **Infrastructure reality**: 120 million people offline means the product cannot assume internet connectivity. The toll-free/SIP trunk research in `efd7d7d2` shows the team recognized this and was exploring phone-call-based delivery.
