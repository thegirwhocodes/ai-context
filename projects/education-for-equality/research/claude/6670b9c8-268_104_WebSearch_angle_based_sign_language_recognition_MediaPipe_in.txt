A recent study proposes a set of 20 inter-joint angles computed from MediaPipe static hand keypoints, which are provably invariant to rotation, translation, and isotropic scaling. Because the angle embedding is intrinsically invariant to rigid transforms, it requires no spatial normalization across camera setups and is more privacy-preserving than RGB pipelines.

Research utilizing the 21 hand keypoints predicted by MediaPipe calculates additional 3D geometric features, including the angles of each finger joint, which are then subjected to deeper feature extraction using a Multilayer Perceptron (MLP).
