Naomi's goal for the Sabi site (stated 2026-08-17) is to rank first when people
Google "Sabi". The bare one-word query is not winnable in any near-term horizon.

Who holds the "Sabi" namespace:

- **sabi.am** — Nigerian B2B commerce company turned critical-minerals exporter.
  #2 on the FT ranking of Africa's fastest-growing companies, $46.5M revenue in
  2024, WEF member. Same country, same brand word, vastly more authority.
- Sabi Sabi — well-known South African private game reserve
- Wikipedia disambiguation page, plus two musicians named Sabi
- joinsabi.com (AI language learning), meetsabi.com (AI executive assistant),
  sabilearn.online (AI tutor, also Nigerian-Pidgin branded)

**Why:** Setting the target as "#1 for Sabi" makes the work look like a failure
even when it succeeds. The reachable, valuable targets are the qualified brand
queries — "Sabi AI tutor", "Sabi Education for Equality", "Sabi phone tutor",
"AI tutor over phone call Nigeria" — plus answer-engine visibility.

**How to apply:** Frame SEO progress against qualified brand queries, not the
bare word. Be direct that the one-word goal is out of reach rather than implying
it is coming.
