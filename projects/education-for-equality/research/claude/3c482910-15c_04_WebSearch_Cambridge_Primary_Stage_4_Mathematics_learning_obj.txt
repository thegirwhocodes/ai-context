The Cambridge Primary Mathematics Curriculum Framework is available to download on the Mathematics (0096) page of the Cambridge Primary support site and provides a comprehensive set of learning objectives that give a structure for teaching and learning.

The learning objectives are divided into three main areas called 'strands': Number, Geometry and Measure, and Statistics and Probability.

From the search results, I can see examples of Stage 4 learning objectives across different areas:

**Number objectives** include:
- Count on and count back in steps of constant size: 1-digit numbers, tens, hundreds or thousands, starting from any number, and extending beyond zero to include negative numbers
- Use knowledge of factors and multiples to understand tests of divisibility by 2, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100

**Fractions and Percentages:**
- Understand that a fraction can be represented as a division of the numerator by the denominator and understand percentage as the number of parts in each hundred, and use the percentage symbol (%)

**Geometry and Measure:**
- Estimate and measure perimeter and area of 2D shapes, understanding that two areas can be added together to calculate the area of a compound shape, draw rectangles and squares on square grids, and derive and use formulae to calculate areas and perimeters
- Estimate, compare and classify angles, using geometric vocabulary including acute, right and obtuse

For the **complete list** of all Stage 4 learning objectives, the Cambridge Primary Mathematics Curriculum Framework contains the learning objectives that provide a structure for teaching and learning and is included on the Cambridge support site.
