## DTIC Citation Access by Accession Number

To find a specific citation by accession number, you can take the accession number and add it to the end of the link: https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/citations/ (for example, https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/citations/ADA540204).

## STINET Database Overview

STINET is DTIC's flagship Scientific and Technical Information Network and one of DoD's largest repositories of scientific and technical information. Public STINET is available to the public, free of charge, and provides access to citations of unclassified, unlimited reports describing research efforts and other scientific and technical information held by DTIC.

Private STINET is a password-protected, value-added service for individuals who have registered with DTIC, offering online full-text versions of unclassified, unlimited documents, while STINET offers unclassified, unlimited full text documents in its Full Text Collection from September 1998 to present.
