## Overview
USAID's Development Innovation Ventures (DIV) is a program that sources proposals for innovations addressing international development challenges to improve the lives of people living in poverty in developing countries. Since 2010, DIV has provided $106 million in flexible, tiered grant funding to over 190 innovations, across 46 countries.

## Application Process
DIV is open year-round and reviews applications on a rolling basis. Proposals for funding are accepted year-round across three stages, from any type of organization, in any country in which USAID operates.

## Funding Tiers
DIV offers three main funding stages: Stage 1: Proof of Concept ($50,000 to $200,000 – up to 3 years), Stage 2: Testing and Positioning for Scale ($200,000 to $1,500,000 – up to 3 years), and Stage 3: Scaling ($1,500,000 to $5,000,000 – up to 5 years).

## Eligibility
DIV welcomes applications from individuals; public, private, for-profit, and nonprofit organizations; institutions of higher education; faith-based organizations; public international organizations; non-governmental organizations; U.S. and non-U.S. governmental organizations; multilateral and international donor organizations.

## How to Apply
For more information, visit www.usaid.gov/div/apply.
