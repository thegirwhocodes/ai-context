Quick answer to both:

**Learners vs Kids vs Calls:**
- **Learners** = every profile Sabi has ever created — one row per phone number, including you and adult testers. It's the master roster with each person's level, journey map, and history.
- **Kids** = the filtered pre-pilot view: only profiles marked as real children (consent, caregiver phone, school status, network…). Adult testers never appear here. This is the tab your board uses to track the 10-child pre-pilot.
- **Calls** = the phone sessions themselves, not people. Every call with its audio, transcript, quality status, and STT provider — click any row to review what the child said, what Sabi heard, and what Sabi replied.

So: Kids is a subset of Learners (who), and Calls is the evidence (what happened).

Help design systems and evaluate architectural decisions.

## Framework

### 1. Requirements Gathering
- Functional requirements (what it does)
- Non-functional requirements (scale, latency, availability, cost)
- Constraints (team size, timeline, existing tech stack)

- Component diagram
- Data flow
- API contracts
- Storage choices

- Data model design

- Load estimation
- Horizontal vs. vertical scaling
- Failover and redundancy
- Monitoring and alerting

- Every decision has trade-offs. Make them explicit.
