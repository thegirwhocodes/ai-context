## How to Calculate Year 3 SOM

The most practical approach is bottom-up modeling. Estimate how many customers your current team can realistically close in a year, multiply by your average contract value, and project this forward 1-3 years with reasonable growth assumptions.

For example, you might target 0.1% market share in year 1 (500 customers × $2,000 ACV = $1M), growing to 0.5% by year 3 through referrals and feature expansion.
