Question: Which international body can give E4E/Sabi a recognized "stamp" — like

## BOTTOM LINE
No international body will badge a self-authored curriculum "Cambridge-accredited
curriculum" or equivalent. The May 2026 finding HOLDS in 2026. Prestige names
accredit WHOLE SCHOOLS/INSTITUTIONS, not standalone third-party curricula.

## WHY THE BIG NAMES DON'T WORK (verified, high confidence)
- CAMBRIDGE INTERNATIONAL — registers SCHOOLS (4-phase, ~12 wks). Only third-party
  route "Endorsed resources" is for RECOGNISED PUBLISHERS producing textbooks that
  teach Cambridge's OWN syllabus; explicitly refuses independent/proprietary
  curricula. Endorsement "does not mean it is an official resource." → Only honest
  claim for a self-authored curriculum = "ALIGNED TO CAMBRIDGE STANDARDS", never
  "accredited". Cambridge uses "registration/approval" not "accreditation".
  Sources: cambridgeinternational.org /programmes-and-qualifications/, /join-cambridge/,
  /endorsed-resources/
- COGNIA (ex-AdvancED/SACS) — policy: "Cognia does not accredit providers of
  curriculum only or homeschools." Seal reserved for institutions/systems.
  Third-party vendors must state their accreditation does NOT extend to curriculum.
- CIS (Council of International Schools) — "accreditation is not offered to a part
  of a school"; requires CIS membership + 2 full academic years operating.
- ASIC — DOES issue a displayable "Badge of Accreditation" to whole institutions
  incl. online/distance providers, holistic (not per-programme). BUT documented
  diploma-mill criticism; NOT CHEA / US-DoE recognised. Using it could HARM
  credibility with serious funders (Gates/Mastercard/UNICEF). Avoid.
- IB / WASC / COBIS / Oxford AQA / Ofqual / EdReports — no verified self-authored-
  curriculum pathway surfaced (school-scoped, qualification-scoped, or US-K12-only).

## WHAT E4E CAN LEGITIMATELY DISPLAY (the real options)
1) EDUEVIDENCE / ICEIE (eduevidence.org) — BEST NEAR-TERM FIT.
   - Certifies evidence-of-IMPACT of the tech PRODUCT (Sabi voice tutor OR the web
     app), not curriculum content, not the school. 5Es framework (efficacy modeled
     on ESSA evidence tiers). Bronze/Silver/Gold. "Badge is for product, not provider."
   - Free database registration + ~$250 fee + 5-7 business days + valid 24 months.
   - 1,500+ products from 70+ countries; awardees incl. Kahoot!, Age of Learning,
     GraphoGame. Current via BETT 2025 + ISTE+ASCD EdTech Index 2025.
   - Claim: "EduEvidence-certified EdTech."
   - Caveat: fees-2 URL intermittently 404s; "$250 + free registration" live-confirmed,
     "5-7 days" partly from cached snapshot. ICEIE acronym rendered inconsistently.
2) NCFE (UK Ofqual-recognised awarding body, 175+ yrs) — genuine awarding-body
   recognition of SELF-AUTHORED content via (a) Customised Qualifications (unregulated
   quals written by the org, then accredited by the awarding body) or (b) Endorsed
   Programmes (in-house informal learning endorsed, no learning outcomes/assessment).
   Fees/timeline/Nigeria-eligibility NOT in verified set. Real but more effort.
3) ASSURED BY PEARSON (rebranded from "Pearson Assured", Dec 18 2024) — quality-
   assures the ORG'S OWN training programmes (no Pearson curriculum needed). Yields
   logo + Credly learner badges. BUT assures QUALITY PROCESSES not content/standard/
   rigour; requires >=18 MONTHS PRIOR DELIVERY (blocks E4E until after the pilot);
   CONTRACTUALLY BANS the words "accredited/certified/qualified/official" and any
   level, and "cannot reference any national or international standards." Post-2027 option.

## MARKETING-CLAIM MATRIX (verified)
NONE permit a generic "X-accredited curriculum" for self-authored content.
- Cambridge → "aligned to Cambridge standards" (only)
- EduEvidence → "EduEvidence/ICEIE-certified [product]" (product efficacy)
- Pearson → "Assured by Pearson [programme]" + Credly badges (org QA process)
- NCFE → "NCFE-accredited customised qualification" / "NCFE-endorsed programme"
- ASIC/CIS/Cognia → institution seal ONLY if the org is accredited as an institution

## RECOMMENDATION
1. NOW: EduEvidence-certify Sabi (~$250, days). Display "EduEvidence-certified EdTech."
2. Keep "Cambridge-aligned" for Grade 4 curriculum; never upgrade to "accredited".
3. Real credibility lever = pilot data (0.30 SD TaRL) + named expert/UBEC sign-off,
   NOT a purchasable badge. Ties to the external-validator step in the 6-month plan.
4. Post-pilot (2027+, after 18 mo delivery): Assured by Pearson for a brand-name QA seal.

## FOLLOW-UP SWEEP (2026-06-05, 107 agents — closing the gaps above)
- ISO 21001:2018 = the ONE genuinely org-agnostic option. Scope expressly covers "any
  organization that uses a curriculum to support the development of competence through
  teaching, learning or research, regardless of type, size or method of delivery." So a
  non-school voice/web tutor IS eligible in principle. Excludes pure content/product
  vendors. Cheapest near-term LEGIT path among verified options. (Concrete cost/timeline
  for a tiny org NOT nailed down — needs a quote from an accredited cert body.)
- UNESCO prizes are CASH AWARDS, NOT seals, and all require third-party NOMINATION +
  ~1yr track record E4E lacks now:
  • King Hamad Bin Isa Al-Khalifa Prize for ICT in Education — 2 winners/yr, US$25k each,
    medal+diploma. Self-nomination BARRED; must be nominated by a National Commission for
    UNESCO or an NGO in official partnership; project implemented >=1 year. Non-school type
    is NOT a disqualifier. Win → "laureate of the UNESCO King Hamad ... Prize."
  • International Literacy Prizes (King Sejong + Confucius) — US$30k each, medal+diploma;
    NGOs in literacy promotion eligible in principle; same nomination gate.
- UNICEF Innovation/Venture Fund = equity-free investment up to $100k, restricted to
  FOR-PROFIT open-source tech startups registered in a programme country. Explicitly NOT
  nonprofits → E4E INELIGIBLE as structured. It's funding, not a recognition mark.
- Net: no cheap self-earnable international seal in 6-12 mo. ISO 21001 is the only
  eligible-in-principle near-term path; UNESCO prizes are aspirational post-pilot (need a
  nominator + track record); UNICEF fund needs a for-profit entity.
