## AIVA Program Overview

The AI Ventures Accelerator (AIVA) is a free, 12-week online venture accelerator from Technovation and Generation Unlimited, designed for young women between the ages of 18 and 25 from UNICEF Program Countries and the U.S. who want to turn bold ideas into real, AI-enabled startups.

## Pitch Deck and Submission Requirements

By the end of the program, participants produce a working prototype or MVP, a demo video, and an investor-ready pitch deck. A pitch deck is a brief and enticing visual presentation used to overview your business plan for potential investors or customers, often created with Google slides, PowerPoint, Prezi, or other presentation tools.

## Top 10 Selection and Demo Day

The AIVA program culminates in a Demo Day where the top 10 teams receive $10,000 in equity-free seed funding, and 15 additional teams will receive further incubation support. Top teams are invited to pitch live at an in-person Demo Day for a chance to win a share of $10,000 in seed funding and continue building their ventures beyond AIVA.
