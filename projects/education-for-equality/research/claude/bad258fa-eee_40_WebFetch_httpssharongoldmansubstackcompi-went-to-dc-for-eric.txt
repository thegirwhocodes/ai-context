# AI Expo 2025: Sharon Goldman's On-the-Ground Report

## Atmosphere & Vibe

The Walter E. Washington Convention Center buzzed with an intense focus on national security. Goldman describes a "crowded" scene where "drones buzz overhead" among thousands of government, academic, and private sector professionals. The dominant mood: urgency around U.S.-China technological competition.

## Key Observations

**Defense Integration**: The expo normalized Big Tech's embrace of military contracts. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick stressed the stakes: "We must win the AI race, the quantum race" and emphasized building additional power infrastructure for AI data centers.

**Military Focus**: Discussions centered on AI's battlefield applications—particularly Ukrainian drone attacks on Russian bases—rather than productivity gains or job displacement concerns.

## Notable Exhibitor Highlights

- **OpenAI**: Showcased its o3 reasoning model deployed on a secure Los Alamos National Laboratory supercomputer
- **Meta**: Partnered with defense contractor Anduril on EagleEye, a military augmented reality headset
- **Scale AI**: Expanding government and DoD contracts for data labeling services

## Gala Event

The Washington AI Network gala featured sponsorships from OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft, and Amazon, with IBM as a table sponsor.
