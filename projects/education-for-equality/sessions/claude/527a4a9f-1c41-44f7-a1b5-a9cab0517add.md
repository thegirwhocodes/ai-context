### Is 91GB "System Data" normal?
**No — it's about 3x high.** Healthy System Data on current macOS is ~12–30GB. Yours is inflated by three things: (1) the **iOS Simulators** (legitimately big for a dev machine), (2) the **`mediaanalysisd` bug** — Apple's photo-analysis daemon is leaking cache (people have hit 80–140GB; 8.9GB is a clear instance of the bug), and (3) **cloud-sync app caches** (OneDrive, Spark, Spotify, VoiceMemos).

## RAM situation
8GB on an M1, and it's **maxed out**: only ~60MB free, and the memory compressor holds ~12.7GB of data squeezed into 3.4GB of RAM, with active swapping to disk (18M swapouts lifetime). This is normal-ish for 8GB in 2026 but means you're constantly swapping — which is *worse* when the disk is full. **Freeing disk space will directly help RAM performance**, since swap needs free disk. There's no RAM "leak" to fix; 8GB is just tight for your workload (multiple Electron apps: Cursor, Code, Antigravity, Brave, Slack, Teams, Spotify). Closing apps you're not using is the only lever short of a new machine.
