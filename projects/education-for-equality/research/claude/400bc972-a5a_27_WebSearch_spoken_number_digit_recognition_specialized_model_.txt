## Recognition Models and Accuracy

A hybrid structure combining residual convolutional neural network and bidirectional gated recurrent unit has been designed for speaker-independent digit recognition, with recognition accuracy of 98.53%, 96.10%, and 95.9% for training, validation, and test respectively.

Historical approaches included hidden Markov models (HMMs) and MLP neural networks achieving 99.1% accuracy for discrete and 83.7% for continuous numbers, and Gaussian mixture models with Delta-Delta MFCC feature extraction achieving 99.3% accuracy in Arabic digit recognition.

## Robustness in Noisy Environments

A critical challenge is noise robustness. An LSTM-based network achieved 91.7% accuracy in noise-free conditions but dropped to an average of 69.22% when various noises were added. More advanced methods show better performance—proposed hybrid methods exhibit an average performance improvement of 26.88% over phoneme unit-based LSTM methods for Persian numbers in noisy environments.

## Telephony Applications

Automatic recognition of spoken connected digit strings has become important in the telephone industry, with telephone numbers and credit card numbers being examples of connected digits. Specialized word models have been designed specifically for connected digit recognition.
