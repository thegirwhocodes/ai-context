Deep research (session f8d1fd1f) into how Sabi actually gets recruited by Lagos State + regulatory landmines, layered on top of the existing goal docs (`5. Cost & Feasibility/goals/GOAL_2_LAGOS_STATE_STEPS.md`).

**Entry path (Goal 2 — 2.4M kids):** EKOEXCEL was NOT a competitive tender. NewGlobe came in as **technical partner to LASUBEB** under Gov. Sanwo-Olu's **THEMES agenda** (launched 2019), carried by evidence. Sabi's route mirrors it: Dr. Sonia → LASUBEB relationship, published pilot data, then a THEMES-aligned proposal. Formal large-deal vehicle = **Lagos State PPP Law + Office of PPP** (ppplagos.lagosstate.gov.ng). Lagos disfavors *unsolicited* proposals EXCEPT those with "genuine innovation and/or proprietary technology" — voice-AI on a feature phone qualifies. Unsolicited proposal requires CAC registration + **valid tax clearance certificate** + authorized rep. School-level approval runs through **OEQA (Office of Education Quality Assurance)** + Ministry of Basic & Secondary Ed: CAC cert, ID, ¾ Nigerian teachers, site inspection ~14 days.

**Two landmines our docs under-flag:**
- **ARCON** pre-vets EVERY advert before it runs and prohibits ads that "exploit the inexperience or credulity of children" (Advertising Code Art. 80; children's personal info to third parties only with parental consent). → Goal 1 CPM model MUST be ARCON-cleared and stay strictly brand-as-prop, never a call-to-buy. Real gate on "THE BUSINESS."
- **NDPA 2023**: child = under 18; parental/guardian consent + govt-ID age verification (§31) required. There IS an education exemption (processing for education under a professional's responsibility), but consent-first is safest. GAID leaves 13–17 grey.

**Why gov applications die:** no published RCT-style data · claiming "NERDC approved" before formal review · child cohort without guardian-consent infrastructure · pitching as a NewGlobe competitor instead of household complement · no registered Nigerian entity · burdening families with airtime cost.
