## General Voice API Pricing Information

Voice pricing is quoted per minute, although charges apply per second for all countries. With the Voice API, you pay per minute for calls made and received via the API.

## Inbound vs. Outbound Calls

If you purchase a voice-enabled virtual number, the price of an inbound leg of a call is also billed per second, at the rate specified on the pricing page. The billing structure varies based on your setup—if forwarding to SIP, you only pay for the inbound leg from the caller to Vonage, but if forwarding to phone, you pay for both the inbound and outbound legs.

## Toll-Free Numbers

Toll-free numbers are inbound-only, unless stated otherwise. For toll-free and other non-fixed/mobile networks, the default price of €0.414 is charged (€0.01314 in the US).
