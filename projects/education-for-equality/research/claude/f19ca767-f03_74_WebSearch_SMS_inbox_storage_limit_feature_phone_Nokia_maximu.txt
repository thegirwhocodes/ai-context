## SMS Storage Capacity

Nokia phones have varying SMS storage limits depending on the model:

- The Nokia 6230i can store up to 150 maximum-size text messages in addition to SIM card inbox messages
- The Nokia 8290's inbox and outbox can contain a total of up to 50 messages, depending on the SIM card
- The Nokia 5190's Inbox and Outbox can contain a total of up to 50 messages, depending on the SIM card

## Message Storage Location

SIM card storage generally only holds about 20 messages, and some Nokia models allow messages to be saved in either phone memory or SIM card memory.

## Handling Full Inboxes

When the inbox reaches capacity, the message indicator blinks, the phone displays "No space: message waiting," and users should delete one or more old messages to make room for new messages.

## Expanding Storage

Nokia E-line phones with Symbian OS allow use of memory cards and customizable storage for SMS messages, providing greater capacity for users who need to store thousands of messages.
