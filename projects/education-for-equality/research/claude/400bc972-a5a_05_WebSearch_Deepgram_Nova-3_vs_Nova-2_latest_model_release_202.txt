## Release Timeline

Deepgram released Nova-3 in January 2025 as the replacement for Nova-2, its previous flagship speech recognition model. Nova-3 achieved General Availability (GA) in February 2025, with multilingual GA in April 2025.

## Accuracy Performance

Streaming word error rates dropped from 8.4% to 6.84%, with a 54.3% reduction in streaming errors. More specifically, Nova-3 boasts a median WER of 6.84% on real-time audio streams, marking a 54.2% improvement over the next-best competitor at 14.92%.

## Key Differentiators

**Multilingual Capabilities:**
While Nova-2 transcribes different languages well, Nova-3 can seamlessly follow conversations that jump between languages mid-sentence. Nova-3 can transcribe audio that switches between languages mid-sentence across 10 languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, and Dutch).

**Language Coverage:**
Nova-3 launched with 36 supported languages and Deepgram expanded it through 2025 to 50-plus languages.

**Advanced Features:**
Keyterm Prompting allows developers to inject up to 100 domain terms, providing up to 6× lift on domain vocabulary without retraining. Nova-3 performs PII redaction on up to 50 entities in real time—critical for finance and healthcare compliance.

**Latency:**
Nova-3 maintains parity latency with Nova-2 (< 300 ms).

## Pricing

Nova-3 streaming starts at $0.0077/min (batch tier $0.0066/min).

Nova-3 is Deepgram's current flagship model, representing a significant advancement over Nova-2 with substantially improved accuracy, multilingual capabilities, and enterprise features.
