## Shelf Life

KIND bars have a shelf life of around 6 to 12 months from the date of production, though some varieties may last longer or shorter. For specific varieties, some pressed KIND bars have a 12-month shelf life.

## Understanding Expiration Dates

KIND Bars come with a Best By date printed on the packaging, provided by the manufacturer as a guideline for when the product is expected to retain its optimal quality in terms of flavor and texture. The labeled "best by" dates are an estimate of optimal flavor and quality and not a date of expiration.

## Storage Tips to Maintain Freshness

To keep KIND bars fresh longer, store them in their original packaging in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and any heat sources. High humidity can lead to moisture absorption, making the bars soggy and accelerating spoilage.

## Signs of Spoilage

Watch for stale taste or texture, an unusual or rancid odor (especially from the nuts), and visible mold, which all indicate the bar should not be consumed.
