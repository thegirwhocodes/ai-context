## Overall Architecture

Freedom uses three main approaches to block distractions: Host file blocking on Mac and Windows by modifying your computer's host file to redirect blocked websites to localhost; VPN-based blocking on iOS and Android by creating a local VPN to filter internet traffic; and app termination on desktop by forcibly quitting and preventing launching of blocked programs.

## VPN/Proxy System

When your traffic passes through the blocking proxy/local VPN, no logs are created, stored, saved, or transmitted, and the blockers look for matches on the websites accessed and make real-time block/no-block decisions. Freedom uses Apple's Network Extensions technology to install a "Limited VPN" that blocks content on your phone where it is fast and secure and never sent to servers, and while the Freedom Profile uses VPN technology, it is not a VPN because it never connects to a remote service and never sends your traffic off the phone.

## Privacy & Data Handling

The purpose of syncs is to send your blocklist to the device and to inform it when a session starts or ends, and Freedom does not upload any personal data from the clients to the Freedom.to service.

## Multi-Platform Syncing

A key feature is session syncing - start a 2-hour focus session on your phone and it automatically activates on all your other devices too.
