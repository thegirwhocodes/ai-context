## Main Reasons for Call Abandonment

Long hold times are the #1 cause of abandonment across every industry surveyed, and this hasn't changed in 20 years. More specifically, 61% of phone customers say being placed on hold is their top grievance.

## Impact of Pre-Call Announcements and Wait Time Communication

Pre-call announcements appear to be effective at reducing abandonment. Announcing estimated wait time and position in queue isn't just polite, it's proven to reduce early hang-ups, with a simple announcement like "You're third in line, estimated wait time is under two minutes" keeping many from giving up.

## Specific Survey Abandonment Data

54% of callers hang up after eight minutes on hold. Additionally, 60% of callers won't wait on hold for more than 1 minute.
