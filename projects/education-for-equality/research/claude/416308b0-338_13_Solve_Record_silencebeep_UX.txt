### Key conclusions

1. **`timeout` IS a real, supported `<Record>` attribute.** The PHP SDK's `buildRecord` emits `timeout="..."` and validates it numeric; the Node SDK's Joi schema declares `timeout: Joi.number().integer().positive()`. It is the per-action time budget (in seconds) for capturing the recording — i.e. how long AT waits before giving up. Combined with `trimSilence`, this is the mechanism that ends the partial recording without forcing the child to wait the full `maxLength` or press `#`.

2. **Important nuance / risk:** AT does **not** document a dedicated "end recording N seconds after the caller stops speaking" silence-detection attribute. `trimSilence` only *trims silent sections from the saved file* — it does **not** auto-stop the live recording. The honest reality is: with AT's partial `<Record>`, the recording ends when (a) `finishOnKey` is pressed, (b) `maxLength` is reached, or (c) `timeout` elapses. There is no per-utterance voice-activity auto-stop. So for a child who can't press `#`, the practical lever is to **lower `maxLength`** so the wait after they stop talking is short and tolerable, and **add `timeout`** as a safety cap, and **enable `playBeep`** so they know when to talk.

3. **The docs' canonical "partial recording" form nests `<Say>`/`<Play>` INSIDE `<Record>`** (`<Record ...><Say>...</Say></Record>`). Your current code uses the separate-sibling form (`<Play/>` then `<Record/>`), which also works (it's the "play then record" flow). No change needed there — keeping siblings is fine and is what's deployed; I'm only changing the `<Record>` attributes.

4. `playBeep="false"` is the direct cause of "no audio cue to talk." Set it to `true`.

5. Keep `callBackUrl` (capital B, U) exactly — per your instruction it was just fixed and must stay. (Note: AT docs/SDK use `callbackUrl`, but your deployed/working code uses `callBackUrl` and you confirmed that is the correct working casing — do not touch it.)

### Recommended config for a young child

- `playBeep="true"` — audible cue so the child knows to start talking.
- `maxLength="6"` — short enough that the post-speech wait is bearable (down from 10s). A young child's answer is a few seconds; 6s gives room without a long dead-air tail.
- `timeout="4"` — gives up ~4s if the child says nothing at all, so the loop advances instead of hanging.
- `finishOnKey="#"` — kept (harmless; an adult can still short-circuit).
- `trimSilence="true"` — kept; trims dead air off the saved clip before it hits STT.
