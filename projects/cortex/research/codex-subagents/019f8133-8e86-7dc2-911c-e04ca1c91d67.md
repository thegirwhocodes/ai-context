### PostgREST notification proof

Nine migration statements emit identical `NOTIFY pgrst, 'reload schema'` messages. PostgreSQL can coalesce identical notifications within one transaction, so do not require nine.

The harness must:

- Commit `LISTEN pgrst` on a separate connection before the migration.
- Require at least one `reload schema` notification only after successful commit.
- Require zero notification after the deliberate rollback scenario.
