I've performed the web search on stablecoins and their impact on emerging markets. Here are the key findings:

## Deposit Drain in Emerging Markets

Standard Chartered has estimated that banks in emerging markets could lose as much as $1 trillion in deposits to stablecoins. This represents a significant threat to traditional banking systems in developing economies.

## Specific Country Examples: Nigeria, Argentina, Turkey

In countries like Nigeria, Argentina, and Turkey, households are already using dollar-pegged stablecoins to protect savings from devaluing local currencies, bypassing official exchange rates and domestic banking systems entirely. In a 2024 Visa-sponsored survey across Brazil, Turkey, Nigeria, India, and Indonesia, about 47% of crypto-technology users reported using stablecoins (or digital dollars) to save.

## Impact on Local Credit Supply

When people hold tokens instead of bank deposits, banks lose the funding base they use to make loans. This directly constrains the credit available in these economies. For each $100 billion of net deposit drain not recycled to banks, empirical pass-throughs imply a $60 to $126 billion contraction in bank lending.

## Macroeconomic Concerns

Stablecoins can accelerate the structural dependence of developing economies on the dollar while making it easier to evade capital controls, leading to larger inflows during stable periods and faster capital flight during stress.

## Scale of Cross-Border Usage

The scale of stablecoin adoption in these regions is growing rapidly. In 2024, Turkey alone processed over $63 billion in cross-border stablecoin payments, while Argentina and Nigeria also saw sharp rises in usage.
