# Sabi — Deep Synthesis

## What Sabi precisely is

**Sabi is the voice-AI *delivery channel* of Education for Equality** — not "the product," not "the platform." "Sabi" = "to know" in Nigerian Pidgin, pronounced **SAH-bee**. It's an adaptive AI tutor that teaches Nigerian children foundational literacy and numeracy through **5–7-minute phone calls on any $5 feature phone**, no internet, no smartphone, no cost to the family. One of two E4E channels — the other is the curriculum-app web platform; the pipeline is *child masters foundations via Sabi → graduates to Curriculum App*.

Two product lines emerge from one pipeline:
- **Sabi for Organizations** — managed delivery ($2–15/student depending on scale; governments, UN, foundations, telco CSR)
- **Sabi for Developers** — hosted Africa-optimized voice AI API (the picks-and-shovels play; $0.05–0.15/min, undercuts Vapi/Retell 40–65%)

## The problem it exists to solve

- **18.3M out-of-school children** in Nigeria (highest in the world; UNICEF 2024)
- **70% of 10-year-olds cannot read a simple sentence** (~23M enrolled non-readers; World Bank 2022)
- **80% pipeline attrition**: 30M start primary → 6M reach SS3
- **$40–58B/year economic cost** (CSEA/IDRC + World Bank HCI+ 2026)
- **16% of planned learning actually happens** even at EKOEXCEL (the gold-standard Lagos program): 45% pupil attendance × 45% lesson completion × 79% teacher attendance. **Sabi exists to deliver the missing 84%.**

The unfair-advantage data: Naomi has access to EKOEXCEL Year 5 (343K pupils), NALABE 2017 (43,200 students), Dr. Sonia Ivie's 2021 LASUBEB special-schools field memo (with ~30 named head-teacher contacts and phone numbers — pre-vetted pilot recruitment list), NewGlobe Outcomes Fund Lagos market engagement, and the EKOEXCEL Riverine schools roster. Most pitch decks cite UNICEF stats; Sabi cites ground-truth pupil-level data.

## Four personas

1. **Never-reader enrolled child (~23M).** P5 reading at P2 level, class of 80, one textbook, generalist teacher absent 1/5 days. Sabi delivers structured pedagogy after school.
2. **Out-of-school child in Lagos (704K, per LASUBEB 2023–24).** Hawking happens during school hours; evenings child is idle — that's when Sabi calls. ~484K–684K reachable in Lagos on feature phones + smartphone households avoiding data.
3. **Lagos special-needs / inclusive-unit pupil** (Dr. Sonia's 2021 LASUBEB study). 5 Special Needs Schools + 2 Correctional Centres + 31 Inclusive Units inside EKOEXCEL. Voice format adapts to age range (4-yr-olds with 17-yr-olds in one room is current reality). Audio-only is itself an accessibility win.
4. **Adult women / market traders (~45M illiterate; ~90M phone-accessible).** Lagos market woman ~₦28K/mo, illiterate but doing mental arithmetic. Revenue-generating: ₦500–2,000/mo carrier-billed = $0.31–1.25/mo. 1% of 90M × ₦1K = **$6.8M/yr**.

## Pedagogy (the 7-framework stack)

1. **TaRL — Teaching at the Right Level** (Pratham J-PAL): diagnostic places child at actual level, not grade. 0.10–0.71 SD across 6 RCTs, 20+ countries.
2. **Jolly Phonics 7 letter groups** — validated in Nigeria with 72,127 pupils (+60% word recognition, British Council).
3. **Naira-first numeracy** (Nature 2025 Sokoto, n=5,997) — Nigerian kids solve market math **9× better** than abstract: 48.5% vs 5.4%. Hardcoded into the system prompt: *"NEVER say 'What is 15 plus 7?' ALWAYS say 'You buy pure water for fifteen naira and biscuits for seven naira.'"*
4. **Adaptive branching** — right → harder; wrong → scaffold with smaller numbers/rephrase; 3+ correct → advance module.
5. **Spaced repetition** — returning students get one recall question from last session (doubles as identity check on shared phones).
6. **Never says "wrong"** — only "Almost!", "Good try!", "Let me help you think differently."
7. **Two-phase literacy** — Phase 1 oral-only (3 mo); Phase 2 oral + SMS letter cards (~₦200–500/child print materials).

**Emotion tags** `[laugh]`/`[chuckle]`/`[sigh]` are inserted in 30% of 3,624 dialogue lines, rendered expressively by Chatterbox via the auto-detected exaggeration slider. I saw them embedded directly in the lesson script for Module 2 Lesson 13 — the AI literally says `[chuckle]` and Chatterbox renders the breath.

**Lesson architecture (5 beats, every call):**
1. Greeting + recall (30s)
2. Today's lesson — ONE concept via market scenario (90s)
3. Guided practice — 1–2 problems together (90s)
4. Independent check — 1–2 problems alone (60s)
5. Wrap-up + hook for next time (30s)

## Claude · 2026-06-07T23:09:54.818Z

**LeetCode** is the site that hosts thousands of algorithm and data-structure puzzles (reverse a linked list, find the shortest path in a graph, dynamic-programming pyramid problems, etc.).

A **"LeetCode gauntlet"** is the multi-round interview process — usually phone screen + 3–5 onsite rounds — where the company makes you solve those puzzles under time pressure on a whiteboard or in a shared editor, often with little connection to the actual job.

**Who does it:** Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, Stripe, big banks, top consulting firms with eng arms — most of FAANG and big-co eng.
**Who increasingly doesn't:** YC seed-stage AI-native startups (they prefer "build a feature in 2 hours with AI tools" or "walk me through code you've shipped"). Cursor, Vercel, Anthropic Fellows all skew this way.

So when you wrote *"roles that screen primarily on leetcode"* in your "not a fit for" line, you're saying: I don't want the 5-round Google-style algorithm gauntlet — I want the "show me what you've shipped" screen. Which is exactly the YC-startup interview format your résumé crushes.

It's an industry-fluent move. The founders reading WaaS profiles all know the term.
