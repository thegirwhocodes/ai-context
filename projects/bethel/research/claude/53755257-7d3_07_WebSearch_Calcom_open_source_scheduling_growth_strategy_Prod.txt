## Cal.com Overview

Cal.com is described as "The open source Calendly alternative" that allows users to "Self host it, or have it hosted." The platform stands out for open-source, embeddable scheduling with APIs, workflows, and team routing for product teams and marketplaces.

## Growth Strategy

Cal.com aims to empower the next generation to build scheduling applications on top of the platform, similar to how WordPress built an entire ecosystem around its open core. To grow and finance their team, they've launched a hosted plan while also allowing users to self-host the entire codebase.

Cal.com has ambitious plans to propel the platform to be a critical part of remote human-to-human connections, with a goal to connect 1 billion people by 2031.

## Product Hunt Community

Thanks to the huge traction gained during their Product Hunt launch, they've been working hard to improve the product with the help of the community. On the 15th of each month, on the anniversary of their Product Hunt, they release version updates and announce new funding support, which generates media coverage and social media spikes in new customers.

## 2025-2026 Focus

Cal.com's engineering organization consists of five core teams including consumer, enterprise, and platform-focused teams, plus a Community Team responsible for quickly reviewing PRs from the open source community.
