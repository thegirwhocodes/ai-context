## Open Up Resources

Open Up Resources offers curricula designed around excellent, whole texts that help students build real-world content knowledge as literacy skills flourish, with active support for diverse classrooms including advanced tools for ELLs, MLLs, and students with disabilities. The 2025 Edition improves upon the nationally acclaimed 2017 Edition by incorporating the latest literacy research and teacher feedback. You can access these resources at openupresources.org.

## Other Free Grade 4 ELA Curriculum Options

Several other free ELA curriculum options are available:

1. **EL Education Curriculum**: All of the curriculum's supporting and student-facing materials are available as downloadable Word files, and to access module assessments and answer keys, you can create a free account in their Resource Library.

2. **Fish Tank Learning**: Ready to teach resources for 4th grade ELA are available for download.

3. **Louisiana ELA Guidebooks**: The Louisiana Department of Education created ELA Guidebooks as an English language arts curriculum for whole-class instruction, with resources made by teachers for teachers.

These resources offer free or partially free access to comprehensive Grade 4 ELA curriculum units and materials.
