## CASA Framework & Google's Security Assessment

Google uses the App Defense Alliance and the cloud application security assessment framework (CASA) to standardize security assessments. CASA is Google's official cloud app security audit for restricted scope APIs.

## Tier 2 Assessment Requirements

You only hit Tier 2 when you request sensitive or restricted scopes - when your app wants to read a user's calendar, access their contacts, or sync their email. For Tier 2, Google assigns you an approved security assessor who runs a DAST (Dynamic Application Security Testing) scan against your production app, generates a findings report, and gives you a Self-Assessment Questionnaire to complete.

## Assessment Costs

Typical assessment costs range from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars per year depending on tier. More specifically, a small design decision can be the difference between a $540 DAST scan and a $5,000+ penetration test. The cost for such a service is agreed on between the developer and the assessor without any involvement from Google.

## Annual Revalidation

To keep access to any verified restricted scopes, apps must be reverified for compliance and complete a security assessment at least every 12 months after your assessor's Letter of Assessment (LOA) approval date.
