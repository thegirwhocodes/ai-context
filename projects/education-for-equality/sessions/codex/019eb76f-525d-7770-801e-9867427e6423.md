1. **24 minutes is from multiplier math**
   In [adaptive.ts](/Users/naomiivie/kai/src/lib/adaptive.ts), Kai starts from your baseline, then multiplies by time-of-day, focus ratings, streak fatigue, and calendar fit. 2. **6 minute break is from break scaling**
   Short break defaults to `5`, but if your focus streak is `>= 3`, Kai does `5 * 1.2 = 6`. So it says 6 minutes. Technically intentional, but it feels random.

3. **“You’ve been at it a while” is too loose**
   That line triggers when `focusStreak >= 3`, not when Kai has measured that you have actually worked for a long time. So if you did a few shorter blocks or restarted oddly, it can say that after only ~30 minutes. That’s the bug-feeling part.

What I’d change:
- Snap focus blocks to clean increments: `15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50`
- Snap breaks to clean increments: `5, 10, 15`
- Only say “you’ve been at it a while” after actual accumulated focus time, like `75+ minutes`
- For 30 minutes, say something more accurate like: “Take five to reset before the next block.”
