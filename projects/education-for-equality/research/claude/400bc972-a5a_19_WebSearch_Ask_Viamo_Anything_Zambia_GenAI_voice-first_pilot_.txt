## Overview

In May 2023, Viamo piloted its voice-first Generative AI solution, "Ask Viamo Anything" (AVA), in Zambia. AVA builds on Viamo's existing IVR powered platform, combining GenAI with speech technology to provide cross-sectoral information to mobile users who do not have access to the internet or face barriers in using mobile internet.

## Pilot Details

The GenAI Voice Assistant was deployed initially to 500 of the most active users on the Viamo Platform in Zambia. Within the first three weeks, 62% had opted into the voice assistant and had asked 555 questions. To date, 40,000 callers have asked over a million questions.

## Key Findings

**User Engagement:** In the first three weeks of the study, more than a third of users' questions were related to physical or mental health. Users identifying as women are choosing to engage with the voice assistant just as much as men users.

**Trust and Accuracy:** GSMA found that users trusted the service, found it easy to use and appreciated the anonymity to ask questions that might be considered taboo. Users consistently cited accuracy, anonymity, and reliability as reasons for continued use, and GSMA underscores the need for deliberate content moderation, cultural sensitivity, and clear safeguards.

## GSMA Partnership and Report

The GSMA, in partnership with Viamo has released a case study on "Ask Viamo Anything" (AVA) assessing how voice-first Generative AI can make a difference in connecting the 3.4 billion people who still lack meaningful internet access.
