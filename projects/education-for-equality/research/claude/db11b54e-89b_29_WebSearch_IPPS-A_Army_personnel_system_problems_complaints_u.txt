## Overview

The Integrated Personnel and Pay System - Army (IPPS-A) is a cutting-edge, online Human Resources (HR) system that will provide integrated personnel, pay and talent management capabilities in a single system to all Army Components.

## Historical Issues (2021-2024)

The system faced significant challenges during its initial rollout. The Army delayed the rollout of IPPS-A until 2022 following a year of technical snafus with its online tools. Fielding was delayed due to issues importing data from existing personnel systems.

## Documented User Experience Problems

One of the most prevalent issues is inaccuracies in retirement dates recorded within the system. More broadly, many users encounter specific challenges that can significantly affect their experience and ultimately their benefits.

For example, a former soldier whose advanced training is not reflected in the system may struggle to obtain a job that values those unique skills, and a lack of accurate qualification records might impede veterans from accessing programs designed to assist with their post-service career development.

## Recent Updates (2023-2025)

The Army has continued addressing issues. Current issues occurred since a system upgrade, with the Army working with developers to identify the source of the issues. More recently, Team IPPS-A is tracking known issues and working through resolution as quickly as possible.

The system appears to still be in an improvement phase with ongoing efforts to enhance user experience and system reliability.
