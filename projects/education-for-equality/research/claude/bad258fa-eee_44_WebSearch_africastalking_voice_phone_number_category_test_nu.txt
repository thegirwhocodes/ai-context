## SIP Phone Setup

Once the SIP number is mapped to your account, you can use your credentials to register it on your softphone. For Zoiper you also have to change a few settings.

## Voice Phone Number Types

SIP uses the internet and the client's account is billed for incoming and outgoing calls. Africa's Talking offers several types of voice phone numbers including SIP, virtualized physical lines, toll-free, and premium numbers.

## SIP Trunk Connection

Using SIP trunks, Africa's Talking can connect to your Private Branch System (PBX) and allow you to make and receive voice calls. To set up the trunk, you apply for a voice phone number, sign a KYC document, pay for the phone number, and share your trunk's IP address for whitelisting.

## Outbound Calls

You can make calls to SIP addresses using the Africa's Talking API, with examples like test.user@ke.sip.africastalking.com.

The official documentation is available at the Africa's Talking developers portal and help center, which provide more detailed guides on setup and configuration.
