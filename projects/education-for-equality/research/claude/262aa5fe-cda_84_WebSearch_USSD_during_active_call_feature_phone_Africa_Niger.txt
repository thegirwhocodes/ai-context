## USSD Technology Overview

USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) is a real-time, session-based communication protocol built into the GSM standard, where you dial a shortcode — like *123# — and interact through text-based menus on your phone screen.

## Compatibility with Feature Phones and GSM

USSD works on every GSM phone, from the simplest feature phone to the latest iPhone. USSD is a communications protocol controlled by mobile operators and supported by 99% of GSM handsets.

## Prevalence in Africa and Nigeria

USSD remains the most used channel for mobile financial services in Africa, handling over 70% of mobile money interactions in 2024. In Nigeria, the Central Bank has explicitly supported USSD for mobile money and banking services, citing its importance for financial inclusion.

## Key Advantage: Works Without Active Data

USSD does not require any data plan or 4G signal - if your customer has GSM coverage and a phone, you can engage them. This makes it particularly valuable for feature phone users across the continent.
