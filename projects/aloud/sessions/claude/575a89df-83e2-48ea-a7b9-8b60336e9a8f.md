## Claude · 2026-08-25T18:58:12.112Z

The core answer: **you pick an archetype on purpose, or you become one by accident** — and the accidental default is the one that burns people out.

**Four ways people actually do it** (from your own `04-operating-systems.md`, §13):
1. **Holdco operator** (Berkshire/Constellation) — you don't run any of them; you appoint CEOs and focus on capital allocation + hiring the right people to run each.
2. **Serial founder / studio** (IAC/Rocket Internet) — you start it, hire an operator, retain equity, move to the next.
3. **Genuine multi-CEO** (Musk) — you're actually CEO of 2+ at once, batching entire days per company. Extraordinarily hard; Dorsey's Square+Twitter run is the canonical failure case.
4. **Portfolio investor** — capital + board seat, no operating role at all.

Most first-time founders end up in (3) by default — not because they chose it, but because they haven't hired real operators yet and can't let go. That's the trap: you're never fully present anywhere, and quality drops everywhere at once.

**How you'd actually know which one you're in / should be in:** run a time audit for a week (hours by business × type of work). Any business getting <20% of your time with no strong operator under it will stall. If you can't free up 20%, that's a delegation gap in your other businesses, not a time-management problem.

**The mechanism, not just the philosophy** — install these in order: one-page Owner's Manual per business (+ one for the holdco itself covering how you allocate cash across them), an Accountability Chart per business (where you occupy more than one seat = your next hire), a weekly scorecard per business, and a standardized monthly report from each so you can compare across the portfolio fast.

This is already fully written up (with failure modes, meeting cadence, centralize-vs-federate rules) in [04-operating-systems.md §13](file:///Users/naomiivie/businesses/research/04-operating-systems.md#L252) — and Holdco Command exists specifically to be the tool that installs it. Want me to open the app and check whether it currently tracks any of this (archetype, time audit, accountability chart), or is it still just a business list?

I spent fifteen years living the problem, and then another two years researching it. I built the tutor that bridged that divide.

Sabi works through 5-7 minute phone calls, in either literacy or numeracy, depending on the child's advancement through the curriculum. Nigerian children are ten times more likely to answer a problem correctly when it's framed in the context of market math and money than when asked as a direct question. When a learner gets two answers wrong, Sabi adapts to their learning and bumps down their level, asking easier questions through the curriculum. When they get three consecutive questions right, their progress speeds up. When the child calls back, Sabi remembers who they are and continues from where they stopped in the curriculum.
