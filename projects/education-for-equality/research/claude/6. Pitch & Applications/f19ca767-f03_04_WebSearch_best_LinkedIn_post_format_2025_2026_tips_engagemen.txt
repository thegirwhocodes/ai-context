Based on the latest research, here's what's working best for LinkedIn posts in 2025-2026:

## Top-Performing Formats

Carousel posts are the top engagement driver, boasting a 45.85% engagement rate and over 791 average interactions. The carousel (or multi-page PDF document post) has cemented its position as the top-performing content format for engagement.

LinkedIn reports that video content generates 1.4x more engagement than other formats. Shortform video on LinkedIn is seeing massive year-over-year growth.

## Content Types by Objective

For brand awareness and reach: prioritize carousels or videos. For engagement objectives: favor carousels and conversational posts that encourage comments. For conversion with a call-to-action: consider well-structured written posts with a link and strong CTA. For credibility or expertise: focus on text posts with case study examples.

## What Drives Engagement

Posts that receive strong engagement within the first hour get pushed to second and third-degree connections significantly expanding reach, with the first 60 minutes after posting being crucial for visibility.

Comments and extended discussions carry more weight than simple reactions, with the algorithm favoring posts that generate meaningful conversations rather than just likes.

Curiosity-driven openings execute exceptionally well, such as "What if I told you a single formatting change could triple your post's visibility?"

## Emerging Trends

Punchy, text-based posts with just advice or a quick story—no links, nothing salesy—are a growing trend.

Sharing personal stories is one of the latest marketing trends to take over on LinkedIn, with Gen Z users sharing daily anecdotes, professional horror stories and day-in-the-life videos.

Adding an image can boost reactions, comments, and reposts by up to 40%.
