## Key Results

**Nigerian Accent ASR:**
English speech recognition algorithms failed to correctly transcribe the pronunciations of educators with Nigerian accents during remote learning in 2020. A study fine-tuned NeMo's QuartzNet15x5 English model on accented educational data and achieved a word error rate (WER) of 27%. Additionally, some applications allow users to select "Nigerian English" to adjust transcription accuracy.

**Whisper ASR Performance:**
Whisper is trained on 680,000 hours of multilingual and multitask supervised data, showing improved robustness to accents, background noise and technical language. However, Whisper models perform unevenly across languages, with lower accuracy on low-resource and/or low-discoverability languages or languages where there is less training data.

**Low-Resource Languages:**
While Whisper was trained on approximately 117,000 hours covering 96 languages including Yoruba and Hausa, Yoruba and Hausa collectively contribute less than 600 hours, and Igbo is not represented. Whisper has emerged as the most robust solution for accented and multilingual African speech, though many models show biases when applied to African languages, especially in handling accents, dialects and noisy speech.
