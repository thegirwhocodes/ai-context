## Overview of Special Numbering Services

The Special Numbering Service is a Value Added Service, introduced into the Nigerian market in 2006 to make communication easier for companies and individuals who would want customised and easily memorisable ways of reaching out to them such as 0700CALLNCC.

There are currently only twenty five (25) active licensees rendering this service offering.

## Number Types

The prefix '0700' has been allocated by NCC for VANITY numbers. The numbering format for 0800 Toll-Free Numbers is 0800-COMPANYNAME. The cost of calls made to toll-free numbers is borne by the subscriber.
