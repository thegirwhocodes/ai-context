## Key Concepts

An action title is the most important point of the slide, formulated as a short, simple sentence that ideally serves as the main takeaway or 'so what' of the slide.

## Complete Sentence Format

Writing headlines in the form of complete sentences imposes a discipline on your ideas by forcing you to turn them into coherent thoughts and remove any ambiguity. Using sentence case helps reinforce the notion that titles should be complete thoughts and actions rather than descriptive labels.

## Main Takeaway Focus

An 'action' title is called this because it actively tells the audience what the key takeaway is. The main takeaway should be the first thing your audience sees, and an action title is a slide title worded to reflect the key takeaway or 'so what' of the slide—if written effectively, the audience should only need to read the action title to understand the primary message.

## Best Practices

Titles should be understandable as a stand-alone sentence, and use active words that convey an insight. While it doesn't need to be a complete sentence, you should shoot for sentence-like structure to make it readable. Additionally, most effective headlines typically run between 7 to 14 words.
