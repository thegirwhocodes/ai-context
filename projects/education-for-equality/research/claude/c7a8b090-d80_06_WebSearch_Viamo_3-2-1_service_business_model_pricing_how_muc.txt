## Business Model

The 3-2-1 business model is simple. First, a donor or investor (namely TigerIT) acts as the anchor partner to pay the costs of setting up and launching the service in a new country.

Viamo engages with four types of partnership: 1) Content partners – non-governmental or other organizations looking to disseminate information on a given topic; 2) Government partners – ministry staff who provide additional input and official approval of messages; 3) Communication partners – an MNO agrees with Viamo to offer the mobile-based service free of charge to their subscribers or at a nominal cost to non-subscribers; 4) Resource partners – funding organizations such as USAID.
