## Joint Travel Regulations Overview

The Joint Travel Regulations (JTR) implements policy and law to establish travel and transportation allowances for Uniformed Service members, DoD civilian employees, and others traveling at the DoD's expense.

## Per Diem Information

Per diem is a daily payment to reimburse service members for the out-of-pocket costs of food, lodging and incidental expenses that occur while on military business or temporary duty (TDY) away from their home station. The rates for fiscal 2026 are $110 for lodging and $68 for meals and incidental expenses.
