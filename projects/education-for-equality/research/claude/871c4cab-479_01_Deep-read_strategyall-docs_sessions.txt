## PROJECT VISION & MISSION

- **Education for Equality (E4E)** is the entity (US 501(c)(3), Connecticut). **Sabi** ("to know" in Nigerian Pidgin) is the product. Naomi's standing correction: "Sabi is MY company" — no Bakame attribution; Sabi is the delivery channel.
- **Mission/core insight:** 95% of Nigerian households own a phone; 99% have 2G coverage; 55% (128M, DataReportal Jan 2025) can't afford/use mobile internet. So the way to reach every child is a **phone call**, not an app. "Not another education app that sits in an app store — something a mother hears about at church, dials after dinner, and hands to her child for five minutes. - **Two channels:**
  1. **Sabi voice AI tutor** — child dials a number, AI tutor answers, runs a conversational diagnostic, teaches foundational literacy/numeracy at the child's actual level (not grade level), 5–7 min/call, one concept, all framed in Nigerian market/Naira scenarios, persistent memory across calls (multiple kids on one family phone get separate profiles), scaffolds on wrong answers (never says "wrong").

- **Target audience:** Out-of-school + in-school-but-not-learning Nigerian children ages 8–14 (also "6–14"). Three tiers; **Tier 2 (enrolled but underserved) is the best near-term target.** Phase-2 long-term: ~45–53M illiterate adults.
- **Pedagogy:** Evidence-based, built on 7 frameworks — TaRL (Teaching at the Right Level), Jolly Phonics, Rising On Air, Rori AI, Cambridge Primary, Global Proficiency Framework, XPRIZE. Naira-first principle (always market scenario → then name the math). Literacy is two-phase: Phase 1 oral-only (3 months), Phase 2 adds print/letter shapes (3 months).

## LOAD TEST — CRITICAL CORRECTION
- The Cost Breakdown doc (Mar 9) claimed **1 GPU = 5,000–10,000 students**. The **Mar 31 load test disproved this**: at 5 concurrent calls, STT went 0.08s→12.83s, TTS 1.52s→14.23s, total turn 3.3s→~29s, **13/15 turns failed**. Cause: Whisper + Chatterbox + Ollama contend for one 20GB GPU.
- **Real capacity: ~300–500 students per GPU.** "$0.41/student" claim is wrong; realistic **$5–8/student/year** with telco zero-rating at scale.
- Single-turn solo benchmark: STT 0.08s + LLM 1.66s + TTS 1.52s = 3.26s.
- Solutions: stop idle Ollama (frees 6GB), Whisper medium (2GB), split architecture (voice GPUs + LLM GPUs), bigger GPU (GEX131 96GB), or Cerebras for LLM ($0.10/M tokens, ~2,000 tok/s, $2.38/student).
