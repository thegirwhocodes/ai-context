- Cambridge: `g4-{subject-prefix}-…` (per `cambridge-grade4-curriculum.ts` IDs, not enumerated here but follow same shape)
- Unit IDs: `fn-unit-{N}`, `fl-unit-{N}`
- Sabi RAG modules: `sabi-module-0` … `sabi-module-6` (module names `sabi-diagnostic`, `sabi-counting`, `sabi-addition`, `sabi-subtraction`, `sabi-multiplication`, `sabi-division`, `sabi-word-problems`)

### Lesson HTML structure (every lesson follows this template)

Each lesson `content` field is HTML with 6 standard sections marked by emoji-prefixed h3:
- `<h3>🎯 What You'll Learn</h3>`
- `<h3>🏪 Market Story</h3>` (numeracy) or `<h3>🌟 Let's Start</h3>` (literacy)
- `<h3>📝 Let's Learn</h3>` (numeracy) or `<h3>📚 New Concept</h3>` (literacy)
- `<h3>✏️ Practice Questions</h3>` (numeracy) or `<h3>🎮 Let's Practice</h3>` (literacy)
- `<details><summary>Click to see answers</summary>` … `</details>` (collapsed answer key)
- `<h3>💡 Remember</h3>` (summary)

The RAG chunker (`lib/rag/chunk.ts`) regex-matches exactly these headings to break each lesson into `story | concept | practice | summary | example` chunks for embedding.

### Sample 1 — `fn-2-7` "Adding with Carrying" (Foundational Numeracy Unit 2, lesson 7)

```
🎯 What You'll Learn
You will learn what to do when the ones digits add up to more than 9. This is called carrying or regrouping.

🏪 Market Story
Funke buys bread for ₦45 and eggs for ₦27. She adds the ones: 5 + 7 = 12. But 12 is more than 9! She can't put 12 in the ones place. She needs to "carry" the extra ten over to the tens column. Let's see how.

📝 Let's Learn
When the ones add up to 10 or more, we carry 1 ten to the tens column.
Step-by-step for ₦45 + ₦27:
 1. Add the ones: 5 + 7 = 12. Write down 2 in the ones place, carry 1 to the tens.
 2. Add the tens: 4 + 2 = 6, plus the carried 1 = 7. Write 7 in the tens place.
 3. Answer: ₦72.
Example 1: ₦38 + ₦25. Ones: 8 + 5 = 13, write 3 carry 1. Tens: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. Answer: ₦63.
Example 2: ₦56 + ₦17. Ones: 6 + 7 = 13, write 3 carry 1. Tens: 5 + 1 + 1 = 7. Answer: ₦73.
Example 3: ₦49 + ₦34. Ones: 9 + 4 = 13, write 3 carry 1. Tens: 4 + 3 + 1 = 8. Answer: ₦83.

✏️ Practice Questions
 1. ₦28 + ₦15 = ?
 2. ₦37 + ₦46 = ?
 3. Bola buys a notebook for ₦65 and crayons for ₦28. What is the total?

Answers:
 1. ₦28 + ₦15 = ₦43. Ones: 8+5=13, write 3 carry 1. Tens: 2+1+1=4.
 2. ₦37 + ₦46 = ₦83. Ones: 7+6=13, write 3 carry 1. Tens: 3+4+1=8.
 3. ₦65 + ₦28 = ₦93. Ones: 5+8=13, write 3 carry 1. Tens: 6+2+1=9.

💡 Remember
When ones add up to 10 or more, write down the ones digit and carry 1 to the tens column. Don't forget to add the carried 1!
```
Duration 14 min, order 7.

### Sample 2 — `fn-4-4` "Times Table: 2s"

```
🎯 What You'll Learn
You will memorise the 2 times table. Multiplying by 2 is the same as doubling a number!

🏪 Market Story
Emeka and his twin brother each get the same pocket money. Whatever one gets, the total is always double. If each gets ₦5, the total is ₦10. If each gets ₦8, the total is ₦16. That's the 2 times table!

📝 Let's Learn
The 2 times table:
 1 × 2 = 2
 2 × 2 = 4
 3 × 2 = 6
 4 × 2 = 8
 5 × 2 = 10
 6 × 2 = 12
 7 × 2 = 14
 8 × 2 = 16
 9 × 2 = 18
 10 × 2 = 20
Notice: all the answers are even numbers — they end in 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8. Multiplying by 2 is the same as skip counting by 2s!
Example 1: 7 × 2 = 7 + 7 = 14.
Example 2: 2 pairs of shoes means 2 × 2 = 4 shoes.
Example 3: Amina buys 8 oranges at ₦2 each: 8 × 2 = ₦16.

✏️ Practice Questions
 1. What is 6 × 2?
 2. What is 9 × 2?
 3. Bola buys 5 exercise books at ₦2 each. How much does she pay?

Answers: 12, 18, ₦10.

💡 Remember
The 2 times table gives even numbers. Multiplying by 2 means doubling. If you can double, you know your 2 times table!
```
Duration 10 min, order 4.

### Sample 3 — `fl-6-1` "Letters s, a, t: Sound and Shape" (Phonics unit, lesson 1)

```
🎯 What You'll Learn
You will learn to recognise and sound out the letters s, a, and t — your first three reading letters!

🌟 Let's Start
Today is a very special day — you are going to learn your first letters for reading! We will start with three letters: s, a, and t. With just these three letters, you can already make a real word!

📚 New Concept
Letter s:
 • Sound: /s/ — like a snake hissing: sssssss
 • Shape: It looks like a curvy snake. Start at the top, curve left, then curve right.
 • Words: sun, sit, sat

Letter a:
 • Sound: /a/ — open your mouth wide: aaa (like the a in ant)
 • Shape: Start with a small circle, then add a line going down on the right side.
 • Words: ant, at, am

Letter t:
 • Sound: /t/ — a short, sharp sound: t-t-t (tap your tongue behind your top teeth)
 • Shape: Draw a line going down, then cross it near the top.
 • Words: ten, tap, top

Your first word! Put them together: s-a-t → sat! "The girl sat on the chair." You just read your first word!
Also try: at (a-t) and as (a-s).

🎮 Let's Practice
 1. What sound does the letter s make?
 2. Blend these letters: s-a-t. What word do you get?
 3. Blend these letters: a-t. What word do you get?

Answers: /s/ like a snake sssss; sat; at.

💡 Remember
Your first three letters are s, a, and t. Each one makes a sound. When you blend the sounds together, you read a word! s-a-t = sat. You are reading!
```
Duration 12 min, order 1.
