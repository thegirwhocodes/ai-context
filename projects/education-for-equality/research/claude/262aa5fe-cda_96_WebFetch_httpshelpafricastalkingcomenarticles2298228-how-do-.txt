# Getting a USSD Code in Nigeria Through Africa's Talking

## Supported Networks
Africa's Talking offers USSD services on four major Nigerian networks: "MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile."

## Code Types
The service provides two options:

**Shared USSD Code:** "Getting set up on our shared USSD is cost free, however there is a monthly maintenance fee of NGN 53,750 (Exclusive of 7.5% VAT)." Setup takes approximately one business day.

**Dedicated Codes:** These are raised across all telcos with higher associated costs.

## Costs (Dedicated Codes)
- Regulatory charge: ₦11,500 (paid via Remita)
- Integration fee to Africa's Talking: ₦200,000
- Monthly maintenance: ₦70,000 + 7.5% VAT
- MTN connection fee: ₦500,000
- 9mobile connection fee (financial institutions): ₦770,000
- Minimum session requirements vary by network

## Required Documentation
KYC requirements include: "Certificate of Incorporation," "Letter of request," and "Valid Form Of ID (Int'l Passport, Voter's Card, Driver's License, National ID)." Submit these to info@africastalking.com.

## Network-Initiated USSD
The documentation makes no mention of push/network-initiated USSD capabilities.
