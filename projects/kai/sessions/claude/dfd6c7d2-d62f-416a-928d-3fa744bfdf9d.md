## What its "thing" is
It's a **bedtime enforcement / digital-discipline app** across Mac + iPhone + iPad:

- At your set **bedtime, it blocks your screen** (full-screen blocker on Mac, Screen Time shields on iOS).
- You can't just quit out. To get an **early unlock**, you ask **"Sage"** — an AI agent running on your Mac that **reads your Gmail**, judges whether your reason is legit, and grants/challenges/denies it.
- Unlocks are rationed: a **shared 3-per-week pool** enforced server-side, synced across all your devices via iCloud key-value store.
- The old "type a passcode to escape" system was **removed on June 10** — now everything routes through Sage so you can't cheat your way past it.

So: a hard-to-bypass "go to bed" lock with an AI gatekeeper that holds you accountable using real signals from your inbox.
