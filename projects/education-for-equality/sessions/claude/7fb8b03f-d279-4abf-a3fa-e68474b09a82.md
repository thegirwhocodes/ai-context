## Flash callback — how the payment model works

### The mechanics

1. **Child dials your AT Nigerian number** (their phone rings out, but...)
2. **Server detects the incoming call via webhook** and **rejects before answering** — telcos only bill on answered calls, so child pays **₦0**
3. **Server immediately places an outbound call back to that number** via AT
4. **Child answers the callback** → the lesson starts. From the child's view, it feels like one call; they don't pay a kobo.

This is the same trick Viamo 3-2-1 uses across Africa. Standard pattern — telcos don't penalize it.

### Who pays what

| Party | Pays | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Child** | **₦0** | Flash/missed call doesn't bill |
| **You** | **₦3/min** | Outbound via AT SIP trunk |
| **You** | **₦5,375/mo** | AT number maintenance (the invoice you just got) |

## AT is **prepaid** — wallet model

You fund an account balance on the AT dashboard; every call/SMS/USSD deducts from it in real time. No invoice arrives at end-of-month for usage. Hit zero and AT refuses to place calls — you'd see the **"Credit balance is too low"** error (which actually showed up in your today's session at line 16262).

### How the ₦3/min flow works

```
Child flashes → server places outbound via AT SIP →
  per second of call: balance ticks down at ₦0.05/sec (₦3/min) →
  call ends, balance now lower
```

Real-time. No monthly bill, no net-30, no surprise invoice. You see it deduct in the AT console as calls happen.

### Why you still got a proforma

The ₦5,375/mo voice maintenance fee is the **one exception** — AT bills that as a separate proforma invoice (which is what's in your screenshot), probably because:

1. It's a fixed recurring charge tied to the number, not a per-event API charge
2. They want a paper trail for the regulator (you're a US entity, NGN VAT compliance)
3. Some accounts have it auto-deducted from wallet; yours seems to be invoiced

Either way: **paying the proforma = topping up that ₦5,375 line specifically**. It doesn't add to your usage wallet.

### Two separate buckets to keep funded

| Bucket | How to fund | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Usage wallet** (prepaid) | Dashboard top-up via card/bank | ₦3/min outbound, ₦3/min inbound, SMS, USSD |
| **Maintenance** (proforma) | Pay the invoice they send | ₦5,375/mo for the voice number to exist |

### Practical implications for the pilot

- **Always keep the usage wallet padded.** If a 5-min call to a kid drops mid-lesson because the wallet hit zero, that's a terrible experience. Rule of thumb: keep **2–3 months of projected usage** loaded.
- **Set up low-balance alerts** in the AT dashboard (Settings → Alerts). Default off; enable it so you get an email at e.g. ₦20,000 remaining.
- **No autopay.** AT doesn't auto-charge a card when you run out. You have to manually top up. So for the pilot, monitor it.
