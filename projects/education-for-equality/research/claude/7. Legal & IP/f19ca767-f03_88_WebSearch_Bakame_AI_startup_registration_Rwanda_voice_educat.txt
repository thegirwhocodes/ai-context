## Overview

Bakame AI is an artificial intelligence-powered education system founded by two TCU international students that operates without internet access to bring the resource to all corners of the globe. Co-founders Happy Niyorurema, raised in Rwanda, and Diarra Niang, raised in Senegal, understand firsthand what it means to learn without reliable internet access.

## The Product

Bakame AI is an AI product that teaches students English on the telephone. Rather than relying on broadband infrastructure, the system operates through basic telephone networks. Users can dial a toll-free number to converse on the phone with an AI-driven chatbot.

## Recognition and Funding

The student-led venture recently earned international recognition, winning the Global Best M-Gov Award in Dubai. Bakame AI received $8,000 from the TCU CREATE accelerator program.

## Vision

Most language learning tools require internet, a resource only 34.2% of Rwandans have access to. Bakame AI was developed to address this gap, requiring only a phone, which 97% of Rwandans already have.
