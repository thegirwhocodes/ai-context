## Global Cost of Unaddressed Hearing Loss

WHO estimates that unaddressed hearing loss poses an annual global cost of US$980 billion, which includes health sector costs, costs of educational support, loss of productivity, and societal costs.

## Return on Investment

An annual additional investment of less than US$1.40 per person is needed to scale up ear and hearing care services globally, which over a 10-year period promises a return of nearly US$16 for every US dollar invested.

More specifically, scaling up ear and hearing care interventions to 90% requires an overall global investment of US$238.8 billion over 10 years, which would avert more than 130 million DALYs with a monetary value of more than US$1.3 trillion, corresponding to a return of nearly US$15 for every US$1 invested.

## Accessibility for Deaf People

The search results emphasize broader ear and hearing care services rather than specifically addressing deaf accessibility in terms of sign language interpretation and communication access. However, the WHO notes that the use of hearing assistive technology and services such as frequency modulation systems, alerting devices, captioning services and sign language interpretation can improve access to communication and education for people with hearing loss.
