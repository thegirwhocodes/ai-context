## About the Hult Prize

The Hult Prize is the world's largest student social entrepreneurship competition, where student teams from universities across the world compete to solve pressing social issues by developing scalable, sustainable social enterprises. The competition challenges students to launch for-profit startups that support at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG).

## Application Deadlines for 2026

The application deadlines vary depending on the specific program and location:

- At McGill University, the registration and full application deadline is February 2, 2026.
- At UNSW, Hult Prize applications close on February 15, 2026.
- For the Hult Prize 2026 Impact Fellowship, the application deadline is December 27, 2025 at 23:59 UTC.

## Competition Structure and Prize

The Hult Prize competition runs September through September and consists of 5 successive rounds: Qualifiers, Nationals, Digital Incubator, Global Accelerator and Global Finals. At the end of the competition, one of the top 8 finalists will secure $1M in seed investment to advance their business.

For more information about specific campus deadlines and requirements, you can visit the official Hult Prize website at hultprize.org.
