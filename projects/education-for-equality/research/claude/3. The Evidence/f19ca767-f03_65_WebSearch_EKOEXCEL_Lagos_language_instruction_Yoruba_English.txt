## EKOEXCEL's Bilingual Language Approach

EKOEXCEL was launched in 2019 by Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu to provide quality education to and upskill teachers leveraging technology in Lagos State's public schools.

### Yoruba and English Integration

Some pupils struggle with the English language because of their backgrounds, so apart from teachers using the mother tongue, EKOEXCEL also provides instructional and learning tools in Yoruba. EKOEXCEL says it will continue to encourage the use of the mother tongue as a medium of instruction and uphold excellence as the gold standard of all its operations.

The program's philosophy emphasizes cultural preservation alongside English language development. The goal is to avoid producing pupils grounded in Western cultures and languages but know nothing about theirs.

### Program Impact

EKOEXCEL has moved over 18,000 headteachers and teachers from analogue to digital teaching, using tablets and an updated curriculum. Other impacts include a drastic reduction in the number of out-of-school children in the state, taking several of them out of harm's way on the streets and enrolling them in schools through the 'Leave No Child Behind' policy.
