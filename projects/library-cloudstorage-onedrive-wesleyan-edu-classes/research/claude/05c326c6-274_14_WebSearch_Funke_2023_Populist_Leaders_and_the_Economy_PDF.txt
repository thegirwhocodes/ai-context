## Key Information about the Paper:

The paper "Populist Leaders and the Economy" is by Manuel Funke, Moritz Schularick and Christoph Trebesch, and was published in volume 113, issue 12, pages 3249-88 of American Economic Review, December 2023.

The paper's main findings are that they identify 51 populist presidents and prime ministers from 1900 to 2020 and show that the economic cost of populism is high, with GDP per capita 10 percent lower after 15 years compared to a plausible nonpopulist counterfactual.
