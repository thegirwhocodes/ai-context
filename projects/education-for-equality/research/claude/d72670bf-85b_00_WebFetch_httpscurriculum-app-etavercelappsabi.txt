# Education for Equality - Sabi Page Overview

## Layout & Design
The page uses a modern, gradient-based design with a dark teal color scheme. It features a hero section with navigation, followed by multiple content sections scrolling vertically, and concludes with a comprehensive footer.

## Key Sections

**Hero Section**
Introduces "Sabi" (meaning "to know" in Nigerian Pidgin) as "An AI tutor any child can reach by calling a phone number. No smartphone. No internet. No cost." Two primary CTAs: "Try the Demo" and "Learn More."

**The Learning Crisis**
Highlights the educational gap with statistics:
- "98M" children out of school in Sub-Saharan Africa (UNESCO 2024)
- "7 in 10" Nigerian 10-year-olds cannot read a single sentence (World Bank 2022)
- Only "16%" of planned learning actually occurs in schools

**Infrastructure Opportunity**
Notes that while "600 million people in Africa have no internet," approximately "70% have a mobile phone," with 99% 2G coverage in Lagos.

**How Sabi Works**
Five-step process illustrated with numbered steps:
1. Child calls a toll-free number
2. System greets them by name (remembers returning users)
3. Lesson begins at their level using relatable scenarios
4. Sessions last 5-7 minutes, focusing on one concept
5. System remembers progress for next call

**Sample Conversation**
Demonstrates a realistic interaction between Sabi and a child named Chidi, grounding math education in market transactions (selling groundnuts, making change).

**Science Behind Sabi**
Research-backed claims:
- "9x" more problem-solving success with market-framed math versus abstract (Nature 2025)
- "1.5-2 years" of learning gains in just 6 weeks (World Bank RCT)
- "7% → 45%" reading improvement in Tanzania without teachers
- Cost: "$0.50-5" per student annually

**Built for the Last Mile**
Three key features highlighted:
- Works on "$5 feature phones"
- Persistent memory of student progress
- Completely free, sponsored by the nonprofit

**Call-to-Action Section**
"Try Sabi Right Now" with emphasis on accessibility: "We're not building another education app that sits in an app store."

Notes Lagos pilot planned for Q4 2026 with 100-500 students.

## Footer
Contains links to curriculum app, website, donation page, and social media; lists subject areas and contact information.
