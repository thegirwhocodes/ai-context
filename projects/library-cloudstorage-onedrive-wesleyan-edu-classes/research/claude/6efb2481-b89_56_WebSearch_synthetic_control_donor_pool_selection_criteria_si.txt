## Donor Pool Selection Criteria

It has been advised by Abadie, Diamond, and Hainmueller to restrict the donor pool to those units whose predictors are similar to those of the treated unit.

The predictors must have values for the donor pool regions that are similar to those of the affected region, as this reduces the potential for interpolation bias, which can arise if donor units are chosen with outcomes far from the treated unit.

## Practical Implementation

Actively trimming the donor pool to units with values of the predictors that are close to the values of the predictors for the treated unit can substantially improve the performance of synthetic control estimators.

Non-treated countries are chosen to match as closely as possible the pre-treatment characteristics of the treated country.

## Example Application

In the classic German reunification study, the donor pool consists of a set of industrialized countries, with comparisons made to the nearest neighbor of West Germany in terms of predictors values. The predictors used included GDP per capita, inflation rate, trade openness, industry share, schooling, and investment rate.
