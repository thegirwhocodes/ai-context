## Overview
The Wesleyan Local Food Co-op is a student-organized and -run group whose goal is to bring local, sustainably grown, and delicious food to the Wesleyan community.

## Weekly Orders and Schedule
Pickups are every Wednesday from 5-7 PM in the Usdan Multipurpose Room (Room B25) or in Usdan Courtyard during nice weather over a 9 week period each semester. At the start of each semester, students can choose to get shares of produce, meat/seafood, bread, eggs, dairy, preserved foods, mushrooms, cheese, coffee, tofu, and seitan. Participating students pick up shares weekly and help once each semester with organization and distribution.

## Student-Run Organization
Once a week, about 13 student coordinators from each cooperative hold a pick-up together where members can come grab their shares. The student-run Local Food Co-op sources a large variety of fresh local foods (including Long Lane Farm produce).

## Local and Organic Focus
A student-run co-op makes fresh local foods from farms in Connecticut, Massachusetts, and New York available to over 500 students, faculty, and staff. The co-op partners with various local vendors to provide seasonal, sustainably sourced products to the Wesleyan community.
