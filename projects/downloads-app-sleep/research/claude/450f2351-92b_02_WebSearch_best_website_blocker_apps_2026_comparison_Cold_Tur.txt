## Overview of Key Apps

**Cold Turkey**
Cold Turkey Blocker is an unbreakable website and application blocker with flexible schedules, locked modes, and cross-platform support for maximum productivity. It has a mode called Frozen Turkey, which prevents you from accessing your computer entirely: turn on Frozen Turkey, and you won't be able to log in to your computer without it locking again. Cold Turkey Pro is a one-time $39 fee for up to 5 computers lifetime.

**Freedom**
Freedom is the go-to choice if you need the Best Website Blocker across iOS, Android, and desktop devices. It syncs blocking sessions across all your devices, so starting a block on your laptop also blocks your phone. Freedom pricing: $3.33/month billed annually or $8.99/month billed monthly; $199 for a lifetime subscription.

**SelfControl**
SelfControl stands out for one key reason: it's impossible to undo. Seriously, this app is hardcore. Closing the app does nothing—all of your distractions are still blocked. Deleting the app doesn't do anything, and neither does rebooting your computer. The only way to disable blocking, once you set the timer, is to reinstall macOS entirely. SelfControl is brutally effective, zero cost.

**OneSec**
OneSec is used for friction on mobile devices. OneSec is worth trying if you prefer a gentler approach that builds awareness without hard restrictions.

## Comparison Summary

Freedom is the best overall distraction blocker for Mac thanks to its cross-device sync, Locked Mode, and flexible scheduling. For completely free and unbreakable blocking, SelfControl is excellent. Cold Turkey is the strictest paid option, and one sec is best if you prefer a behavioral approach over hard blocking.
