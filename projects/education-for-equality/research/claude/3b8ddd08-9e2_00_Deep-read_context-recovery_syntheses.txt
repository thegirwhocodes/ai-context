## 1. CANONICAL DEFINITION OF SABI (from bad258fa — the authoritative synthesis)

**Sabi is the voice-AI delivery channel of Education for Equality** — an adaptive AI tutor teaching Nigerian children foundational literacy and numeracy through 5–7-minute phone calls on any $5 feature phone, with **no internet, no smartphone, no cost to the family**. "Sabi" = "to know" in Nigerian Pidgin.

**Two channels under E4E:**
- **Sabi** (voice AI, primary) — for the 95% of Nigerian families with a phone but no smartphone/data
- **Curriculum App** (Khan-Academy-style web, eduforequality.org) — for the smartphone segment

Pipeline: child masters foundational skills via Sabi → graduates to the Curriculum App for full grade-level Cambridge curriculum.

**Critical correction (Naomi's, repeated):** Sabi is *the delivery channel*, NOT "the product" or "the AI tutor platform." E4E is the org; Sabi is how it reaches feature-phone kids.

### Four use-case personas
1. **"Never-reader" enrolled child (~23M nationally)** — P5 reading at P2 level, class of 80, one textbook, gets ~8 hrs/week vs needed 16. Sabi delivers structured pedagogy after school.
2. **Out-of-school child in Lagos (~704,000)** — hawking, petty trading, flooding, couldn't afford uniforms/LASRRA cert. 484K–684K reachable in Lagos.
3. **Lagos special-needs / inclusive-unit pupils** (Dr. Sonia Ivie's 2021 LASUBEB study) — 5 Special Needs Schools + 2 Correctional Centres + 31 Inclusive Units in EKOEXCEL schools; classes merged (4-yr-olds with 17-yr-olds). **Dr. Sonia's memo has ~30 named head-teacher contacts with phone numbers — immediate pilot recruitment list.**
4. **Adult women / market traders (~45M illiterate; ~90M phone-accessible adults)** — revenue-generating: ₦500–2,000/mo carrier-billed = $0.31–1.25/mo; 1% of 90M = **$6.8M/year**.

### Concrete lesson examples
- **Math 4Nn1 (Place Value)** — app 12 min; voice = 3 calls × 5–6 min, anchored on ₦1,000 note (Nature 2025 Sokoto: market math 9× better, 48.5% vs 5.4%)
- **English 4Rn2 (Inferences)** — 2 calls × 6 min: Text Clues + What I Know = Inference
- **Science 4Cp2 (States of Matter)** — stone/water/breath, boiling jollof steam
- **Mapping ratio:** 286 app lessons → ~700 voice calls (Math 1:2.5, English 1:2, Science 1:2, Social Studies 1:1.5)
- **Voice beats app for:** pronunciation/oral fluency, conversational English, pre-literate learners, accessibility. **App beats voice for:** geometry/maps/fractions, written practice.

## 4. PEDAGOGY (7 frameworks)
- **TaRL** (diagnostic places child at actual level) — Pratham/J-PAL, 0.10–0.71 SD across 6 RCTs, 20+ countries
- **Jolly Phonics 7 letter groups** — validated in Nigeria, 72,127 pupils, +60% word recognition (British Council)
- **Naira-first numeracy** (Nature 2025, n=5,997, Sokoto) — 9× better
- Adaptive branching (right→harder, wrong→scaffold, 3 correct→advance); **never says "wrong"**; spaced repetition (recall question = identity check on shared phones); two-phase literacy (oral-only 3 mo → oral+print flashcards ₦200–500/child)
- Emotion tags `[laugh]`/`[chuckle]`/`[sigh]` in 30% of 3,624 dialogue lines (58% laugh, 33% chuckle, 9% sigh); Chatterbox exaggeration auto-detect (0.35 correction → 0.5 neutral → 0.6 warm → 0.75 celebration)
- **Curriculum status:** 8 literacy modules (~140 calls) + 6 numeracy modules (~100 calls) + 2 diagnostics. **240+ scripts written.** Pending: Literacy Phase 2 (Modules 6–8), Numeracy Modules 5–6.
- **Curriculum timeline math:** 120–160 literacy + 80–100 numeracy calls. At 5 calls/week alternating = 10–13 months (too long). **Recommended: one 10-min call split 5+5 literacy/numeracy, 5 days/week = ~6 months.**
