## First Employee and Early Hiring

Chesky combed through thousands of applications, spoke with hundreds of applicants, and after four grueling months, they finally found their first engineer. "I think bringing in your first engineer is like bringing in a DNA chip to the company," Chesky said in a speech given at Stanford in 2014.

## Hiring Culture and Standards

After making that first hire, there would be a thousand people just like that person in the company. After interviewing an early hire, Chesky would ask, "Do I want to work with a thousand more people like this?"

Chesky was physically present during every single one of Airbnb's first 300 hires. Chesky attributes part of Airbnb's success to being at the interviews for the first 300 hires.

## Values-Based Hiring

As part of their hiring process, they've got core values interviews that are run by people outside of the job candidates are interviewing for, in order to filter out people that aren't a cultural fit. The idea is to prioritise the right people over scaling quickly.
