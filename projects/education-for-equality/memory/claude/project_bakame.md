# Bakame AI — Discovery, Partnership & Independence

## What Bakame AI Is
- Voice-based AI education system built by **Happy Niyorurema** (Rwanda) and **Diarra Niang** (Senegal), both TCU students
- Users dial a toll-free number from any phone and converse with an AI tutor
- Tech stack: custom LLM, Rasa, Whisper, Coqui TTS, Asterisk PBX
- Current focus: English language learning with spaced repetition + health/civic info
- Pilot: ~1,000 students in 5 Rwandan schools
- Won **Global Best M-Gov Award** at World Government Summit, Dubai (Feb 2026)
- Website: https://bakame.org
- Contact: Happy Niyorurema (LinkedIn), @bakame.ai (Instagram)

## Timeline of the Relationship

### Feb 25-26, 2026: Discovery & Excitement
- Naomi discovered Bakame AI while researching voice-based education delivery for Lagos
- Saw strong fit: E4E has curriculum content, Bakame has toll-free voice delivery infrastructure
- Created BAKAME_EFE_LESSON_DESIGN.md mapping E4E lessons to Bakame-style voice calls
- Created LAGOS_DATA_REQUEST_BAKAME.md — feasibility data for Bakame partnership
- Initially framed as "content partnership" — E4E provides curriculum, Bakame provides telephony

### Feb 27-28, 2026: MIT Hackathon
- Built Sabi demo for MIT Africa Business Challenge (independently, using Twilio + ElevenLabs + Claude)
- Won 2nd place, $3,000
- Bakame credited as "inspiration for Sabi's voice tutoring approach" on the website

### Mar 3-4, 2026: IP Tension
- Bakame discovered Sabi via LinkedIn
- Bakame's position: Sabi "sounds like Bakame's idea"
- Naomi initially agreed to some form of partnership/attribution
- Website updated: credited Bakame AI as creator/inspiration
- Sabi link removed from navbar temporarily

### Mar 4-5, 2026: Independent Decision
- After reflection and legal analysis, Naomi decided to move forward independently
- **Key legal points** (from BAKAME_AI_DUE_DILIGENCE_MEMO.md):
  - Ideas are not copyrightable — only implementation is
  - No NDA was ever signed
  - Different target markets (Rwanda vs Nigeria)
  - Sabi's implementation is entirely original (different LLM, different TTS, different curriculum, different pedagogy)
  - Multiple other organizations do voice-based education (ConnectEd, Rising On Air, etc.)
- Created Statement of Originality documenting what is original in Sabi
- GitHub repos made private for IP protection
- LinkedIn post about MIT win held back pending clarity
- All "Bakame" references in documents replaced with "Sabi"
- "Cambridge" framing de-emphasized in favor of foundational literacy/numeracy focus

## Current Status
- Fully independent — no ongoing relationship with Bakame
- SABI_COPYRIGHT_BRIEF.md prepared for Nigerian Copyright Commission (IP details removed to protect trade secrets)
- Trademark exploration: Nigeria (~$30-65) recommended over USPTO ($250-350)
- Bakame remains a peer in the voice-education space but is not a partner, competitor in a different market (Rwanda/Senegal vs Nigeria)
