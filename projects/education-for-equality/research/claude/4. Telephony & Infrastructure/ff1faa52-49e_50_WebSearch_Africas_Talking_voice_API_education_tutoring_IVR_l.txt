## IVR Implementation for Learning

Interactive Voice Response (IVR) is a telephony menu system that allows for interaction with callers through dialing different phone keys, working with DTMF (dual-tone multi-frequency) codes which identify the various phone keys. You can use the API's play, say, get-digits and dial actions to accomplish IVR services.
