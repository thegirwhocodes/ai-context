## First Invoice Timing

Billing begins when Hetzner gives you access to a product. For dedicated servers specifically, you will receive the invoice in the billing month AFTER you use the product.

## Retro-Active Billing

Hetzner uses pro-rated billing where you pay AFTER you use the services. More specifically, each invoice contains the services for the entire previous month.

## Invoice Generation Schedule

Since April 2024, Hetzner sends out invoices throughout the month to even out the total volume of invoices and provide faster response times to billing support requests. The system automatically picks the specified invoice date; you cannot pick a date of your choice.

## Payment Terms

Invoices are due immediately, and you must pay within five days for your first invoice or within ten days for all following invoices.

The key takeaway is that Hetzner's billing is retroactive, so you'll be invoiced in the month following your service usage, with the specific invoice date depending on your account's automatically assigned billing day.
