## Major Policy Issues in 2026

Top issues include the Rural Health Transformation Program, Medicaid enrollment and eligibility rules, provider tax changes, and marketplace affordability.

### Rural Health Transformation Program

The Rural Health Transformation Program is a $50 billion initiative established under President Trump's Working Families Tax Cuts legislation to strengthen and modernize health care in rural communities across the country. The program's funds will be allocated to approved states over five years, with $10 billion available each year from 2026 through 2030.

However, there are significant concerns about whether this funding will be sufficient. Fifty billion dollars may sound like a lot of money but it is insufficient to close the gap created by the cuts, as a KFF analysis determined that the funds could only offset about a third of the cuts (37%) to rural Medicaid spending over the 10 years.

### Key Healthcare Access Challenges

Recruiting and retaining clinicians, nurses, and specialists is more difficult in rural areas, leading to persistent gaps in care. Maternity care is a notable area of concern, as many rural hospitals have already closed obstetric units in recent years, and additional financial strain may accelerate this trend, creating more "maternity care deserts" where pregnant individuals must travel long distances for basic care.

Nonmetropolitan children and adults under 65 were more likely than their metropolitan peers to be uninsured. Additionally, many areas lack access to broadband internet and experience slow internet speeds, both of which are barriers to accessing telehealth services, with 13.4% of rural households lacking broadband subscriptions compared to 9.0% of urban households.
