## Time-to-Value Benchmarks

The SaaS activation benchmark sits at 37.5% with a median time-to-value of 1 day 12 hours, which means almost two out of three new signups in the average B2B product still never get to first value. ## Core Best Practices

**Product-Led Approach**: In 2026, PLG user onboarding should mostly be done by the product itself. Your onboarding flows are bumpers, built to keep users on the straight line to first value, shave time-to-value, and catch the people who wander.

**Minimize Steps**: Self-serve B2B SaaS should aim for under 5 minutes from sign-up to aha moment, while complex enterprise products should target under 14 days to first measurable outcome.

**Role-Based Personalization**: The pattern shift in 2026 is toward role-based personalization, with a lift from personalization of 40% retention improvement vs generic flows.

**AI-Assisted Nudging**: An AI-assisted onboarding agent more than doubled activation, not because the AI replaced the product, but because it tightened the bumpers around the steps where users were wandering. AI inside your onboarding should be thought of as a sharper bumper builder, not as a new user class.

**Remove Complexity**: The best onboarding flows have one thing in common: they removed more than they added. Slack won onboarding by removing every step between sign-up and sending a message.
