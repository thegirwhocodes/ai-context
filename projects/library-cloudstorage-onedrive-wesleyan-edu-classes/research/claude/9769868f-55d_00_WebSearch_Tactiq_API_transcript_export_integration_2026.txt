## Transcript Export Capabilities

Tactiq can turn live transcriptions into a range of documents and formats using PDF or TXT export to automate documentation, content for knowledge bases, and task briefs. Users can export transcripts to Google Doc, Notion, Slack or PDF.

## Integration Support

Tactiq is a browser extension that automatically transcribes Zoom and Google Meet meetings to your Google Drive, Quip, Notion or other platforms. The platform supports multiple integrations including:

- OneDrive for saving transcripts and AI outputs directly to storage, with Workflows automation capabilities
- Pipedrive integration for automatically saving and organizing meeting transcripts, with the ability to link transcripts to Pipedrive contacts
- Confluence integration for storing transcripts and sharing highlights
- Google Drive integration for automatically storing and organizing meeting transcripts
- HubSpot integration for sending meeting details, conversations, and transcripts to HubSpot leads

## Integration Depth

However, it's worth noting that while Tactiq can share transcripts to various tools, the integration depth is limited - with CRMs, you can't use Tactiq to automatically fill out the correct fields, requiring manual copying and pasting instead.
