## Background and Current Role

Dr. Sanjeev Mohindra is a senior staff member of the ISR and Tactical Systems Division at MIT Lincoln Laboratory. He leads the AI Technology Group at MIT Lincoln Laboratory, advancing artificial intelligence that strengthens national security.

## Research Interests and Expertise

Mohindra's research interests lie in the area of scalable AI, AI engineering, big data, and distributed computing. His work focuses on making AI reliable and secure for defense applications. Under a program funded by the Chief Digital and AI Office (CDAO), he is developing a library to simplify the AI test and evaluation process by providing user- and developer-friendly interfaces for composing T&E workflows.

## Education and Prior Experience

He received a PhD degree from Cornell University and a BTech degree from the Indian Institute of Technology in Delhi, India. Before coming to MIT Lincoln Laboratory, he worked with a startup developing smart storage technology with computing close to the hardware. Prior to that, he worked at Arthur D. Little, Inc. in a technology consulting role.

## Research Leadership

Sanjeev Mohindra is leader of the Artificial Intelligence Technology Group, and he serves as MIT Lincoln Laboratory Lead on multiple research projects including the DAF-MIT AI Accelerator initiatives.
