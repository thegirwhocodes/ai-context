**Abi**: Abi means "Right" and comes from Yoruba. It can be used as a suffix or prefix to seek approval or inclusion, similar to the English word 'right?' – for example, "You go follow me abi?" means "You will come with me right?"

**Biko**: Biko simply means "please" and is used when begging or requesting.

**Nko**: Nko comes from Yoruba 'Ńkọ́' and is an interrogative meaning "How about?"
