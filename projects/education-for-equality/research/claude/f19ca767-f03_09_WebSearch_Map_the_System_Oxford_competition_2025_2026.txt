## Overview
Map the System is a global learning programme and social innovation competition that equips students to use systems thinking to tackle social and environmental challenges. The Skoll Centre partners with universities worldwide each year, supporting educators to deliver the Map the System programme at their institutions before inviting top student teams from all over the world to convene for a final competition in Oxford.

## 2025-2026 Competition Details
To join for 2025-26, institutions must register before 31st January 2026. One team of finalists from each partner institution is selected to present their findings at the Global Final event in 9-12 July 2026.

## How It Works
Through a structured learning journey involving an online learning module, carefully designed guides and activities, virtual gatherings, and peer interactions, students work in teams to dive deep into a social or environmental topic they are passionate about. Unlike traditional pitch competitions, participants are evaluated based not on a 'solution' to a problem, but rather on their depth of understanding of it.

## Recent Success (2025 Global Final)
The 2025 Global Final has already concluded, with the team from Vanderbilt University earning second place at the 2025 Map the System Global Final for their in-depth analysis of climate vulnerability among Mongolian herders.
