## Overview

The 3-2-1 Service begins with a simple concept: make information freely available to those who do not otherwise have the means to access it. Through partnerships with telecom operators, the service is free for people to access at least many times per month, allowing it to reach major scale.

## How It Works

Callers dial into the service number (321) and are guided through a series of interactive voice response (IVR) menu prompts to retrieve information on a variety of topics, including agriculture, gender-based violence prevention, microfinance and the weather.

Key messages are accessed through a series of listen-and-choose steps, where the user navigates through Interactive Voice Response (IVR) decision trees. Callers navigate menus, play games, take quizzes, schedule call-backs, provide feedback, receive airtime payment, etc.

## Accessibility and Features

The Viamo 3-2-1 platform is accessible to anyone who has access to a mobile device. It leverages SMS and voice technologies to create impact. The service is appropriate for individuals with a low literacy level, as all content is delivered in audio format.

## Nigeria Specific Applications

Viamo has helped partners deliver legal advice to victims of sexual and gender-based violence in Nigeria.
