## Overview of Lelapa AI

Lelapa AI has introduced InkubaLM, Africa's first multilingual AI large language model, aimed at supporting and enhancing low-resource African languages.

## InkubaLM Language Model

The model is starting with Swahili, Yoruba, IsiXhosa, Hausa, and isiZulu. This model is named after the dung beetle for its efficient design and seeks to address the digital underrepresentation of these languages by providing tools for translation, transcription, and various natural language processing tasks.

InkubaLM-0.4B has been trained from scratch using 1.9 billion tokens of data for the five African languages, along with English and French data, totalling 2.4 billion tokens of data.

## Vulavula API - How It Works

Vulavula converts multilingual call centre conversations into structured text, ready for your analytics, QA, and compliance systems. Lelapa turns its models into usable infrastructure through Vulavula, a multilingual API that offers speech recognition, translation, sentiment analysis and intent detection for African languages and code-switching.

The Vulavula API allows seamless integration into your businesses applications. Connect straight to our API, connect through a cloud marketplace or deploy our models on your private on prem.

Lelapa works with expert human linguists, so the language models are able to capture local lingo, local names, accents, and can even handle code switching (when speakers switch between different languages in a conversation).
