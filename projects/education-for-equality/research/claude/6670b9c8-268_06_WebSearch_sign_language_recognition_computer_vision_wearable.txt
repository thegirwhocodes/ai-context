The vision-based approach is non-intrusive, scalable, and capable of accurately capturing entire body gestures, compared to the sensor-based approach. Sensor-based approaches rely on wearable devices like gloves, motion sensors, and accelerometers, but may be highly expensive and cause discomfort.
