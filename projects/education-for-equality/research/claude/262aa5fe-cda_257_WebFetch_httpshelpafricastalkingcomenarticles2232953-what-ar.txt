# Africa's Talking API Endpoints

Based on the provided documentation, here are all listed endpoints:

**Airtime**
- Live: `https://api.africastalking.com/version1/airtime/send`
- Sandbox: `https://api.sandbox.africastalking.com/version1/airtime/send`

**SMS**
- Live: `https://api.africastalking.com/version1/messaging`
- Sandbox: `https://api.sandbox.africastalking.com/version1/messaging`

**Voice**
- Live: `https://voice.africastalking.com/call`
- Sandbox: `https://voice.sandbox.africastalking.com/call`

**Payments**
- B2C Live: `https://payments.africastalking.com/mobile/b2c/request`
- B2C Sandbox: `https://payments.sandbox.africastalking.com/mobile/b2c/request`
- B2B Live: `https://payments.africastalking.com/mobile/b2b/request`
- B2B Sandbox: `https://payments.sandbox.africastalking.com/mobile/b2b/request`
- Checkout Live: `https://payments.africastalking.com/mobile/checkout/request`
- Checkout Sandbox: `https://payments.sandbox.africastalking.com/mobile/checkout/request`
