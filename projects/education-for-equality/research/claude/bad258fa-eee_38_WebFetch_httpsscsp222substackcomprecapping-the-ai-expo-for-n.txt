# AI Expo for National Competitiveness Recap

## Attendance & Scale
The event drew **over 10,000 attendees, 150 exhibitors, and 440 speakers** across two days in Washington, DC. The expo featured 16 stages and multiple specialized venues.

## Key Programming & Speakers
Senior government officials participated prominently, including:
- National Security Advisor Jake Sullivan
- Secretary of Commerce Gina Raimondo
- Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff General C.Q. Brown, Jr.
- Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer

International representation included the UK Chief of the Defence Staff and officials from Singapore and the Philippines.

## Notable Elements
**Demos & Exhibits**: Companies showcased robots, drones, and software. OpenAI demonstrated ChatGPT capabilities, while Palantir's IC2 technology and Google DeepMind offerings were on display. The Department of Energy and National Labs exhibited.

**Talent Marketplace**: The expo included career development opportunities and educational pathways for professionals at all levels.

**Bookstore**: Over 100 books were displayed with author conversations, including David Sanger discussing his work on U.S.-China relations.

## Outcomes
The organizers characterized the event as "a catalyst for collective action" that bridged cutting-edge innovation with national security concerns. Upcoming video content from speakers will be published on SCSP's YouTube channel.
