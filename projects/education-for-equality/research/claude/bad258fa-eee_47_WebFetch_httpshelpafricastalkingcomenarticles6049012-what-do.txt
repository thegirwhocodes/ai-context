# SIP Trunk Setup Requirements at Africa's Talking

## Prerequisites
You must have "A PBX server (on cloud or on premise)" to establish the connection.

## Step-by-Step Setup Process

1. **Apply for a voice phone number** through Africa's Talking

2. **Complete KYC verification** - The provider shares documentation that requires your signature

3. **Payment** - You pay for the phone number according to their pricing structure at africastalking.com/pricing

4. **Submit PBX details** - You provide your trunk's IP address so the company can add it to their whitelist for security

5. **Configure callback URL** - Once whitelisted, update your number's callback URL from your dashboard using the format: "trunk:trunk_ip_address" (example: trunk:127.0.0.1)

6. **Test your setup** - Configure your PBX system and conduct testing

The documentation doesn't specify a timeline for completion, though whitelisting appears to be a gating step before you can proceed with PBX configuration.
