## Is "5 minutes" defensible?

**Depends on what it's attached to.** If you mean "5-minute average ticket resolution" — **no, not defensible** for a university help desk. Recruiters who've worked help desks will know that's too fast across mixed ticket types.

**What IS defensible:**
- **"5-minute average resolution for password/portal login tickets"** ✅ (these are quick by nature)
- **"First-contact resolution within 5 minutes on routine tickets"** ✅
- **"Average call pickup time under 2 minutes"** ✅
- **"~30-minute average resolution across all tickets"** ✅ (industry norm)

**What's NOT defensible:**
- "5-minute turnaround" as a blanket claim across all tickets ❌
- Anything implying hardware/network issues get resolved in minutes ❌

So if you keep "12-hour turnaround," that's safe. If you want to replace with "5 minutes," scope it to specific ticket types.
