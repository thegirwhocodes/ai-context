## Design & Durability

The DJI Pocket 3 is primarily designed for vlogging and is designed to be used while walking and provide a smooth gimbal-steady image. In contrast, the DJI Action 5 is primarily designed for action sports, underwater filming, and mounting in areas that require small and durable cameras.

The DJI Osmo Action 5 is significantly lighter (33g) than the DJI Pocket 3. The Action 5 Pro features a robust, waterproof body (up to 20m without an additional case). Conversely, the Pocket 3 has no weather sealing, and should you drop it, the gimbal can easily break.

## Audio Quality

The Pocket 3 has 4 mics on board, giving you great audio straight out of the device. The Osmo Pocket 3 tends to excel in capturing clearer sound due to its design, making it a better choice for vlogging. Both cameras offer excellent integration with the DJI Mic 2, but the Pocket 3's multi-mic array and control often lead to slightly better overall built-in audio quality, especially for voice.

## Image & Video Quality

The Pocket 3 produces a much nicer image compared to the Action 5, as the larger sensor lets more light in, giving a cleaner low-light image. The fast f/2 lens gives a nice background blur, and the AF ensures that your subject is in focus. If you want the best quality image, the Pocket 3 is a clear winner.

The Osmo Pocket 3 features a dedicated low-light video mode that outshines the Action 5 Pro in darker settings. While the Action 5 Pro has a super night mode, it tends to struggle under low-light conditions.

## Stabilization

The gimbal stabilization in the Pocket 3 sets it apart from the Action 5 Pro. This feature allows for smoother footage, especially in low-light conditions where the shutter speed drops. While both cameras offer stabilization, the Pocket 3's gimbal provides superior performance, especially during dynamic movements. The Action 5 Pro relies on electronic stabilization, which may not match the smoothness of the Pocket 3 in certain scenarios.

## Field of View

The Osmo Action 5 has a wider field of view at 12.7mm. The DJI Pocket 3 has a 20mm lens. The Osmo Action 5 Pro provides a wider field of view, which can be beneficial for capturing expansive landscapes or engaging vlogs.

## Recommendations by Use Case

If you're focused on vlogging in various lighting conditions and value portability, the Pocket 3 is an excellent choice. However, if you're looking for a durable, waterproof action camera that can handle rough use, the Action 5 Pro may be the better fit.

If superior cinematic image quality (especially in low light), incredibly smooth mechanical stabilization, advanced subject tracking, and a more versatile focal length for vlogging and everyday life capture are your priorities, the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 stands out. Its 1-inch sensor and integrated gimbal make it a miniature filmmaking powerhouse.
