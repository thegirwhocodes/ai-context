## Phone Number Categories

Toll-Free (0800), Premium Numbers (0900) and virtualized numbers do not make outgoing calls but the regular numbers attached to them (also known as hunting numbers) can make the outgoing calls.

## SIP Services

Africa's Talking offers two distinct SIP-related services:

**SIP Trunk:** Using SIP trunks, we can connect to your Private Branch System (PBX) and allow you to make and receive voice calls. To set up a trunk, you need a PBX server (on cloud or on premise). You apply for a voice phone number, sign a KYC document, pay for the phone number, and share your trunk's (PBX) IP address for whitelisting.

**SIP Phone:** You can use SIP endpoints to make and receive calls on your browser, which lowers infrastructure costs significantly.

## Billing

SIP calls are 1/- per minute for SIP to SIP calls, while calls to regular numbers and virtual numbers are still charged 5/- per minute.
