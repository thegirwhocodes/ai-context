- **Author:** Benard Akalbeo, **Year:** 2025, **Title:** "Does the resource curse exist? Evidence from oil discovery and production in Ghana," **Journal:** Resources Policy

**Methodology:**
The study uses the synthetic control method for comparative case studies approach, exploiting the quasi-experimental nature of the oil discovery which was due to an exogenous shock.

**Key Findings:**
The oil discovery and production have a positive impact on economic growth and a negative impact on the reduction of unemployment (through a crowding out effect); specifically, over the period 2009-2018, GDP per capita increased by 501.11 USD per year on average, amounting to approximately 45.5% of the 2009 baseline level.

Additionally, the sectoral decomposition documents strong evidence of Dutch Disease through the persistent underperformance of agriculture and the stagnation of services relative to their synthetic trajectories.
