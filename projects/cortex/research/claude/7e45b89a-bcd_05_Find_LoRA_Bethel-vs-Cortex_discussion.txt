### 2. **Cortex: Hybrid Architecture (RAG + optional LoRA)** (efd7d7d2, lines 6754-6885)

**Initial approach (rejected):** Naomi first asked "I want to train an LLM on personal user data" — but Claude explained the cost/feasibility tradeoff.

**What she settled on (efd7d7d2, lines 6789-6887):**

>

**Cost comparison (efd7d7d2, lines 6875-6881):**

| **Real-time** | No (hours to retrain) | Yes (instant) |

**Premium tier (efd7d7d2, line 6885):**
