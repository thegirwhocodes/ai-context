## Current Limitation

You cannot sort photos/videos on an iPhone by file size or duration. iOS 18 does not include a simple "sort by size" option for videos, and this limitation persists even in the latest versions.

## Available Sorting Options

In iOS 18, you can sort by Date Captured, which lists them by when the images were taken. However, there is no built-in native option to sort videos specifically by duration in the Photos app's Videos album.

## Workarounds

If you need to find large or long videos, here are some alternatives:

1. **Storage Settings**: Navigate to Settings > General > iPhone Storage for a storage overview, though it does not specifically show videos by size.

2. **Third-Party Apps**: PowerPhotos ($40) offers many enhancements to Photos, including list views of albums that can be sorted by file size.

3. **Shortcuts App**: The Shortcuts app, which comes preinstalled on iPhones starting with iOS 13, is a built-in automation tool that allows you to create commands, including features for working with videos stored in the Photos app.

Unfortunately, there is no native "sort by duration" option in the iOS 18/19 Photos app for the Videos album.
