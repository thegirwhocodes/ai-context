# The Writing Mentor Program: Philosophy and Structure

## Core Philosophy

The program emphasizes **personalized, collaborative support** for student writers. It operates on the belief that regular peer mentorship builds confidence and develops sustainable writing practices. As one participant noted, "The Writing Mentor Program ignited a new sense of initiative" and helped her realize the importance of seeking help proactively.

## Program Structure

- **Frequency**: Weekly 45-minute sessions throughout the semester (beginning week three, ending at reading period)
- **Format**: Students enroll in a .25 credit tutorial with consistent mentor pairings
- **Cost**: All services are free

## Mentor Work and Values

Mentors address diverse writing challenges across all disciplines and ability levels, including:
- Ideation and brainstorming
- Essay organization and paragraph structure
- Grammar and sentence clarity
- Time management and writing processes
- Building confidence in writing

The program particularly emphasizes **accessibility and approachability**, positioning mentors as peer collaborators rather than authority figures. By design, the program values **consistency and commitment**, expecting mentees to engage regularly even without pressing assignments, recognizing that ongoing collaboration itself strengthens writing development.
