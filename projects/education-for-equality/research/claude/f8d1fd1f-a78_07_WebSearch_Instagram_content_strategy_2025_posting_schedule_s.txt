## Posting Schedule & Cadence

For Reels, an ideal cadence is 3–5 per week for active growth. For most brands, 3–5 feed posts per week (mixing Reels and carousels) plus daily Stories is a realistic baseline. For Stories, aim for daily presence without over-posting.

Posting five strong pieces consistently outperforms posting seven rushed ones. Instagram rewards stable output more than high volume.

## Format Strategy

Use Reels for discovery, Carousels to teach, Posts for authority, and Stories for daily relationship-building. Each format serves a distinct purpose:

- **Reels**: Use for trends, tutorials, product reveals, and short case studies. Ideal cadence: 3–5 per week for active growth
- **Carousels**: Use for education and micro-guides. Excellent for saves and time-on-post
- **Stories**: Use for relationship and retention. Use for polls, updates, behind-the-scenes, and CTAs to longer content

## Growth Optimization

Posting consistently matters more to the algorithm than posting frequency alone, as consistency signals to Instagram that you're an active, reliable creator. Find a sustainable cadence you can maintain rather than posting sporadically.

The Instagram algorithm favors content that earns high engagement quickly, particularly shares and saves, as well as Reels, carousel posts, and content that keeps viewers watching or scrolling.

## Key Metrics to Track

For reels, sends via DM are the most heavily weighted signal for distribution. Carousels and original content with trending audio consistently outperform single images and reshared posts.
