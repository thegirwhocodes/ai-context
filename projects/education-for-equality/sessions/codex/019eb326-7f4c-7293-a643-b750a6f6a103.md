## Codex · 2026-06-10T22:08:45.882Z · final_answer

A **CAC Incorporated Trustee** is Nigeria’s standard legal form for an NGO/nonprofit. It means the **trustees of an association are registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission**, giving the organization legal personality so it can hold property, open accounts, sue/be sued, enter agreements, receive grants, etc.

For E4E, it would be the Nigerian nonprofit/NGO arm: something like **“Incorporated Trustees of Education for Equality”** or similar, used for local Lagos operations, school partnerships, Nigerian grants, FIRS/SCUML compliance, etc.

And no, **you do not need to be an NGO to trademark something**.

A trademark can generally be owned by:

- an individual
- a company
- an NGO / Incorporated Trustee
- a foreign entity
- an association/society

For **Sabi**, the cleanest options are:

1. **If nonprofit is already formed:** have the nonprofit own the trademark.
2. **If not formed yet and you need to protect it now:** you can file as **Naomi Ivie personally**, then assign/license it to the nonprofit later.
3. **If future commercial arm exists:** nonprofit can own the IP and license it to the for-profit.

One correction: trademark registration in Nigeria is not really “through CAC” in the same way NGO registration is. CAC handles entity registration. Trademarks go through Nigeria’s **Trademarks Registry / Nigerian Industrial Property Office** under the Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment.

Sources: [CAC Incorporated Trustees](https://cac.gov.ng/services/incorporated-trustees), [Nigerian Industrial Property Office trademark procedure](https://nipo.gov.ng/trademark.jsp).
