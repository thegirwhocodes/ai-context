## Economic Costs and Returns on Investment

This generation of students risks losing $21 trillion in potential lifetime earnings in present value, or the equivalent of 17 per cent of today's global GDP. Additionally, the cost of 250 million children around the world not learning the basics translates into a loss of an estimated $129 billion.
