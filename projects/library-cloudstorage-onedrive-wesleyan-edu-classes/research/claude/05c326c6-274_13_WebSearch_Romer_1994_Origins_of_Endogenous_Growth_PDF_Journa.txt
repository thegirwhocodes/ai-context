I found the paper you're looking for. Romer, Paul M. published "The Origins of Endogenous Growth" in the Journal of Economic Perspectives, Vol. 8, No. 1 (Winter 1994), pages 3–22.

The paper describes two strands of work that converged under the heading of 'endogenous growth': one primarily empirical strand asks whether poor countries tend to catch up with rich countries, while the other primarily theoretical strand asks what modifications are necessary to construct a theory of growth that takes the economics of discovery, innovation, and technological change seriously.
