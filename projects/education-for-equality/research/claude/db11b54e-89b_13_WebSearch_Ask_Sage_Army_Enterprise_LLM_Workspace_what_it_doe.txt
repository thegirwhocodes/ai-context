## What It Does

The Army Enterprise LLM Workspace is a generative AI tool hosted within the Army's cArmy Cloud, supported by Ask Sage's Impact Level 5 software-as-a-service. It's deployed across IL5 environments and accessible to soldiers, civilians, and contractors through the cArmy Cloud.

## Key Features

The platform is CUI accredited, which ensures compliance with security standards for sensitive information, features a lower barrier to entry through Software-as-a-service capability that eliminates the need for complex installations, and is free for 30 days to allow immediate access for eligible users with CAC registration.

The platform leverages technologies such as Azure OpenAI Gov, Mistral, LLM3A.3.3, AWS Bedrock Gov, Google Gemini and several open-source LLMs.

The SaaS-based nature allows soldiers and Army civilians to access the system without complex installations, with registration streamlined via common access cards for quick and secure onboarding.

## Proven Impact

The Army was able to update 300,000 personnel descriptions in a week using Army Enterprise LLM Workspace, whereas reviewing each description manually would have taken roughly 50,000 hours.

The Army awarded Ask Sage an indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contract with a $49 million ceiling and a five-year ordering period.
