## General Hiring and Onboarding Costs

The average cost per hire is nearly $4,700, though various factors could affect the cost, including your business's size, location and industry, as well as the specific role you must fill.

## Cost Components

**Hard Costs:**
Organizations should expect to spend $7,500–$28,000 in hard costs to find and onboard a new employee, including job board fees, background checks, and the new hire's training. Additionally, businesses spend an average of $1,111 to $1,252 per employee on training.

**Soft Costs:**
Soft costs—such as lost productivity—can add up to as much as 60% of the total cost to hire.

**Expense Categories:**
Costs typically include job advertising, screening, interview coordination, onboarding, training, equipment, and lost productivity — plus compensation, benefits, and taxes.

## External Recruitment Agencies

Employers can expect to pay a commission of 15% to 30% of the hired employee's first-year salary. For example, if the new hire gets paid $50,000 annually, the recruiter will receive between $7,500 and $15,000.
