# Codex subagent report: Peirce

**My Take**
Retire the node/orb idea entirely. Cortex should not look like “AI”; it should look like a quiet personal operating system with an intelligent inner structure.

1. **Cortical Fold C**  
Visual: an open `C` monogram drawn as a calm rounded stroke, with one smaller inner fold. It hints at cortex/sulcus without becoming a brain illustration.  
Pros: name-linked, memorable, works tiny, serious but not sterile.  
Cons: if the curve closes too much it becomes an orb/spiral; keep the right side clearly open.  
SVG guidance: `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`, no fill, `strokeLinecap="round"`, outer stroke `#F4F1EA`, inner stroke `#E3A857`.

2. **Command Frame**  
Visual: four restrained corner strokes forming an open square/frame, with a small gold vertical index line inside. Feels like a focus window, OS shell, or decision aperture.  
Pros: very dashboard-friendly, crisp as favicon, avoids all AI tropes.  
Cons: less emotionally ownable; could read as generic productivity unless paired with a strong wordmark.  
SVG guidance: 24px viewBox, 2px rounded strokes, cream frame, gold center line. At 16px use monochrome only. For macOS, place it on a warm black squircle with the mark occupying about 58% of canvas.

3. **Memory Index**  
Visual: two or three offset vertical rounded “pages” or bands, with one gold inset notch. It should read as accumulated context, not analytics bars.  
Pros: strong fit for life agent/memory/context, mature, non-sci-fi.  
Cons: weaker as a standalone “Cortex” identifier; risks looking like docs/bookmarks if too literal.  
SVG guidance: use filled rounded rects rather than thin strokes. Main fills `#F4F1EA` at 85-95% opacity, one small accent fill `#E3A857`. At 16px reduce to two shapes max.

**Recommendation**
Use **Cortical Fold C**. It gives Cortex a real mark, not just an app icon, and it can flex: mono cream at 16px, cream/gold at sidebar size, and a premium dark squircle treatment for Tauri/macOS. Pair it with an Instrument Serif `Cortex` wordmark, but keep the mark itself geometric and quiet. No glow, no gradient, no dots.
