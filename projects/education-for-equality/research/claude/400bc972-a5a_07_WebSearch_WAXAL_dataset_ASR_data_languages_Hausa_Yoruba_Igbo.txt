## WAXAL Dataset Overview

WAXAL is an open-source speech database designed to support the development of voice-based artificial intelligence for African languages. The dataset was developed over three years to empower researchers and drive the development of inclusive technology across Africa.

## Languages Covered

Languages included in the dataset are: Acholi, Akan, Dagaare, Dagbani, Dholuo, Ewe, Fante, Fulani (Fula), Hausa, Igbo, Ikposo (Kposo), Kikuyu, Lingala, Luganda, Malagasy, Masaaba, Nyankole, Rukiga, Shona, Soga (Lusoga), Swahili, and Yoruba.

## Data Composition

The collection consists of an Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) dataset containing approximately 1,250 hours of transcribed, natural speech from a diverse range of speakers, and a Text-to-Speech (TTS) dataset with over 235 hours of high-quality, single-speaker recordings.

## Nigerian Languages Specifically

For the three major Nigerian languages you asked about:

- **ASR Data**: Languages for ASR include Acholi, Kiswahili, Luganda, Akan, Ewe, Shona, Fula, and Lingala, among others (Note: Hausa, Igbo, and Yoruba are not included in the ASR component)
- **TTS Data**: TTS data is available for Hausa, Igbo, and Yoruba, among others.

The dataset contains more than 11,000 hours of speech drawn from nearly two million individual recordings. The complete WAXAL collection is released under an open license and is available on Hugging Face.
