## Publication Details:

The paper was published in The Quarterly Journal of Economics, Volume 114, Issue 1, February 1999, Pages 83–116, by Robert E. Hall and Charles I. Jones.

## Key Findings:

The analysis shows that differences in physical capital and educational attainment can only partially explain the variation in output per worker. Differences in capital accumulation, productivity, and output per worker are driven by differences in institutions and government policies, which they call "social infrastructure".
