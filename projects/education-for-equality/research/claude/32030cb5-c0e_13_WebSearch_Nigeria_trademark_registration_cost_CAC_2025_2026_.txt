## Trademark Registration Costs (NIPO)

The official cost for obtaining a trade mark in Nigeria is USD80, though this applies to the government filing fee. However, the actual costs when using legal services are much higher:

- Trademark registration (word mark) costs approximately ₦150,000 to ₦350,000 all-in per class
- Costs vary, but range from ₦200,000 above including agent/legal fees, application and certificate fees, and optional fast-tracking services
- Trademark registration costs ₦15,500+ per class through the Nigerian Intellectual Property Office

## Important Note: CAC vs. Trademark Registration

It's crucial to understand that CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission) and trademark registration are completely different. CAC Business Name Registration costs ₦10,000 (business name) or ₦50,000+ (limited company) but does NOT provide trademark protection. CAC registration alone does NOT protect your brand name.

For comprehensive brand protection in Nigeria, you need both CAC business registration and separate trademark registration with NIPO (Nigerian Intellectual Property Office).
