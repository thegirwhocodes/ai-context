## Overview

Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) is an approach developed by the Indian NGO Pratham that aims to build foundational skills in math and reading for all children before exiting primary school.

## Lesson Structure and Progression

TaRL sessions follow a non-linear and interactive approach, providing instructors with significant autonomy in creating session plans, unlike traditional scripted lesson plans where instructors create session plans based on observations and formative assessments.

TaRL classes incorporate whole-group, small-group, and individual activities, which gives children the opportunity to teach and learn from each other.

## Assessment and Grouping

A key feature of TaRL is its approach to student grouping. Children are assessed using a simple tool and then grouped according to their learning level rather than their age or grade. TaRL being an accelerated learning program, focuses on progressing children from one learning level to the next quickly.

## Literacy Levels

The reading tools across countries are progressive and comprise 5-6 levels such as beginner, with additional levels including letter, word, paragraph, and story levels.

## Learning Progression

As children progress, the instructor reassesses and reassigns them to more advanced groups. This allows for continuous advancement through the literacy progression based on demonstrated competency rather than time spent in a level.
