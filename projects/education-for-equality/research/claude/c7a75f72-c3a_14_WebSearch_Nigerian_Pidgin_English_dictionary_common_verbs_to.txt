## Key Verb: "Sabi"

Sabi means "to know" or "to know how to". For example, "you sabi do am?" means "do you know how to do it?"

## Verb Structure in Nigerian Pidgin

While the search results mention "sabi" as the primary verb for knowing, they provide broader information about how verbs work in Nigerian Pidgin that relates to expressing learning and teaching concepts:

Nigerian Pidgin uses simplified verb forms: 'dey' for continuous actions (I dey go = I am going), 'don' for completed actions (I don chop = I have eaten), 'go' for future (I go come = I will come).

Verbs in Nigerian Pidgin do not change based on tense but use auxiliary markers to show time.
