## Caption/Subtitle Style for Christian Worship & Guitar Music Videos on Social Media

### 1. Caption Style Philosophy: Music-First vs. Attention-Grabbing

There are two distinct approaches, and for worship/acoustic content, the research strongly favors the **aesthetic lyric video style** over the "viral attention-grabbing" caption style:

| Approach | Best For | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Viral/Attention-Grabbing** (Hormozi-style) | Talking head, educational, high-energy content | Bold, large, colored, word-by-word pop with zoom effects, high contrast backgrounds |
| **Aesthetic Lyric Video** (recommended for worship) | Emotional, spiritual, music-first content | Soft fade-ins, muted tones, clean fonts, line-by-line reveals, lower opacity |

Lyric videos drive **emotional connection and shareability** -- viewers save and share them because they relate personally to the words. Meanwhile, 85% of social media videos are watched on mute, making some form of on-screen lyrics essential even for music content.

### 2. Font Recommendations (Specific Names)

**Top tier for worship/acoustic content:**
- **CMG Sans** -- purpose-built for worship lyrics, free to download from Church Motion Graphics, works across all applications
- **Open Sans** -- highly readable, clean, works on any background
- **Montserrat** -- the most-used font in lyric/caption videos (used in ~60% of the 2 million videos analyzed by Submagic)

**Secondary options depending on mood:**
- **Lora** or **Playfair Display** -- serif fonts that add tradition/warmth for hymns or reflective worship
- A clean **handwritten font** (e.g., Sacramento, Dancing Script) -- for intimate acoustic/unplugged feel, use sparingly
- **Bebas Neue** -- tall, narrow, modern look for more contemporary worship
- **Raleway** -- elegant sans-serif, good for a refined aesthetic

**Avoid:** Impact, Gotham Bold, overly decorative/script fonts that sacrifice readability. Overly thin fonts also cause readability issues on small mobile screens.

### 3. Colors and Contrast (Hex Values)

**Primary text color:**
- **White `#FFFFFF`** -- the universal standard for lyrics over video. Works on virtually any background.

**Text shadow/outline for readability:**
- **Black drop shadow at 40-60% opacity** (`#000000` at 0.4-0.6 alpha) -- softens the shadow so it doesn't look harsh but ensures readability against bright backgrounds
- Alternatively, a **soft dark text glow/outline** of 1-2px

**Accent color options for worship genre (use sparingly, e.g., for highlighted words):**
- Warm gold: `#D4A853` or `#C9A96E`
- Soft cream/warm white: `#F5F0E8`
- Muted dusty rose: `#C4A4A0` (for intimate/reflective mood)
- Soft sky blue: `#A8C4D4` (for praise/hope themes)

**Background overlay (if used behind text):**
- Semi-transparent black bar: `#000000` at **20-30% opacity** (much lower than the 70-80% used for talking-head content -- you want the visual/performance to show through)

### 4. Lyrics Display Method

**For worship/acoustic content: Line-by-line or phrase-by-phrase is recommended over word-by-word.**

- **Line-by-line fade-in**: Entire lines appear and disappear smoothly. This is the gentler approach, perfect for ballads and slower worship songs. It feels contemplative rather than frenetic.
- **Phrase-by-phrase**: 3-6 words at a time, synced to natural vocal phrasing. A good middle ground.
- **Word-by-word highlight**: Better for upbeat praise songs where you want dynamic energy. Each word appears or highlights timed to vocals.

**Key stat**: Precisely synced lyrics show up to a **60% jump in fan engagement** versus unsynchronized or absent lyrics.

**Recommendation for acoustic worship**: Use **phrase-by-phrase** (matching natural vocal breath groups), with a **soft fade-in** transition. Limit to **1-2 lines on screen** at any time to avoid clutter.

### 5. Opacity, Size, and Positioning

**Opacity:**
- Text opacity: **85-95%** (slightly less than full white to feel softer/more integrated with the visual)
- If using a text background bar: **15-25% opacity** black
- Drop shadow: **40-60% opacity**

**Font size (relative to 1080x1920 vertical frame):**
- Primary lyrics: **42-56px** (large enough to read on mobile but not overpowering)
- Secondary text (song title/artist credit): **24-32px**
- Never go below 36px for lyrics on mobile -- readability drops significantly

**Positioning:**
- **Lower third** (bottom 33% of screen) -- the standard for worship lyric projection and music content. Places the text below the visual action (your hands on guitar, your face).
- **Center-bottom** is the sweet spot -- vertically about 70-80% down the frame
- Keep text horizontally centered with comfortable margins (at least 10% padding from screen edges)
- Limit to **2 lines maximum** to avoid clutter

### 6. Animation and Timing

**Recommended transitions for worship content:**
- **Fade in / Fade out (soft dissolve)**: 300-500ms fade duration. This is the most appropriate for contemplative/spiritual content.
- Avoid: pop-in, bounce, zoom, shake, or any "punchy" animation -- these feel jarring against acoustic/worship music
- A gentle **slide-up** (moving 10-20px upward while fading in over 400ms) adds subtle motion without distraction

**Timing specifics:**
- Lyrics should appear **0.1-0.2 seconds before** the vocal begins (gives the viewer time to start reading)
- Lyrics should linger **0.3-0.5 seconds after** the vocal phrase ends before fading out
- Between phrases, allow a brief **0.5-1.0 second gap** with no text on screen -- this breathing room is important for worship content, letting the music sit
- Transition duration: **~500ms** (half a second) -- fast enough not to feel sluggish but slow enough to feel smooth and intentional

### 7. Successful Creator Approaches

Successful Christian worship/music creators on social media tend to:
- Show the **performance first** (guitar playing, singing) with lyrics as a supporting overlay, not the focus
- Use **clean, minimal aesthetic** -- the video itself (warm tones, natural lighting) does the emotional heavy lifting
- Post lyrics in the **description/caption** as well, expanding on the meaning to encourage community discussion
- Post **4-7 Reels per week** for algorithmic reach
- Pair lyric captions with a question or reflection prompt in the post caption (e.g., "This line changed everything for me -- has a song ever spoken to you like that?")

### 8. Summary: Technical Spec Sheet

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Font** | CMG Sans, Open Sans, or Montserrat |
| **Font weight** | Regular or Medium (not Bold, not Light) |
| **Font size** | 42-56px on 1080x1920 canvas |
| **Text color** | White `#FFFFFF` at 90% opacity |
| **Drop shadow** | Black `#000000`, 40-50% opacity, 2-4px offset, 4-6px blur |
| **Text background** | Optional, black at 20% opacity with rounded corners |
| **Position** | Center-bottom, ~75% down the frame |
| **Max lines** | 2 |
| **Display mode** | Phrase-by-phrase (3-6 words per unit) |
| **Fade in** | 400ms soft dissolve |
| **Fade out** | 400ms soft dissolve |
| **Pre-roll** | Lyrics appear 150ms before vocal |
| **Post-hold** | Lyrics linger 400ms after vocal ends |
| **Gap between phrases** | 500-800ms empty screen |
| **Accent highlight** | Warm gold `#D4A853` on key emotional words (optional) |
