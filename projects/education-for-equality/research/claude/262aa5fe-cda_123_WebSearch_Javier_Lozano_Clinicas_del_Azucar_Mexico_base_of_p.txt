Clínicas del Azúcar's mission is to give every person, regardless of his or her socioeconomic background, access to specialised diabetes care, and after 9 years of operations, CDA, with 18 clinics, is the largest private provider of specialised diabetes care in Mexico today, reaching more than 100,000 patients, 95% of whom have access to specialised treatment for the first time in their lives.
