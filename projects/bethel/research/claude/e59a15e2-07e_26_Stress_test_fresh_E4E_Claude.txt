**Education for Equality** is a nonprofit organization founded by Naomi Ivie (Wesleyan University student) that aims to deliver free, world-class education to Nigeria's 20M+ out-of-school children. It operates through two channels:

- **Sabi** (primary channel): An AI voice tutor accessible via basic phone calls -- no smartphone, internet, or reading ability required. Children dial in and Sabi teaches foundational literacy (8 modules, ~140 calls) and numeracy (6 modules, ~100 calls) through adaptive, patient voice interactions.
