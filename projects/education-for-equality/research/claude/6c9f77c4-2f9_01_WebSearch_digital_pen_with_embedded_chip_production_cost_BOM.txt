## General Embedded System BOM and Production Costs

Unit BOM can range from $20 to over $200 depending on manufacturing volumes and complexity. For higher volumes, per-unit BOM and assembly may stay under $30–40, though this can increase significantly if the design includes displays, sensors or RF modules.

## Component Breakdown Categories

The costs for embedded systems are typically divided into several categories:

- PoC at $20k–50k, hardware engineering and PCB iterations at $50k–200k, software and QA at $100k to over $500k, mechanical and tooling at $50k–150k, and certification costs from $30k–100k

## Mechanical and Tooling Costs

For a product like a digital pen, a single steel mold for a plastic enclosure can cost between €10,000 and €50,000 depending on complexity and surface finish.

## Key Trade-offs

BOM costs are essentially the production costs for the end product while NRE costs are dominated by engineering labor, and attempts to minimize the BOM cost can cause NRE to skyrocket and vice-versa.
