## Parsec Financial Management -- Research Summary

**Note:** Parsec Financial was acquired by Modera Wealth Management on April 25, 2023. The information below reflects the firm's standalone profile at the time of acquisition, plus post-acquisition context.

### Basic Profile

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Parsec Financial Management, Inc. |
| **SEC CRD#** | 104919 |
| **Headquarters** | 6 Wall Street, Asheville, North Carolina |
| **Additional Offices** | Charlotte (1999), Southern Pines (2014), Tryon (2016), Winston-Salem (2017) |
| **Founded** | 1980 by Bart Boyer |
| **AUM (pre-acquisition)** | ~$4.2 billion (6,171 accounts) |
| **Employees** | ~75 |
| **Fee Structure** | **Fee-only** (fiduciary). Asset-based fees typically 0.50%--2.00% of AUM, charged quarterly. Also offers hourly/fixed-fee arrangements. No minimum account size. |

### Leadership (at time of sale)

- **Rick Manske** -- CEO (became Modera's Chief Growth Officer + board member)
- **Bill Hansen** -- CIO (became Modera's co-CIO)
- **Harli Palme** -- COO (retained role, joined Modera board)
- **Bart Boyer** -- Founder. Retired as CEO in 2012, board chair in 2020. Started the firm from the lower level of his Asheville home on Sunset Mountain.

### Client Focus / Niche

- Serves families, high-net-worth individuals, pension/profit-sharing plans, charitable organizations, and small businesses
- ~1,890 families and businesses served across 6 NC offices
- 874 high-net-worth individual accounts; largest segment by count is retail investors
- Services: investment management, financial planning, tax planning, trust services, business retirement planning

### Awards, Recognition, and Community Impact

- One of the first fee-only fiduciary advisory firms in the United States (est. 1980)
- Created the **Parsec Financial CFP Certification Diversity Scholarship** with CFP Board -- awards up to $5,000 (certificate) or $10,000 (baccalaureate) annually to underrepresented candidates in the Southeast pursuing CFP certification
- Post-acquisition, Modera (including Parsec) made the **Barron's Top 100 RIA Firms** list for 2023 and 2025

### The Acquisition

- **Acquirer:** Modera Wealth Management, LLC (Westwood, NJ)
- **Date:** April 25, 2023
- **Combined entity:** $10.5B AUM, 180+ employees, 14 offices (MA to FL), 61 employee-owners
- All 75 Parsec employees joined Modera; 24 became Modera owners
- Parsec continues to operate under its brand name for existing clients

### What Made Parsec Attractive as an Acquisition Target

1. **Scale and geography** -- $4.2B AUM with deep roots across North Carolina, giving geographic expansion into the Southeast for a Northeast-based acquirer
2. **Fee-only purity** -- One of the original fee-only firms in the US (since 1980), strong fiduciary brand with no commission conflicts
3. **Cultural and philosophical alignment** -- Both firms were founded in the 1980s with identical fiduciary, fee-only philosophies, making integration smoother
4. **Strong leadership bench** -- Experienced C-suite (Manske, Hansen, Palme) willing to stay on in senior roles, reducing key-person risk
5. **Employee ownership culture** -- 24 employees became owners in the combined entity, ensuring retention and alignment
6. **Diversified revenue** -- Services beyond basic portfolio management (tax planning, trust services, pension consulting, estate planning) create stickier client relationships
7. **Community/brand equity** -- 43-year track record in Asheville, the CFP diversity scholarship, and deep local relationships make the client base loyal and hard to replicate
8. **Multi-generational stability** -- Founder had already retired, succession was clean, and the next generation of leaders was already running the firm
