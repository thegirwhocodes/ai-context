For **Short description of the initiative**, I’d paste:

Education for Equality is building **Sabi**, a voice-first AI tutor that helps out-of-school and under-supported children in Nigeria learn foundational literacy and numeracy through ordinary phone calls. Children do not need a smartphone, internet, data, or an app. They call Sabi, speak naturally, and receive short adaptive lessons in warm Nigerian English/Pidgin-informed conversation.

Sabi focuses first on ages 8-14 who have missed basic schooling or cannot yet read or do grade-level math. The system places each learner through a diagnostic, teaches in 5-7 minute sessions, adjusts difficulty based on answers, and saves progress by phone number so children can continue over time. Lessons use evidence-backed approaches including Teaching at the Right Level, oral numeracy, phonics, spaced repetition, and localized market examples.

Our goal is to make high-quality tutoring reachable for children excluded by cost, connectivity, and device access. Sabi turns the most common technology families already have, a basic phone, into a personal tutor.

**What To Share First**
Share only:
- The problem.
- The Sabi user experience: “child receives/calls a phone lesson.”
- Pilot size/timeline.
- What you need from them: child recruitment, caregiver trust, consent support, local coordination.
- What they get: impact data, co-branded pilot report, community learning support.

Do **not** share yet:
- Full prompts.
- Full curriculum.
- Architecture/vendor setup.
- Cost model details.
- Source code.
- Internal partner list.
- Telco strategy.
- Raw child data.
- Full grant/funder strategy.

**Before Anything Serious**
Use a simple **Pilot MOU + NDA**, not just a friendly call. The agreement should say:

- **Education for Equality owns Sabi**, including name, curriculum, prompts, AI tutor design, software, data systems, and pilot methods.
- Slum2School can use Sabi materials **only to run the agreed pilot**, not to copy, adapt, rebuild, or share them.
- They cannot use confidential information to build or commission a competing phone/AI tutoring product.
- They cannot contact your vendors, technical partners, telcos, funders, or school/government leads around Sabi without written permission.
- E4E controls child data; Slum2School only helps collect consent/onboard families.
- No child photos, stories, audio, transcripts, or results can be published without written approval and consent.
- Pilot data/reporting is jointly acknowledged, but raw data and Sabi analytics remain E4E property.

**Safer Framing**
Don’t pitch them as “help us build Sabi.” Pitch them as:
