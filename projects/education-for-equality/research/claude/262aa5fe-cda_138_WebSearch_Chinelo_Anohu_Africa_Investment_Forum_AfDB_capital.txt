## Career and Role

Chinelo Anohu is a highly accomplished international corporate lawyer who serves as head and senior director of the Africa Investment Forum. The Africa Investment Forum (AIF) is charged with enhancing the quality of deals, raising capital, and accelerating financial closure of impactful transactions in Africa.

## Capital Mobilization Focus

Under Anohu's leadership, the AIF held double boardrooms within a calendar year, showcasing 92 projects with a total deal value of $102 Billion generated across 24 countries on the continent. Anohu has cited the abundance of capital and projects in Africa, while stressing the importance of project preparation and due diligence.

## Background and Experience
