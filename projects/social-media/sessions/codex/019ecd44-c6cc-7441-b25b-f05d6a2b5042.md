## Codex · 2026-06-15T22:12:12.660Z · final_answer

Yes, the **Sony ZV-1 can absolutely do wall/arm mounting**, and the quality is good. Like, real-camera good.

It has a standard **1/4-20 tripod socket** on the bottom, so it works with normal camera wall mounts, desk arms, clamp arms, tripods, ball heads, etc. Sony’s guide confirms the ZV-1 tripod hole supports **1/4-20 UNC screws**; just use a screw under **5.5mm** so it does not damage the camera. [Sony Help Guide](https://helpguide.sony.net/dc/1910/v1/en/contents/TP0002928640.html)

For your “easy mounting on a wall” goal:

- **Best stable setup:** small wall camera mount + ball head.
- **Best renter/easy setup:** clamp arm on shelf/desk/bedframe.
- **For vertical videos:** add a cheap **ZV-1 L-bracket/cage** so you can mount it vertically without awkward balancing.
- **Do not rely on adhesive magnets** for this one. It is light, around **294g**, but still an expensive camera. Screwed-in mount or clamp, please. No tears, no gravity drama.

Quality-wise: yes. The ZV-1 has a **1-inch sensor**, **24-70mm equivalent f/1.8-2.8 lens**, **4K up to 30p**, flip screen, good Sony autofocus, mic input, product showcase mode, and background blur features. [Sony specs](https://www.sony.com/electronics/support/compact-cameras-zv-series/zv-1/specifications)

My honest read: **ZV-1 is better than Pocket 2 for image quality**, and very strong for mounted bedroom/talking/music content. But **Pocket 3 is easier** for solo creator mounting/framing because of tracking + gimbal + simpler workflow. If the ZV-1 is cheap, like around **$250-$350 used**, it is a very good buy. MPB currently shows used ZV-1 listings from **$184-$639**, but the cheap ones may be rough/spares, so inspect condition carefully. [MPB ZV-1](https://www.mpb.com/en-us/product/sony-zv-1)

Short version: **yes, it mounts. yes, the quality is good. but get a real 1/4-inch mount/arm, not a sticky magnetic wall thing.**
