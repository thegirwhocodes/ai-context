## HCI Plus Structure

The Human Capital Index Plus (HCI+) converts health, education, and labor market indicators into a common metric of expected lifetime productivity, measured as the expected contribution to log earnings.

The HCI Plus comprises three main components:

1. **Health Component**: The health component captures the productivity effects of surviving to working age and achieving adequate early-life physical growth, using two proxies: Adult survival rate (ages 15–60) and Share of children under age 5 who are not stunted.

2. **Education Component**: Education outcomes related to schooling, including learning-adjusted years of schooling and tertiary education.

3. **On-the-Job Learning Component**: The On-the-Job Learning Component measures human capital accumulation after age 18 from work experience.

## Weighting Methodology

Each of the eleven components in the HCI+ is weighted based on research linking it to earnings impact. Additionally, the additive structure of the HCI+ allows the index to be decomposed by pillar (health, education, on-the-job learning) and by individual components within each pillar.
