## Students Operating Food Businesses on Campus

Wesleyan does not approve of students opening food venues on campus. Specifically, students are not permitted to operate a business from their residence, and none of the campus dorms/houses have a commercially rated kitchen.

## Food Sales and Events with Food

For student organizations and departments that want to sell food at events, there is a formal process: All fundraisers and events that will be selling or giving away food items must be registered and have the space reserved through WesNest, and the individual responsible must meet with the Director of the Usdan University Center who, in consultation with Bon Appétit Food Management and the Middletown Health inspector's office, will determine if the activity may proceed, with requests made at least five business days prior to the event.
