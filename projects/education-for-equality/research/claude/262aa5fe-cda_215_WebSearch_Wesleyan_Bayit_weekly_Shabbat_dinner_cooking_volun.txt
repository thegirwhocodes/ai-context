## Volunteer-Run Shabbat Dinners

Each week, there is fresh-baked challah followed by a meal cooked on Friday afternoon by fellow students. Shabbat Dinners are made with Kosher ingredients in the Bayit's kosher-style kitchen, can be vegetarian or meat meals, and always include options for those with dietary restrictions.

## Cooking Coordination

Rodriguez is the WJC's cooking coordinator and has the daunting task of coordinating a Shabbat dinner for a huge group. Based on the recipes for the weekly dinner, Rodriguez puts together a shopping list and sends it to the Office for Religious and Spiritual Life.

The coordinator provides two tips for volunteers: prepare on Thursday night to streamline the cooking process on Friday, and make a dish that lends itself to feeding a large group.

## Volunteer Opportunities

For many volunteers, cooking every week is a great way to give back to the WJC community, as well as meet new people through the universal language of food. However, since the cooking is done entirely by volunteers, there are concerns about whether there will be enough people willing to lend their time in coming years.
