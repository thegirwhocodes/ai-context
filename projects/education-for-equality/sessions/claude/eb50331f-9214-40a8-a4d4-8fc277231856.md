Wrote [sleep_gpa_regression.R](sleep_gpa_regression.R) in the project root.

**What's in it**
- Pulls the CMU Sleep Study CSV directly (no manual download — 634 first-year students, Fitbit-tracked sleep, registrar GPA across 5 studies / 3 universities)
- Converts sleep from minutes → hours
- Scatter + linear fit plot (saves `sleep_vs_gpa.png`)
- **Model 1:** `term_gpa ~ sleep_hours` — the raw question
- **Model 2:** adds prior cumulative GPA, course load, gender, first-gen status, race — important because students who already do well may also sleep more; the simple model would overstate sleep's effect
- Prints tidy coefficients w/ 95% CIs and runs residual diagnostics

**To run:**
```bash
Rscript "/Users/naomiivie/Education for Equality/sleep_gpa_regression.R"
```
(or open in RStudio and source it). First run will install `ggplot2`, `broom`, `dplyr` if missing.

The CMU team's own published finding from this data: ~one extra hour of nightly sleep ≈ +0.07 GPA after controls — your output should land near that.

Sources:
- [CMU Sleep Dataset](https://cmustatistics.github.io/data-repository/psychology/cmu-sleep.html)
- [Direct CSV](https://cmustatistics.github.io/data-repository/data/cmu-sleep.csv)

## Claude · 2026-05-31T18:50:13.736Z

The egg — by a lot.

Egg-laying animals existed ~300+ million years before chickens. Reptiles, dinosaurs, fish — all laying eggs. The first "chicken" was hatched from an egg laid by a bird that was *almost* a chicken (a red junglefowl ancestor) — a mutation in the embryo crossed whatever line you draw for "chicken."

So: egg first, chicken hatched out of it.
