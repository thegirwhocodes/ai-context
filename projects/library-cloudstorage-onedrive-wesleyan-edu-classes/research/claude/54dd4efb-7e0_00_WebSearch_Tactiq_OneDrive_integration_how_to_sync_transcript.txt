## How to Connect Tactiq with OneDrive

After signing up and installing the Tactiq Chrome Extension, go to your Tactiq dashboard, open Account & Settings, select Integrations, scroll to Storage, and click Connect next to OneDrive. Sign in with your Microsoft account and review permissions and select Accept.

## Automatic Transcript Syncing

After connecting OneDrive, you can control how transcripts are saved by opening the OneDrive settings under Integration and turning on Automatically save after meeting. This option works when OneDrive sync is enabled on your device.

## How Transcripts Are Saved

Tactiq saves meeting transcripts directly to OneDrive as Word files, and OneDrive handles version tracking as notes are updated, summaries are added, or follow-ups are written. Tactiq captures meeting conversations from Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet and saves transcripts directly to OneDrive as editable files.

## Additional Integration Support

Tactiq offers help on connecting your OneDrive account so Tactiq can save your transcripts and AI outputs directly to your storage, and how to automatically send transcripts and AI outputs from your workflows to OneDrive.
