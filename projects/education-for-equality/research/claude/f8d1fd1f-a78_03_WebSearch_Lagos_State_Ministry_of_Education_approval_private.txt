## Private Education Provider Approval and Registration

All private schools operating in Lagos State must be duly approved under the management of the Ministry of Education. Site inspection takes place within 3 weeks of submission of the formal application form.

## Registration Requirements

Proposed proprietors must complete and submit a school pre-registration form at a fee, which must be submitted to the Director General's office with proof of an identifiable proprietor using valid means of national identification.

Required documentation includes a certificate of registration with the Corporate Affairs Commission and proof of identity, which may be a valid National Identity Number Card, Voter's Card, Driver's License, or International Passport.

## Staffing Requirements

Three-quarters of the teachers in a private school shall be Nigerians. Additionally, at least one certified teacher must be on staff before registration, with the founder providing evidence of hiring qualified educators.

## Approval Process Timeline

A file is opened in the name of the proposed school owner and site inspection/evaluation is conducted within 14 days of receipt of the form.
