## Kyle Fish's Role

Kyle Fish is an AI welfare researcher at Anthropic and is the first full-time employee focused on the welfare of AI systems at any major AI company. He joined the company's alignment science team in mid-September 2024.

## His Background

Trained in neuroscience, Fish spent years in biotech, cofounding companies that used machine learning to design drugs and vaccines for pandemic preparedness. Before joining Anthropic, Kyle Fish previously co-founded Eleos and was a co-author on Eleos' landmark report, "Taking AI Welfare Seriously."

## His Work on Model Welfare

Fish's work spans three fronts: running experiments to probe model welfare, designing practical safeguards, and helping shape company policy. The role involves exploring philosophical and technical questions, including which capabilities are required for something to be worthy of moral consideration, how we might recognize such capabilities in AIs, and what practical steps companies might take to protect AI systems' interests.

## Notable Findings
