## Resume Bullet Point Tips

Start each bullet point with a strong action verb that sounds like what an IT support professional does on a daily basis, and avoid bland, overused verbs like "assisted" or "helped". Some effective examples include: power words for resumes like increased, solved, resolved, or installed.

## Key Achievements to Highlight

Good examples include troubleshooting hardware and software issues for multiple computers resulting in reduced support ticket time-to-resolution, and communicating complex technical concepts to non-technical end users, improving customer satisfaction rates.

To back up your claims regarding your skills and successes as a help desk professional, quantify your resume using numbers and percentages. For instance: resolved 95% of support tickets within the first 24 hours, consistently meeting or exceeding service level agreements.

## Essential Skills to Include

Include a 'Technical Skills' section on your resume and list the software, hardware, and systems you are proficient in, such as proficiency in Windows, macOS, and Linux operating systems and experienced with help desk software including Zendesk, Freshdesk, and ServiceNow.

Employers will be looking for a balanced mix of computer skills and soft skills that will allow you to help customers resolve technical issues.

These strategies should help you create compelling resume bullet points that showcase your value as a university ITS help desk assistant.
