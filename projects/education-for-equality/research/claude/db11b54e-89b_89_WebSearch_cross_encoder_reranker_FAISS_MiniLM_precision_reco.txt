## Key Components:

**MS MARCO Dataset & Training Data:**
The training data consists of over 500k examples, while the complete corpus consists of over 8.8 million passages.

**Cross-Encoder Models (MiniLM variants):**
The most popular and widely used reranker today is the cross-encoder, particularly models like cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2. MiniLM-L6-v2 offers the best balance of speed and quality — recommended as the default for most reranking tasks.

**Integration with FAISS:**
A bi-encoder encodes all your documents once at index time with O(n) complexity. At query time, you just encode the query and conduct an approximate nearest-neighbor lookup with FAISS or any ANN index, which is effectively O(1).

**Performance Characteristics:**
For the ce-msmarco-MiniLM-L6-v2 model, benchmark results show approximately 12.3 ms/op for 1 batch, 58.7 ms/op for 10 batches, and 740.4 ms/op for 100 batches.

**Precision Benefits:**
Cross-encoders are favored because of their high accuracy and deep semantic understanding. They can make far more precise judgments about relevance, which is essential in reranking tasks.
