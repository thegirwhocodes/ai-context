## Voice AI and Language Accessibility

One of the significant challenges and opportunities in this market involves voice-based AI. Voice-based learning tools and AI tutors rarely support African languages, limiting accessibility for non-literate or low-literacy users. However, there are emerging solutions, such as Benin deploying AI in education that includes a Fon language speech recognition model to improve access for rural and elderly populations.

## UNICEF and World Bank Procurement

UNICEF and ITU's Giga initiative is moving from mapping to large-scale procurement to connect approximately 500,000 African schools by 2030. For EdTech providers, this pipeline expands the addressable market for school-based licenses, device bundles, and teacher training at a national scale. Giga's approach includes aggregated demand, standardized contracts, and blended financing to lower per-site costs.

The World Bank supports pilots across low and middle-income countries, including adaptive learning programs in Côte d'Ivoire, The Gambia and Mali; WhatsApp-based tutors in Ghana, teacher-focused solutions in Ethiopia; and youth skills programs in Tanzania and Mauritius.
