# 2025 Global Learning Challenge at MIT Solve

## Overview
The challenge addresses gaps in global learning accessibility and outcomes, asking: "How can we make quality learning opportunities more accessible, while improving outcomes for all learners?"

## Key Focus Areas
MIT Solve prioritizes solutions that:
- Expand access to quality learning in emergency/conflict zones and low-internet areas
- Improve engagement for learners with disabilities and learning differences
- Build essential skills (problem-solving, AI literacy) with educator support

## Application Timeline
- **Opens:** February 3, 2025, 9:00 AM EST
- **Closes:** April 17, 2025, 12:00 PM EDT
- **Semifinalist Announcement:** June 27, 2025
- **Finalist Announcement:** July 30, 2025
- **Challenge Finals:** September 22, 2025

## Funding & Prizes
Selected teams average $40,000 each, plus access to $1M+ in prize pools:
- Seeding the Future Prize: up to $150,000
- E Ink Innovation Prize: up to $100,000
- AI for Humanity Prize: up to $150,000
- GM Prize: up to $150,000
- Citizens Workforce Innovation Prize: up to $100,000
- Crescent Enterprises AI Prize: up to $100,000

## Solution Stage Requirements
Acceptable development stages include prototype, pilot, growth, and scale. Concept-stage ideas only qualify for the Indigenous Communities Fellowship.

## Judging Criteria
Evaluators assess:
- Alignment with challenge goals
- Impact potential
- Implementation feasibility
- Innovation level
- Human-centered design approach
- Scalability and financial viability
- Partnership potential
- Technical feasibility (for novel technologies)

## Eligibility
Applications welcome from diverse backgrounds. MIT Solve maintains nondiscrimination policies across race, gender, age, disability, sexual orientation, and other protected characteristics.
