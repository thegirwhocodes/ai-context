## HCI+ 2026 Overview

The Human Capital Index Plus (HCI+) is a newly expanded global index that provides country- and regional-level data tracking human capital accumulation from birth to age 65 and a metric for how gaps translate into forgone future labor earnings.

## Three-Component Structure

The HCI+ measures how effectively a country builds human capital by tracking progress across three components - health, education, and employment. It integrates measures from these three dimensions of human capital into one index using evidence-based weights.

## Decomposition and Additive Framework

By breaking down deficits by component, the HCI+ helps countries understand where investments are paying off and where more action is needed to strengthen future earnings and economic growth. The "percentage of future earnings lost" represents the additional earnings a child could receive—expressed as a percentage of their current expected earnings—if their country's human capital matched top performers at similar income levels.

## Scale and Earnings Loss Methodology

The index ranges from 0–325, and units on this scale correspond to percentage increases in labor earnings.
