## HNG Internship Overview

The HNG Internship Program was initially created by hotel booking startup Hotels.ng in 2016 as a funnel to find pro developers, and eventually opened up the program by supplying developers to other organizations. HNG is Nigeria's largest remote developer internship and has launched careers for thousands of Nigerian frontend developers.

Participants are incentivized with weekly stipends, and those who successfully complete the program are placed at local and international technology companies, including leading fintechs like Paga and Flutterwave.

## Talent Pipeline & Recruitment

Many Nigerian engineers landed their first roles through referrals from community connections rather than through direct applications. A significant number of mobile developer roles in Nigeria are filled through referrals or direct outreach before they are ever publicly advertised.

Ingressive For Good's community of over 100,000 members across Nigeria provides networking and job referral opportunities alongside structured skill development.

## Community Engagement

Software developer communities like ForLoop Africa and Data Science Nigeria host frequent masterclasses on machine learning and front-end development, and run vibrant online and offline communities.

The search results show that HNG serves as a critical talent pipeline, connecting vetted developers with companies while creating a robust alumni network that facilitates referrals and community-driven hiring.
