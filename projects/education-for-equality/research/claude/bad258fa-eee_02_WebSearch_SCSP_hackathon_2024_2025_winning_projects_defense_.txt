## 2024 Hackathon (November)

SCSP hosted an AGI House x SCSP Hackathon focused on cutting-edge AI and national security challenges, with nearly 100 attendees including experts from the DOE, CIA, NSC, DIU, and CISA. Over 80 hackers formed 20+ teams to tackle real-world problems.

George Mason University provided $50K in prizes to the three winning teams. The prize breakdown was $25k for 1st Place, $15k for 2nd Place, and $10k for 3rd Place.

One notable project from this hackathon was Port Sentinel by the team of Kshitij Kumar Parashar, Thomas Ewing, Sara Hekmaty, and Yingquan Li—a comprehensive platform that combines port city vulnerability scanning with maritime port digital twin visualization to provide security insights for port infrastructure.

## 2026 Hackathon (Upcoming)

SCSP's 2026 National Security Technology Hackathon is convening the top AI engineers from academia, research institutions, the private sector, and the U.S. government, hosted in three locations (San Francisco, DC, and Boston). Winners may share a pot of $70,000+ in cash prizes and will demo their applications to 20,000+ attendees at the AI+Expo in Washington DC on May 9th.
