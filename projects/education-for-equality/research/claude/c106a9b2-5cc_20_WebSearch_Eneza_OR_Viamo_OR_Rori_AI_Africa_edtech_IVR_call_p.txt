## Eneza Education

Eneza leverages the widespread availability of basic mobile phones in Africa to increase access to quality educational materials for underserved students. Students using Eneza score 22.7% higher than their peers and spend an additional 2 hours per week studying. More broadly, as of 2022, Eneza has reached over 9 million learners, tracking an improvement of 23% in student performance after using their platform.

Eneza Education received a grant from the GSMA Ecosystem Accelerator Innovation Fund to expand the solution in Côte d'Ivoire, through improvements to platform quality, raising awareness through a rewards system, as well as content creation and an IVR channel. By the end of the GSMA grant in March 2020, Eneza had increased the number of users on the platform to 27,545.

## Viamo

Viamo has integrated Generative AI with its interactive voice response (IVR) platform. In May 2023, Viamo piloted its voice-first Generative AI solution, "Ask Viamo Anything" (AVA), in Zambia. AVA leverages GenAI and speech technology to deliver essential, cross-sectoral information to mobile users who lack internet access or face barriers in using mobile internet. By combining IVR with GenAI, Viamo ensures inclusive access, supporting low-literacy users and expanding AI-driven services to underserved populations.
