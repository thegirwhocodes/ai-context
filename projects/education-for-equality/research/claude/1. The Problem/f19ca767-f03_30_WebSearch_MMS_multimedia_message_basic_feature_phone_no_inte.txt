## What is MMS?

MMS is a standard way to send messages that include multimedia content to and from a mobile phone over a cellular network. Unlike text-only SMS, MMS can deliver a variety of media, including up to forty seconds of video, one image, a slideshow of multiple images, or audio.

## Key Features for Feature Phones

Unlike chat apps, MMS works over cellular networks. This makes it particularly relevant for basic feature phones in Africa. Some networks limit file sizes (~300 KB – 1 MB). Specifically for Nigeria, it is generally recommended to keep the multimedia file size to less than 300kb in size due to some limitations on the WAP gateways of the mobile service providers when sending picture messages to Nigeria.

## Costs

Sending MMS is more expensive than SMS. MMS messages cost a bit more because more data is transmitted.

## Nigeria Carrier Support

As part of its commitment to providing world-class telecommunication experience for Nigerians, MTN has opened its General Packet Radio Service (GPRS) and the Multimedia Message Service (MMS) platforms to its entire subscriber base.

## Device Compatibility

If the receiver's handset is not MMS capable, the message is usually delivered to a web-based service from where the content can be viewed from a normal web browser. The URL for the content is usually sent to the receiver's phone in a normal text message.
