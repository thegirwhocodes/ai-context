**Core Ethos:** "Opening a premium leather journal by candlelight in a quiet room." Luxury tech product for the spiritually curious -- NOT a church app.

**Why:** Existing Bible/spiritual apps look "churchy" (crosses, doves, stained glass) which limits appeal. Bethel should feel like Arc Browser meets Apple Books.

**How to apply:** Every UI decision should pass the "would this feel at home next to Linear/Arc/Bear?" test. Naomi said: "sleek, black and modern -- new and innovative."

## Color Palette
- **Gold**: `#D4A853` (primary accent, interactive elements)
- **Base**: `#0C0C14` (deep blue-black background)
- **Lavender**: `#A78BFA` (dream features)
- **Teal**: `#5EEAD4` (success states)
- **Off-white text**: `#E8E4DE` (never pure white)
- **Warm near-black**: never pure black
- Surface: `#141420`, Card: `#1A1A2E`

## Typography
- Font: Literata (Bible text), system font for UI
- Bible text: 18px default, adjustable 14-28px, line-height 1.75
- Generous whitespace throughout

## Reading Themes
1. Midnight (default dark)
2. Warm Night (amber-tinted)
3. OLED (true black)

## UI Rules
- Zero religious iconography (no crosses, doves, praying hands)
- Glassmorphism for floating elements (tab bar, suggested prompts)
- Auto-hiding chrome in Bible reader
- Mood picker uses abstract colored circles, NOT emoji
- Bethel avatar: small abstract stone/pillar or star icon in gold
- "Thinking" state: gentle lavender pulsing glow, "Bethel is reflecting..."
- No social media pressure, no feeds, no likes
- No aggressive CTAs, no upselling -- free, no subscriptions, no ads
- Structured around the AI -- it should feel like an AI app, not a traditional sectioned app

## Design References
Glorify (luxury Bible), Bear Notes (dark writing), Arc Browser (warm dark UI), Apple Books (reading comfort), Claude App (warm AI chat), Day One (journal structure)

## Highlights
4 colors for Bible highlights: gold, lavender, teal, rose (warm palette)

## What NOT to Do
- Don't make it look like Glorify or other "Christian apps"
- Don't structure it like a traditional 5-tab app with Home/Profile
- Don't use religious imagery
- Don't make it feel like a church app
