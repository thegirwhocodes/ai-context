- **Lagos BCG pay:** Directionally correct: you should not expect U.S.-level absolute pay. But exact bonus/salary claims are not verifiable publicly.

- **Texas taxes:** Yes, Texas has **no state personal income tax**. That part is real. But Texas still has sales tax, property tax, and business/franchise-tax complexity. The “zero corporate income tax” framing is too simplistic because Texas has a franchise tax system. See [Texas Comptroller](https://comptroller.texas.gov/economy/fiscal-notes/archive/2023/dec/proptax.php) and [Tax Foundation Texas profile](https://taxfoundation.org/location/texas/).

- **Federal tax:** Correct. Moving to Texas does **not** avoid federal tax. The $150k example is roughly plausible for federal income tax, but it ignores filing status, deductions, FICA, tax treaties, and nonresident rules. IRS sources: [federal brackets](https://www.irs.gov/filing/federal-income-tax-rates-and-brackets), [substantial presence test](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/substantial-presence-test).

- **International student visa part:** This is the biggest issue. O-1 or International Entrepreneur Parole can help you work on your startup, but they do **not automatically authorize you to work for BCG**. IER is parole, not a visa, and work authorization is tied to the startup. O-1 needs a petitioner and strong evidence. See [USCIS IER](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/international-entrepreneur-rule) and [USCIS O-1](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/o-1-visa-individuals-with-extraordinary-ability-or-achievement).

- **Texas growth:** It’s fair to say Texas is growing strongly. But “locked in for the next decade” is overconfident. Also, DFW being “#1 tech hub” depends on the ranking used. [Site Selection ranked DFW #1](https://siteselection.com/press-release/dallas-ft-worth-top-tech-hub-north-carolina-no-1-in-workforce-development/), but [CBRE’s 2025 tech talent ranking still puts the Bay Area #1 and Austin top 5](https://www.cbre.com/insights/books/scoring-tech-talent-2025).

My corrected take for you: **Texas is a strong option, especially Austin or Dallas, but not because it magically solves BCG + immigration + startup all at once.** Dallas is better for BCG/corporate buyers/enterprise networks. Austin is better for startup density and founder energy. Bay Area is still best for AI capital/network, but expensive. Apply to the BCG office where your story sounds most inevitable, not the one an internet answer claims is “easiest.”
