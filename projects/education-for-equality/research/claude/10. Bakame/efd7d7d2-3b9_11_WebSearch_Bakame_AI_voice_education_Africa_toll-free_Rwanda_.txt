## Bakame AI Voice Education in Rwanda

Bakame is a talking rabbit character featured in edutainment episodes produced by Rwanda's Education Board (REB), with voice-over artists recording episodes in REB's studio. The videos featuring Bakame help students with counting, sign language, English language pronunciation, and reading skills.

## Support and Expansion (2022-2026)

A GPE Multiplier grant of $30 million along with $100 million in cofinancing from the World Bank for 2022-2026 are supporting development, dissemination and use of the episodes. To date, 96 lessons have been recorded, and 96 more are being produced, with all episodes embedded with sign language.

## AI and Education Initiatives in Rwanda (2025-2026)

In July 2025, Day of AI and MIT RAISE, in collaboration with the Rwanda Ministry of Education and the Rwanda Education Board (REB), launched a first-of-its-kind national initiative to bring AI literacy to every classroom in Rwanda.

Additionally, a November 2025 education agreement included 2,000 Claude Pro licenses for educators across Rwanda, AI literacy training for public servants, and the deployment of a Claude-powered AI learning companion across eight African countries.
