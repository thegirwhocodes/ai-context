# Star & Crescent Operational Details

**Food Preparation:**
Chef Ryan Talbot, a non-student professional who has worked there 12 years, handles all cooking and menu creation. Students do not prepare meals. Talbot explained: "I'm back here most of the time" and "It's really just me making it."

**Service & Staffing:**
Student wait staff serve meals. All kitchen and front-of-house staff are ADP members who work shifts in exchange for food rather than wages. Two student stewards manage operations: "Every year there's a junior and a senior steward...we are the managers of the restaurant."

**Payment & Access:**
Students purchase meal plans using points: "Any student on campus can have a contract, assuming you have enough points." Additionally, freshmen receive promotion: "first three freshmen eat for free." ADP members must work minimum one shift weekly.

**Membership & Operations:**
The 175-year-old literary society operates Star & Crescent as its dining venue. Membership requires "all members must work at least one shift per week at S&C." The stewards "pay Ryan, who is the chef; we pay the staff, we organize all of the contracts."

**Operational Capacity:**
The restaurant typically "prepare[s] food for around 120 people" and frequently runs out during busy nights.
