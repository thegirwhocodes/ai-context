## Basic Setup Steps

To get started, navigate to Phone Numbers in the ElevenLabs dashboard and add your Twilio phone number using your Account SID and Auth Token. ElevenLabs will automatically configure voice webhooks for your Twilio number.

First, fetch your Twilio credentials by going to your Twilio Console and scrolling down to the Account Info section to locate your Account SID and phone number.

Then navigate to the Telephony > Phone Numbers section in ElevenLabs, click the Import number button, select "From Twilio," and enter your Twilio Phone number, Account SID, and Auth Token in the sidebar modal. Give it a Label and click Import.

Once your phone number is added, select which AI agent handles calls by clicking on the "No agent" dropdown and selecting your agent from the list.

## Key Features

The integration provides AI-powered conversations on every call, real-time voice processing with sub-second response times, global scale with enterprise-grade reliability, and seamless integration with your existing Twilio setup.

This integration handles both inbound and outbound calls.

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, verify your Account SID/Auth Token in the ElevenLabs dashboard, check Twilio Console phone number settings, and verify the audio format is correctly configured as μ-law 8kHz.
