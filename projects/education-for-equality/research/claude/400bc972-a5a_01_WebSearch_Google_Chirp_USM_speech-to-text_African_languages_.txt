## Key Information About Google Chirp and USM

**Overview of Chirp and USM:**
Google's Universal Speech Model (USM) is a family of state-of-the-art speech models with 2B parameters and support for transcriptions of 300+ languages. Chirp is the latest version of the USM family, fine-tuned for Cloud-specific use-cases and announced in May 2023 at Google I/O.

**Multilingual Capabilities:**
Chirp 3: Transcription was built using self-supervised training on millions of hours of audio and 28 billion sentences of text spanning 100+ languages. It can accurately convert voice to text in over 85+ languages and variants.

**African Language Support:**
USM can perform automatic speech recognition on widely-spoken languages like English and Mandarin, but also on under-resourced languages like Amharic, Cebuano, Assamese, and Azerbaijani.

**Latest Generation - Chirp 3:**
Chirp 3: Transcription is the latest generation of Google's multilingual Automatic Speech Recognition model, delivering state-of-the-art ASR accuracy and multilingual capabilities, available exclusively in the Speech-to-Text API V2.

**Advanced Features:**
Chirp 3 includes powerful new capabilities, including speaker diarization to identify different speakers and automatic language detection for multilingual audio.
