## Key Editing Techniques

**Jump Cuts & Pacing:** Jump cuts should happen every 2-3 seconds to maintain rapid pacing, and jump cuts are best when you need to remove dead space from continuous footage. To make jump cuts cleaner, pair them with a slight reframe, B-roll insert, screen capture, caption change, or cutaway.

**B-roll Usage:** B-roll breaks up dialogue, adds context, and covers jump cuts—it's one of the simplest ways to increase visual interest. Add pattern interrupts (b-roll, pop-ups, zooms) every 15 seconds.

**Captions & Text:** For social clips, a hard cut plus a strong caption or B-roll insert often communicates faster. Captions aren't optional in 2026. Research on video accessibility shows that proper captioning and visual descriptions dramatically expand your audience reach and engagement.
