## General Salary Information for Community Health Workers in Nigeria

The average salary for a Community Health Worker is NGN 89,991 per month in Nigeria. However, salaries vary by experience level:

- With less than two years of experience, you can expect to earn around ₦62,000 per month.
- With two to five years of experience, your salary increases to about ₦82,000 per month.
- If you've been in the field for five to ten years, you can look forward to earning around ₦110,000 per month.

## Lagos-Specific Data

In Lagos, Community Health Workers employed by the Lagos State Government Health Service Commission earn between ₦120K-₦130K per month with a median of ₦125K.

## NGO Employment

NGOs, international organizations, and private companies usually pay more than government jobs for Community Health Workers. Additionally, many community health workers also get extra perks like health insurance, help with housing costs, money for transportation, and chances to learn more on the job.

## Legal Framework

It's worth noting that Nigeria employs a private model, with a public-sector wage floor, and there are no specific laws, regulations or policies on the payment of CHWs.
