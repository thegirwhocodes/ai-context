# Voice Phone Number Categories - Africa's Talking

Based on the available documentation, here are the voice phone number types offered:

**Toll Free**
- Who pays: Client/business bears the cost
- Quote: "numbers where the user is not dialed but you as a client gets billed for incoming calls"
- Limitation: Cannot make outgoing calls independently
- Billing: Client account charged for incoming calls

**Premium**
- Who pays: End user at elevated rates; client also billed
- Quote: "numbers where the client is billed above normal rates and Client's account billed for incoming calls"
- Limitation: Cannot make outgoing calls independently
- Billing: Dual billing model

**Regular**
- Who pays: Both parties share costs
- Quote: "The user is billed normal call rates and the Client's account billed for incoming and outgoing calls"
- Capability: Supports both incoming and outgoing calls
- Billing: Standard rates for all call directions

**SIP**
- Technical basis: Internet-based
- Quote: "Uses the internet Client's account is billed for incoming and outgoing calls"
- Billing: Client account covers all directions

**Virtualized Physical Lines**
- Who pays: Client handles all charges
- Quote: "Billed normal call rates Client's account billed for incoming and outgoing calls"
- Limitation: Cannot independently make outgoing calls
- Billing: Standard call rates

**Note:** The documentation does not contain information on "Regular Business Number," "SIP Trunk," or "Test Number" categories.
