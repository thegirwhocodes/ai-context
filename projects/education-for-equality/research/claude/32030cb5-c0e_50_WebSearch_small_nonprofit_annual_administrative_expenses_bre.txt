## Administrative Expense Components for Small Nonprofits

Administrative expenses cover the behind-the-scenes operations that keep your nonprofit compliant, organized, and running smoothly, and are often fixed costs tied to internal operations and governance.

Key administrative expense categories include:

- Administrative personnel covering salaries and benefits for staff who handle your organization's general operations, such as your executive director, finance team, public relations, and HR staff
- Professional services including nonprofit accounting, legal fees, and consulting services needed to ensure compliance with federal regulations
- Facilities expenses including rent, utilities, and other costs incurred to maintain office space
- Office supplies, equipment, and technology covering general office technology and equipment for day-to-day operations
- Insurance and risk management costs for coverage needed to protect the entire organization, such as property insurance

## Accounting and Compliance

Auditors use the statement of functional expenses to assess your organization's financial health, operational efficiency, and compliance with GAAP and IRS Form 990 filing. The IRS doesn't require that nonprofits spend any particular portion of their income on each category—it just wants nonprofits to report how they spend their money.

## Expense Ratios

The administrative expense ratio measures the percentage of an organization's expenses that are being allocated to administrative costs. As a general guide, this ratio should be less than 35%.
