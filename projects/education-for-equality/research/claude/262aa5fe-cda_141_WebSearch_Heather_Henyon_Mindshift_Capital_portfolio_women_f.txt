## About Heather Henyon and Mindshift Capital

Heather Henyon founded Mindshift Capital in 2019, a venture capital firm designed to challenge traditional biases in the investment landscape by investing with a gender lens. Mindshift Capital is focused on post-seed early-stage companies in the U.S., Europe and Middle East.

## Portfolio and Investment Approach

Mindshift Capital has made 18 investments across five countries and focuses on companies with at least one female founder or cofounder. Through a global network, Mindshift identifies companies with scalable technology, exceptional leadership and high growth potential, investing with a gender lens to ensure diversity of thought while narrowing the funding gap for female-led ventures.

## MENA Thesis

Henyon's team is more in tune with the challenges that entrepreneurs in the MENA region face. At the time Mindshift was founded, the average investment in companies founded or cofounded by women in the U.S. was half of that raised by male-founded companies, with an even lower percentage in the Middle East. However, women-owned companies generated higher revenues: the average return on investment for women-owned companies was double that of men-owned businesses.
