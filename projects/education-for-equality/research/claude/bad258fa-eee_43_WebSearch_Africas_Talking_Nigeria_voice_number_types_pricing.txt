## Voice Number Types

Africa's Talking offers several types of voice phone numbers: Toll Free (where the user is not charged but you as a client get billed for incoming calls), Premium (where the client is billed above normal rates and your account is billed for incoming calls), Regular (where the user is charged normal call rates and your account is billed for both incoming and outgoing calls), SIP (which uses the internet and your account is billed for incoming and outgoing calls), and Virtualized Physical Lines (billed at normal call rates with your account billed for incoming and outgoing calls).

## Key Limitations

Toll-Free (0800), Premium Numbers (0900) and virtualized numbers do not make outgoing calls, but regular numbers attached to them (also known as hunting numbers) can make outgoing calls.

## Premium Rated Services

You can build and monetize services using Premium Rated numbers, with services such as interactive dating or marketing audio content enhanced by the personal nature of voice.
