# Calendly Cons and User Complaints

## Major Pain Points Driving Users Away

**1. Restrictive Free Plan**
The free tier limits users to one event type, one calendar connection, no reminders, no branding customization, and displays Calendly's logo on all pages. Users report frustration: "we eventually moved to Google Calendar as it offered similar functionality with the added benefit of being integrated into our existing workflow."

**2. Apple Calendar Integration Discontinued**
New users cannot integrate with iCloud Calendar, forcing Apple ecosystem users to switch to Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook as intermediaries or risk scheduling conflicts.

**3. No Native CRM Capabilities**
Calendly offers only basic contact viewing with notes. It cannot group contacts by company, track interaction dates, or support custom tracking fields—critical gaps for businesses managing complex relationship data.

**4. Lack of Phone Call Booking**
The platform has no solution for inbound phone calls or proactive calling. Businesses must manually create bookings from phone conversations, requiring separate phone systems and call handlers.

**5. Limited Customization**
Users can only add logos and choose colors; cannot add videos, custom widgets, or significantly alter page layout. Lower-tier plans offer even fewer options, creating brand inconsistency.

**6. GDPR Complexity**
US-based servers require additional contractual arrangements and special data handling for European compliance, rather than offering native GDPR compliance.

**7. Variable Support Quality**
Free users receive email-only support with inconsistent response times. Phone support is exclusive to Enterprise customers.

## Pricing Concerns

Entry-level pricing starts at $10/user/month for reminders and paid appointments; $16/user/month for lead routing. Users note: "Too expensive. No customization. I feel as if they stationed in the industry for granted and did not indulge in and attempting to satisfy customers."
