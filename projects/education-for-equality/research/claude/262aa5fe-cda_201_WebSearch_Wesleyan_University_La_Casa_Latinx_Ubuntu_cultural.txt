La Casa de Albizu Campos aims to serve as a resource on issues pertaining to the Latino culture by hosting social, educational, traditional, and political events on campus. Through this mission, La Casa strengthens, supports, and builds community among Latino students, while bridging the cultural gap between Latino students and the Wesleyan community.

## Dinner and Culinary Events

La Casa de Albizu Campos hosted a student forum around Latinx foods and culinary traditions. Forum participants continued to study cooking techniques, reflect on personal relationships with culture and food, and produced dishes indicative of both historical and present times.
