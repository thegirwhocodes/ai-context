# Signapse: AI Sign Language Translation Platform

## What Signapse Does
Signapse provides "AI-powered sign language translation software providing real-time BSL and ASL translation" for broadcasts, digital content, events, and transport announcements. The company specializes in making audio-visual content accessible to deaf communities.

## Products

**SignStream API**: Real-time translator for "live, embedded, and scalable sign language translation" that generates videos on-demand. Users can test it at ai.signapse.com by typing sentences.

**SignStudio Platform**: A video translation service enabling "seamless BSL and ASL sign language translations" for post-production content with customizable digital signers.

**Transport Solutions**: Digital signage displaying "BSL and ASL" announcements for transit systems, generating "over 5,000 BSL train announcements daily throughout the UK."

## Technical Approach
The translation process involves:
1. Large library of qualified sign language videos
2. AI selects appropriate video combinations based on content
3. AI blends videos with natural transitions and grammar accuracy
4. Integration into final content

The company uses "photo-realistic" generative AI but doesn't specify if avatars are synthetic, motion-captured, or human video compilations (disclaimer notes: "Not all videos are synthetic videos").
