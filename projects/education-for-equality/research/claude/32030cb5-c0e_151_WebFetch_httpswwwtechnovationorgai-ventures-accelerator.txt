# AI Ventures Accelerator Overview

## Official Description
The AI Ventures Accelerator is described as "a free accelerator from Technovation and Generation Unlimited that fast-tracks teams of young women from idea to investor-ready AI venture" with potential access to seed funding.

## Target Audience
The program targets young women aged 19-24 who are interested in technology and entrepreneurship. Applicants should have a problem they want to solve and be willing to commit 10-15 hours weekly. Prior AI or coding experience is not required.

## What It Provides
Over 12 weeks, participants receive:
- Training in foundational AI, entrepreneurship, and human-centered design
- Mentorship from experienced tech entrepreneurs and AI professionals
- Curriculum and tools at no cost
- Opportunity to compete for $10,000 in equity-free seed funding (top 10 teams)
- Connection to a global network of 1,000+ young women entrepreneurs

## Technovation's Pitch
Technovation frames this initiative as addressing critical gaps: "women hold only 22% of AI roles, leading to biased systems that fail entire populations." The organization positions the accelerator as providing "world-class AI skills and entrepreneurial training" to develop diverse leaders who will build a more inclusive tech industry.
