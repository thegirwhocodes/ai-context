## Overview

Spitch AI has a mission to make AI speak African languages in both directions: speech-to-text and text-to-speech. Rather than building end-user apps, the Lagos startup offers simple APIs and SDKs that let any team plug local-language voice capabilities into call centres, media tools, or learning platforms, without the need for Machine Learning expertise.

## Supported Languages

Spitch currently provides support for Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and Nigerian-accented English, with Pidgin English coming in a few months. Starting with Yoruba, the team has since added Hausa, Igbo, Nigerian-accented English and, after demand from East Africa, Amharic.

## How It Works

Spitch offers various solutions across speech generation in Nigeria's top languages, including Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa, in different voices. It provides transcription of audios into Nigerian local languages, diacritics, and translation to local languages.

## API & Deployment

Spitch offers a Python SDK (pip install spitch) and improved documentation at docs.spi-tch.com. Localized text-to-speech functionality via Spitch TTS enables auditory feedback in native Nigerian accents.

## Key Challenges Addressed

The biggest challenges include data problems and computing problems. Languages are low-resource, meaning there is not a lot of representation of these languages on the internet, which makes getting data difficult. SpitchAI has stitched together an internal data-collection and labelling pipeline and leans on partners for proprietary corpora.
