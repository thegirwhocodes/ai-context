## What is a 0800 Toll-Free Number License?

A 0800 toll-free license allows access to toll-free (0800) telecom numbers. These services are used for specialized communication needs like campaigns, large-scale helpdesks, or paid information lines.

## License Type Required

A Value-Added Services (VAS) License is a specific license category granted by the NCC that allows businesses to deliver non-core telecommunications services over existing telecom networks. The 0800 range is shared by Value Added Service – Special Number Services (VAS – SNS) Licensees, meaning you don't need to own your own network to operate this service.

## How to Apply for a 0800 License

Sign up on eservices.ncc.gov.ng and click on Licensing Application Management System to apply. Fill the form and upload relevant documents: Certificate of Incorporation, Tax Clearance Certificate, Certified True Copy of Articles & Memorandum of Association, and Feasibility report of proposed service.

## Application Requirements

Applications should be submitted when all documents have been uploaded. On submission of the application, a License fee of Ten Thousand Naira (₦10,000.00) only, is payable via the Licensing Application Management System.

All VAS (Content Service using Short Code) applicant(s) must present evidence of an Agreement or a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) entered between the company and any of the VAS Aggregators.

## Processing Timeline

An applicant must receive a response with respect to license application within 90 days of the submission of the application. However for a Class License, once an application is complete, an offer letter will be issued immediately.

For more details, you can visit the official NCC website at www.ncc.gov.ng or contact the Nigerian Communications Commission directly.
