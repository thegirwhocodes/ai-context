## Student Employment at Wesleyan

Before a student employee's first day of work, campus employers must verify students are eligible for work and onboard them to Workforce Time, report hires, set pay rates, and select position codes/smart keys for hourly wages.

Campus jobs currently pay at least the Connecticut minimum wage of $15.69 per hour, with jobs requiring specific skills, training, or certifications paying more.

## WSA Budget Structure

The Student Activities Fee (SAF) is managed through an electronic system where funds are moved between Smartkeys electronically. The Student Budget Committee (SBC) consists of 7 members who meet weekly with student groups to allocate funds, with a mission to foster a high-quality extracurricular environment by assisting student groups with funding.

Clubs and organizations like Sound Co-op, The Argus, and the FGLI Advisory Board are allocated money from the Student Activities Fee (SAF) to pay certain students for their work.
