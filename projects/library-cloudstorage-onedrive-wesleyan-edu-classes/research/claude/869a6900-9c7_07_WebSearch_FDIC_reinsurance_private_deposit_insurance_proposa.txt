## FDIC Reinsurance and Private Deposit Insurance Options

The FDIC has proposed a system in which private insurance companies sell deposit insurance to individual banks at market prices, with the FDIC serving as the ultimate reinsurer. This represents one approach to deposit insurance reform that incorporates private sector involvement while maintaining a government backstop.

The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation Improvement Act of 1991 directed the FDIC to study and report to Congress on the feasibility of establishing a private reinsurance system.

## Contingent Capital and Funding Approaches

Contingent funding can come from private markets and from central bank backstops such as the Federal Reserve's discount window and Standing Repo Facility. This reflects regulatory approaches to ensuring banks have access to emergency funding sources beyond traditional deposits.

## Deposit Insurance Reform Options

The FDIC released options for deposit insurance reform in 2023, believing targeted coverage best meets the objectives of deposit insurance of financial stability and depositor protection relative to its costs, though these proposed options would require Congressional action.

The search results also reveal recent discussions about expanding deposit insurance limits and the tradeoffs between protecting depositors and managing moral hazard risks in the banking system.
