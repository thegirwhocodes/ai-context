## USSD Display During Active Voice Calls

Based on the GSM technical specifications (GSM 03.90), USSD operations may not be possible when an MS (mobile station/phone) is in certain states, such as during certain conditions. However, the specifications do indicate that for a USSD notification, the MS shall display the text provided and send back a response, and while awaiting a response, the MS may receive a network-initiated USSD request or notification on the same transaction.
