## Government Tutoring Contract Models

Government tutoring contracts typically use per-student hours billing from per-student fund allocation, where each assigned student is allocated a set number of hours with a specified price per hour, and group session time is invoiced at this rate for each student on the session from that student's fund.

Alternatively, some contracts use per-student hours but with overall funds allocation, where a group session with 3 students would still invoice for 3 hours, but the funds are for the entire contract rather than per student.

## General Tutoring Business Models
