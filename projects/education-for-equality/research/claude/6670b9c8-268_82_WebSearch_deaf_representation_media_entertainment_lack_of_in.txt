## Key Statistics on Deaf Representation

More than half of deaf consumers, some 56%, say they "rarely" or "never" see their identities represented in film and television. Additionally, only 6% of viewers have ever seen a deaf LGBTQ+ person portrayed in fiction.

## Negative Stereotyping and Portrayal Issues

Sixty three percent of deaf consumers say movies and shows that feature deaf characters use negative images of the community. More specifically, 70% of deaf consumers say deaf characters are seen as objects of pity or in need of help. Some 76% of deaf people believe that the way their community is portrayed in fiction influences how they are perceived in daily life, shaping attitudes and calcifying prejudices.

## Lack of Diversity

In the U.S., hearing consumers are over twice as likely to have seen media featuring white deaf people as they are to have seen media featuring deaf people of color.

## Industry Support Needed

Some 82% of deaf consumers believe the entertainment industry needs to offer more career support for deaf professionals in order to create more authentic on-screen representation. Additionally, the deaf and hard of hearing community is underrepresented throughout the entertainment industry in the fields of writing, directing and producing.

## Recent Progress

Despite ongoing challenges, a recent study by NRG shows at least 79% of deaf consumers believe that there has been more representation of their community in TV and film compared to a year ago.
