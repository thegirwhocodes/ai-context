## Position and Background

Ibrahim Hassan Hadejia (born 1965) is a Nigerian lawyer and politician who has served as the deputy chief of staff to the president of Nigeria (office of the vice president) since 2023. He assumed office on June 2, 2023.

## Career History

He previously served as the Senator representing Jigawa North-East from 2019 to 2023, and was Deputy Governor of Jigawa State from 2002 to 2007 and from 2015 to 2019. He was appointed Commissioner for Justice and Attorney General in Jigawa State in 1999 and promoted to secretary to the state government two years later.

## Education Policy and Development Work

Regarding education and development initiatives, Hassan brought many developmental projects to his JNE Senatorial zone including JAMB/NOUN Centres, CBE exam centers, College of Precision Agriculture, Federal College of Education, and the SUBEB model school Hadejia. Additionally, as Deputy Governor, he facilitated partnerships with the UK Department for International Development (DFID), establishing programs such as the State and Local Government Programme (SLGP) and initiatives in health, justice, and economic empowerment.
