## About AR 600-8-22

Army Regulation AR 600-8-22, known as "Military Awards," provides administrative instructions on the United States Army's system for awarding decorations, service medals, badges, ribbons, and appurtenances. This regulation outlines the criteria, eligibility, and process for Soldiers to receive recognition for acts of valor, meritorious service or achievement, and various other accomplishments during their military service.

The January 19, 2024 version is the most recent update and includes the latest procedures for military awards and decorations within the Army.
