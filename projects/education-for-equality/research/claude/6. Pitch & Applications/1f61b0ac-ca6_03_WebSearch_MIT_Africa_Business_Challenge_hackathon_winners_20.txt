## 2025 MIT Africa Business Challenge Winners

The winning team, AgriQ-Connect, comprising Mariam Hamzat, a first-year PhD student in Marine and Environmental Sciences, alongside Qudus Rafiu, Toheeb Obidara, and Azeez Akinyemi, PhD students in Chemical Engineering, emerged victorious, securing the $5,000 prize and also winning the Audience Choice Award.

Their innovation includes a Tinder-like marketplace that directly connects farmers with processing companies, coupled with an AI-integrated silo for real-time crop monitoring, seeking to address the $4 billion annual post-harvest losses in sub-Saharan Africa. Other winning teams included Charted AI, which proposed an AI-powered bookkeeping solution for SMEs in East Africa, securing second place with a prize of $3,000.

## Event Details

The hackathon was organized by the MIT Sloan School of Management Africa Business Club and took place on February 28 & March 1, 2025, at the Martin Trust Center for Entrepreneurship. The hackathon was organized as part of the 14th edition of the MIT Africa Innovate Conference, themed 'By Africa, For Africa: Path to Economic Sovereignty'.

From over 1,000 applicants across 26 institutions, 13 teams from 14 institutions participated in the hackathon, including participants from Harvard University, Dartmouth College, MIT, Wageningen University, Yale University, and HULT International Business School.
