## How Bethel Was Conceived

Naomi records voice memos of ideas into Apple Notes. In session efd7d7d2 (March 12, 2026), she asked Claude to read two Apple Notes:
- **Note 1236**: "Bible App + Other App" -- voice-transcribed ideas
- **Note 1235**: "Apps" -- additional app concepts

These were later organized into `/Users/naomiivie/cortex/RAW_TRANSCRIPTION_NOTES.md` (5 sections) and `/Users/naomiivie/Life LLM/` (7 IDEA.md files).

## What Naomi Originally Said (from voice memos)

### Chronological Bible + LLM
- Bible in chronological order (NKJV) -- Psalms placed after the events that inspired them, prophecies next to their fulfillments
- LLM trained on Scripture for cross-referencing and study Q&A
- Click a verse and see where it came from / what it connects to

### Dreams & Notes Journal
- Log dreams God gives you, with interpretation notes
- Smart resurfacing -- the right dream surfaces at the right time
- OCR for handwritten notes (take a photo, text extracted)
- LLM trained on YOUR notes/dreams so you can ask "what dream was about X?"

### The Founding Decision
Naomi corrected Claude when it tried to separate these into 3 apps: "shouldn't dreams and notes and bible be one app? Why putting them in separate notes?" This merged the ideas into what became Bethel.

## What Naomi Said About Cortex (voice memo in 0d91dd3c session)

The Cortex voice memo (pasted directly into session 0d91dd3c) described:
- 3-layer architecture: Bottom (data ingestion roots), Middle (user interface), Top (agentic actions)
- LLM trained on all personal data, automatically creates spheres
- Email management: "a small interface, like a small face, and then it's like, it just does one email at a time"
- "Non-anxiety inducing way of managing your email"
- Jet black interface design
- B2B, B2C, API sales potential
- Sub-LLMs per domain (email sub-LLM trained on email data)
- Voice-driven interface for mundane tasks
- Apple Pay integration with Face ID confirmation
- Prayer woven throughout: "I prophesy this is gonna reach the ends of the earth"

## Key Insight for Bethel

Bethel inherits the same architectural pattern as Cortex but applied to spiritual life:
- Cortex: personal data -> per-user LoRA -> RAG -> agentic actions
- Bethel: Bible + dream journal + theological training -> Bethel LoRA -> biblical RAG -> dream interpretation/resurfacing

The per-user LoRA concept came from the Cortex vision first, then was applied to Bethel for users with 500+ dreams.
