## Family Monthly Budget Overview

According to Numbeo's January 2026 data, the estimated monthly costs for a family of four are approximately $1,393 (₦2 million) including rent, while a single person needs around $500 (₦715,000) monthly excluding rent. A family of four should expect around $2,740/month.

For Nigerian naira figures, a family of four living comfortably including school fees needs between 1.5 million and 3 million naira per month all in.

## Housing/Rent

Rent remains the largest expense for most households and accounts for up to 40 percent of total living costs. Middle-ground options like Ojodu and Berger offer balance with rents of ₦800k to ₦1.5M.

## Food Expenses

Food & groceries represent the second-largest expense at approximately $280 monthly. However, research indicates that lower-income households spend up to 70% of their income on food.

## Transportation

Danfo (Yellow Buses) typical trip costs ₦300 to ₦500, with a monthly budget of ₦30,000 to ₦50,000. BRT (Bus Rapid Transit) announced an 18% fare increase effective February 2025.

## Utilities

Generator fuel and prepaid electricity costs range from ₦40,000 to ₦100,000 per month.

## Cost Geography Note

Lagos operates on two different economies: the Mainland economy and the Island economy. The cost gap between these areas is massive, often 50% to 100% for equivalent housing and services.
