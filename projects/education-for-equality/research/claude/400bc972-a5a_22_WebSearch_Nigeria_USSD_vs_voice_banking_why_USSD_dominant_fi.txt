## Why USSD Dominates in Nigeria

**Accessibility Without Internet:** Unlike banking apps that need internet access and smartphone functionality, USSD codes can work on any mobile device, including feature phones. This is crucial given that the number of smartphones in Nigeria is estimated to be around 25-40 million, while at least 20 million people in the banking sector don't have a smartphone.

**Cost-Effectiveness:** Unlike internet banking and mobile banking which means additional costs on internet fees, USSD banking provides a no-cost means of enjoying banking services on your mobile phone.

**Financial Inclusion:** USSD works on virtually any mobile phone, making it accessible to a vast majority of the population, and in areas with limited or unstable internet connectivity, USSD shines. Millions of Nigerians in rural areas rely on USSD for banking.

**Adoption and Growth:** USSD kicked off as a ubiquitous banking channel with GTBank's *737# in 2012 which coincided with the launch of Nigeria's cashless policy by the CBN. In 2022, Nigerians processed 515 million USSD transactions.

**Trust and Reliability:** In Nigeria, banks report that USSD adoption actually increased after several high-profile mobile app breaches, as trust is earned, and USSD has decades of unbroken reliability behind it.
