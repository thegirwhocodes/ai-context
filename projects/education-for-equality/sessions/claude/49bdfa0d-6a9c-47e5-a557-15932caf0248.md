## Claude · 2026-06-20T16:35:48.111Z

In your Focusrite Control 2 mixer, the **M** and **S** buttons at the bottom of each channel strip are:

- **M = Mute** — silences that channel in your direct monitor mix. Click it on *Analogue 1*, for example, and you'll stop hearing that mic in your headphones/monitors (it does **not** affect what gets recorded into your DAW — only what you hear live).

- **S = Solo** — isolates that channel. Engaging Solo on one strip mutes *all the others* so you hear only the soloed channel(s). Useful for checking one source on its own. You can solo multiple strips at once.

Quick read of your screenshot:
- **Analogue 1 / Analogue 2** = your two physical inputs (mics/instruments on the front of the Scarlett).
- **Playback 1-2** = audio coming back from your computer/DAW.
- **Direct Mix** (far right) = the combined low-latency monitor mix of all those strips, which is what you actually hear when **Direct Monitor** (the green toggle up top) is on.

So M/S only shape your **live monitoring** — they don't change your recordings. Mute = "shut this one up," Solo = "let me hear only this one."
