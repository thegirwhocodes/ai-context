## Business Model

Rori is an engaging, friendly, after-school chatbot tutor built for students to accelerate their math skills, for free, via WhatsApp. This means the service is free for end users, with the costs being borne by schools or educational organizations.

Since being launched to the public in November 2022, Rori has reached over 40,000 users across multiple countries for a customer acquisition costs of less than $0.10c per user.

## Key Features

Rori operates on mobile devices with low-bandwidth accessibility, and Rori has students work their way through a curriculum of over 500 micro-lessons, based on the Global Proficiency Framework, an internationally recognized set of math learning standards.

The model is designed to be highly scalable in low-resource educational contexts like Ghana, making quality math tutoring accessible to disadvantaged students without requiring expensive infrastructure.
