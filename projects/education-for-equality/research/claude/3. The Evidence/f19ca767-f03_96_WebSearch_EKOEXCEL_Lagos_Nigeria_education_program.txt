## Overview

EKOEXCEL (EKO Excellence in Child Education and Learning) is Lagos State Government's statewide program launched by Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu in 2019 to transform Early Childhood Care, Development and Education (ECCDE) and Primary education in all Lagos State government schools, bringing 21st Century education that is child-centred and enabled by modern practices and technology to the state.

## Scale and Scope

EKOEXCEL is a government designed and funded programme to drive excellence in learning for 500,000 pupils across all 1,016 public primary schools. Over 14 thousand government teachers are being re-trained and upskilled through EKOEXCEL.

## Key Features and Implementation

EKOEXCEL is an education reform programme that is successfully developing more highly skilled teachers by training, supporting and motivating existing government teachers to succeed in their classrooms.

The programme is managed by the Lagos State Universal Basic Education Board (LASUBEB) in partnership with NewGlobe, a technical partner renowned for its innovative, technology-driven educational solutions.

## Learning Outcomes

The program has achieved remarkable results:

- An average Primary 3 pupil under EKOEXCEL now reads at nearly the same fluency level as an average Primary 5 pupil before the programme's launch.

- In just 80 instructional weeks, schools under the EKOEXCEL program have reduced learning deprivation from an average of 94.7% to 58.5%, which effectively amounts to a reduction of 36.2% in the learning deprivation metric.

- Pupils under the EKOEXCEL programme achieved top scores in the 2023 National Common Entrance Examinations with pupils scoring as high as 199, just 4 points away from the national best score of 203.

## Data-Driven Approach

The situation room is EKOEXCEL's data-driven education control centre located at the heart of SUBEB to provide real-time monitoring and feedback on lesson deliveries and completion, attendance, student learning outcomes, and other key indicators, creating digital transparency throughout the entire EKOEXCEL system through the bank of computers and wide screens, using a computer program called "spotlight".
