## Voice Memos and iCloud

Yes, you can sync Voice Memos:

1. System Settings → your name → iCloud.
2. Click **See All** beside “Saved to iCloud.”
3. Turn on **Voice Memos**.
4. Confirm your recordings appear on another Apple device.

Apple confirms that Voice Memos will then appear across devices signed into the same Apple Account. [Apple Voice Memos iCloud instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/voice-memos/see-your-recordings-on-all-your-apple-devices-vma6cc4d0571/3.2/mac/26)

However, Apple does not provide a Voice Memos equivalent of OneDrive’s “Free Up Space.” Syncing preserves and synchronizes the collection, but may not remove the nearly 5 GB of recordings from your Mac.

To make them genuinely cloud-only:

1. Create a `Voice Memos Archive` folder in OneDrive.
2. Drag older recordings from Voice Memos into that folder. Apple officially supports dragging recordings into Finder. [Apple Voice Memos export instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-lamr/guide/voice-memos/vm05f9fa82d4/mac)
3. Wait for OneDrive to finish uploading.
4. Verify several recordings through OneDrive’s website or another device.
5. Right-click the archive folder → **Free Up Space**.
6. Only then delete the archived recordings from Voice Memos and empty Voice Memos’ Recently Deleted folder.

Important: deleting a synced memo removes it from Voice Memos on every device. Exporting also may not preserve Voice Memos-specific edit layers or transcription metadata, although it preserves the playable audio.
