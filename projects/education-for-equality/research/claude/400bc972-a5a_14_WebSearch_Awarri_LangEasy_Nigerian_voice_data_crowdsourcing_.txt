## LangEasy Data Collection

LangEasy is an app launched by Awarri to crowdsource voice and text data from users across Nigeria. The platform allows users to record audio samples by translating English sentences into Nigerian languages such as Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Pidgin, and Ibibio, with voice recordings being created by reading the sentences aloud in their chosen language.

This approach aims to gather extensive data to train their LLM, with initial targets of 24,000 hours of audio and long-term goals reaching up to 500,000 hours. Awarri built LangEasy.ai to collect thousands of voice samples from fellows in the government's 3 Million Technical Talent (3MTT) programme.

## N-ATLaS Integration

N-ATLaS (Nigeria – Automatic Transcription and Language Systems) consists of four Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) models and one Text Large Language Model (LLM) for Nigerian languages (Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, and Nigerian-accented English). The project was powered by Awarri Technologies in partnership with the National Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (NCAIR) and the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA), under the Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy.

The voice data collected through LangEasy serves as the foundation for training these speech recognition models within the N-ATLaS framework.
