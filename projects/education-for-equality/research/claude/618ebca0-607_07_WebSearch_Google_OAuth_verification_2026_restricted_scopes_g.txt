## Scope Classification & Timeline

Apps requesting sensitive Gmail scopes like gmail.readonly typically require 2-4 weeks for Google's full security assessment when the submission is complete. However, gmail.readonly is a restricted scope, which triggers Tier 3: a full penetration test that costs thousands of dollars and takes weeks.

Importantly, gmail.modify is classified as sensitive (Tier 2), not restricted. It lets you read, draft, send, and modify labels - but critically, it does not allow permanent deletion of emails. This distinction can save significant time and cost.

## CASA Security Assessment

Google uses the App Defense Alliance and the cloud application security assessment framework (CASA). Apps requesting restricted scopes must complete a CASA Tier 2 security assessment in addition to Google's own review (4-12+ weeks), which is an independent security audit conducted by a Google-approved lab.

Apps must be reverified for compliance and complete a security assessment at least every 12 months after your assessor's Letter of Assessment (LOA) approval date.

## Cost & Assessment Changes

Typical assessment costs range from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars per year depending on tier. Authorized labs such as TAC Security, Leviathan, and DEKRA handle the testing, reporting a Letter of Validation (LOV) to Google.

Tier 2 self-scan is no longer an option. While this means developers will incur a cost paid to an independent assessor, Google has worked to ensure this doesn't pose an undue burden.

## Workaround Strategy

Choosing minimal scopes can move you from the restricted (CASA-required) track to the sensitive (free review) track, saving time and cost. Instead of requesting gmail.readonly which triggers Tier 3, developers can use gmail.modify, which is classified as sensitive (Tier 2).

## Test Mode & Verification Status

Until an app passes review, it is considered unverified - meaning users see a warning screen and developers are limited to 100 test users maximum.
