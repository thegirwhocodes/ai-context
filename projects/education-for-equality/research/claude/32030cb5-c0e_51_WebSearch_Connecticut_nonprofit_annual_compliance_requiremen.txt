## Annual Filing Requirements and Fees

**Connecticut Secretary of State - Annual Report**
Nonprofits must file an annual report form online to the Connecticut Secretary of State with a $50 filing fee, due each year based on the incorporation or registration date.

**IRS Form 990**
Nonprofits must submit Form 990 each year to the IRS as a free filing due within four and a half months after the end of the fiscal year.

**Connecticut Attorney General - Charitable Registration Renewal**
A charitable organization form must be submitted to the Connecticut state attorney general each year within five months of the end of the fiscal year with a $50 filing fee, and must include a financial audit for statements over $500,000.

## Financial Audit Requirements

Charities with gross revenue between $500,000 and $1,000,000 must have an independent audit or review report completed by an independent certified public accountant, while organizations with gross revenue greater than $1,000,000 must have an independent audit report.

## Late Filing Penalties

Connecticut charges a $25 per-month late fee for every month or portion thereof that a charitable registration renewal is late, and if a charity fails to renew within six months of expiration, it must go through the entire reinstatement process.

## Deadline Information

Connecticut nonprofits must file annual reports with the Secretary of State and renew charitable registration with the Department of Consumer Protection by the last day of the 11th month following their fiscal year end.
