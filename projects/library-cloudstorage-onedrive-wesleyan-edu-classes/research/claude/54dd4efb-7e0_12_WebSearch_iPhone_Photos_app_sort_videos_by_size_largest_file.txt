## Current Limitations

Apple's Photos app doesn't let users sort videos by size on iPhone, and even in iOS 26, there is no simple "sort by size" option for videos. Apple gives you options to sort media by date (newest to oldest or vice versa), organize by albums, or filter by favorites, but there's no built-in way to see which videos are taking up the most storage.

## Manual Workaround

As a basic workaround, you can open the Photos app on the iPhone, go to the Media Type section and find Videos, then select a video and tap the info button to check details, like the file size.

## Alternative Methods

Since the native Photos app lacks this feature, there are several workarounds:

1. **Third-Party Cleaner Apps**: Cleaner apps like Clever Cleaner have a "Heavies" feature that automatically sorts all your videos by file size and shows you exactly how much storage each one occupies.

2. **Duration as a Proxy**: You can sort videos by length on iPhone as a proxy for finding large files - in the Videos album, tap the three-dot menu icon in the top right corner and choose to sort by duration.

3. **Files App**: You can open the Files app on an iPhone, look at the top corner and tap the three dots to reveal sorting choices, and choose "By Size" to arrange files starting with the largest.
