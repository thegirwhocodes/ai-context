## Key Challenges in Telephony Speech Recognition

ASR models trained on clean studio audio degrade significantly on telephony audio, background noise, accented speech, or callers using speakerphone. Individual models can struggle with spoken numbers, as they may be transcribed differently by different models.

## Performance Under Real-World Conditions

WER can jump to 30–50% for strongly accented speech versus 2–8% for native speakers on the same model. Background noise, telephony compression, and overlapping speakers compound the challenge — accuracy can drop from 92% on clean headsets to 65% on mobile calls.

## Solutions for Noisy and Accented Speech

The fix is not a better model in isolation; it is a model specifically trained and evaluated on telephony-grade audio. Models built for voice activity detection and trained on noisy audio (like Deepgram's Nova-3 Medical or noise-robust Conformer architectures) significantly outperform generic models in these conditions.

Low-fidelity channels, such as compressed IVR or mobile calls, can be enhanced using signal processing techniques to preserve speech clarity without introducing latency.
