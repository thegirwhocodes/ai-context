## Overall Broadband Access in Kansas

An analysis by the nonpartisan Kansas Health Institute shows 5.8% of Kansas households lack a computer and 12% don't have access to high-speed internet services.

## County-by-County Breakdown

The percentage without high-speed internet includes: Johnson, 5%; Riley, 9.5%; Sedgwick, 10.7%; Shawnee, 17.2%; Lincoln, 26.2%; Gove, 24.2%; Jewell, 22.8%; Neosho, 19.6%; Marshall, 16.9%; and Wallace, 11.8%. This shows significant variation across counties, with urban counties like Johnson having the lowest lack of access and rural counties like Lincoln having the highest.

## Income-Based Digital Divide

Thirty-one percent of low-income Kansas households making less than $20,000 annually didn't have high-speed connections, while 4.5% of Kansas households earning more than $75,000 were in the same predicament.

## Rural Challenges

The challenge of responding to the state's technological divide has been more difficult in rural communities due to insufficient infrastructure that elevated the cost of adding high-speed internet service.

## State Investment Efforts

In September 2024, Gov. Kelly announced an additional $22.7 million for broadband projects across 14 rural counties.
