## Dataset Overview

The dataset currently consists of 158 hours of audio recordings in 4 languages. This dataset is specifically curated to address the gap in speech and language datasets for African accents, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers working on Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Speech-to-text (STT), Text-to-Speech (TTS), Accent recognition, and Natural language processing (NLP) systems.

## How to Download

The dataset can be downloaded and prepared in one call to your local drive by using the load_dataset function. To download the Igbo config, simply specify the corresponding language config name (i.e., "igbo" for Igbo): from datasets import load_dataset dataset = load_dataset("benjaminogbonna/nigerian_common_voice_dataset", "igbo", split="train")

## Background

The creators were working on a speech to speech (stt & tts) conversational model for Nigeria languages, but along the way they had a bottleneck: The few data (audio) available were scattered and from different sources (Kaggle, Hugging Face, and many other websites). They gathered them into one repository, structured them, cleaned them (remove/edit wrong transcriptions), and trimmed most of them to 30 seconds chunks.

The dataset is hosted on Hugging Face at: https://huggingface.co/datasets/benjaminogbonna/nigerian_common_voice_dataset
