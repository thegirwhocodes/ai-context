Based on the search results, here's comprehensive information about YarnGPT, the Nigerian TTS model created by Azeez Saheed from UNILAG:

## Overview
YarnGPT is a text-to-speech AI model that can read text aloud in a Nigerian accent. Saheed Azeez, a final year student of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Lagos, has successfully built this model.

## Architecture
YarnGPT is a text-to-speech model designed to synthesize Nigerian-accented English leveraging pure language modelling without external adapters or complex architectures. Azeez took a language model called SmolLM2-360M from Hugging Face and added speech functionality to it, a process that involved major algorithmic changes.

## Training Process
This process was made possible by a wave tokenizer, using resources from Hugging Face, Oute AI, and other Nigerian repositories. The final-year Mechanical Engineering student had to spend $50 to train the model, and the training took three days.

## Training Data
The major chunk of data used in this project was data from movies – their audio and transcriptions. Audio files were preprocessed and resampled to 24Khz and tokenized using wavtokenizer, from publicly available Nigerian movies, podcasts (using subtitle-audio pairs) and open source Nigerian-related audio data on Hugging Face.

## Capabilities
The model could not only pronounce English in a Nigerian accent but could also read Nigerian languages—Hausa, Igbo, and Yoruba.
