## Logo Placement & Strategy

Client logos should be placed in the hero section, with 4-6 quality logos working better than many less prominent brands. Additionally, a floating trustmark at the bottom corner will remain visible regardless of where the landing page is positioned.

## Social Proof Impact on Conversions

Positive star ratings and reviews on a homepage convince 86% of customers to buy. More broadly, about 66% of consumers say they buy more often when they see trust signals.

## Quality Over Quantity

An important best practice is moderation: Your site works better with one or two strong, recognizable signals instead of dozens of logos.

## Strategic Placement Across the Site

For homepage optimization, trust signals above the fold play a vital role in building immediate credibility. For reviews specifically, display reviews alongside relevant content, such as below product listings or next to CTAs, to reinforce user confidence and encourage conversions.

## Overall Conversion Impact

Social proof builds trust, credibility, and validation among potential customers by showing that real people have had positive experiences with your product or service, and it helps users feel confident in their decisions and can be more persuasive than promotional copy.
