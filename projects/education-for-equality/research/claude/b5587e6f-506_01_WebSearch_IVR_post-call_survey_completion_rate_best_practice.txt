## Completion Rates

Post-call voice surveys can get up to 35% response rate, which is 3 times higher than online surveys sent via email. Some organizations report even higher results: clients normally achieve around a 92% survey completion rate and a 20-25% survey acceptance rate.

However, it's important to note that the completion rate being high is often the completion rate for people who agree to be surveyed, so overall response rates may be lower.

## When to Ask: In-Call vs. End of Call

Two recommended approaches are: asking customers if they'd like to leave feedback at the end of the call and directing them to the IVR survey if they agree, or providing a recorded message at the beginning of the call letting customers know they'll have an opportunity to leave feedback after the call ends.

## Key Best Practices

- **Timing of questions:** Completion rates drop dramatically after the two-minute mark, so start with your most important questions as many respondents abandon surveys partway through.

- **Survey length:** It's recommended to limit IVR surveys to 3–5 questions and keep the total duration under 2–3 minutes.

- **Fresh context:** The key benefit to post-call IVR surveys is timing, as the interaction with the agent is still fresh in the customer's mind.

- **Question types:** Ask more than just quantitative questions, as while numerical ratings are important, it's difficult to understand why a customer gave a low rating if you don't allow them to explain in their own words.
