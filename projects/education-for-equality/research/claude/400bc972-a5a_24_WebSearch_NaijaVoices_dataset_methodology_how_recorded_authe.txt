## Dataset Overview

The NaijaVoices dataset is a 1,800-hour speech-text dataset with 5,000+ speakers, covering Igbo, Hausa, and Yoruba languages. The dataset consists of circa 1,917,686 instances – each instance is made up of audio, a transcript, the language of the transcript, the speaker ID, gender, and age bracket.

## Methodology: Two-Phase Approach

The NaijaVoices dataset creation followed a distinctive two-phase methodology:

### Phase 1: Sentence Generation

Culturally-rich and authentic sentences were manually composed from scratch by 144 language users and experts ("Sentence Generators"), with 48 generators per language. These generators were guided by 100 diverse themes and prompts. Each batch of generated sentences underwent a double review system, with errors corrected and challenging linguistic issues resolved collaboratively with language supervisors.

### Phase 2: Speech Recording

The recording process involved two sub-stages:

- **Phase 1 Recording**: Each voice donor was required to record 240 sentences under the guidance of the Facilitator, obtaining a total of 1,269.21 hours recorded from approximately 5,220 voice donors.

- **Phase 2 Recording**: This phase focused on capturing longer audio recordings per speaker to support applications like text-to-speech, requiring each voice donor to record no fewer than 6,000 sentences, obtaining a total of 598.31 hours recorded from approximately 105 voice donors.

## Authentic and Culturally Rich Content

The NaijaVoices dataset captures the essence of Nigerian culture through authentic, expert-generated, and culturally rich sentences, featuring a wide range of speech patterns influenced by age, education levels, accents, and speaking styles – from broken to formal speech, ethnic and dialectal influences. Additionally, the NaijaVoices method intentionally incorporated discourse about marginalized populations, such as women, children, and people living with disabilities, as well as underrepresented topic areas, such as traditional counting systems and agriculture.
