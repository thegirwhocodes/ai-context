## Major News Aggregator Databases

Nexis Uni is the academic market version of LexisNexis and the best place to search the fulltext New York Times. Dow Jones FACTIVA is the home of the fulltext Wall Street Journal. Dow Jones Factiva combines more than 30,000 sources to give students, researchers, professors, and librarians access to premium content from 200 countries in 32 languages.

## Download Limitations and Text Mining

For Nexis Uni specifically, individual Nexis Uni users can manually download up to 100 full-text documents at a time, and up to 2,500 total documents per day. However, the license prohibits "accessing or using the online services via mechanical, programmatic, robotic, scripted or any other automated means" (e.g., APIs, etc.), and neither the online services nor the materials may be collected via automated or robotic methods, or used in conjunction with a generative artificial intelligence (AI) solution.

## Text Data Mining Tools

ProQuest TDM Studio and similar services provide researchers with access to data and resources that were previously unavailable for TDM to this extent. ProQuest TDM (Text and Data Mining) Studio allows you to create and analyze datasets from ProQuest content.

The LexisNexis Web Services API (WSAPI) enables researchers to download and build text corpuses from Nexis Uni (including many major world news sources) for further analysis.
