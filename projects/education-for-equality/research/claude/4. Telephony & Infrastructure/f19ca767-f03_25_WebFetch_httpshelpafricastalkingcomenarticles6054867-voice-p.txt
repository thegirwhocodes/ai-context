# Voice Call Pricing for Nigeria

Based on Africa's Talking's pricing documentation:

**Call Costs Per Minute:**
- Regular Outgoing: NGN 15.00
- SIP Outgoing: NGN 3.00
- Regular Incoming: NGN 3.00
- SIP Incoming: NGN 3.00

**Monthly Maintenance:** "NGN 5000 + 7.5%VAT" for regular phone numbers

**Free Tier:** There is no free tier mentioned. The service requires a monthly maintenance fee with no deposit or setup charges listed.

The pricing structure distinguishes between regular phone lines and SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) connections, with SIP offering significantly lower per-minute rates for outgoing calls.
