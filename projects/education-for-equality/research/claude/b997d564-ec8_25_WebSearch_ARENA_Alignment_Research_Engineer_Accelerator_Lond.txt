## ARENA (Alignment Research Engineer Accelerator) - London Application Information

Based on the search results, here's what you need to know about applying to ARENA in London:

### Current Status
Applications to ARENA 8.0 are now closed. ARENA 8.0 will be running in-person at the London Initiative for Safe AI (LISA) from May 25th – June 26th.

### About the Program
ARENA provides in-person programming bootcamps, equipping people with the skills, community and confidence to contribute to technical AI safety. The bootcamps last for 4-5 weeks.

### What's Covered
ARENA covers the costs of travel to/from London, any visa expenses, accommodation for the full duration of the programme, and provides meals, drinks and snacks on programme days.

### Future Applications
If you're interested in applying to future iterations of ARENA but won't be able to attend ARENA 8.0, you can fill in an expression of interest form to ensure you're kept up-to-date for when applications open for future iterations.

### Contact
For more information, you can contact the program at info@arena.education or visit their official website at arena.education.
