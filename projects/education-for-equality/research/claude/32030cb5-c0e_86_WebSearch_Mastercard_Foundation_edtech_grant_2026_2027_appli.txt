## Mastercard Foundation EdTech Grant 2026-2027: Application Information for Africa

Based on the search results, here's what you need to know about applying for Mastercard Foundation EdTech grants in Africa:

### **Program Overview**
The Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship is an entrepreneurship acceleration program designed to support promising African EdTech ventures, providing select EdTech companies with critical business and financial support, as well as insight into the science of learning.

### **Multiple Regional Programs**
The fellowship operates through different regional partners across Africa. Several cohorts are currently open or recently closed:

- **Egypt**: A 7–8 month fellowship designed to scale growth-stage EdTech startups in Egypt while driving inclusive, technology-enabled learning across Africa, offering equity-free funding of up to $60,000, expert mentorship, Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) learning sessions, in-kind business services, and two years of post-program support.

- **Pan-African (via CcHUB)**: The Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship 2026, delivered in partnership with Co-creation HUB (CcHUB), is now accepting applications for its fourth cohort.

- **Ghana**: Deadline for application is January 30th, 2026.

- **Tanzania**: Deadline: 31 January 2026 (23:59 EAT).

- **South Africa**: The 2026 applications are now closed.

### **How to Apply**
Submit online application form before January 30, 2026. Each regional hub has its own application portal—you should identify which partner organization covers your country and apply through their specific platform.

### **Eligibility Requirements**
Your business must be registered in your country and have been in operation for at least 3 years, offer a tech or tech-enabled solution aimed at enhancing educational outcomes (covering K-12, tertiary education, and vocational skills training), and be at the growth stage with achieved product-market fit and active users or customers.

### **Benefits**
Selected companies receive $60,000 in equity-free funding and access to further strategic funding opportunities to fuel business growth and sustainability.
