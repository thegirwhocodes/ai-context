## Overview
Viamo developed the "3-2-1 Service," which partners with local mobile phone operators to provide on-demand information on a range of topics from farming to trafficking simply by having people call a toll-free hotline.

## Technology & Access
The 3-2-1 Service is a mobile phone-based information system designed to increase access to reliable information on a variety of topics including health, nutrition, agriculture, gender-based violence prevention, microfinance and weather. Callers can dial into a toll-free number 321 (or the country-specific short code) 24 hours a day, with IVR being the most prominent and useful among callers with a low literacy level.

## Cost Model
The 3-2-1 Service is completely free of charge to subscribers of partner mobile network operators and can be accessed on any phone, no matter how basic. Telecom partners make this life-changing information available for free (worth millions of dollars of airtime) in exchange for a subscriber base.

## Education & Reach
The service provides women with easy access to a variety of content on topics such as livelihoods, digital literacy, healthcare, agriculture, and education. In some countries like Rwanda, 3-2-1 is reaching more unique users than Facebook, and in Mali, there are more than 2,000 people listening to the 3-2-1 content at any given moment.
