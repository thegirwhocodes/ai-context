Concretely, here's the chain in [sage.py](email-agent/desktop/voice/sage.py) — and the key caveat: **only when you've launched Sage.** The orb in the app doesn't hear you; Sage is a separate program.

When it *is* running, "hearing" works like this:

1. **Mic capture** — it opens your microphone with `sounddevice` and records raw audio in real time ([sage.py:61](email-agent/desktop/voice/sage.py#L61)). It watches the volume, and when you go quiet for ~1 second it knows you're done talking and stops the recording. (In `--wake` mode it just keeps doing this on a loop, hands-free.)
2. **Speech → text** — it sends that audio clip to **Whisper** running locally on your machine (`http://127.0.0.1:2022`, the voicemode service that's up right now) and gets back the words you said. Nothing goes to the cloud for transcription.
3. **Thinking** — that text goes to **Claude**, which decides what to do (read your inbox, draft a reply, etc.) via the email backend.
4. **Talking back** — Claude's reply is sent to **Kokoro TTS** (`http://127.0.0.1:8880`) which turns it into speech, and `afplay` plays it through your speakers ([sage.py:49](email-agent/desktop/voice/sage.py#L49)).

So: **your mic → local Whisper → Claude → local Kokoro → speakers.** The voice never leaves your computer except the actual reasoning step.
