## 1. CPM (Cost Per Thousand Impressions) for Audio Ads in Nigeria / Africa

### Radio Ad Rates -- see Question 3 below

### IVR Ad Rates
No publicly available CPM data exists for IVR-based advertising anywhere in the world. This is an emerging format. Advertising within IVR is typically sold on a per-campaign or sponsorship basis, not CPM.

### General Nigerian Radio Market
- **30-second spot**: N8,500-N40,000 (~$5-$25) depending on station, time, location
- **60-second jingle**: ~N100,000 (~$63) at premium stations
- **Program sponsorship** (10-60 min): N98,000-N1,000,000+ ($61-$625+) depending on program and market
- **Peak time** (6am-11am, evening drive): rates are 50-100% higher than off-peak (11am-3pm)
- **Agency-negotiated rates**: Can be 30% lower than direct station rates

### Kenyan Radio Rates (for comparison)
- **30-second prime morning drive**: KSh 30,000-50,000 ($230-$385)
- **30-second off-peak**: KSh 15,000-25,000 ($115-$192)
- **Hope Media**: KSh 25,200/30-sec ($194)
- **Volume discounts**: 10-15% weekly, 20-25% monthly

### Nigeria Total Radio Ad Market
- **Total market size**: $50.1M in traditional radio advertising (2025 forecast)

## 4. Product Placement in Educational Content -- Developing Markets

There is very little published pricing data specifically for product placement in educational content in developing markets. Here is what exists:

### Global Product Placement Pricing (for context)

- **General product placement**: $50,000-$1,000,000+ depending on medium
- **Most product placements are unpaid** -- brands loan/give products to reduce production costs

### Africa-Specific Branded Education Examples with Known Costs
- **Lifebuoy (Unilever) handwashing program**: EUR 30M multi-year campaign across South Africa, Ethiopia, India, Indonesia, Vietnam -- covers curriculum integration, school kits, board games. They halved cost-per-contact over time but exact per-student cost is not disclosed
- **Airtel-UNICEF education partnership**: $57M over 5 years across 13 African countries -- this is zero-rating/infrastructure, not branded content per se
- **GSMA Innovation Fund grants for edtech**: $200K per company (e.g., ScholarX/LearnAM)
- **Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship**: Up to $100K equity-free per startup

### The Relevant Model for Sabi
Product placement in voice-based education (like "If Amina has 3 packets of Indomie noodles...") has no established market rate because it barely exists yet. The closest analog is radio sponsorship (N98,000-N1M per program) or podcast sponsorship ($25-$50 CPM for mid-roll host-read). The fact that this is an unpriced market is actually an opportunity -- Sabi would be defining the rate.

- **Services**: Targeted mass messaging, IVR advertising, SMS, inbound call lines
- **Reach**: 50M calls received in 12 months; operates in 20+ markets
- **Contact**: viamo.io -- pricing is provided through custom proposals from their sales team

Viamo's commercial arm is essentially a B2B marketing agency using their IVR/SMS infrastructure. They do not sell on a CPM basis -- campaigns are priced as custom packages.
