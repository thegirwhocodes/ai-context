## Official NIPO Trademark Registration Fees (2025-2026)

The official government costs include ₦1,500 for availability search, ₦16,749 for trademark application, and ₦11,529.80 for trademark certificate issuance.

Another source provides slightly different figures: The application fee is ₦10,000 per class (filing fee) plus ₦500 search fee, with a registration fee of ₦2,000 and certificate fee of ₦1,000.

## Renewal Fees

The renewal fee is ₦20,000 per class (must be paid before expiry) with a grace period of 6 months after expiry (with additional late fee).

## Important Notes

These fees are payable per class; Nigeria does not permit multi-class applications, so a separate application must be filed for each class of goods or services.

Applicants should also budget for professional fees and taxes, particularly when instructing a local trademark attorney (which is mandatory for foreign applicants).

The search results show some variation in the exact fee amounts, which may reflect updates or different sources. For the most current and authoritative fee schedule, you may want to visit the official NIPO website directly at nipo.gov.ng/feesandcharges.jsp.
