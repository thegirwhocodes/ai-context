## Overview

WAXAL, named after the Wolof word for "speak," was developed over three years to empower researchers and drive the development of inclusive technology across Africa. Google launched WAXAL on February 3, 2026, a dataset for 21 African languages, including Acholi, Hausa, Luganda, and Yoruba.

## Dataset Scope

The collection provides data for 21 languages and contains over 11,000 hours of speech data from nearly 2 million individual recordings. This includes approximately 1,250 hours of transcribed speech for automatic speech recognition (ASR) and over 20 hours of studio recordings for text-to-speech (TTS) voice synthesis.

## TTS Component Details

WAXAL-TTS is designed to facilitate the creation of natural-sounding synthetic voices and contains over 565 hours of high-quality, phonetically balanced audio. For each target language, the team created a phonetically balanced script of approximately 108,500 words and recorded 72 community participants in professional studio-like environments to reduce background noise and preserve audio fidelity.

## Open Access

The complete WAXAL collection is released under an open license and is available on Hugging Face. The dataset is owned by African partners who worked on the project, not Google, with a framework ensuring partners retain ownership of the data they collected while making these resources available to the global research community.
