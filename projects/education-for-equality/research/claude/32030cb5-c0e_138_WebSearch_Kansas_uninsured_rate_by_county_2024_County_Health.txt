## Key Resources Available

The County Health Rankings & Roadmaps website provides 2024 Kansas data in spreadsheet format (520.54 KB), along with 2024 Kansas Health Outcomes and Health Factors maps.

The 2024 County Health Rankings were released on March 20 by the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation (RWJF) and the University of Wisconsin Population Health Institute (UWPHI).

## Data and Methodology

The uninsured measure is based on one year of survey data and is created using complex statistical modeling by The Small Area Health Insurance Estimates. The numerator is the number of people currently uninsured in the county under the age of 65, with the denominator being the number of people in the county under age 65.

## Additional Kansas-Specific Information

The Kansas Health Institute's Annual Insurance Update includes detailed county-level data on uninsured adults age 19-64 by county, uninsured children age 0-18 by county, and uninsured rates by county for urban-rural classification for ages 0-64.

The data is available directly through countyhealthrankings.org and through the Kansas Health Institute's county profile reports.
