# Signapse's AI Sign Language Translation Roadmap

## Current Position: Tier 2

Signapse has developed a **Sign Language Production Framework (SLPF)** to measure their AI signer Rae's proficiency across seven tiers. They're currently at Tier 2: "Beginning to Make Sense."

**Current capabilities:**
- Photo-realistic appearance with fluid movement
- Some facial expressions and mouthings
- Short phrase signing with emerging grammar application
- Heavy reliance on English word order (Sign Supported English)
- "100% understandability" achieved through mandatory human review by Deaf translators

## Key Challenges Being Addressed

The framework identifies three priority obstacles:
1. BSL grammar structures like "topic-comment" and "role shifts"
2. Consistent spatial positioning and directional signing
3. Reducing dependency on manual quality checks

## Roadmap Timeline

**Stage 1 (Foundational) - Near-term:**
- Grammar improvements: expected within 6 months
- Space and direction handling: within 1-2 months

**Stage 2 (Tier 3/4) - Transitional:**
- Target: 80% understandability
- Goal: "Full use of BSL/ASL grammar structures" independent of English

**Stage 3 (Tier 5/6) - Advanced:**
- Target: 95% understandability with instant processing
- Goal: Near-human fluency with minimal manual intervention

## Existing Success

Signapse has already achieved **Tier 6 in transport**, using their digital signer Marcel for train messaging with accurate, expressive output.

## Expansion Plans

Following BSL mastery, they're developing American Sign Language (ASL) capabilities using the same technology framework, with plans to expand further.
