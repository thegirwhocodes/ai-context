Before making significant edits or building new features, do research on best practices first, then update the implementation plan, then ask Naomi for permission before coding.

**Why:** Naomi doesn't want "wonky code" — she wants well-researched, thoughtful implementations. The previous pattern of just writing code without thinking led to issues she had to correct.
**How to apply:** For any non-trivial feature: (1) Research best practices, (2) Propose plan update, (3) Get Naomi's approval, (4) Then code.
