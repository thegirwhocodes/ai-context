Based on the search results, here's the information about Connecticut nonprofit incorporation filing fees for 2026:

The state filing fee to incorporate a nonprofit in Connecticut is $50. If you want expedited processing, you'll pay an extra $50 for a total of $100.

This fee covers the filing of the Certificate of Incorporation (also called the Certificate of Incorporation for a Nonstock Corporation) with the Connecticut Secretary of State's Commercial Recording Division. You can file the Certificate by mail or online.

It's worth noting that this is just the state incorporation fee. You will also need to pay the IRS $275 or $600 for your 501(c)(3) application (Form 1023-EZ is $275, full Form 1023 is $600).
