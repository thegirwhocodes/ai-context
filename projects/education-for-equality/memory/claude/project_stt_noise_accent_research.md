Research (Jul 7 2026) on which STT survives Sabi's real conditions: very noisy (crying baby, generator, market), thick Nigerian accent + Pidgin/Yoruba/Hausa/Igbo code-switch, over 8kHz Africa's Talking telephony. - 400bc972

**Anchor benchmark — AfriSpeech-MultiBench (arXiv 2511.14255, Nov 2025), Nigeria WER (VERIFIED verbatim vs PDF):** Intron-Sahara V2 **12.83%**, Azure 26.41, Gemini-2.0 Flash 26.47, Whisper-large-v3 **26.53**, Google Chirp V3 27.10, AWS Transcribe 28.16, Parakeet-tdt-0.6B-v2 32.60, GPT-4o Transcribe **40.03** (a critique-catch: the 60.41 figure floating around is a MISQUOTE; real = 40.03). - 400bc972

**⚠️ HEAVY caveat on that table:** paper is authored 100% by Intron Health employees, benchmarking Intron's own Sahara model, on Intron-owned datasets, at **16kHz not 8kHz telephony**, on **adults not children**. Trust the *direction* (Intron ~2x better on NG-accented English; global models all cluster ~26-28%), NOT the decimal. Independent reality check: Nollywood ASR paper (arXiv 2407.02631) reports Whisper **124% WER** on Nigerian film audio — real-world noisy is catastrophically worse than any lab number. NO independent benchmark exists for noisy-8kHz-Nigerian-CHILDREN; must measure on real Sabi calls. - 400bc972

**Intron "noise robustness" claim was REFUTED:** its "0% false triggers on silence" tests hallucination-on-silence/short-clips/pauses, NOT background noise. On the actual noisy test (Parliamentary: overlapping speakers + high background noise), **Distil-Whisper-v3.5 (11.5%) BEAT Intron-Sahara V2 (12.01%)**. Distil-Whisper-v3.5 is a serious cheap noise-robust fallback. Call-center domain (VERIFIED): Intron 13.45, Parakeet 20.96, Gemini 22.39, GPT-4o 23.20, Whisper-large-v3 24.7. - 400bc972

**Whisper failure modes (primary source):** goes sub-4% LibriSpeech → 30-45% general African-accented; defaults to FRENCH on Fongbe, misclassifies HAUSA as ARABIC on religious/musical content; confidence scores unreliable for tonal langs. Off-the-shelf MMS (53.77% Ika) / XLS-R (32.91% Zambian) unusable without fine-tune. Literature unanimous: **domain fine-tune of a SMALL model beats bigger zero-shot** (Yoruba Wav2Vec2 28%→17% on just 120hrs; NaijaVoices gave Whisper 75.86% WER improvement). - 400bc972

**Production noise stack (the actual answer):** 3 layers drop noisy WER 25-40% → 8-12%: (1) neural denoiser front-end, (2) noise-robust model, (3) domain fine-tune + keyterm biasing. Denoisers: **DeepFilterNet v3** = best free pick (~40ms, MIT/Apache, modern); RNNoise cheap (~10ms, BSD) but unmaintained since 2024; Krisp best-but-paid (went metered May 2026). Do NOT triple-stack denoisers (erases sibilants). **Silero VAD is critical** for Sabi: `vad_filter=True` + `no_speech_threshold=0.6` + `min_silence_duration_ms=600` kills Whisper's hallucinate-on-noise (the crying-baby → garbage-text failure). 8kHz telephony itself costs +3-5 WER pts. - 400bc972

**Africa's Talking architecture reality:** AT Voice API has **NO built-in ASR** — only free TTS (`say`), audio `play`, and call-recording to MP3. But it exposes **SIP endpoints + SIP trunking** → bridge calls into own Asterisk/self-hosted STT for real-time. Confirms server-side-only denoise (feature phones = no client-side RNNoise possible). AT bundles Google Chirp3 TTS at $0.00006/char. - 400bc972

(2) **Awarri's NG-accent model is NOT better for Sabi — it's WORSE on noise.** It's Whisper-Small + no VAD + trained on adult formal NG English, so on noisy child turns it hallucinates FLUENT adult sentences ("the federal government has ordered ongoing legal proceedings…", "i feel like you're not validating my experiences in our relationship", "how can we reduce the stigma around seeking mental health support"). On CLEAN turns it was fine/more-Nigerian ("mummy" not "mommy"). (3) **Sabi's problem is NOISE+children+telephony, not accent** — every model disagreed wildly on noisy turns; accent-tuning alone doesn't fix it. **Implication for fine-tuning:** LoRA must train on NOISY children's telephony data + noise augmentation + keep a VAD guard — NOT just Nigerian-accent adult English.
