## IVR Technology for Education and Literacy in Africa

**What is IVR:**
Interactive voice response (IVR) systems are automated telephony systems that interact with callers, gather information, and route calls to the appropriate recipient, operating using voice recognition and Dual-Tone Multi-Frequency (DTMF) input from a telephone keypad.

**Effectiveness for Low-Literacy Populations:**
IVR systems have emerged as a widely used form of mHealth technology that is particularly effective among people with lower levels of education, with previous research demonstrating the effectiveness of IVR systems in educating and engaging users with limited literacy skills.

**Application in Distance Learning:**
IVR has proven to be the most effective technology for distance learning in rural Africa. With IVR, the system automatically calls training participants, who listen to a pre-recorded message about the training content, and pre-recorded messages can include questions about the content or quizzes, which the participant can respond to using the keypad on their phones.

**Feature Phone Accessibility:**
Because many women only own a basic phone, they need to press a number to interact, and these automated responses are designed to be used by a group where the majority still have basic phones.

**Real-World Example:**
Call-a-Kahaani uses stories and an interactive voice response (IVR) platform to give learners life skills and a mindset for continuous learning, accessible using any mobile phone or landline without the need for the internet, allowing learners to access four-minute-long narrated stories interspersed with scenario questions.
