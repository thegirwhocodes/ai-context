## Scale of Poverty in Lagos Slums

65 percent of the people living in Lagos are urban poor who live in slums and settlements. Additionally, over 70% of Lagos residents live in informal settlements or slums, lacking access to basic services like clean water and sanitation.

## Monthly Earnings

For informal sector workers in Lagos slums, 79% of informal sector employees, employers and small business owners in Lagos State earn at least 39,000 naira per month. However, this income proves insufficient for many households. 83% disagreed that the current income level could sustain a household or an individual, that is, it could barely meet basic standard of living.

A specific example: Prior to the pandemic, a worker at the University of Lagos earned 18,000 Naira (US$44) a month.

## Poverty Vulnerability

20 percent of the Lagos population are vulnerable to poverty, and the intensity of economic deprivation in Lagos stands at 41.1 percent.

## Multidimensional Poverty

Research on five major Lagos slums (Makoko, Iwaya, Ilaje, Ijora Badia, and Amukoko) found that Makoko and Iwaya are particularly deprived in areas like schooling, sanitation and nutrition, explaining their higher poverty levels compared to other communities.
