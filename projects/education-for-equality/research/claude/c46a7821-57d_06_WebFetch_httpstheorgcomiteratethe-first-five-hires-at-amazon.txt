# Amazon's First Five Hires

**1. Shel Kaphan - VP of Research & Development**
- Prior role: Worked at Kaleida Labs (Apple/IBM joint venture)
- Recruitment: Connected to Bezos through his network in Santa Cruz (1994); the two "interviewed each other, and decided it would be a good fit"
- Role: Programmed the earliest website version; advanced to CTO
- Current status: Retired from engineering; runs Kaphan Foundation in Seattle

**2. Paul Davis - Developer**
- Prior role: Computer Science and Engineering Department at University of Washington
- Recruitment: Hired by Kaphan as his first engineering team member
- Role: Led backend development of first Amazon.com iteration
- Tenure: Approximately one year; left in 1996
- Current status: Founder of Linux Audio Systems

**3. Tom Scohonhoff - Technical Program Manager**
- Background: Fresh college graduate with computer science degree
- Recruitment: Joined in 1995 as fifth employee
- Role: Led entire customer service department initially; personally delivered packages to post office
- Tenure: Left in 2001
- Current status: Program manager at Microsoft

**4. Nicholas Lovejoy - Recruiting and Operations**

- Recruitment: Recruited from teaching position
- Role: Credited with proposing packing tables for warehouses
- Tenure: Left 1998; rejoined September 2016 for Amazon Transportation Services

**5. Mike Hanlon - Business Development and Operations**
- Prior roles: Seventh employee (1995-1997) managing real estate and telecom; later software engineer developing finance and customer service applications
- Tenure: Left in 2001
- Current status: Founder and CEO of Abett (HR/benefits SaaS platform)
