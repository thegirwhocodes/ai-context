# UNICEF Venture Fund 2026 - Eligibility Requirements

## Registration & Company Status
Yes, applicants must be registered companies. The fund requires entities to be "Registered as a private company at the time of the Innovation Fund award (projected: 3 months from submission)."

## Eligible Countries
Startups must be "registered in a UNICEF programme country." The page does not specify which countries qualify as UNICEF programme countries.

## Key Eligibility Criteria
Mandatory requirements include:

- **Open Source Commitment**: Solutions must use approved licenses—"GNU General Public license, MIT or BSD" for software, CERN/MIT/TAPR for hardware, or CC-BY for design
- **Proven Concept**: "An existing prototype of the open source solution with promising results from initial pilots"
- **Social Impact**: The solution must have "potential to positively impact the lives of the most vulnerable children"
- **Data Generation**: Must be "generating publicly exposed real-time data that is measurable"

## Application Process
Applicants submit via a JotForm application link. The funding period runs 12-18 months, with awards of "up to US$100,000 in ETH, BTC or USDC" plus technical mentorship.

**Deadline:** March 10, 2026
