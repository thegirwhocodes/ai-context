## State-of-the-Art Accuracy Results

The Spatial-Temporal Transformer Network (STTN) for continuous SLR outperforms at 95.2% accuracy on CSL and PHOENIX-2014 datasets.

A transformer-based model leveraging a pretrained ResNet-18 for spatial feature extraction and a Transformer Encoder with Bidirectional LSTM achieved 99.02% accuracy in signer dependent mode and 77.71% in signer independent mode on the KAU-CSSL dataset.

A hybrid CNN-transformer framework achieved 99.40% accuracy, F1-score of 99.31%, and AUC of 0.992.

## Advanced Transformer Architectures

Vision transformers (ViTs) have emerged as a breakthrough technology, offering superior feature extraction through self-attention mechanisms.

The Adaptive Transformer (ADTR) framework achieved a BLEU4 score of 22.73 on gloss-free sign language translation, significantly outperforming existing models like GASLT (15.74), STMC-T (15.74), and SimulSLT (14.10).

Despite reaching 99.97% accuracy on the ASL Alphabet dataset, a hybrid CNN-Transformer model maintains real-time inference speeds and optimized computational complexity.
