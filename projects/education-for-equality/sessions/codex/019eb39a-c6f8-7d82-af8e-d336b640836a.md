## Codex · 2026-06-10T22:18:18.776Z · final_answer

As of **June 10, 2026**, here’s the clean way to do it.

**Best path for you in the US**
Since you’re in Connecticut, your likely mission is the **Nigerian Consulate in New York**. Their site lists Connecticut under the NY consulate’s jurisdiction and says passport applicants are handled **Monday–Thursday, 9am–1pm**, no walk-ins.

**Step-By-Step**
1. **Make sure your NIN is correct first.**  
   Your NIN name must match your passport exactly: surname, first name, middle name spelling/order. If it doesn’t, fix NIN first or the passport process can stall.

2. **Choose your route: contactless or in-person.**
   - Try **contactless renewal** first if this is a normal renewal/reissue and you are not changing/correcting data. Use the **NIS Mobile** app.
   - If the app says you’re ineligible, or if you need a data correction/first passport/lost passport process, do the **in-person appointment** route.

3. **Apply online through the official NIS passport portal.**  
   Use the official portal: [passport.immigration.gov.ng](https://passport.immigration.gov.ng/) or the linked NIS appointment/application pages. Select:
   - `Apply for Renewal/Re-issue`
   - Processing country: `United States`
   - Mission: likely `New York` for you

4. **Pay online and print everything.**  
   Print:
   - completed passport application form
   - payment receipt
   - acknowledgement slip
   - appointment confirmation if going in person

5. **Prepare your documents.**  
   Bring/mail:
   - current or expired Nigerian passport
   - photocopy of passport data page
   - NIN slip/details
   - birth certificate or declaration of age
   - local government/state of origin or national ID if requested
   - marriage certificate if applicable
   - prepaid self-addressed return envelope with tracking
   - $50 consulate/admin fee where required

6. **If in person, attend biometrics.**  
   You’ll go to the consulate for fingerprinting, digital photo, and signature. For NY, bring your appointment confirmation and do not rely on walk-ins.

7. **Track it.**  
   Use [track.immigration.gov.ng](https://track.immigration.gov.ng/) and also keep your USPS/FedEx/UPS tracking number for the return envelope.

**Costs**
Budget roughly **$200–$300+** depending on booklet type, admin fee, bank charges, mailing, and travel. Official pages list government passport fees around the $142–$242 range, while the NIS 2026 service document lists diaspora prices around **$150 for 32-page/5-year** and **$230 for 64-page/10-year**. The portal checkout is the one that ultimately controls.

**Important**
Do not pay random third parties. The Embassy explicitly says passport payments should be made through official NIS channels.

Sources: [Nigeria Immigration Service](https://immigration.gov.ng/), [NIS Passport Portal](https://passport.immigration.gov.ng/), [Nigerian Embassy DC passport page](https://nigeriaembassyusa.org/passport/), [Nigeria Consulate New York passport guide](https://nigeriaconsulatenewyork.org/apply-for-a-nigerian-passport/), [Embassy consular fees](https://nigeriaembassyusa.org/consular-services-fee/).

## Codex · 2026-06-10T22:51:05.567Z · final_answer

64 pages. Specifically **64-page, 10-year** if the portal lets you choose it.

Why: passport renewal from the US is annoying enough that you don’t want to repeat it sooner than necessary. The 64-page/10-year option gives you more visa/stamp space and twice the validity. The official NIS page lists normal passports as **32-page/5-year, 64-page/5-year, and 64-page/10-year**, and the diaspora fee difference is usually worth it.

Choose **32-page/5-year** only if you’re trying to spend the absolute minimum and you barely travel.

Small caveat: **10-year is generally adults only**, so if the portal blocks it, pick **64-page/5-year**. The portal’s final checkout price is the source of truth.

## Codex · 2026-06-10T22:57:02.764Z · final_answer

Yes. For this upload, think **Nigerian passport portal photo**, not US 2x2 passport photo.

Use this checklist:

- **Size:** under `2MB`
- **Resolution:** exactly `600 x 800 px` portrait
- **Background:** plain white, no shadows
- **Face:** centered, looking straight at camera
- **Hair:** pulled back so **face and ears are visible**
- **No accessories:** no headphones, mask, hats/head covering unless religious, and safest is no glasses
- **Lighting:** even natural light, no harsh glare, not dim
- **Clothing:** normal dark/contrasting top helps against white background

Best way to take it: stand 3-5 feet from a white wall, face a window or soft light, pull hair behind ears, take from chest/shoulders up, then crop to **600 wide by 800 tall**.

On Mac: open photo in **Preview → Tools → Adjust Size → set 600 x 800 px**, then **File → Export → JPEG**, adjust quality until it’s under `2MB`.
