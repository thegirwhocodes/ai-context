## Age and Language Requirements
Echoing Green Fellowship applicants must be 18 years or older by June 1, 2026. To be eligible for the Fellowship and fully participate in programming and support offerings, applicants must be able to proficiently communicate in English.

## Founder Status and Decision-Making Authority
Applicants must be "original founders" who launched or are launching an organization that is their original idea, and cannot have assumed a leadership position within the organization after its founding. To be eligible, applicants must be a leader with decision-making power within the organization.

## Years of Operation and Organizational Stage
Eligible organizations are typically early-stage, defined as those with fewer than five years of full-time operations or those still requiring significant resources to grow.

## Full-Time Commitment
Applicants must work full time throughout the 18-month Fellowship, defined as 40 hours a week on the work outlined in their application, and it must remain as their main professional priority.

## Organizational Structure
Organizations must operate independently, even if fiscally sponsored, provided the fiscal sponsor does not exercise decision-making authority.

## Other Requirements
Echoing Green does not have any educational requirements to apply for a Fellowship. Any organizational structure is eligible as long as the mission focuses on social impact.
