## Paper Overview

The paper examines how the empirical literature on economic growth has shifted from studying proximate determinants to analyzing deeper, more fundamental factors rooted in long-term history, with a focus on historical variables' effects on contemporary income while accounting for ancestral composition of current populations, finding that economic development is affected by traits transmitted across generations over the very long run.
