# Administrative Burdens on Army Companies

## Monthly Reporting Load
Companies submit "anywhere from three dozen to four dozen monthly reports," with the completion process consuming approximately one week monthly for command teams.

## Time Impact
The article states that "Key leaders at the company level are stuck behind computers for most of their workdays and on many of their off days just trying to keep up."

## Named Systems
The author identifies several systems pulling administrative resources:
- Digital Training Management System
- Defense Travel System
- Global Combat Support System–Army
- Army Records Information Management System
- MEDPROS (Medical Protection System)

## Problematic Reports
Specific examples include troops-to-task reports, which the author notes "rarely feed actual decisions, processes, or systems, but instead pull platoon sergeants and operations sergeants away from warfighting operations."

## Proposed Solutions
The author argues that meaningful change "can only occur from the top down," emphasizing that "senior leaders recognized the breadth of company functions and scope of required tasks" must "decided when, where, and how to reduce them." He advocates for "meaningfully cutting back on current administrative priorities from all Army entities."
