## Overview

YarnGPT turns text into Nigerian-accented speech in seconds, supporting Igbo, Yoruba, and Hausa, while redefining AI accessibility in Nigeria. The platform has 19k+ users and converts 1.2 billion tokens per month.

## Quality

Unlike many TTS models that rely on complex external adapters, YarnGPT uses pure language modelling to deliver high-quality speech synthesis, making it both efficient and impactful. It provides 24kHz high-quality audio output. The model can generate audio on its own but is better when using a voice to prompt the model, with about 11 voices supported by default (6 males and 5 females).

## Reliability & Limitations

However, there are important limitations to consider. The model may not capture the full diversity of Nigerian accents and could exhibit biases based on the training dataset, and a lot of the text the model was trained on were automatically generated which could impact performance. Training data includes auto-generated content and the model may not capture all Nigerian accent variations.

## Development

Saheed Azeez, a University of Lagos student, built an artificial intelligence text-to-speech model with Nigerian accent called YarnGPT. The model was trained on a dataset of publicly available Nigerian movies, podcasts (using subtitle-audio pairs) and open source Nigerian-related audio data on Huggingface.
