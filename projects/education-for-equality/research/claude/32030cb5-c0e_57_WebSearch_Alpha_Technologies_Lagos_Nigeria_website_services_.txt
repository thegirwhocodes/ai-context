## Company Overview

Alpha Technologies Limited is located at 1b Imam Agusto Close Off Olosa Street Victoria Island, Lagos Nigeria, with its website at [alphatechnologieslimited.com](https://alphatechnologieslimited.com/).

## Services and Business Model

Alpha Technologies Limited is a cross network service provider whose service functionality depends on the existing primary network providers. The company offers memorable branded Toll Free (0800) or Non-Toll Free (0700) SMAT Numbers with calls as low as N14/minute.

Their aim is to be the leading provider of enhanced value added services to the telecommunications industry by deploying cutting-edge technology and the best qualified & motivated staff.

## Company History

The company was incorporated in 1990 as a telecom contractor and consultant, repositioned as an enhanced value added services provider to the telecom industry in 2003, and granted value added network services (special numbering) license by NCC in December 2005. The company is the pioneer of toll free service, special numbering, and other novel telecom services in Nigeria.
