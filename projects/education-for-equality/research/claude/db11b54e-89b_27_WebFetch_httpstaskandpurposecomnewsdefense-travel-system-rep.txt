# Specific Complaints About DTS

**User Experience Issues:**
- Former Army Col. Arnold Strong called it the "Don't Travel Service," noting it's "challenging for soldiers, leaders, NCOs, officers" to navigate
- Tickets not paid for (Chamberlin's case), leaving travelers stranded at airports
- System set payout dates incorrectly, leaving Monahan without per diem for 10 months
- Produces "chaotic" routing with "long layovers" and "poor connections" because it "find[s] the cheapest source available"

**Financial/Credit Damage:**
- Travel card declines after extended delays
- Soldiers forced to use personal funds or unit funds as workarounds
- Monahan paid "a couple thousand dollars out of pocket"
- Young soldiers' personal credit scores damaged by late government card payments, making them vulnerable to predatory lending

**Process Bottlenecks:**
Van Saun described approval delays: units with "only one, two, or three people" authorized to approve vouchers creates "a huge bottleneck, a huge delay"
