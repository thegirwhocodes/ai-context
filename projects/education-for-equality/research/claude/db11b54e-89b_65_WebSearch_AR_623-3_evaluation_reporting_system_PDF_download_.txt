## AR 623-3 Overview

Army Regulation AR 623-3, EVALUATION REPORTING SYSTEM, prescribes the policies for completing evaluation reports that assess the performance and potential of officers, warrant officers, and enlisted personnel in the United States Army.

This regulation prescribes the policy and tasks for the Army's Evaluation Reporting System, including officer, noncommissioned officer, and academic evaluation reports focused on the assessment of performance and potential, and includes policy statements, operating tasks, and rules in support of operating tasks.
