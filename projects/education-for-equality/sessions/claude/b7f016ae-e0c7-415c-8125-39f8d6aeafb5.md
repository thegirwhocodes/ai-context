## What "our data" actually is

This is the part that changes the answer. I measured it:

- **Call recordings + turns: 868 MB.** That's the whole production dataset.
- **Code: 17 MB**, already on GitHub.
- **Supabase: small** (learner rows + call metadata), can't size it until keys are refreshed.
- **The other 214 GB is training junk** — 199 GB of `whisper-finetune`, 9 GB qwen3-finetune, plus 263 GB of Docker build cache and 233 GB of reclaimable images. Regenerable, not data.

So migrating the *data* is a one-hour job. What's hard is moving the *machine*: Asterisk + SIP trunk, AT's IP allowlist, and the RTX 4000 that runs Chatterbox, local Whisper and Ollama for every caller who isn't you.

## Free routes, ranked for E4E specifically

**1. AWS Education Equity Initiative — the best fit by a wide margin.** $100M programme, and it names *"non-profits, governments, socially-minded EdTechs"* building for underserved learners. That is a literal description of Sabi. Two doors: a rolling self-serve request for **up to $10,000** (answer in ~3 weeks) and the full initiative via YouNoodle for **up to $100,000** over 12 months, reviewed quarterly (apply Jul–Sep → answer in October). Note the two figures come from different AWS pages — verify which door you're in. **This is the mirror image of the Google for Startups Cloud rejection**: Google named nonprofits and education as exclusions; AWS names them as the target.

**2. Oracle Cloud Always Free — this is almost certainly what your brother meant.** A genuinely free-forever VM, no expiry, no credit clock. But it just got worse: Oracle **halved it on June 15 2026** from 4 OCPU/24 GB to 2 OCPU/12 GB ARM, without announcing it, and started **terminating over-limit instances on Aug 18** — three days ago. It has no GPU. It could host Asterisk + the FastAPI app; it could not host Chatterbox, Whisper or Ollama.

**3. Microsoft, two doors.** Founders Hub: $1,000 self-serve, up to $5,000 after business verification, **no investor or accelerator needed** — the top $150k tier does need a referral. Separately, Microsoft for Nonprofits gives a **$2,000/year Azure grant**, auto-approved in about a day.

**4. AWS Activate Founders** — $1,000–$5,000 self-serve for bootstrapped startups, pre-Series B, needs a company website and a paid-tier AWS account. Framed entirely for-profit; no stated nonprofit exclusion, but no pathway either.

**5. AWS Nonprofit Credit Program (via TechSoup)** — $2,000/fiscal year, but **requires 501(c)(3)**, which is still pending via Goodstack. Schools are explicitly excluded; an edtech nonprofit is not.

**6. GitHub Student Pack** — still worth claiming for the $100 Azure credit, but DigitalOcean pulled its $200 student credit on **Aug 1 2026**.

**7. NVIDIA Inception** — free to join, up to $100k DGX Cloud credits, but requires an incorporated entity and excludes consultancies; aimed at startups.

## The catch worth more than any of the above

**Credits are a 12-month subsidy, not a free server.** Your box is a Hetzner GEX44 — €184/mo with a 20 GB RTX 4000. The nearest AWS equivalent, `g6.xlarge`, is **$0.8048/hr ≈ $587/mo**. So $10,000 of AWS credits buys roughly **17 months** of that one instance — while the same $10,000 would pay Hetzner for **four and a half years**. On GPU workloads, moving to AWS to spend free credits is a way of paying 3× as much with someone else's money, and inheriting a bill when it runs out.

And the migration risk lands exactly where you're most fragile: the SIP trunk. AT allowlists our IP, and Asterisk only accepts from `102.223.37.68`. Moving means re-plumbing the trunk on both ends — the thing that's been half-broken all summer — right as you're recruiting five testers. Latency baseline to compare against: **121 ms RTT** Falkenstein → AT today.

## What I'd do

Apply to the **AWS Education Equity Initiative** this week — it costs an afternoon, you're squarely in scope, and even the $10k door is real. Then **don't move the voice box.** Spend the credits on the things that aren't the telephony server: the STT fine-tuning that's eating 199 GB, Postgres if you ever leave Supabase, and object storage for recordings. Keep Asterisk where the trunk already works.
