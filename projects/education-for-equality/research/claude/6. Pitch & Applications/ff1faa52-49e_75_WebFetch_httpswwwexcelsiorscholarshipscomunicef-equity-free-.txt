# UNICEF Blockchain Venture Fund: Three Solution Areas

## Area 1: Improving Accountability and Transparency

This area focuses on enhancing existing services through blockchain solutions. Key applications include reducing operational costs via automation and disintermediation, creating verifiable impact proofs (both digital and physical), expanding financial inclusion for unbanked populations, and implementing tamper-proof audit trails. Solutions should enable automated payments through blockchain oracles and facilitate due diligence processes like KYC screening.

## Area 2: New Financing and Fundraising Models

This section emphasizes innovative web3 mechanisms to improve lives and create self-sustaining economies. It encompasses marketplaces connecting lenders to SDG impact, enabling reporting and tokenization of real-world impact delivery, developing cryptocurrency donation mechanisms, and engaging communities in data collection for resource allocation decisions.

## Area 3: Digital Public Goods and Sustainable Business Models

The final area promotes open-source development and community engagement. Solutions should aggregate platforms for DPG fundraising, create decentralized bounty systems for open-source contributions, facilitate matchmaking between projects and clients, generate impact valuations, implement DAOs and distributed storage, and incentivize diverse community contributions to public backlogs.
