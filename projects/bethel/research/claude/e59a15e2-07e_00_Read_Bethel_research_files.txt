### 3. Architectural Decisions and Technical Specs

**4-Layer AI Architecture:**
1. **Personal Context (RAG)** -- user's dream journal, past interpretations, personal symbol dictionary, instantly updated
2. **Bethel LoRA** -- fine-tuned adapter (~16-20MB) on Phi-3.5 Mini (3.8B params), trained on Bible + Hagin + Kuhlman + Jackson + Virkler + dream frameworks, monthly retrain
3. **Biblical Knowledge Base (RAG)** -- full NKJV (31,102 verses) in Supabase pgvector with 384-dim embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2), dream symbol dictionary, cross-references
4. **Cloud Model Fallback** -- Claude Haiku API (~$0.0045/call) for complex reasoning

**Serving**: LoRAX on Hetzner RTX 4000 SFF Ada (20GB VRAM), sharing with existing Sabi infrastructure (Whisper + Chatterbox). Total additional cost for Bethel: ~$25/month. At 10K users: ~$0.07/user/month.

**On-device inference** (future): CoreML/MLX on iOS (iPhone 15 Pro+), llama.cpp on Android. Phi-3.5 Mini INT4 (~2GB) + 16MB LoRA adapter. 15-30 tokens/sec on A17 Pro.

**Training data validation pipeline**: Every theological claim scored as Aligned, Extrapolated, Testimony, or Contradicts against NKJV scripture. Contradicting items excluded entirely. Testimonies labeled as such (not doctrinal claims).

**Database schema**: Supabase with tables for `bible_verses`, `dream_symbols`, `user_dreams`, and `user_notes`, all with pgvector embeddings.

**App platform**: React Native / Expo (evidenced by the existing project structure).

### 5. Design Philosophy

**Core Design Ethos:** "Opening a premium leather journal by candlelight in a quiet room." The app should feel like a luxury tech product for the spiritually curious -- NOT a church app.

Key principles:
- **Zero religious iconography** -- no crosses, doves, praying hands, stained glass
- **Warm gold + deep blue-black palette** -- `#D4A853` gold on `#0C0C14` base, with lavender (`#A78BFA`) for dream features and teal (`#5EEAD4`) for success states
- **Never pure black/white** -- warm off-white (`#E8E4DE`) on warm near-black to prevent "halation"
- **Generous whitespace**, no cramped layouts
- **No social media pressure** -- no feeds, likes, sharing pressure
- **No aggressive CTAs or upselling** -- free, no subscriptions, no ads
- **Glassmorphism** for floating elements (tab bar, suggested prompts)

**Design references:** Glorify (luxury Bible app), Bear Notes (dark writing), Arc Browser (warm dark UI), Apple Books (reading comfort), Claude App (warm AI chat), Day One (journal structure).

**Theological Design Philosophy:**
- **Grounded like Hagin** -- always points to Scripture, systematic, teaches principles
- **Warm like Kuhlman** -- humble, reverent toward the Holy Spirit, never clinical or academic
- **Never preachy** -- practical, not performative
- The inward witness is positioned as primary guidance; dreams are valid but secondary
- The Bible (NKJV) is the ground truth that validates all other sources
- Denominational neutrality -- spans Pentecostal, Reformed, Anglican, and independent traditions
- Testimony is labeled as testimony, not doctrine
- Dream interpretations must always cite scripture and acknowledge subjectivity

**Competitive Positioning:** "The first app that treats your dreams and Bible reading as one connected spiritual journey."
