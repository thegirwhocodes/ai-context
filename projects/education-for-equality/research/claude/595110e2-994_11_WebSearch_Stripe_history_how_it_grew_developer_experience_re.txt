## Founding and Developer-First Strategy

Stripe was founded in 2010 by brothers Patrick and John Collison with the core idea of simplifying payment integration for developers. While other payment processors focused on sales teams and complex contracts, Stripe empowered businesses to accept payments online with a few lines of code, a focus on the developer experience that fostered rapid adoption and network effects.

## Developer Experience Focus

Stripe started as a bottom-up company without traditional outbound sales, with its focus on the developer experience paying off because purchasing decisions for payment providers were increasingly made by developers. A core Stripe advantage is its developer-first ecosystem: with more than 45 programming languages supported, businesses can launch and customize payment flows, subscriptions, and invoicing in hours.

## Revenue Growth

Stripe's 2025 revenue in net terms is estimated at approximately $5.84 billion, while gross revenue for 2025 is estimated at $19.4 billion. Stripe's revenue CAGR from 2018–2023 was 45%, versus PayPal's 12%.

## 2025 Valuation and Milestones

Stripe's valuation reached $91.5 billion in February 2025 and $159 billion in February 2026. Stripe generated $1.9 trillion in total payment volume in 2025, up 34% from $1.4 trillion in 2024, equivalent to roughly 1.6% of global GDP. Stripe now powers 90% of the Dow Jones Industrial Average and 80% of the Nasdaq 100.
