# 2025 MIT Africa Business Challenge Winners

## First Place: AgriQ-Connect ($5,000)

**Team Composition:**
- Mariam Hamzat (PhD, Marine and Environmental Sciences)
- Qudus Rafiu (PhD, Chemical Engineering)
- Toheeb Obidara (PhD, Chemical Engineering)
- Azeez Akinyemi (PhD, Chemical Engineering)

**Their Solution:**
AgriQ-Connect developed an agricultural technology addressing post-harvest losses across sub-Saharan Africa. The innovation combines a Tinder-like marketplace connecting farmers directly with processing companies, plus an AI-integrated silo enabling real-time crop monitoring. This tackles an estimated $4 billion in annual post-harvest losses.

**Why They Won:**
The team secured both first place and the Audience Choice Award, suggesting judges valued the practical scalability and sustainability of their approach to a genuine continental challenge.

## Other Finalists

**Second Place: Charted AI ($3,000)**
- AI-powered bookkeeping solution for East African SMEs

**Third Place: MamaCare ($1,000)**
- AI voice hotline providing medical guidance and appointment scheduling for mothers in Nigeria

## Competition Context
The hackathon drew over 1,000 applicants across 26 institutions, with 13 teams ultimately competing from prestigious universities including Harvard, MIT, Yale, and Dartmouth.
