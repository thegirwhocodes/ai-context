## Background and Role

Sandeep Farias is Founder and Managing Partner at Elevar and a founding team member at EPIC World. He founded Elevar in 2008, being passionate about working with early-stage companies that foster economic development for low income communities, specifically delivering affordable and quality essential services like MSME finance, education, healthcare and agricultural services.

## Investment Strategy and Geographic Focus

Sandeep leads the firm's investment strategy, and engages with entrepreneurs in India and Latin America, with teams in India, Mexico and the United States.

## Entrepreneurial Households Thesis

Sandeep Farias pioneered the entrepreneurial households thesis at Elevar Equity, focusing on backing over 40-50 million micro-entrepreneurial families in underserved emerging markets, rather than focusing on the poorest populations. Elevar Equity builds its entire investment strategy around "Entrepreneurial Households" – low-income but economically active families with multiple income streams.

## Portfolio Performance and Impact

Over the past 15 years, Elevar has expanded beyond microfinance into broader financial services, education, and healthcare, scaled over 50 companies, and supported more than 50 million 'entrepreneurial households'.

## Professional Experience

Sandeep has over 25 years of work experience in diverse areas including emerging markets investing, governance and regulation, organizational design, transactions, and previously was a Head, Corporate Law Practice at Nishith Desai Associates.
