## Acquisition Announcement

On January 28, 2025, Sorenson announced the acquisition of OmniBridge, an Intel internal venture, and Hand Talk, to accelerate company innovation in AI-driven automated sign language translation.

## OmniBridge Technology

OmniBridge is an AI-driven sign language translation technology that enables bi-directional, real-time conversations between people who use American Sign Language (ASL) and those who speak English. 

The Deaf person signs into a camera, and OmniBridge uses its database of ASL signs to understand the statement. OmniBridge translates the ASL to English for the hearing person to read. The hearing person speaks into a mic, and OmniBridge captions their speech for the Deaf person to read.

## Technical Features

Sorenson OmniBridge is breaking new ground in language accessibility with the first scalable sign language translation Software Development Kit (SDK). It enables fast, real-time communication between Deaf and hearing people directly within your own application, for moments when interpreters aren't readily available.

Sorenson OmniBridge runs on an AI PC — a computer designed for AI workloads — without an internet connection, so no need to worry about lag or connectivity.
