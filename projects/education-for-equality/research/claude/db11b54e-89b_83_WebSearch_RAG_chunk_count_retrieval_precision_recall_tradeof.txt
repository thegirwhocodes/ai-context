## Key Findings on RAG Chunking and Retrieval Metrics

**Precision vs. Recall Tradeoff:**

Reducing chunk size from 1,024 tokens to 64 tokens improved fact-based recall by 10-15 percentage points on entity-heavy datasets, though small chunks lose surrounding context, which hurts narrative-style queries that depend on broader passages. This demonstrates a fundamental tradeoff: smaller chunks improve ranking precision by isolating relevant text but risk losing broader context, while larger chunks preserve context but can reduce the retriever's ability to match the query precisely.

**Chunk Count and Index Implications:**

More chunks mean more vectors, which means a larger index, more memory, and longer build times, with chunk size determining vector count, which determines index memory.

**Evaluation Metrics:**

Recall shows whether you're retrieving relevant chunks, while precision shows whether those chunks are actually useful. In RAG, precision is critical because LLMs are sensitive to noisy context.

**Recommended Defaults:**

Recursive character splitting at 400-512 tokens with 10-20% overlap works well for most text content and is the recommended starting point.
