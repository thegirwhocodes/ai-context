## Background and Education

Chinelo Anohu is a Nigerian lawyer, public servant and administrator. She has an LLB from University of Nigeria, Enugu, and an LL.M in Computer and Communications Law from the London School of Economics. She has also undergone Executive Education at the KSG Harvard University, Colombia Business School and the Insead Business School.

## Pension Leadership Background

Anohu previously served as Director-General of the National Pension Commission (PENCOM), where she spearheaded reforms aimed at strengthening transparency, enhancing regulatory oversight and boosting stakeholder confidence in Nigeria's pension administration system. She was a member of Pension Reform Committee of 2004 that introduced contributory pension scheme in Nigeria.

## Mutandis Africa Group

Anohu currently leads Mutandis Africa Group, where she focuses on investment facilitation, policy advisory and strategic development initiatives across African markets. Mutandis Africa is described as a pan-African investment and trade facilitation platform. Her firm has been instrumental in unlocking multibillion-dollar investment opportunities, strengthening sovereign dialogue, and creating the conditions for sustainable economic growth across the continent.

## Recognition

Chinelo Anohu was selected as Woman of Impact in Africa 2026 by ARISE Media Group, with the recognition celebrating her contributions to public sector reform, private sector development and Pan-African economic growth.
