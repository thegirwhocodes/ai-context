# The co-founder search (Aug 18 2026)

Built from primary evidence only — **4,636 of Naomi's own messages across 120 sessions, Mar 19 →
Aug 18**, plus all 32 memory files, the Founder OS and the live server state, deliberately **without**
opening `DREAM_TEAM_TARGETS.md`, `TEAM_BUILDING_ROADMAP.md` or the partnerships research (she asked
for that). Output: **The Lagos Seat** — `COFOUNDER_BRIEF_THE_LAGOS_SEAT.html` at the project root and
artifact `fc15250f-978a-4007-8ab1-721188c92baa`.

**First draft (wrong, and she said so):** a senior Lagos distribution/operations co-founder who had
personally moved a product through a Nigerian telco or state government — because the tutor works and
the distribution doesn't exist.

**Her first correction:** *"shouldn't this be someone with a huge passion for the children and
business chops? I had never built something like this until I did. Wouldn't we be barring a lot of
people?"* She was right — the spec would have filtered out **her** (February: never built a voice
pipeline, never ran telephony; built one in a night and took 2nd at MIT). Credential gates
("has closed an MNO deal", "has run field ops at volume") were replaced with **Access** (satisfiable
by producing one live meeting during a trial), **Slope** (has taken one hard thing from zero to real
in Nigeria, any domain — a market stall, a church programme), and **Durability**. The
salary-*and*-equity disqualifier was cut: it selects for people who are already wealthy, which is
backwards for this organisation. A **30-day paid trial** at a proper Lagos rate replaced the résumé
filter — one concrete task, vest backdated to day one if it converts.

**Her second, deeper correction:** *"this is a long-term business — we're going to grow together…
someone around my age-range… probably recent ALA alumni… is it not possible for them to be from
literally anywhere else, not even from Nigeria?"* The research says yes on both counts:

| Pair | Met | Built together *first* |
|---|---|---|
| Apple — Jobs + Wozniak | as teens | years of blue boxes |
| Microsoft — Gates + Allen | Lakeside School | years of programming |
| Google — Page + Brin | Stanford, same cohort | research together |
| **Paystack** — Akinlade + Olubi | Babcock, first-year CS | years, then reconnected |
| **Chipper Cash** — Serunjogi + Moujaled | Grinnell College | built together on campus |
| **Wave** — Durbin + Quirk | college | Sendwave first, then Wave |

⚠️ **Amazon is a solo-founder company** — Bezos started it alone; it's the wrong example to cite.
The pattern is *peers who met at school and had already built something together*. ALA alumni have
started **852 ventures**, so it is a pool pre-selected for that trait. Founder Institute, across
250,000 assessments: co-founder failure is rarely a skills gap — it's trait mismatch that was
predictable on day one, and traits (curiosity, perseverance, self-reliance) predict outcomes better
than IQ, education or the idea.

**Location: they do not need to be in Nigeria.** Chipper Cash is San Francisco-headquartered with both
founders outside Africa serving 5M+ African customers; Sendwave was built for African remittances from
the US. The condition is that all of those are **consumer products an individual adopts**, while
Sabi's path to scale runs through a state government — and the cautionary case there is **Bridge
International Academies**, foreign-founded, standardised tech-driven curriculum, ordered closed by
courts in *both* Kenya and Uganda, criticised for exactly a rigid non-local curriculum. So: co-founder
anywhere; a **Nigerian face where the company touches government** (Sonia, a country director, a
Nigerian board chair); somebody physically in Lagos eventually, but that can be **hire #1**, far
cheaper than founder equity.

**Five gates before any public post.** 1) **Equity in what?** — E4E is a nonprofit and cannot issue
equity; her own legal roadmap has the shape (Nigerian Ltd or Delaware C-Corp licensing the curriculum)
and this is the biggest one. 2) A dialable Nigerian number — the exact person she wants will ask to try
it on the first call. 3) One number showing a child learned, even n=7. 4) **The name** — "Sabi" is
trademarked and no replacement was ever landed; don't recruit into a brand you may rename. 5) Runway,
and a LinkedIn headline that still says junior studying economics. Strategy flag: a cold public post
broadcasts "no team, no distribution" to the same audience that funds her (and there was public
pushback over Bakame in April) — **twenty warm names first**, starting with EKOEXCEL/NewGlobe Lagos
alumni, a pool that appears nowhere in five months of transcripts as a systematic search.

**The line from her own transcripts worth remembering** (May): *"I don't have a ton of confidence that
we will progress with her at the helm - and that reduces my confidence in the whole project."*

**Blocked on:** there is **no LinkedIn connections export** on this machine (the OneDrive file is a
67-byte stub). Export it at Settings → Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections — but note
it contains **no education field**, so it can't be filtered for ALA directly; it gives profile URLs,
company/title (which isolates current students and new grads) and connection date. Higher-yield route:
ask ALA's alumni office directly for intros to alumni in their junior/senior year already building
something. Apollo is connected as a colder third option.
