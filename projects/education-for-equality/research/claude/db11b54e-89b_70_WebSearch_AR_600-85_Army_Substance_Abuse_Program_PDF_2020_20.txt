## What AR 600-85 Covers

AR 600-85 prescribes policies and procedures to implement, administer, and evaluate the Army Substance Abuse Program (ASAP). The regulation details the Army's program to address the abuse of alcohol and drugs, including procedures for testing (screening and confirmation), with special sections outlining drug testing procedures for the Civilian Corps, Army National Guard, and Army Reserve.
