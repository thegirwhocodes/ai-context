Some shorter two-syllable words include:

- "Oga" (Boss or someone in authority)
- "No Wahala" (No problem)
- "Wetin" (What)
- "Chop" (Eat or enjoy)
- "Gist" (Story or gossip)
- "Nawa" (Expression of surprise, similar to "Wow")
- "Yawa" (Problem or trouble)
- "Baffs" (Stylish clothes)

These expressions reflect the everyday warmth and accessibility of Nigerian Pidgin in daily communication.
