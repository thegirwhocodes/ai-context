## Key Examples of AI-Powered Education in Africa

In Africa, two prominent learning platforms—Daptio and Eneza Education—harness AI to enhance educational accessibility, with Eneza Education having over 860,000 subscribers and delivering lessons and assessments through web communication or SMS messages, offering quizzes and an "Ask-A-Teacher" feature.

Nigerian edtech company uLesson uses AI-powered analytics to monitor learners' progress and deliver targeted content through mobile devices, while in Kenya, M-Shule utilizes SMS-based AI technology to reach students without access to smartphones or the internet.

## USSD Technology for Education

Unlike SMS messages, USSD messages create a real-time connection during a USSD session with the connection remaining open, allowing two-way exchange of data without the latency usually experienced with SMS-based services, making USSD more responsive. Both SMS and USSD work across all phones and network carriers, even in rural areas not covered by 3G.

## AI Quiz and Learning Platforms

Notable African AI education tools include SuaCode (an AI-powered app for learning to code), Kwame (a bilingual AI teaching assistant), and Brilla AI (an AI contestant for the National Science and Maths Quiz competition).

Luma Learn is an AI-powered tutor platform with over 10,000 learners, offering tutoring in multiple languages including Swahili and IsiZulu.
