## Overview
The Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL) is a global research center working to reduce poverty by ensuring that policy is informed by scientific evidence, anchored by a network of more than 1,100 researchers at universities around the world who conduct randomized impact evaluations.

## Fellowship Programs
The J-PAL Scholars and Fellows programs enable researchers from low- and middle-income countries to drive the research agenda in their regions through the use of randomized evaluations. There are fellowship opportunities in J-PAL's South Asia and MENA offices, including the Research for Impact (RFI) program for PhD students at universities in India and the MENA Scholars Fellowship for PhD students and professors from the MENA region.

## Fellowship Support
The MENA Scholars Fellowship creates a pathway for MENA region-based researchers to build skills and experience to design and conduct randomized evaluations of poverty reduction and social development policies and programs, with fellows building networks, having access to mentorship from the J-PAL affiliate network, participating in a dedicated training program, and having the opportunity to contribute to a randomized evaluation over a two-year fellowship. Each Fellow receives a USD$15,000 stipend over the two-year fellowship period to support participation and commitment to fellowship program activities.

## Education-Related Evaluations
J-PAL sectors guide research and policy work by conducting literature reviews and managing research initiatives that promote the rigorous evaluation of innovative interventions, including questions about education quality.
