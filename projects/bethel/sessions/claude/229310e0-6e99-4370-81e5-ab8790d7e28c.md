## Claude · 2026-08-04T22:10:39.332Z

A few concrete places to look at Wesleyan:

- **Music Department & the Electronic Music/Recording Studios** — the Music Studios (in the CFA) house students working with Alvin Lucier's legacy and current faculty like Ron Kuivila and Paula Matthusen. Anyone taking MUSC 109 (Intro to Experimental Music) or the electronic composition seminars is producing regularly.
- **Private Lessons / Composition students** — the composition track majors are your best bet for people who actually finish tracks vs. just theorize.
- **WESU 88.1 FM** — the student radio station. DJs there overlap heavily with the producer/beatmaker scene.
- **Student groups** — Cardinal Sounds (a cappella arrangers who often produce), the Wesleyan Hip-Hop collective, and the annual "Battle of the Bands" / Eclectic Society shows surface producers who don't formally major in music.
- **Eclectic & Art House** — program houses that host student-run shows; the people booking and running sound are often producers.
- **Ask the department office** for the current student mailing list or the composition faculty (Matthusen, Kuivila, or Anthony Braxton's former students still around) — they'll know who's actively making stuff this semester.

If you want, tell me what kind of production (beats, film scoring, experimental, worship/gospel-adjacent) and I can narrow it.
