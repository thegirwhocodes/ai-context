## Founder & Background

Deepak Ramola founded Project FUEL in 2009 while still in his first year of college. His mother was pulled out of school in grade 5, but she emphasized that life is our biggest classroom. This inspired his philosophy behind the organization.

## What Project FUEL Does

Project FUEL celebrates the practical wisdom of everyday people around the globe through creative interventions and participatory tools in education, arts, culture, and films. More specifically, FUEL is an organization that collects life lessons from people of all ages and turns them into innovative education, art and media projects.

FUEL stands for 'Forward the Understanding of Every Life Lesson'.

## Education Focus

Using their award-winning methodology, they design hard-earned life lessons into meaningful and immersive experiences, making them accessible to diverse communities. Deepak's methodology has been recognized as world's top 100 innovations in education by Finland based organization HundrED and has also been adopted by the Education Board of Antwerp in Belgium.

## Impact

The organization has gathered over 400,000 life lessons. According to the One Young World Impact report, Project FUEL generates a social return on investment of 1:12, meaning $12 of social impact for every dollar invested.
