## Key Findings:

**Time Management & Systems:**
Millionaires batch similar tasks together, delegate extensively, and create systems that maximize efficiency. Time periods most millionaires use for focused work ranged from 15 to 45 minutes. Self-made millionaires often begin their day intentionally, don't hit the snooze button but wake up early, and spend mornings journaling, meditating, or reviewing daily goals.

**Delegation Practices:**
Self-made millionaires understand that their time is the most valuable resource, don't try to manage every detail themselves, and instead delegate, hiring accountants, assistants, or specialists who handle tasks more efficiently. They delegate tasks that are not their highest and best use of time and build teams of experts—investment managers, operations specialists, personal assistants, legal counsels, and strategic advisors.

**Financial Discipline:**
Living below their means is a cornerstone habit of millionaires—they don't spend for show, don't let lifestyle creep eat into earnings, and instead prioritize savings and allocate money toward investments. A significant habit is creating a budget and tracking expenses—budgeting isn't about limiting yourself, but about knowing exactly where your money goes.

**Daily Habits:**
Success is attributed to a sense of discipline—"It takes a lot of discipline to get up when you don't feel like it, get to work before everyone else, stay after everyone else, and keep working when people say you're working too hard." Millionaire entrepreneurs apply principles of time management, focusing on tasks that drive results rather than busywork, and many follow strategies like the Pareto Principle (80/20 rule) to identify actions with the greatest impact.
