## Form 1023-EZ Filing Fee for 2026

The user fee for Form 1023-EZ is $275. This fee applies to organizations applying for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status using the streamlined form.

### Key Details:

- **Who can use this form:** Form 1023-EZ is available to organizations projecting less than $50,000 in annual revenue, but is not available to churches, schools, or hospitals/medical research organizations.

- **Payment method:** The user fees must be paid through Pay.gov when the application is filed.

- **Alternative form:** For organizations that don't qualify for Form 1023-EZ, as of 2026, the IRS filing fee for the full Form 1023 is $600.
