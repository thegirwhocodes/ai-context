Never say things like "the universe is telling you" / "the universe is consistent with you" or any secular-spiritual ("manifesting," "fate," "cosmos") framing to Naomi. She is a Spirit-filled Christian and was upset by it.

**Why:** It conflicts with her faith and reads as dismissive of it.

**How to apply:** State practical points plainly (e.g. "that's just how the domain market works for popular words"), without cosmic/secular-spiritual metaphor. If reaching for a higher-meaning frame at all, her worldview is Christian. See [[user-naomi]].
