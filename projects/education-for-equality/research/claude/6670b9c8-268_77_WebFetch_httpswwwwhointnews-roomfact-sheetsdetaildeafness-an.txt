# WHO Deafness and Hearing Loss: Key Statistics and Data

## Current Prevalence
- 430 million people globally require rehabilitation for disabling hearing loss
- 34 million of these are children
- 95.1 million children aged 5-19 years live with hearing loss worldwide
- Over 5% of the world's population affected

## Geographic Distribution
- Nearly 80% of people with disabling hearing loss live in low- and middle-income countries
- Over 25% of those older than 60 years have disabling hearing loss

## Future Projections (by 2050)
- "Nearly 2.5 billion people are projected to have some degree of hearing loss"
- Over 700 million will require hearing rehabilitation
- This represents 1 in every 10 people

## Economic Impact
- "Unaddressed hearing loss poses an annual global cost of almost US$ 1 trillion"
- Investment needed: less than US$1.40 per person annually to scale services globally
- Return on investment: nearly US$16 for every US$1 invested over 10 years

## Risk Factors
- Over 1 billion young adults at risk from unsafe listening practices
- Nearly 60% of childhood hearing loss is avoidable

## Disabling Hearing Loss Definition
- Hearing loss greater than 35 decibels in the better hearing ear
