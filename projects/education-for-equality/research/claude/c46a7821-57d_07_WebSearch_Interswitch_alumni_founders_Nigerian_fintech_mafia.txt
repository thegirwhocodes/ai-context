## Moniepoint Founders from Interswitch

Tosin Eniolorunda is one of Nigeria's finest entrepreneurs and the CEO of Moniepoint, one of the most dominant fintech players in Nigeria. He joined Interswitch in 2009, spending five months as a data engineer.
