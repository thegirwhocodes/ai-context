## Key Findings on Nigeria's Digital Divide

130 million Nigerians are not connected to mobile internet, making Nigeria one of the largest contributors to the global usage gap. Nigeria stands among the five countries globally with the largest "usage gap," along with India, China, Pakistan, and Bangladesh.

### Affordability as a Major Barrier

For the unconnected in Low and medium-income countries (LMICs), which include Nigeria, device affordability and digital skills and literacy are the main barriers to mobile internet adoption. Across Sub-Saharan Africa, "the affordability of an entry-level device is 87% of monthly income for the poorest 20%."

### Smartphone Ownership Challenge

72 percent of Nigerian adults still do not own smartphones, limiting access to internet services, even though coverage reaches over 90 percent of the country. Actual internet usage hovers around 35 percent.

### GSMA Recommendations

GSMA recommends reducing taxes and duties on low-cost smartphones, expanding device financing, and investing in local language content and digital literacy to help bring more people online.
