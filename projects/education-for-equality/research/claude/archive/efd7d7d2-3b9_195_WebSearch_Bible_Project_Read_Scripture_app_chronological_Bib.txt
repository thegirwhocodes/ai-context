## Bible Project Reading Plans

The Bible Project offers a variety of study topics and plans designed on the Bible App by YouVersion. The One Story That Leads to Jesus reading plan in YouVersion's Bible app offers a complete journey through the Bible, featuring their full library of animated videos to guide you.

However, it's important to note that the Bible Project also offers an alternative to strictly chronological reading. One plan reads the Old Testament in the TaNak order (ancient literary order), followed by the books of the New Testament.

## Chronological Bible Reading Features

While the search results primarily show apps from other developers rather than a specific "Bible Project Read Scripture" app, many chronological Bible apps offer similar features:

- Chronological reading order with the full biblical text organized so you can follow the narrative from Creation to Revelation
- 365 daily reading portions that allow readers to complete the Bible in one year while gaining better understanding of the order of biblical events and historic context
- Features like quick navigation, bookmarks, and reading history to return to where you left off

The Bible App itself is noted as being "completely free, with no advertising and no in-app purchases".
