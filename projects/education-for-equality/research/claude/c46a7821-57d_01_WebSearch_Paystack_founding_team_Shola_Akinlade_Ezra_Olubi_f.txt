## Founding Team

Paystack was founded in 2015 by software engineers Shola Akinlade and Ezra Olubi. Both founders studied computer science at Babcock University in Nigeria, and they were old schoolmates.

## Background of Founders

Before founding Paystack, Akinlade started working at Heineken as a trainee for the company's database management and later worked as a software engineer with several banks. While working as a software engineer at banks and dealing with payment integrations, Akinlade recognized a massive gap in the Nigerian market, and together with his co-founder Ezra Olubi, who brought complementary technical skills and business acumen, they decided to build the solution Nigeria desperately needed.
