## Team Composition & Roles

Favor high compatibility over just recruiting MVPs. A recommended team mix includes 2 coders, 2 business-minded consultants, and 1 powerful speaker. The coders can focus on technical analysis and visuals, the business people can design simpler graphs while fulfilling the vision of answering business questions with insight and domain expertise, and the speaker can tackle continuous research, finalize the presentation, and compose the narrative the data tells.

## Focus on Insights Over Complexity

DataFest is like an idea tank, not a test of how many lines of code you can push out. This suggests that winning teams prioritize meaningful insights over technical complexity.

## Time Management Considerations

Someone will likely have to sacrifice half their day to clean the dataset. Plan accordingly for the necessary data preparation phase.

## Key Success Factors

The 48-hour time limit is thrilling and presents an extreme challenge for analytical ability, familiarity with data manipulation, and communication skills, with intensive immersion in teamwork.

## Presentation Strategy

After two days of intense data wrangling, analysis, and presentation design, each team is allowed a few minutes and no more than two slides to impress a panel of judges. This highlights the importance of concise, impactful presentations.

## Prize Categories

Prizes are given for Best in Show, Best Visualization, and Best Use of External Data. Understanding these categories can help teams tailor their approach accordingly.
