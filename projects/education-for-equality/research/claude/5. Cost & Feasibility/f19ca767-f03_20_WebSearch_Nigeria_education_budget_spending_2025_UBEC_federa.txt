## Overall Education Budget
Nigeria's education budget has jumped to ₦3.52 trillion in 2025 under President Bola Ahmed Tinubu's administration, which is a significant increase from ₦1.54 trillion in 2023. This amounts to 7.3 percent of the total budget.

## UBEC (Universal Basic Education Commission) Allocations
The Universal Basic Education Commission (UBEC) has distributed ₦92.4 billion in matching grants to 25 states and the Federal Capital Territory. However, there's also reference to a larger federal allocation: UBEC alone got N738 billion in the federal budget allocation.

Additionally, individual state UBE grants have increased from approximately ₦1.3 billion to over ₦3.3 billion, allowing states to access more than ₦6.6 billion through counterpart funding arrangements.

## Other Key Education Funding Agencies
The Tertiary Education Trust Fund (TETFUND) budget grew from ₦320.3 billion in 2023 to ₦683.4 billion in 2024, and now stands at ₦1.6 trillion in 2025.

The newly created Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND), established under the Student Loans Act of 2024, has already disbursed ₦86.3 billion to over 450,000 students in 218 tertiary institutions nationwide.
