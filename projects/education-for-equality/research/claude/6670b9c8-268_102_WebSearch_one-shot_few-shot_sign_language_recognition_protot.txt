## Key Findings:

**Siamese Networks for Few-Shot Learning:**
Siamese Neural Networks are used for few-shot learning, a setting where models must learn to classify with limited training samples per class, with the twin-network architecture demonstrating strong learning performance even in real-world scenarios where the number of images per class is extremely small.

A Siamese Network consists of two identical networks accepting different inputs with bound weights to ensure similar images are mapped close in the feature space. During training, it learns to differentiate between pairs of images that belong to the same class and those that belong to different classes. In the inference phase, a test image is compared with one image per novel class and a similarity score is computed.
