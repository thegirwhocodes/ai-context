# Signapse: AI Sign Language Translation Journey

## Founding Story

Signapse emerged from the convergence of personal experience, academic expertise, and technical innovation:

- **Sally Chalk (CEO)**: Former founder of Clarion Interpreting Limited (UK's largest BSL interpreting business), trained in social work and linguistics
- **Ben Saunders (CTO)**: Exposed to sign language through his mother; studied AI at University of Surrey
- **Richard Bowden (Chief Scientist)**: Developed finger-spelling recognition systems during PhD research

The company was "born during lockdown and officially launched in spring 2022" with a mission to revolutionize accessibility.

## Technical Evolution & Key Breakthroughs

**Core Innovation**: The team transitioned from academic research to commercial product by developing photorealistic digital signers rather than graphical avatars. They created "AI-driven video generation techniques" producing signers "indistinguishable from a human signer."

**Major Achievement**: Achieved unconstrained translation for British Sign Language (BSL), expanding beyond initial limitations.

**Academic Partnership**: Close collaboration with University of Surrey's Centre for Vision, Speech, and Signal Processing (CVSSP) drove research advancements.

## Photo-Realism Achievement

Signapse prioritizes "authenticity and inclusivity" by rejecting avatars in favor of lifelike representations that "preserve the nuances and cultural richness of the Deaf community."

## Challenges Overcome

- **Sally**: Navigated startup dynamics after leaving established SME; relearned fundamentals in team-building and fundraising
- **Ben**: Scaled AI-driven technologies from research into viable products; managed engineering optimization hurdles
- **Richard**: Bridged perception gaps between hearing and Deaf communities; educated both on technology-human interpreter symbiosis

## Current Capabilities

- Real-time BSL/ASL for broadcast and digital platforms
- SignStream API for integration
- SignStudio platform for content creation
- Transport solutions

Their vision extends toward expanding into "the global video translation market" while enhancing Deaf accessibility worldwide.
