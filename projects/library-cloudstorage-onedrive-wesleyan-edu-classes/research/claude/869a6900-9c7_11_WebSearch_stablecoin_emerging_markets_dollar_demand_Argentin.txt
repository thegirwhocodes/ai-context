### Turkey

### Nigeria
In Nigeria, importers use USDC to bypass banking delays, and 33% of Nigerians used stablecoins for payments or savings in 2024.

## Retail Savings and Store of Value

In emerging economies, stablecoins are often considered a store of value, protecting against local currency depreciation, inflation and inadequate banking infrastructure, and allow individuals and businesses in non-U.S. dollar economies to effectively hold a digital U.S. dollar account at a relatively low-cost.

## Market Growth

In September 2025, the total stablecoin market capitalization reached $300 billion, a 75% increase from a year earlier. The stablecoin market has expanded dramatically, more than doubling from under $120 billion in early 2023 to over $215 billion in early 2025.
