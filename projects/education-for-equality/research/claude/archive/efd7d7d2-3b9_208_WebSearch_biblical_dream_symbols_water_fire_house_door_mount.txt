## Water
Water is a potent symbol in the Bible, often representing life, purification, and the presence of the Holy Spirit. More specifically, clean water is a means of sanctification and healing through the Holy Spirit, while dirty water means there is one area of your life that sin is destroying.

## Fire
Fire in the Bible can symbolize both God's presence and purification. The burning bush in Exodus 3:2-4 is a manifestation of God's presence, while the refining fire in Malachi 3:2-3 represents purification and judgment.

## House, Door & Mountains
If a door is locked, it indicates a closed opportunity or a hindrance to your progress. An opened door is an open opportunity, or the opening you should travel. Mountains represent a place of prayer, Heaven, refuge, place of meeting with God (positive), or impossibility or obstacles (negative).

## Colors
Colors carry significant symbolism. Blue could symbolize having a heavenly visitation from the Lord and the dreamer may receive a divine revelation, being chastened by the Father, or water to cleanse or drink freely by the spirit. Amber represents fire, God's glory, the brilliance of His presence and His judgments, and may indicate the dreamer is receiving a visitation from the Lord.

## Numbers & Interpretation Method
To decipher the meaning of objects and symbols in the Bible, a principle of biblical interpretation called the "law of first mention" can be used. It states that the first mention of an object, number, color or symbol in the Bible sets up a pattern of interpretation for it that holds true throughout the rest of Scripture.
