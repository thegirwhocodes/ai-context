# Voice Testing in Africa's Talking Sandbox

Yes, voice testing is included in the sandbox. According to the documentation, you can "create a free 2-way SMS short codes, USSD channels, Payments channels, **Virtual Voice numbers**, Airtime Distribution resources."

## How to Test Voice Calls

To set up voice testing:

1. **Create a virtual voice number** using the [designated creation link](https://account.africastalking.com/apps/sandbox/voice/phones/create)
2. **Configure the webhook/callback** at the [callback configuration page](https://account.africastalking.com/apps/sandbox/voice/phones)
3. **Use the simulator** at https://simulator.africastalking.com:1517/ for all testing—not an actual handset

The resource emphasizes that "interactions with these resources occur in the **SIMULATOR** and **NOT the HANDSET** for all development and tests on the sandbox."
