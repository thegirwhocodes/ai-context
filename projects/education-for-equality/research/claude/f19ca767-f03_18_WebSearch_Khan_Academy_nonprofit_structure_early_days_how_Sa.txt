## 501(c)(3) Incorporation

The organization was incorporated as a 501c(3) nonprofit in 2008. Public response, including coverage in USA Today, led Khan to incorporate Khan Academy as a nonprofit organization in 2008 and leave his job in finance to work on the project full time.

## Early Days and Motivation

Sal founded Khan Academy as a nonprofit in order to give employees and partners the focus and staying power to have a long-term impact on education across the globe. 

Before the official incorporation, he decided to begin recording videos and posting them on YouTube in 2006 so everyone could watch at their own pace. Sal continued to work on Khan Academy during his spare time until the fall of 2009, when he quit his hedge fund job and decided to pursue the endeavor full-time.

## Early Funding

He lived off of his savings for the first nine months, until he received his first significant donation from Ann Doerr. In September 2010, Khan Academy received large grants from Google ($2 million) and the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation ($1.5 million) and began to build out an organization.
