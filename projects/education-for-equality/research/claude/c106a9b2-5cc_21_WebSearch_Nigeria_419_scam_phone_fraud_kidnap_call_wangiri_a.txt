## Wangiri Scam in Nigeria

Awareness is necessary about wangiri scam, a phone fraud currently sweeping across Nigeria. "Wangiri" is a Japanese word meaning "one ring and drop", and a one-ring scam is a telephone scam where fraudsters trick phone users into calling premium rate numbers.

A person finds a missed call at odd hours of the day from an international number and is tempted to call back (social engineering). It is difficult to block the "strange" wangiri calls as the numbers keeps changing for each missed call.

The only way to protect yourself from this form of social engineering fraud is to stay away from a missed call from a strange international number at midnight or during working hours. Do not call back! A legitimate caller will call back later or send an SMS.

## 419 Scams and Kidnapping

The number "419" refers to the section of the Nigerian Criminal Code dealing with fraud. Sometimes victims are ransomed, kidnapped, or murdered. According to a 1995 U.S. State Department report, over fifteen persons were murdered between 1992 and 1995 in Nigeria after following through on advance-fee frauds.
