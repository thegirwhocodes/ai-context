## Khan Academy 4th Grade Math Curriculum Overview

Khan Academy offers a comprehensive 4th grade math course consisting of 14 units covering various topics such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, and geometry. The platform is free to use and provides tools for learners, teachers, and parents to track progress.

### Main Topic Categories (from the 4th Grade U.S. Math Mission)

The curriculum includes these main sections:
- Mission Foundations
- Addition and subtraction
- Multiplication and division
- Fractions
- Decimals
- Measurement and data
- Geometry
- Factors, multiples and patterns
- Place value and rounding

This mission consists of continued practice of operations on different types of numbers, introduction of remainder when dividing, relationships between decimals and fractions, practice with applications and word problems and continued expansion of basic geometric and statistical concepts.

### Eureka Math/EngageNY Aligned Modules

Khan Academy also offers content aligned to Eureka Math/EngageNY with these modules:
- Module 1: Place value, rounding, and algorithms for addition and subtraction
- Module 2: Unit conversions and problem solving with metric measurement
- Module 3: Multi-digit multiplication and division
- Module 4: Angle measure and plane figures
- Module 5: Fraction equivalence, ordering, and operations
- Module 6: Decimal fractions
- Module 7: Exploring measurement with multiplication

### Available 4th Grade Math Courses

Khan Academy offers several versions of 4th grade math aligned to different state standards:
- 4th grade math (Common Core aligned)
- 4th grade math (FL B.E.S.T.) - Florida standards
- 4th grade math (TX TEKS) - Texas standards
- 4th grade (Eureka Math/EngageNY)
- 4th grade math (Illustrative Math-aligned)
- Get ready for 4th grade math (preparation course)

For the most complete and current list of all topics and videos, I recommend visiting the Khan Academy website directly at **https://www.khanacademy.org/math/cc-fourth-grade-math** where you can browse through all 14 units with their associated lessons and videos.
