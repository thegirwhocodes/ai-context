## The hard truth first

**No international body will badge a curriculum you wrote yourself as "Cambridge-accredited curriculum" — or any equivalent.** Your May 2026 finding holds in 2026. The reason is structural: the prestigious names **accredit whole schools/institutions, not standalone third-party curricula.**

- **Cambridge International** — registers *schools* (4-phase, ~12 weeks). Its only third-party route, "Endorsed resources," is **for recognised publishers only**, and only endorses textbooks that teach *Cambridge's own syllabus*. It explicitly refuses to endorse independent/proprietary curricula. → For a self-authored curriculum, **"aligned to Cambridge standards" is the only honest claim.** Not "accredited."
- **Cognia** (ex-AdvancED/SACS) — official policy: *"Cognia does not accredit providers of curriculum only."* Seal is for institutions.
- **CIS** (Council of International Schools) — *"accreditation is not offered to a part of a school"*; requires membership + 2 full academic years operating.
- **IB / WASC / COBIS / Oxford AQA / Ofqual / EdReports** — none surfaced a verified self-authored-curriculum pathway (school- or qualification-scoped, or US-K12-only).

## What you *can* legitimately display

Three real, attainable seals — but note carefully **what each one actually certifies** (none certify "curriculum content"):

| Seal | What it actually certifies | Cost / time | Attainable now? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EduEvidence / ICEIE** (eduevidence.org) | Evidence-of-**impact of the tech *product*** (Sabi or the web app) against a 5Es / ESSA-style framework. Bronze/Silver/Gold. *"Badge is for product, not provider."* Awardees incl. Kahoot!, Age of Learning, GraphoGame | Free registration + **~$250**, **5–7 business days**, valid 24 mo | ✅ **Best near-term fit** |
| **NCFE** (UK Ofqual-recognised awarding body, 175+ yrs) | Genuine awarding-body recognition of **your own self-authored content** — via "Customised Qualifications" or "Endorsed Programmes" | Fees not published in dataset; requires dev/QA work | ✅ Viable, more effort |
