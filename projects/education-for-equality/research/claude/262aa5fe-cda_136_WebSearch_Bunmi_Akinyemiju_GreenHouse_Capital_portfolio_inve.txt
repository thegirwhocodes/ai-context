## Investment Portfolio and Focus

Through GreenHouse Capital, Bunmi Akinyemiju has invested in over 25 innovative technology companies in Africa, including Flutterwave, Wallets Africa, Helium Health, and Resource. GreenHouse Capital is Africa's largest fintech fund by portfolio size and has invested in leading companies like Flutterwave, Max, and Pezesha.

## Core Investment Philosophy

Bunmi's philosophy, "founders backing founders," has guided GHC's success, and beyond investment, the firm actively nurtures startups through strategic partnerships and ecosystem support.

## Recent Strategic Direction (2025-2026)

In 2025, GreenHouse Capital is taking a notably different approach than traditional Silicon Valley models. Akinyemiju argues "It's time we stop applying Silicon Valley clocks to Sokoto roads," stating "That model was imported. It doesn't reflect how things work here."

Greenhouse's first fund was an evergreen structure, its second blends debt and equity, and the firm is testing catalytic capital, sovereign-backed venture studios, and joint ventures in Uganda—none of them designed for fast flips but all designed for patient, compound returns.

The firm has made Uganda one of its most active markets in the last four years, writing early-stage cheques of $250,000 to $500,000 into Ugandan startups—seven of them so far. In Uganda, the firm sees a chance to prove that African venture capital funding can build long-term value in manufacturing and help align policies and public institutions, with Akinyemiju describing it as a deliberate, thesis-driven experiment to create conditions for innovation to root and endure.
