# Stephanie Ike Okafor & The Power of Your Dreams

## Author Bio
Stephanie Ike Okafor is an author and speaker based in California. She maintains active social media presence across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok, engaging with audiences about her work and messaging.

## Book Overview
**The Power of Your Dreams** is a bestselling title that achieved recognition on USA Today, Publishers Weekly, and ECPA bestseller lists. The work explores divine communication through dreams, offering readers a framework for understanding God's voice during sleep.

## Core Themes & Content

**Central Premise:**
The book proposes that gaining "years of wisdom, guidance, and strategy while you were asleep" becomes possible through understanding dreams as a channel for God's communication.

**Key Questions Addressed:**
- Are all dreams from God?
- How to identify messages from God in dreams?
- Dream interpretation methods
- Meaning and significance of nightmares

**Theological Framework:**
Okafor grounds her teaching in biblical foundations, drawing from scriptural accounts of God communicating with people through dreams in ancient times and arguing this practice continues today.

**Teaching Approach:**
The work emphasizes recognizing and responding to divine voice within dreams, positioning this practice as enabling spiritual clarity, confidence in personal purpose, and strengthened relationship with God.
