## Incorporated Trustee Registration Fees

The incorporation of trustees costs ₦30,000.00, which is the main registration fee.

However, when registering an Incorporated Trustee (for organizations like NGOs, churches, and associations), there are additional costs beyond just the CAC fee:

1. **Name Reservation**: ₦500
2. **Incorporation Fee**: ₦30,000
3. **Newspaper Publication**: Between ₦40,000 and ₦60,000 for publishing notice in two national newspapers

Altogether, you should budget between ₦70,500 and ₦90,500 for this process.

Additionally, you may need Certified True Copies (CTCs) of documents when dealing with banks or tender processes, each CTC costs ₦6,500.
