## OneDrive Integration Setup

Tactiq allows you to connect your OneDrive account so Tactiq can save your transcripts and AI outputs directly to your storage. After connecting OneDrive, you can control how transcripts are saved by opening the OneDrive settings under Integration and turning on "Automatically save after meeting."

## How It Works

Tactiq saves meeting transcripts directly to OneDrive as Word files, and OneDrive handles version tracking as notes are updated, summaries are added, or follow-ups are written. This keeps meeting records accurate over time without manual downloads, duplicate files, or copy-paste work.

## Exporting Transcripts

To download a transcript, go to the transcript page and choose one of the download options at the top. You can export your transcript as either a PDF or a text file. Both formats include the meeting name, date, duration, speaker names, highlights, and the full transcript.

Tactiq also supports using Workflows with OneDrive to automatically send transcripts and AI outputs from your workflows to OneDrive.
