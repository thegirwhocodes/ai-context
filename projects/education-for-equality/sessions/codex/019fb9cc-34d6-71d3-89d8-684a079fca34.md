# How to film it beautifully

- Main video: **4K 30fps, horizontal 16:9**.
- Slow-motion B-roll: **4K 60fps**.
- Use the 1× lens for most shots. Use 0.5× only for one or two establishing shots because it can make the furniture look distorted.
- Lock focus, exposure and white balance so the room does not change brightness mid-sentence.
- Film in late-afternoon indirect daylight. Your windows are bright enough to blow out, so partially close the curtains if necessary.
- Turn off the overhead dorm light. Use the window plus your warm floor lamps.
- For speaking shots, position yourself at a 45-degree angle to the window—not directly in front of it.
- Put the camera on a tripod for dialogue. Use handheld stabilization only for B-roll.
- Record two versions of every section: one relaxed and one 20% shorter/more energetic.

For audio:

- Use a wireless/lavalier mic for walking dialogue.
- If you only have a desk microphone or Q2U, record the introduction, desk section, outro and voiceover with it.
- A second phone running Voice Memos under your shirt can be backup audio.
- Turn off the humidifier, fan and AC during dialogue.
- Clap once at the start to synchronize audio.
- Record 30 seconds of silent room tone.

# Edit plan

Editing style:

- Cold-open shot length: 1–2.5 seconds.
- Main-tour shot length: 3–6 seconds.
- Remove every repeated explanation and all footage of you silently repositioning.
- Use your **Lifestyle Bright** or **Base Warm** look gently—bright cream, accurate skin, warm gold, no heavy orange cast.
- Use Poppins for small on-screen labels and Montserrat for major section text.
- Add minimal labels such as “the prayer wall,” “tiny founder HQ,” and “the robe.”
- Use clean cuts, two or three smooth whip transitions, and one mirror transition.
- Put light instrumental music beneath dialogue.
- Let the guitar section breathe and use a few seconds of your original worship music.
- Aim around **–14 LUFS integrated**, with peaks below –1 dBTP.
- Upload proper closed captions, but avoid constant giant word-by-word captions.
