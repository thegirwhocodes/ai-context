## Overview

Long Lane Farm is Wesleyan's student-run and collectively organized organic farm, operating year-round at 243 Long Lane in Middletown, Connecticut. The farm's mission is to be "a collectively-run organic farm that employs ecological practices to provide the Middletown and Wesleyan community with good, clean food" while striving "to collapse the boundary between work and play, between productivity and creativity, through applied practice and experiential agricultural learning."

## Community Engagement & Food Sharing

The farm provides a place for people from all sectors of the community to work, eat, talk, and enjoy the beautiful farm environment together at Community Work-Days, pot-lucks at the farm, and educational programs for local elementary school students during the summer.

The produce grown on Long Lane is sold at the Holden Farmer's Market, donated to Amazing Grace Food Pantry, or served to students in Usdan.

## Cooking & Community Activities

Students took turns cooking for each other and maintaining shared apartments on Lincoln Street. Additionally, the farm ran the Middletown Food Project, a Community-Supported Agriculture (CSA) program that engaged six low-income families, with cooking seminars offered to adults along with free produce.

The farm has a stand at the Middletown Farmer's Market, sells produce to Bon Appetit, and hosts community events and programs such as PumpkinFest and May Day.
