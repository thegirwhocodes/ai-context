## Edge Computing for Smart Glasses

Wearable technologies like AI-augmented smart glasses impose stringent requirements on energy efficiency. Operating at 18 frames per second while consuming less than 100 mW, such systems support real-time face and object recognition entirely on local devices through compressed and quantized CNNs, enabling extended battery life and always-on vision.

## Latency and Performance

Millisecond-level latency is critical for the stability of virtual imagery, and edge computing eliminates the transmission delays of cloud processing. AI vision and voice-processing chips serve as edge computing engines, allowing glasses to perform object recognition, voice interaction, and scene modeling within millisecond-level latency.

Meta's approach involves developing methods to reduce user-perceived latency by starting to understand and process user requests before they finish speaking, enabling speculative operations to occur in parallel with speech recognition and significantly reducing response times.

## Battery Life Challenges
