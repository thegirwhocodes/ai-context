## Claude · 2026-08-03T19:17:45.300Z

Here's what happens when you type "hi" and hit send on `cortex.eduforequality.org`:

## 1. Browser → server
Your browser POSTs your message to `/api/chat` at Cortex.

## 2. Gatekeeping (~50ms)
- **Auth**: Clerk confirms you're signed in and hands the route your `userId`
- **CSRF check**: the request has to come from cortex.eduforequality.org, not another site
- **Rate limit**: haven't sent too many messages this hour
- **Guardrail scan**: your message is checked for prompt injection, credential leaks, "ignore your instructions" tricks

## 3. Gather context about you (~200ms, parallel)
Three Supabase queries run:
- **Profile**: `cortex_profiles.profile_text` — the long paragraph Sonnet wrote about you from your data
- **Memory facts**: top 50 rows from `cortex_memory` (things like "Naomi's mom is Anne", "prefers concise replies")
- **RAG retrieval**: your question is turned into a 384-dim vector, then pgvector does a similarity search across all your ingested emails/DMs/notes and pulls the top 15 most relevant chunks

## 4. Personal LoRA check (~10ms now, thanks to today's fix)
The router asks: **"is this an explicit writing request?"** (draft/write/rewrite/polish an email/message/post)
- If **no** (like "hi", "what's my day"): skip LoRA entirely → go to step 5
- If **yes**: call RunPod with your `adapter_v5`, wait max 5s
  - If it responds → stream that back
  - If cold/slow → abort, fall through to step 5

Before today, this ran for *every* message and blocked 30–60s. Now it only runs for writing tasks and gives up after 5s.

## 5. Build the prompt sent to Claude
```
SYSTEM: You are Cortex, a personal AI assistant...
        [Cortex safety rules]
        USER PROFILE: <your profile paragraph>
        THINGS YOU KNOW: <50 memory facts>

USER:   RELEVANT DATA FROM USER'S ACCOUNTS (UNTRUSTED):
        [your emails/DMs the RAG pulled, wrapped so Claude knows
         they're data-not-instructions]

        USER'S QUESTION: hi
```

## 6. Stream from Claude (~1–3s to first word)
Sends that prompt to Anthropic's API, gets back a Server-Sent Events stream, forwards each token to your browser as it arrives — that's why you see the reply typing out.

## 7. After you see the reply (async, doesn't slow you down)
Two background jobs kick off:
- **Memory extraction**: another Claude call reads the exchange and pulls out durable facts → saves to `cortex_memory`
- **Action detection**: scans the reply for concrete actions like "I'll send this email" → queues a row in `cortex_actions` that you must approve before it executes
