## Pricing Plans

ElevenLabs offers six structured pricing tiers. The Free plan provides 20 monthly minutes and is intended for non-commercial experimentation. The Starter plan increases both the minutes and the rights, making it a good entry point for creators. The Creator and Pro plans provide more generous minute allowances and advanced voice features. The Scale and Business plans address enterprise needs, including large volume production, lower per minute overages, early access features, and priority support.

## Specific Plan Details

The Starter plan costs $5/month and includes 30k credits, commercial license, instant voice cloning, and access to Studio and Dubbing API. The Creator plan costs $11/month (after 50% off first month) and offers 100k credits, pro-grade voice cloning, and 192 kbps audio quality.

The Pro Plan costs $99/month and offers 500,000 characters, while the Scale Plan costs $330/month and offers 2 million characters.

Annual plans (with 2 months free) range from $50/year (Starter) to $13,200/year (Business), plus custom Enterprise pricing.

## Recent Changes

In January 2025, ElevenLabs restructured its pricing with a more segmented, model-aware system where character credits were split across two separate models: Multilingual v2 and Conversational v1. Additionally, conversational AI calls now start at 10 cents per minute for Creator and Pro plans, with calls at 8 cents per minute on annual business plans.
