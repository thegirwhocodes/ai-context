**Publication Details:**

The paper was published in 2020 in the American Economic Review, Volume 110(4), pages 1104–44.

**Core Findings:**

The paper examines long-run growth as a product of the effective number of researchers and their research productivity, presenting evidence showing that research effort is rising substantially while research productivity is declining sharply. A key example is Moore's Law, where the number of researchers required today to achieve the famous doubling of computer chip density is more than 18 times larger than the number required in the early 1970s.
