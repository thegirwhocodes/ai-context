## Key Findings

**Language Preference Among Children:**
A significant trend exists in Nigeria whereby children of Yoruba-speaking parents acquire English as their First Language and Yoruba as their second language. Research shows that English was the preferred language used by children in eight out of the twelve domains of language use.

**Decline in Mother Tongue Use at Home:**
For many middle-class Nigerians, especially in the south, English is now their mother tongue and some may not speak any local languages, partly as a result of marriages between members of different ethnic groups, and people moving to cities, where English is the lingua franca. In Lagos specifically, some children speak English at home, with "just seems easier" to speak English at home because that is the language the children are taught in at school.

**Educational Context:**

**Impact of Language Instruction:**
