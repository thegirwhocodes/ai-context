# Foundational Numeracy Course Overview

## Course Structure
This comprehensive mathematics program from Education for Equality targets ages 8-14, comprising **6 units with 100 total lessons** spanning approximately 1,210 minutes of instruction.

## Learning Pathway

**Unit 1: Counting & Number Sense** (15 lessons)
Develops foundational skills using Nigerian market contexts. Learners progress from counting 1-10 with groundnuts to understanding place value with hundreds, comparing, and ordering numbers.

**Unit 2: Addition** (18 lessons)
Builds from concrete object manipulation through abstract problem-solving. Topics include adding with carrying, mental strategies like "making 10," and real-world applications involving Naira currency.

**Unit 3: Subtraction** (18 lessons)
Mirrors addition's progression but focuses on take-away and making change scenarios. Includes borrowing techniques and word problems about market transactions.

**Unit 4: Multiplication** (18 lessons)
Introduces repeated addition and equal groups before advancing to times tables (2s, 3s, 4s, 5s, 10s) and multi-digit multiplication with practical shopping contexts.

**Unit 5: Division** (15 lessons)
Teaches fair sharing and grouping principles, progressing to division facts and problems involving remainders and real money distribution.

**Unit 6: Word Problems—Mixed Operations** (16 lessons)
Integrates all four operations through authentic scenarios like "Market Day: Shopping with a Budget" and multi-step challenges.

## Pedagogical Approach
Lessons consistently anchor mathematics to Nigerian cultural contexts—groundnuts, Naira notes, market stalls, and transport costs—making abstract concepts tangible and relevant.
