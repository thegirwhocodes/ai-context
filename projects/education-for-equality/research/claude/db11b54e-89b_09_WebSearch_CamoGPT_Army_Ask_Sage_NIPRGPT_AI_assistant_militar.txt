## CamoGPT

CamoGPT started development in the fall of 2023 and was first deployed in the spring of 2024, and currently boasts 75,000 users. CamoGPT is a machine learning platform that optimizes equipment maintenance, logistics, and supply chain management using data analytics and algorithms.

The Army has no plans to deactivate CamoGPT or cut off personnel's access to other preexisting, purpose-built frontier AI models, despite the Pentagon's broader GenAI rollout. CamoGPT's team is working to transition it into a program of record and develop an Army-specific LLM that would perform better at recognizing Army acronyms and providing recommendations founded in Army doctrine.

## NIPRGPT

NIPRGPT is an AI platform created by Collen Roller, a senior computer scientist at the Air Force Research Laboratory. NIPRGPT is a natural language processing tool that analyzes and generates text to support planning and operations using a GPT model.

However, the Air Force announced plans to phase out NIPRGPT. The Army blocked NIPRGPT from its networks, citing cybersecurity and data governance concerns, with the block occurring on April 17.

## Ask Sage

After the NIPRGPT block, the Army deployed its Army Enterprise LLM Workspace, a platform powered by Ask Sage, which bills itself as a secure and extensible Generative AI platform. The Army Enterprise LLM Workspace is used for tasks ranging from drafting press releases to reclassifying personnel descriptions.
