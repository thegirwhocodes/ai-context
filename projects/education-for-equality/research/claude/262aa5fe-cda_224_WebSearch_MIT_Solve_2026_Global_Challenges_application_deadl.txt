## Application Deadline

The submission deadline for MIT Solve's 10th Anniversary Global Challenge is May 21, 2026 at 12:00pm EDT.

## About the Challenge

MIT Solve was founded on the idea that innovative solutions to global challenges already exist and just need the right support to scale. Over the past decade, the initiative has launched more than 100 challenges and supported innovators who have collectively reached over 370 million people and raised more than $1.1 billion.

## Prize and Support

The ten winners will get a big stage and serious support. They'll be featured at the Solve Challenge Finals in New York City during UN General Assembly and NY Climate Week in September 2026, with travel costs covered. Each winner will receive at least $100,000 in unrestricted prize funding.

Winners will gain access to MIT's innovation ecosystem, including specialized mentorship, pro-bono legal support, discounted access to technology, and connections to the network of past Solver teams.

## Eligibility

The challenge is open to applicants worldwide, including nonprofits, startups, and social enterprises, with solutions that are already operating and demonstrate potential to scale.
