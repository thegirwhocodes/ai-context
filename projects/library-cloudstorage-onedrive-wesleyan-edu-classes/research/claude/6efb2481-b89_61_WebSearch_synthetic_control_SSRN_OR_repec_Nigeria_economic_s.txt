1. **Synthetic Control Method Overview**: The Synthetic Control Method has become a widely used tool in estimating the causal impact of policies, shocks and interventions of interest on economic and social outcomes. The SCM is a data-driven design that provides a systematic way of constructing a comparison group that looks very similar to the group implementing the policy, allowing researchers to estimate policy impacts by comparing post-policy outcomes between policy and comparison groups.
