## Manual Voice Memos to OneDrive Transfer

You can manually transfer Voice Memos to OneDrive by opening the Voice Memos app, navigating to a recording, selecting "Save to Files," choosing OneDrive from the Files app, and selecting a destination folder. You can upload multiple Voice Memos at once by selecting them in the Voice Memos app before tapping "Share."

## Shortcuts and Voice Memos Limitations

There are some important limitations with automating Voice Memos through Shortcuts. While Shortcuts can interact with Voice Memos to jump into recordings or make new ones, there's limited access to retrieve Voice Memos with Shortcuts or via iCloud Drive. Saving a Voice Memo directly via Shortcuts doesn't appear to be possible - you can do it manually, but cannot complete the save steps as part of a bigger automation.

## OneDrive and Shortcuts Integration

Interestingly, when creating a shortcut that saves a file to the cloud, "service" options appear for iCloud and Dropbox, but there may not be a dedicated service option for OneDrive. However, you can see your OneDrive files and folders inside the iOS Files app, which is important for working with Shortcuts.

The most practical current approach would be to manually share Voice Memos to OneDrive using the Files app integration, rather than trying to fully automate the process through Shortcuts.
