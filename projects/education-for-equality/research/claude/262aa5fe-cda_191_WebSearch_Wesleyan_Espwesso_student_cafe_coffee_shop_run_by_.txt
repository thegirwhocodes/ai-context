Espwesso is an entirely student run, sustainability-focused specialty coffee shop on the Wesleyan campus located in the basement of the Allbritton Center.

Founded by a dedicated group of students in 2011, it is a self-sustaining, student-run café offering the only artisan coffee available on campus. By providing affordable specialty drip coffee and espresso drinks, it seeks to educate the campus and surrounding Middletown community about socially and environmentally sustainable coffee practices and raise the bar for coffee quality at Wesleyan.

Regarding sourcing, their beans come from New Harvest Coffee Roasters in Pawtucket, Rhode Island, whose Source Direct model for coffee trading means they establish relationships with coffee farmers in Central and South America, Africa and East Asia. Their milk comes from Wildowsky Dairy in Lisbon, CT (about 45 minutes away from campus).

The café emphasizes sustainability in multiple ways. The sustainable-minded café provides free drip coffee to customers who bring their own mugs and the staff uses rags instead of disposable towels or napkins.
