## Folashade (Folasade) Adefisayo

**Full title:** Hon. Mrs. Folasade Adefisayo (referred to as "Honourable" and "Mrs." across official sources)

**Commissioner of Education, Lagos State:** August 2019 -- May 2023 (approximately 4 years, serving Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu's first term)

**Total career experience:** Over 40 years spanning banking operations, organizational restructuring, human resource management, international trade, and education.

**Education:**
- B.Sc. Zoology, University of Ibadan (University Scholar)
- MBA, Lagos State University
- iPGCE and MA Education, University of Nottingham (Distinctions in both)

**Key career roles:**
- Executive Director and CEO, Corona Schools' Trust Council (8 years), then Director of Corona Secondary School, Agbara -- retired 2014 after 12 years total at Corona
- Principal Consultant/CEO, Leading Learning Limited (educational consultancy, founded 2014) -- still active post-commissioner role
- Board member, African Leadership Academy
- Board member, Teach For Nigeria
- Board of Trustees member, Kizazi
- Editorial Board member, EduTimes Africa
- Merit Award winner, Federal Republic of Nigeria
- Nonresident Fellow, Brookings Institution (Center for Universal Education)

**EKOEXCEL connection -- direct and central:**
- As Commissioner, she oversaw the launch and expansion of EKOEXCEL (Excellence in Child Education and Learning), Governor Sanwo-Olu's flagship public primary school reform program launched in 2019
- EKOEXCEL is implemented through LASUBEB (Lagos State Universal Basic Education Board) with technical support from NewGlobe
- She inaugurated the EKOEXCEL "Situation Room" -- a real-time data monitoring center tracking pupil attendance, learning outcomes, and teacher delivery across Lagos public primary schools
- She showcased EKOEXCEL at international forums including the British Council and Brookings Institution
- Under her tenure, Lagos WAEC pass rate surged from 39% to 80%, and EKOEXCEL schools showed 3x faster literacy gains and 2x faster numeracy gains compared to non-program schools
