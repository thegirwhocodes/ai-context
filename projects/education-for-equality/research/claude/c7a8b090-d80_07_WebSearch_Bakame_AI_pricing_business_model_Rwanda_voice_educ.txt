## Pricing and Cost

Bakame AI offers no cost to users. More specifically, if purchased by the Rwandan government, Rwandan taxpayers enjoy Bakame AI for free. This is a key aspect of the business model—the service is funded at the government level rather than through user fees.

## Business Model

Bakame AI is an AI product that teaches students English on the telephone. The model is designed to be highly accessible: users get the Bakame AI phone number, dial the toll-free number, and converse on the phone with an AI-driven chatbot.

The business model targets accessibility in Rwanda where 97% of Rwandans have phones, while only 34.2% have access to internet. 

To reach global scale, the founder hopes neighboring countries acquire Bakame AI for their taxpayers, suggesting the business model is based on government partnerships rather than direct user payments.
