## Call Forwarding and Virtual Numbers

Africa's Talking Voice API allows you to direct incoming and outgoing calls to a virtual phone provider or your company's private network. You can send a caller to another phone number or agent when needed, such as for wrong recipients or transfers to different departments.

## Virtual Number Functionality

To build applications with the service, you need a Virtual-number, which you can request through your dashboard - this will be the number that people call. When someone calls your virtual-number, the API sends a POST request to your callback with parameters such as the caller's phone number and a session-id.

## Toll-Free Numbers

When a person dials your virtual number, they are charged normal call rates for calling unless you have a toll-free number. Toll Free numbers are where the user is not charged but you as a client gets billed for incoming calls.
