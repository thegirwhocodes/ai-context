## Faculty and Staff Meal Program with Students

Each faculty and staff member receives nine (9) guest meal passes on their WesID card that can be used to provide lunch for up to three students per meal period in the DFC (Daniel Family Commons), the Usdan Marketplace, or the Food Truck. This program is designed to encourage informal, yet meaningful, interactions with colleagues and students while sharing a meal.

## Department Reservations and Dinners

The DFC (Daniel Family Commons) is a dedicated dining space for faculty and staff to connect with colleagues and students, and reservations can be made by emailing dfcreservations@wesleyan.edu. There is a limit of one table per day per department.

Faculty/Staff must be present and arrive with their guests, and should inform the cashier they are using guest swipes. For large groups of 4+ students, the DFC is limited to 3 student guests per host.

## Off-Campus Events

For larger departmental dinner receptions, Wesleyan's on-campus caterer (Bon Appétit) simplifies billing (direct to SmartKey) and logistics (set-up/clean-up included) once you confirm your room reservation first via EMS and contact Bon Appétit to design your menu.
