## Overview

Sorenson OmniBridge is the first scalable sign language translation Software Development Kit (SDK) that enables fast, real-time communication between Deaf and hearing people directly within your own application.

## Technical Architecture and How It Works

With Sorenson OmniBridge on an AI PC, you can embed instant two-way conversations between American Sign Language (ASL) and English into your software.

The bidirectional workflow operates as follows:

- **ASL to English**: The Deaf person signs into a camera, and OmniBridge uses its database of ASL signs to understand the statement, then translates the ASL to English for the hearing person to read.

- **English to ASL**: The hearing person speaks into a mic, and OmniBridge captions their speech for the Deaf person to read.

## Key Technical Features

Sorenson OmniBridge runs on an AI PC — a computer designed for AI workloads — without an internet connection, so no need to worry about lag or connectivity, and maintains control and preserves privacy with no data transfer.

OmniBridge uses computer vision to capture and translate sign language into text or speech, making it suitable for real-time interactive applications.
