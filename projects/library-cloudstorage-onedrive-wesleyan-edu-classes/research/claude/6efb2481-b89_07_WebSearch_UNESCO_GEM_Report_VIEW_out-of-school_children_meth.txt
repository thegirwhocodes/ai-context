## Bayesian Model Methodology

The GEM Report team has developed a Bayesian hierarchical model that constructs underlying out-of-school rate curves for cohorts over time, with curves smoothed over time such that changes in cohort behavior is meaningful but gradual.

## Data Sources

The data used to estimate out-of-school rates separates into two broad categories: administrative enrollment counts provided by the UNESCO Institute of Statistics (UIS), which collects single-age enrollment counts from Ministries of Education combined with population estimates from the UN World Population Prospects, Eurostat, and SingStat; and survey-based data including Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS), Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys (MICS), country-specific household surveys, and population censuses.

## Model Output and Aggregation

The model produces out-of-school rates for country-age-years, and after extracting these values, age-specific out-of-school rates are aggregated by level using population estimates, with additional aggregations by region, income, and other groups, then multiplied by corresponding population estimates to produce estimates of the number of out-of-school children.

## Technical Background

The developed model is a Bayesian hierarchical model inspired by the approach used to estimate health indicators but adapted to the education context. A technical background paper is available for download from the GEM Report VIEW data section.
