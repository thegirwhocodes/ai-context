## FDIC Pass-Through Insurance Prohibition

FDIC Chair Travis Hill said in a speech that the agency will move to codify stablecoins as ineligible for deposit insurance — which is required under the GENIUS Act — and that the prohibition likely will include pass-through deposit insurance arrangements.

Pass-through insurance is a mechanism by which customer funds deposited at a bank through a third party are insured, and frequently is used by fintechs and their partner banks.

## Rationale for the Prohibition

Hill stated: "It seems hard to rationalize the GENIUS Act's firm prohibition on marketing stablecoins as subject to deposit insurance if stablecoins were intended to serve as an access mechanism for FDIC-insured deposit accounts."

Additionally, if stablecoin arrangements were eligible for pass-through insurance, it could make payment stablecoins more closely compete with bank deposits and could further increase the FDIC's Deposit Insurance Fund's exposure to stablecoin market forces.

## Broader Concerns

Banks' increased exposure to stablecoins could substantially change the "nature and distribution" of deposits, a major concern for the nation's primary deposit insurer.
