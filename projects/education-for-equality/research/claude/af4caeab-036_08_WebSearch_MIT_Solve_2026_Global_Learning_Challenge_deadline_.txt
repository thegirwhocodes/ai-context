## MIT Solve 10th Anniversary Global Challenge 2026

The submission deadline is May 21, 2026 at 12:00 PM EDT.

### About the Challenge

MIT Solve is seeking 10 exceptional solutions built for the next decade: solving urgent problems and proving credible pathways to scale, whether through markets, policy, procurement, or platforms.

Participants must submit solutions that address key global areas such as climate change, health systems, learning and education, economic opportunity, and community development.

### Eligibility

Anyone, anywhere around the world aged 13 and older can submit a solution to Solve's Challenges. You can be an individual, a team, or an organization.

### Prizes and Support

The ten winners will be featured at the Solve Challenge Finals in New York City during UN General Assembly and NY Climate Week in September 2026, with travel costs covered. Each winner will receive at least $100,000 in unrestricted prize funding.

Winners will gain access to MIT's innovation ecosystem, including specialized mentorship, pro-bono legal support, discounted access to technology, and connections to the network of past Solver teams. Solve supports its portfolio for the long-term, with more funding opportunities, skills training, media exposure, and speaking engagements on an ongoing basis.

For more information, you can visit the official MIT Solve website at solve.mit.edu.
