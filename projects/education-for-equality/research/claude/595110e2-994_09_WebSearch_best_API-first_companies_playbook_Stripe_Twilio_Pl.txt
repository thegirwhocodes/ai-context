## Key Success Factors

**1. Laser-Focused Problem Solving**

API-first companies focus on solving a very specific problem. Stripe started with payments, Twilio started with messaging and calling, Plaid does bank data, and Algolia does search. This focus means that everything the company does is oriented towards solving all of the problems related to that particular area.

**2. Developer-First Mindset**

A huge part of their success has been relentless focus on developers with a PLG (product-led growth) strategy. Stripe made it trivial for startups to accept online payments by offering clean RESTful APIs and excellent docs. Companies like Stripe and Plaid have made it easy for developers to integrate their products into their workflows in a way that puts real emphasis on customer service and developer support.

**3. Unique Business Model**

Most API-first companies charge customers by usage of the product, either based on Pay Per Call or as a percentage of transaction size, rather than the traditional per-seat SaaS model.

**4. Creating Network Effects**

These companies carved out unique market positions precisely because their API-centric model lowered the barrier for others to build on their services, creating network effects and loyalty.

**5. Community Building**

Companies like Twilio take care of their developer communities with blog posts, meetups and other events. Twilio has an amazing track record of attracting and retaining top talent, and it also supports a wide range of open-source projects that help developers build more powerful applications on its platform.

**6. Strategic Expansion**

Winners like Stripe and Twilio continue to build more products and acquire API companies to become platforms. Twilio acquired Sendgrid for $2B and Segment for $3.2B.
