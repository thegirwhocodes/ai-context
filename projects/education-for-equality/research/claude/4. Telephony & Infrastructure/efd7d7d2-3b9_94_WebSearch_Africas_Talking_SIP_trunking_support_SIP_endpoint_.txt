## Africa's Talking SIP Trunking Support

Africa's Talking can connect to your Private Branch System (PBX) through SIP trunks to allow you to make and receive voice calls.

### SIP Endpoint Connection

You can use Africa's Talking's SIP endpoints to make and receive calls on your browser, which lowers infrastructure costs significantly.

### Setting Up a SIP Trunk Connection

To set up a SIP trunk connection with Africa's Talking, you need:
- A PBX server (on cloud or on premise)
- To apply for a voice phone number
- To share your trunk's (PBX) IP address for whitelisting, and then configure your number's callback URL from your dashboard using the format: trunk:trunk_ip_address (e.g., trunk:127.0.0.1)
