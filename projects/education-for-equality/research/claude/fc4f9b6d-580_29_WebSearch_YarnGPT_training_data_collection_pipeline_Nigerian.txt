## Data Collection

YarnGPT was trained on a dataset of publicly available Nigerian movies, podcasts (using subtitle-audio pairs) and open source Nigerian-related audio data on Huggingface. More specifically, the developer used movies that were available online and extracted their audio and subtitles.

However, the creator faced significant challenges with this approach. While there were thousands of movies to choose from, the audio wasn't up to the standard desired, and their subtitles were inaccurate. To compensate, Azeez turned to Hugging Face, an open-source platform, and used resources from Hugging Face, Oute AI, and other Nigerian repositories.

## Audio Preprocessing

Audio files were preprocessed and resampled to 24Khz and tokenized using wavtokenizer. The model was built with WavTokenizer and inspired by OuteTTS.

This represents a practical approach to building a Nigerian-accented text-to-speech model despite the data collection challenges typically faced in developing regions.
