## Overview

Collin Burns is an AI researcher at Anthropic. Previously he worked at OpenAI, and before that he was a PhD student at Berkeley.

## Research Focus

Burns' research centers on superalignment challenges. He has introduced a new unsupervised algorithm for eliciting skills from pretrained language models, addressing the key alignment problem of how to align superhuman models whose behavior humans cannot reliably supervise.

One of his major contributions is his work on weak-to-strong generalization. His paper "Weak-to-Strong Generalization: Eliciting Strong Capabilities With Weak Supervision" was published at ICML 2024 as an oral presentation. For the ultimate superalignment problem, humans will have to supervise models much smarter than them, and his work studies an analogous problem: using weak small models to supervise strong large models.

## Additional Work

His unsupervised algorithm for Llama 3 pretrained models matches the performance of fine-tuning on dataset labels and outperforms crowdsourced human labels across three benchmarks: GSM8k-verification, TruthfulQA, and Alpaca reward modeling.
