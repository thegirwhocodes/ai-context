## How Whisper was actually trained
From the [OpenAI Whisper paper](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/whisper.pdf) (Radford et al., 2022):

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Scale** | 680,000 hours (438k English transcription + 117k non-English + 125k X→en translation) |
| **Data source** | Scraped internet audio-transcript pairs, **weakly supervised** — no manual labeling, just filtering |
| **Filtering tricks** | Trained a language detector to drop mismatched pairs; dropped ASR-generated captions (all-uppercase, no commas = telltale signs); fuzzy dedup |
| **Audio prep** | Resampled to 16kHz, 80-channel log-Mel spectrogram, 25ms window, 10ms hop |
| **Architecture** | Encoder-decoder Transformer (Small = 244M params, Large = 1.55B) |
| **Training** | 2²⁰ updates (~1M), batch 256, 2-3 epochs, AdamW, FP16 |
| **Augmentation** | **NONE** in v1 base — they relied on data diversity. v2 added SpecAugment |
| **Key scaling law** | **WER halves for every 16× more training data** (r² = 0.83, Fig 3) |

**The takeaway:** you don't retrain Whisper — that's ~$5M in GPU-hours. You **fine-tune** Whisper Small (244M) on your target distribution. The [HF fine-tuning tutorial](https://huggingface.co/blog/fine-tune-whisper) is 10–20 GPU-hours on a single L40S for ~120hrs of data.
