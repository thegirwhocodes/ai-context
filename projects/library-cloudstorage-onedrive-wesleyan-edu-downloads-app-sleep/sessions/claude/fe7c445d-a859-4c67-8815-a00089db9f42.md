Shipped to production. Here's what I found and what's still on you.

## The site was invisible for one reason

`alternates.canonical` was set in [app/layout.tsx](app/layout.tsx) — and Next inherits root metadata down the entire tree. Every route on the site shipped `<link rel="canonical" href="https://eduforequality.org">`. `/about`, `/curriculum`, `/sabi/pricing`, the Sabi page itself — all of them told Google "I am not a page, index the homepage instead." That's why `site:eduforequality.org` returns nothing.

Three faults compounded it:
- On `sabi.eduforequality.org`, `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` were caught by the subdomain catch-all redirect and returned `Redirecting...`. The product host had no crawlable robots file and no sitemap at all.
- `public/robots.txt` and [app/robots.ts](app/robots.ts) both existed; the static one was dead, so its AI-crawler rules never applied.
- No `opengraph-image` anywhere, while `twitter:card` claimed `summary_large_image` — every share preview was blank.

## Fixed and live

Per-page canonicals (Sabi pages → subdomain, org pages → apex), middleware exempts crawler files, one robots source, a real sitemap across both hosts, generated OG cards, entity structured data in [lib/seo.ts](lib/seo.ts) (Organization with logo/sameAs/awards/founder, Sabi as a named `SoftwareApplication` with a stable `@id`, `VideoObject`, `FAQPage`), a visible FAQ on `/sabi` rendered from the same source as the schema, and meta keywords cut from 45 to 8. Verified against the live domains.

## The honest part

**You will not rank #1 for the bare word "Sabi."** The namespace is held by [sabi.am](https://sabi.am/) — a Nigerian commerce-turned-minerals company, [#2 on the FT's fastest-growing companies in Africa](https://weetracker.com/2026/05/13/sabi-nigeria-fastest-growing-company-mining-pivot/), $46.5M revenue, WEF member. Same country, same word, incomparably more authority. Behind it: [Sabi Sabi game reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabi_Sabi), a [Wikipedia disambiguation page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabi), two musicians, plus [joinsabi.com](https://www.joinsabi.com/blog/ai-explain), [meetsabi.com](https://meetsabi.com/) and [sabilearn.online](https://www.sabilearn.online/) in the AI-tutor space specifically.

What's winnable is "Sabi AI tutor", "Sabi Education for Equality", "Sabi phone tutor", "AI tutor over phone call Nigeria" — and answer engines, which is why I left the FAQ copy open to AI crawlers while keeping the paid demo routes blocked.
