# Codex subagent report: /root/beginner_numeracy_design

Audit complete. The current problem is structural, not merely prompt wording.

### What is wrong now

- `GeminiLiveNumeracyTools` hashes the call UUID across the entire ten-item bank. Any item can open a first lesson.
- In a 20-call sample, 16 calls opened with items such as 8×4, 7×5, 6×6, 3×7, 5×6, or 4×4. `call-7` opened with the exact three-baskets-of-seven-eggs item.
- Moving easy items to the top of the tuple will not fix it; the hash can still choose any index.
- The bank ignores the saved curriculum lesson. A learner on Lesson 45, “What Is Multiplication: groups of,” receives content belonging to later lessons:
  - 3×7 belongs with the 3-times-table work.
  - 6×6 belongs with 6s/mixed facts.
  - 7×5 belongs with the 7-times table.
  - 8×4 belongs with the 8-times table.
  - 5 bags at 6 naira each is a rate problem intended for the later naira lesson.
- Hints are not deterministic. The tool merely tells Gemini to give “one short concrete scaffold,” so the model can over-explain, reveal the answer, or generate an unsuitable hint.
- “I don’t know” and “help me” contain no number, so they currently enter the unclear-audio branch and Sabi asks the learner to “repeat just the number.” That is the opposite of patient teaching.
- The current independent/scaffolded distinction is sound: a correct response after a conceptual cue is scaffolded. It should be retained.
- Existing regressions only protect unique products and the prior repeated-12 issue. They do not test curriculum alignment, opening difficulty, explicit help, or hint quality.
- Nearly all easy story items would share `item_form="equal_groups"`. Removing the hard array/rate items without adding small repeated-addition and groups-language items would make the two-item-form mastery criterion impossible to satisfy.
- All items are recorded under the broad skill `multiplication`. Five tiny equal-group responses could therefore mark all multiplication secure. Mastery should be attached to `multiplication_equal_groups`, not the entire module.

### Selection and progression logic

Filter the bank by the saved curriculum lesson and allowed tier first. Only then use the call hash to rotate among equivalent eligible items.

For Lesson 45:

1. No valid prior evidence:
   - speak the short model;
   - register a Tier 1 item;
   - never open above two groups or product ten.

2. Emerging learner:
   - remain in Tier 1;
   - rotate away from recent item IDs and recent products;
   - do not repeat a commutative equivalent in the same call.

3. After two consecutive first-attempt independent Tier 1 successes:
   - allow one Tier 2 item;
   - do not leap again during that call.

4. After an incorrect response or help request:
   - keep the same registered problem;
   - give its exact first scaffold;
   - a supported success receives a fresh same-tier transfer item, not a harder item.

5. Lesson 45 becomes secure only under the existing 4-of-5 independent rule across two calls and two forms. Then advance to Lesson 46. Do not unlock hard facts inside Lesson 45.

Target three or four scored items per call. A five-to-seven-minute teaching call should also contain modelling, a tiny learner-led counting step, an encouraging connection, and recap—not six rapid-fire tests.

### Explicit “I don’t know/help me” behavior

Classify response intent before the generic no-number branch:

- Repeat request: “say it again,” “what did you say?”
  Repeat the exact problem. Preserve independence.
- Help request: “I don’t know,” “help me,” “abeg help,” “show me,” “how do I do it?”, “I no know.”
  Return `help_requested`, `not_scorable`, `indeterminate`; keep the item active and return the exact first scaffold. The next answer is scaffolded.
- Unintelligible/noise: technical repeat, not wrong, independence preserved.
- A response containing one usable number still gets graded even if hedged: “I’m not sure, is it six?” should grade six.
- After a second help request or second incorrect attempt, use the exact answer-model scaffold and tag the repeat `modelled`.

Support requests should be logged as instructional evidence but excluded from both wrong counts and accuracy denominators.
