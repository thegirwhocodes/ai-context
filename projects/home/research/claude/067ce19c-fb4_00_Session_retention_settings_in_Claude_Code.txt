**Session Retention Settings:**

1. **Setting name**: `cleanupPeriodDays` (top-level in settings.json)
2. **Default**: 30 days
3. **To prevent deletion**: Set `cleanupPeriodDays` to a very high number (e.g., `365` for a year, or `3650` for 10 years)

Add this to your `~/.claude/settings.json`:
```json
{
  "cleanupPeriodDays": 365
}
```

**Recovery**: Unfortunately, once the JSONL is deleted, **it cannot be recovered**—the `session_context.txt` summary is what remains after cleanup. The conversation data is permanently lost.

To prevent this in future:
- Increase `cleanupPeriodDays` now
- Export important sessions with `claude export <session-id>` before they age out
- Consider setting it to 365+ days for long-term student projects
