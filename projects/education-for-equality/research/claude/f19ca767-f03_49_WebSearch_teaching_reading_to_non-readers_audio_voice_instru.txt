## Audio-Assisted Reading (AAR) Methodology

Audio-assisted reading is a reading strategy where students read along in their books while listening to a fluent reader read aloud on an audio recording.

### Key Steps in the Methodology

When implementing an AAR strategy, several sequential steps should be considered. Initially, it is vital to choose a text suitable for the students' reading level. Following this, teachers procure the audio version of the chosen text.

The approach includes progressive stages:
- Ask students to listen to the audio while following along on the paper copy of the passage
- Have students read out loud along with the audio recording
- Ask students to read the passage without the audio

### Benefits for Non-Readers

By listening to good models of fluent reading, students learn how a reader's voice can help written text make sense. Additionally, exposure to audiobooks enabled them to grasp accurate word and phrase pronunciation, thereby fostering the development of both reading fluency and pronunciation abilities.

### Recording Quality Considerations

Most researchers recommend that teachers (or other models of fluent reading) create the audiotapes or recordings. The recording should not include distractions such as sound effects or music.

Human-read recordings are particularly effective, as audiobooks read by human voices and digital TTS books read by computer-generated voices allow students to hear books read aloud.
