## YarnGPT Overview

YarnGPT is a text-to-speech (TTS) model designed to synthesize Nigerian-accented English leveraging pure language modelling without external adapters or complex architectures.

## Training Recipe

The model uses HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-360M as its base, trained for 5 epochs on an A100 GPU, using data from Nigerian movies, podcasts, and open-source audio with a pure language modeling approach.

## Data Source and Processing

The model was trained on a dataset of publicly available Nigerian movies, podcasts (using the subtitle-audio pairs) and open source Nigerian-related audio data on Huggingface, with audio files preprocessed and resampled to 24Khz and tokenized using WavTokenizer.

More specifically, the creator extracted audio and subtitles from movies available online.

## How to Replicate

He took the language model SmolLM2-360M from Hugging Face and added speech functionality to it, a process that involved major algorithmic changes.

After adding speech functionality, training the model cost $50 and took three days.

## WavTokenizer Role

Audio tokens can be used to train the AI, and the model can reassemble them to generate speech that sounds natural—this entire process was made possible by a wave tokenizer.

The GitHub repository (https://github.com/saheedniyi02/yarngpt) contains the implementation code and additional details for replication.
