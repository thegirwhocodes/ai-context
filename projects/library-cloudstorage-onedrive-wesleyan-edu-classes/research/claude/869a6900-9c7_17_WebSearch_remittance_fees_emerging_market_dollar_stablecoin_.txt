### Traditional Remittance Costs

According to the World Bank's Remittance Prices Worldwide data, global remittance costs average around 6% per transaction, significantly above the UN's Sustainable Development Goal target of 3%. Fees on B2B cross-border payments can range from 1.5% to 2.9% of the value of the funds being sent, with the World Bank finding that the average cost of sending cross-border remittances was greater than 6% on average.

### USDC and Stablecoin Impact

Stablecoin transfers typically cost a few cents in network fees, depending on the blockchain, which is a sharp contrast to traditional cross-border payments where the average cost of remittance fees is nearly 6.5% per transaction. Mizuho's analysis of the U.S.-Mexico corridor, one of the world's largest single remittance routes, found that stablecoin fees already sit below 1%.

### Latin America and Africa Adoption

Latin America leads adoption with 71% of respondents in a Fireblocks survey of 295 financial institution executives in March 2025 reported using stablecoins for cross-border payments, with 48% citing speed as the primary benefit. High-fee corridors like Nigeria, Kenya, Brazil, and the Philippines are where stablecoins are transforming payments, with traditional routes swallowing over 6.6% of a transfer in fees, but stablecoin-powered flows routinely slashing that to under 3%.

### Cost Reductions

Businesses can reduce cross-border payment costs by up to 80% using crypto and stablecoin rails. Airtm helped businesses and contractors save an average of 35% on cross-border payouts using blockchain infrastructure and USDC.
