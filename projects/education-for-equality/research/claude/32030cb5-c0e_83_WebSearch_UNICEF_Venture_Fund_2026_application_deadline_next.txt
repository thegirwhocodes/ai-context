## Current Application Deadlines

The application deadline for the UNICEF Venture Fund Climate and Health 2026 is May 17, 2026.

Note: There was also a separate Blockchain Ventures cohort with an application deadline of March 10, 2026, so be sure to check which specific fund you're interested in.

## How to Apply

You can start your application at https://www.unicef.org/innovation/call-for-application-climate-and-health-2026.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify, companies must meet these mandatory requirements:

- Be registered in a UNICEF programme country
- Have an existing prototype of the Open Source solution with promising results from initial pilots
- Be working on an Open Source frontier technology solution
- Have the potential to positively impact the lives of the most vulnerable children
- Generate publicly exposed real-time data that is measurable

## Funding Details

The program offers up to $100,000 in equity-free funding to early-stage startups building innovative solutions at the intersection of climate change and health.

For more comprehensive information about the program and application requirements, you can visit the official UNICEF Venture Fund website.
