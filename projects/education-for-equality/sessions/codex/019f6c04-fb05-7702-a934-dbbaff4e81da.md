## Lagos Program Coordinator

**Organization:** Education for Equality — Sabi Pilot
**Location:** Lagos State, with fieldwork in Mushin, Ajeromi-Ifelodun, and nearby communities
**Type:** Six-month renewable contract
**Reports to:** Founder / Program Lead
**Compensation:** ₦350,000 per month, plus transport and approved field expenses
**Start date:** Flexible

### About Education for Equality

Education for Equality is building Sabi, a phone-based AI tutor that helps children learn foundational literacy and numeracy through ordinary voice calls. Sabi is designed for children who may not have smartphones, internet access, or consistent school support.

We are preparing a Lagos pilot to determine whether children can use Sabi safely, consistently, and meaningfully—and whether the program produces measurable learning progress.

### Role summary

The Lagos Program Coordinator will be the on-the-ground lead for the Sabi pilot. The coordinator will recruit schools, families, and community partners; explain Sabi to parents and guardians; support enrolment and consent; coordinate assessments and learning materials; monitor participation; and report field updates.

This is a community-facing operations role, not a technical role. The right person is organized, trusted by schools and families, comfortable working with children, and able to turn a new education technology into a well-run pilot on the ground.

### Key responsibilities

**School and community recruitment**

- Identify and approach schools, learning centres, churches, community groups, and out-of-school-child networks.
- Enrol children according to the pilot’s agreed targets.
- Build trust with school leaders, teachers, parents, and guardians.
- Maintain accurate records of interested schools, families, and enrolled learners.

**Parent and guardian engagement**

- Explain Sabi in clear, parent-friendly language.
- Collect parent or guardian consent before any child participates.
- Explain the difference between consent to use Sabi and optional consent to retain recordings for product improvement.
- Serve as the local contact for parent questions, concerns, and follow-ups.

**Pilot operations**

- Coordinate when and how children use Sabi.
- Follow up with families when calls are missed, dropped, or incomplete.
- Resolve non-technical issues involving scheduling, phone access, caregiver confusion, or low participation.
- Track attendance, call completion, and participation notes.

**Assessments and learning evidence**

- Administer baseline and endline literacy and numeracy assessments using the provided protocol.
- Record assessment results accurately and submit them in the required format.
- Help verify whether children’s progress matches Sabi’s call data.
- Support human review of learner outcomes when required.

**Materials and logistics**

- Distribute and track printed learning materials, consent forms, parent flyers, flashcards, and learning aids.
- Coordinate transportation and field visits across target communities.
- Maintain accurate records of materials distributed and returned.

**Safeguarding and child protection**

- Follow Education for Equality’s child-safeguarding policy at all times.
- Never be alone and unobserved with a child; ensure a teacher, guardian, or second adult is present wherever possible.
- Report child-safety concerns immediately through the designated safeguarding process.
- Complete safeguarding orientation and sign the Safeguarding Code of Conduct.
- Provide a recent Police Character Certificate or agreed interim references before beginning fieldwork.

**Reporting**

- Submit brief daily updates covering field activity, enrolment, calls, parent issues, school feedback, safety concerns, and blockers.
- Maintain accurate records of enrolled children, consent status, assessments, and field notes.
- Attend weekly check-ins with the founder or program lead.
- Escalate urgent matters quickly, especially safeguarding concerns, parent complaints, school concerns, or repeated technical failures.

### Required qualifications

- Based in Lagos, ideally with easy access to Mushin, Ajeromi-Ifelodun, or similar communities.
- Experience in education, school operations, NGO fieldwork, community outreach, or child-focused programming.
- Comfortable communicating with parents, teachers, school leaders, and children.
- Strong organizational skills and reliable daily communication.
- Able to collect and manage sensitive information responsibly.
- Fluent in English; Nigerian Pidgin and/or Yoruba strongly preferred.
- Comfortable using WhatsApp, phone calls, Google Forms, Google Sheets, or similar reporting tools.

### Preferred qualifications

- Experience with public schools, low-income communities, out-of-school children, literacy programs, or TaRL/ASER-style assessments.
- Existing relationships with Lagos schools, teachers, education officers, churches, NGOs, or community leaders.
- Experience with safeguarding, consent collection, or child-protection protocols.
- Familiarity with the Lagos public-education system.

### Success looks like

- The required number of children are enrolled with proper consent and accurate records.
- Families understand how to use Sabi and whom to contact for assistance.
- Baseline and endline assessments are completed accurately.
- Field problems are surfaced and addressed promptly.
- No child participates without consent.
- Safeguarding concerns are escalated immediately.
- The pilot produces credible evidence that can support a larger Lagos rollout.
