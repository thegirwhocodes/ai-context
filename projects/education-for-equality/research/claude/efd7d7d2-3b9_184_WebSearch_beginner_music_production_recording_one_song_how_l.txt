## Recording Time for One Song

For the average or beginner artist, it may take a whole day, or anywhere between four to twelve hours of recording. However, the time can vary significantly depending on several factors:

**Factors That Affect Recording Time:**

- Recording time will vary depending on a few variables such as the efficiency of the studio you're working at, how prepared you are for the session and your own ability to deliver a great performance under pressure.
- Easy acoustic music would probably take just a few hours, while a full-band production with harmonies and more than one layer can take days.
- Your skill level as a vocalist as well as how much you've rehearsed can also play a role in how long this will take.

## Additional Time for Mixing and Mastering

Keep in mind that recording is just the first step. Mixing one song usually lasts not less than 4 hours, and can take up to several days. The more complicated the project, the greater the time.

## Home Studio Setup

The good news for beginners is that with DAWs such as Pro Tools or Ableton Live, it's literally possible to record an entire album on a laptop with a microphone and a little bit of hardware.

**Preparation Tip:** At the bare minimum, you should have already written the lyrics and arrangement, and have a good idea of how you'd like the vocals to sound. Recording a demo ahead of your session is a pre-production technique used by many musicians that might offer you and the engineer a good idea of your idea for the final mix.
