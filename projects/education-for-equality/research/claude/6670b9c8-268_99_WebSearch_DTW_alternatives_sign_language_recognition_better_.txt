## Deep Learning Alternatives to DTW

Transformers have drawn widespread attention for their better capacity to capture spatial and temporal dependencies for gesture-based recognition tasks compared to CNN and Transformer based encoder-decoders, and have been applied in sign language recognition. Vision transformers (ViTs) have emerged as a breakthrough technology, offering superior feature extraction through self-attention mechanisms.

## Recent Improvements and Hybrid Approaches

A recent approach combines Bi-directional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) with jointly weighted Fast Dynamic Time Warping (FastDTW) to extract hand coding similarity and features while reducing parameters, showing that DTW variants like FastDTW are still being used in hybrid models with modern deep learning.

Statistical DTW (SDTW) has been proposed for time warping alone, while classifying warped features with discriminative feature detectors (CDFD) and quadratic classification on Fisher mapping (Q-DFFM), which outperform HMM and SDTW.

## Recent Transformer-Based Approaches

Vision transformer-based models have been shown to match or beat traditional deep learning in terms of performance. Recent systems use MediaPipe to extract hand and upper body pose landmarks, compute temporal sequences of inter-segment angles, and measure similarity with Dynamic Time Warping, achieving 66% accuracy in top-5 results and 75% in top-10 on a 1,113 sign lexicon.
