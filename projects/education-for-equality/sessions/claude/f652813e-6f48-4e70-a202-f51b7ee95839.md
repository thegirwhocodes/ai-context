Here's the plan end-to-end, and why each piece is shaped the way it is.

## The shape

**One doc, one tester, one week, six calls, ~1.5 hours total.** No coordinator on the ground (per your answer). Each call is <10 minutes on the phone plus ~10 minutes writing it up. Testers log through a Google Form (link TBD) or a WhatsApp voice note to your US number.

## Why six calls and not one long test session

Because Sabi's failure modes are **conditional** — they only surface in specific contexts:
- Some only show up in noise
- Some only show up on the *second* call from the same phone (identity)
- Some only show up when the tester is bored or annoyed (frustration → hotkey)
- Number-recognition failures are only findable if you say the specific broken numbers on purpose

One long call catches maybe 30% of what six targeted calls catch.

## The 6-call arc

| # | Call | What it isolates |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Numeracy, quiet room, natural speech | Baseline. If she can't do this, nothing else matters. Also first name capture. |
| 2 | Literacy, quiet room, next day | Tests day-anchored subject routing (per your Jun decision — literacy and numeracy are separate calls, not alternated within one). Different STT prompt path than numeracy. |
| 3 | Shared-phone, same number, different name | The "greet-by-name confidence + correction" flow from `project_sabi_identity`. Nobody else solves this — you called it a fundable moat. Currently gated behind `SABI_ONE_PROFILE_PER_PHONE=TRUE`; flip it for this call. |
| 4 | Noise test | Kitchen fan / TV / market / crying baby. The Awarri-hallucinates-"federal-government-has-ordered-legal-proceedings" failure mode. Testers watch the transcript for text they never said. |
| 5 | **Accent stress test — the main event** | Deliberate say-it-3-ways passes on the known-broken numbers (30, 40, 45, 15, 50, 5), then Pidgin-inflected ("fatti-five", "tati"). Explicitly triggers the keypad DTMF fallback so we know it fires. |
| 6 | Frustration test | Silence, barge-in, wrong answers on purpose, the `*` hotkey, mid-call feedback continue/stop. Everything you shipped Jul 8 that no human but you has ever driven. |

Right — the concrete list. This is what each call actually probes on Sabi's side.

## Call 1 — Numeracy baseline
- **Can Sabi hear a Nigerian name and store it as the learner's name?** (fail mode: names getting captured as "Sorry", "Not available", "Olorevi")
- **Can she diagnose your math level correctly through the 17-item numeracy diagnostic?** (`diagnostic_flow.py`)
- **Do her questions arrive in the right order, at the right pace?**
- **Does she wait long enough for you to answer, and not too long?** (mid-note silence tuning)
- **Does she close the call at the right time?** (min 300s, target 360s, hard cap 480s wrap window)

## Call 2 — Literacy baseline
- **Does she route you to literacy, not numeracy again?** (day-anchored subject routing — the whole "2 calls/day, literacy and numeracy separate" decision)
- **Does the literacy STT prompt path work?** (literacy uses a separate Whisper prompt + local-salvage — different code path than numeracy)
- **Can she hear you saying single letters, sounds, and short CVC words** (cat, mat, bat, sun, run)?
- **Can she hear a full word repeated back?** (blending/decoding)

## Call 3 — Shared-phone identity
- **When you call back from the same number, does she greet you by the right name?**
- **When you say "no, this is [different name]", does she believe you and mint a new learner?**
- **After two learners exist on the phone, does she confirm ("Is this Amara or someone else?") instead of guessing?**
- **Does the fuzzy-match + playback-confirm work, or does she overfit to the first name?**
- **Does `learner_key = phone::name` actually create two rows in Supabase?** (you verify this server-side, tester just triggers it)

## Call 4 — Noise
- **Does the Silero VAD guard fire and return empty on non-speech?** (or does Whisper hallucinate "Subtitles by Amara.org" / "the federal government has ordered..." into the transcript)
- **Does she cut you off while you're still talking over background noise?** (barge-in false positive)
- **Does the RMS threshold correctly distinguish your voice from generator hum / fan / TV?**
- **Does she over-retry on noisy turns and eat the call clock?**
- **Are her prompts still audible when there's noise on your end?** (Chatterbox TTS volume vs. ambient noise)

| 50 | same 4 | Baseline sanity — 50 should be less broken than 40/45 |

Then trigger the fallback ladder:
- **After 2 unclear answers, does Sabi ask you to say the digits slowly?**

## Call 6 — Frustration + hotkey
- **5-second silence after a question** — does she retry with a gentler prompt, or hang up?
- **Barge-in mid-sentence** — does she actually stop when you interrupt, or does she talk over you?
