## Bakame AI Overview

Bakame AI is an AI product that teaches students English on the telephone with no internet and no cost. It only requires a phone, which 97% of Rwandans already have, making it particularly relevant given that only 34.2% of Rwandans have access to internet.

## Pilot Program and Expansion

Bakame AI is set to be tested on five Rwandan schools with a total of 1,000 students and aims to have 10-to-20 schools in Rwanda using Bakame AI by December 2025. If Bakame AI works in the Rwandan schools, the creator plans to sell it to the Rwandan government, and if purchased, Rwandan taxpayers would enjoy Bakame AI for free.

## Broader Rwanda AI Education Context

Beyond Bakame specifically, Rwanda has broader AI education initiatives. Through a partnership with Anthropic and ALX, the Rwandan government will bring AI tools directly into the national education system, enabling AI training for up to 2,000 teachers.

Additionally, in July 2025, Day of AI and MIT RAISE, in collaboration with the Rwanda Ministry of Education and the Rwanda Education Board, launched a national initiative to bring AI literacy to every classroom in Rwanda, marking a major step toward integrating artificial intelligence education into Rwanda's primary and secondary school ICT curriculum.
