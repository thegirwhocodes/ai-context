## USSD Push (Network-Initiated) Overview

The initiation of the communication can either be USSD-PUSH (Mobile Terminated & Provider Originated) or a USSD-PULL (Mobile Originated & Provider Terminated). Network-initiated (NI), also known as USSD Push or Mobile Terminating USSD, is when the application starts the USSD session on the handset.

NI USSD messages are notifications sent from a network to a mobile device, with an OTP from banking apps being an example of this.
