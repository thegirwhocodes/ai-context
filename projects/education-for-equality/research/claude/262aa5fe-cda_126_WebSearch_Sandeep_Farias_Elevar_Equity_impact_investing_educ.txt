## Sandeep Farias and Elevar Equity

Sandeep Farias is Founder and Managing Partner at Elevar and a founding team member at EPIC World. He founded Elevar in 2008, being passionate about working with early-stage companies that foster economic development for low income communities, including MSME finance, education, healthcare and agricultural services.

## Investment Philosophy

Rather than focusing on the traditional "base of the pyramid" approach, Sandeep Farias pioneered the entrepreneurial households thesis at Elevar Equity, focusing on backing over 40-50 million micro-entrepreneurial families in underserved emerging markets. Elevar Equity builds its entire investment strategy around "Entrepreneurial Households" – low-income but economically active families with multiple income streams.

## Impact Investing Results

The firm has invested in ~50 companies over 18-20 years, serving as the founding investor in about half and reaching 50-55 million+ households across India and Latin America. He has played a leadership role in the Indian microfinance sector's scale and transformation over a decade, as the sector moved from the non profit space to formal banking.

## Background

Sandeep has an integrated law and arts degree from the National Law School of India University (NLS). Prior to founding Elevar, he led the corporate law team at NDA, a leading multi-locational and research based international law firm in India.
