## Key Results

**Can founders be paid?**
Founders can be paid, provided they are performing a legitimate role (like Executive Director) and the compensation is reasonable.

**What is "reasonable compensation"?**
The IRS defines "reasonable" compensation as "the value that would ordinarily be paid for like services by like enterprises under like circumstances." However, the IRS doesn't really define reasonable in a way that you could look up in a dictionary.

**How to establish reasonable compensation:**
To follow 501c3 salary rules and support a finding of reasonableness, many nonprofits follow the "Rebuttable Presumption of Reasonableness" process which involves reviewing comparability data showing what similar organizations pay for similar roles. The person whose compensation is being set (for example, the founder) does not participate in or control the vote, and the board documents the data considered and the decision in the meeting minutes.

**Early-stage considerations:**
Founders often work voluntarily (sweat equity) in the beginning until the nonprofit secures enough donations or grant funding to support a payroll.

**Conflict of interest:**
The founder should recuse themselves from the board vote regarding their own salary to prevent a conflict of interest.
