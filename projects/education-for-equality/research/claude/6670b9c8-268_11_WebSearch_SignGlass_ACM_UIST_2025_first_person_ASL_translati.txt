## SignGlass: First-Person View ASL Translation Wearable

SignGlass is the first smart glasses equipped with three wearable cameras for comprehensive capture of both manual and non-manual ASL markers, supported by advanced algorithms for real-time recognition and translation into English.

### Key Features

The system integrates a jitter-aware spatio-temporal attention mechanism for robust recognition of hand movement patterns, and a dual-camera, temporally-aware facial module captures the subtle facial expressions essential for ASL comprehension. A cascaded data augmentation strategy improves model generalization to support diverse signing styles across individuals.

### Performance & User Study

The system achieved a WER of 16.98% and a BLEU-1 score of 83% in leave-one-user-out testing, showing strong generalizability. In a user study with 14 Deaf participants, SignGlass achieved high translation accuracy and was well-received, demonstrating its effectiveness in bridging communication gaps. Additionally, a user survey with 46 Deaf participants revealed that 39 expressed willingness to use the SignGlass system for daily communication.

### Publication

The paper was presented at UIST 2025 - Proceedings of the 38th Annual ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology.
