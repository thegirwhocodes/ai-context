DO NOT TALK DOWN TO ME OR OVER-SIMPLIFY — I UNDERSTAND THE TECH, THE TRADEOFFS, AND THE PLAN. JUST DO EXTREMELY EXCELLENT, THOROUGH, COMPLETE, CORRECT WORK EVERY SINGLE TIME.
