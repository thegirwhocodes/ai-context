# Voice Sandbox Testing Information

**Can you test in sandbox?**
No. According to the article: "For Voice, the sandbox is not operational."

**Alternative for testing:**
Instead of sandbox testing, you must obtain a live test number. Here are the steps:

1. Log into your Account
2. Click on your app
3. Navigate to Voice > Phone number
4. Go to Voice-Phones section
5. Fill in the request form with these details:
   - Product section: Voice Service
   - Country: Select your country
   - Category: Select "Test Number"
6. Agree to the terms

**Additional support:**
For technical assistance, contact voice@africastalking.com

The key takeaway is that Africa's Talking doesn't offer a functional sandbox environment for Voice—you'll need to request a live test number instead.
