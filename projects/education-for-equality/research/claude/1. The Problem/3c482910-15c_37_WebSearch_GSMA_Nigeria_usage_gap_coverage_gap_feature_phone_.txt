## Nigeria-Specific Findings

About 120 million Nigerians did not have access to mobile internet by the end of 2023, creating a huge usage gap for the country. Mobile internet subscriptions in Nigeria stood at 163.3 million in December 2023, while actively connected mobile lines amounted to 224 million, indicating that about 61 million of the connected lines were not used to access the internet.

## Device Types and Usage Barriers

Among those already using mobile internet, the most common barriers to increased use include safety and security concerns, affordability (especially for data but also phones) and the connection experience. While the vast majority of people worldwide now access the internet on a 4G or 5G smartphone, one in five mobile internet subscribers still use 3G smartphones or a feature phone, reaching nearly two-thirds in Sub-Saharan Africa.

## Regional Context: Digital Divides

In Nigeria, more than half of those who are not using mobile internet, despite being aware of it, report barriers related to literacy and digital skills, with difficulties with reading and writing most commonly reported. In low- and middle-income countries, adults in rural areas are 28% less likely than those living in urban areas to use mobile internet, and women are 15% less likely than men to use mobile internet.
