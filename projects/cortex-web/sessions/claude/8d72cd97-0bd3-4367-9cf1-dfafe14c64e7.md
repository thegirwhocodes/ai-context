[Center for Positive Organizations](https://positiveorgs.bus.umich.edu/news/dont-start-a-company-with-your-friends/); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/leadership/harvard-business-school-professor-says-65-of-startups-fail/370367).

## 3. Cold DM on X — does it actually work?

Yes, but only inside a narrow band of behavior.

**Baseline reply rates**: generic cold DMs run **2–5%**. Well-targeted, well-warmed DMs run **15–30%+** in the founder / indie-hacker segment when the DM cites a specific recent tweet (last 7–30 days). [Medium — Sal, A.I.R. formula](https://medium.com/@salwriter/the-ultimate-formula-for-sending-cold-dms-on-twitter-x-that-get-replies-no-spam-just-results-89fa4e188bda); [EarlyCustomers 2026 templates](https://earlycustomers.com/resources/cold-dm-templates/); [DMpro reply-rate calculator](https://www.dmpro.ai/tools/reply-rate-calculator).

**The reply-first / warmup pattern that actually converts**: comment thoughtfully on 1–2 of their posts, connect, wait 3–7 days after acceptance, then DM. This documented sequence takes reply rates **from ~2% to ~25%** on LinkedIn and is directionally the same on X. [Gromming — LinkedIn DM templates](https://gromming.com/blog/linkedin-dm-templates).

**The anatomy of a DM that gets replied to**, distilled from the current-year sources:
- **≤ 240 characters**, casual, founder-to-founder tone, no LinkedIn formality.
- **One specific recent artifact** — a tweet, a repo, a demo they shipped last week. Prove you actually paid attention.
- **Proof-of-work in one line**: link to your live product, not a pitch deck.
- **One clear, small ask** — a 15-min call, or "can I send you a 60-second Loom of the thing" — never "want to chat about a startup?"
- **Absolutely no Calendly link on first touch.**

A workable structure (A.I.R.: Acknowledge / Interest / Response):
&gt; *"Read your thread on personal-memory retrieval last week — you're right that vector search alone caves in on long timelines. I've been shipping Cortex ([link]), a per-user LoRA + Supabase memory stack, and hit the exact failure you described at ~3k messages. Curious what you tried on the eval side — 15 min this week?"*

**What kills the DM instantly**: mass sends, "quick question 👀", pitch decks, treating the DM like a fundraising email, using a Calendly link before they've said hello, sending on a Friday night. Followers can tell within one line.

**Cold email vs DM for senior people**: cold email out-performs DMs when the target is senior enough to have a real inbox. Hunter.io's analysis of 11M emails: **deep personalization drives 52% higher reply rates**; two custom attributes lift replies from 3.6% to 5.6%; **sequences of 21–50 recipients outperform 500+ recipients by +158%**; sending from a custom domain (naomiivie.com, not gmail) gets **+108% higher reply rate**. [Hunter.io — how to write email outreach](https://hunter.io/cold-email-guide/how-to-write-email-outreach/); [thedigitalbloom benchmarks](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/cold-outbound-reply-rate-benchmarks/); [shno.co 2026 stats](https://www.shno.co/marketing-statistics/cold-email-statistics).

**Who to target on X specifically**: engineers who reply to (not just like) tweets from Simon Willison, Swyx, John Rush, Guillaume Verdon, Karpathy, Anthropic staff, retrieval/memory researchers; ex-employees of Character.ai, Adept, Inflection, Rewind, Mem; people who starred adjacent OSS repos on GitHub. **Interaction volume beats follower count** — a 3K-follower engineer who ships is a far better co-founder candidate than a 100K-follower thread-lord.

## 4. What to actually evaluate — the vetting side

The single most-cited piece of advice from Michael Seibel and Sam Altman: **don't interview a co-founder, work with them**. Altman: for the first 10 people, work with them as much as possible before making it official. YC's canonical trial is **2 weeks of shipping something together, full-time or near-full-time**. [YC Startup Library — co-founder equity mistakes](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/LP-co-founder-equity-mistakes-to-avoid); [Sam Altman startup advice — LTSE summary](https://ltse.com/insights/six-startup-tips-from-yc-founder-sam-altman); [OfficeChai — Seibel on equity](https://officechai.com/learn/y-combinator-partner-michael-seibel-on-why-its-better-for-founders-to-be-generous-while-giving-equity-to-co-founders/).

Non-negotiables before signing anything:
- **Standard 4-year vest, 1-year cliff.** Seibel is emphatic. No cliff = you're one year of misalignment away from a dead cap table.
- **Near-equal equity split.** Seibel's argument: the psychological cost of a lopsided split usually exceeds the accounting value. Small tilt for founder-of-record is fine; 80/20 rarely survives year two.
- **Explicit alignment on: exit terms, decision rights (who breaks ties on product, hiring, fundraising), and outcomes both would be happy with** (lifestyle $2M/yr vs. $2B unicorn ambition — mismatches here are silent killers).
- **Complementarity check.** For a technical solo founder who already ships full stack, a second identical technical person is usually the wrong hire. Distribution, sales, or a design-partner-hunter who owns GTM is more valuable — unless you're going deep-infra where you need a peer research partner. Wasserman's data: complementary skill sets correlate with survival; overlapping skills correlate with power struggles.
- **Values / integrity / conflict-recovery style.** Test with a real disagreement inside the 2-week trial, not just easy work.

# 4. So — should you cold-text people on X?

**Yes, but as the last step in a funnel, not the first.** Cold DM works, but only in a narrow band.

**Base rates**:
- Generic cold DM: 2–5% reply
- Well-targeted, warmed DM (comment on their tweets for a week, then message): **15–30%+** in the founder/indie-hacker segment
- The classic warmup sequence (engage 1–2 posts → connect → wait 3–7 days → DM) moves LinkedIn replies from ~2% to ~25%, and X is directionally the same ([source](https://gromming.com/blog/linkedin-dm-templates))

**Anatomy of a DM that gets replied to** (distilled from 2026 sources):
- ≤ 240 characters
- Cites a **specific tweet, repo, or demo from the last 30 days** — proves you actually read them
- One line of **proof-of-work**: a live link (cortex-web-one, voice-email-app), never a deck
- One clear small ask ("15 min this week?" or "can I send you a 60-second Loom?")
- Founder-to-founder tone, no LinkedIn formality
- Zero Calendly link on first touch. Zero pitch deck. No "quick question 👀."

**A working template (A.I.R. — Acknowledge / Interest / Response), which you can send tonight**:

> Read your thread on personal-memory retrieval last week — you're right that vector search alone caves in around 3k messages. I've been shipping Cortex (link), a per-user LoRA + Supabase memory stack, and hit the exact failure you described. Curious what you tried on the eval side. 15 min this week?

**Kill list** — instant no-reply: mass DMs, "hey saw your profile," Calendly first touch, "want to hop on a call about a startup?", pitch decks, Friday-night sends, complimenting their follower count.

**Who to target**: engineers who *reply thoughtfully* (not just like) to tweets from `@simonw`, `@swyx`, `@karpathy`, `@dzhulgakov`, `@jerryjliu0`, `@LangChainAI`'s core team, `@kwindla` (voice/Pipecat), and Anthropic applied-AI staff. Ex-employees of Character.ai, Adept, Inflection, Rewind, Mem. People who starred adjacent OSS repos. **A 3K-follower engineer who ships beats a 100K-follower thread-lord every time.**

**Cold email out-performs DM for senior people**: Hunter.io analysis of 11M emails — deep personalization = +52% reply rate; custom domain (naomiivie.com) = +108% vs Gmail; sequences of 21–50 recipients out-perform 500+ by +158%. ([source](https://hunter.io/cold-email-guide/how-to-write-email-outreach/)) For anyone senior — Rauch, Karpathy-tier — email, not DM.
