## The Core Founding Team

Steve Jobs founded the company on April 1, 1976, with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne. However, with Markkula's guidance and funding, Apple ceased to be a partnership between Jobs and Wozniak, and was incorporated as a company on January 3, 1977.

## Mike Markkula's Role

Markkula was introduced by Regis McKenna and venture capitalist Don Valentine to Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak while they were looking for funding to manufacture the Apple II. Markkula provided Apple with funding of $80,000 - $92,000 personally in addition to securing a $170,000 - $250,000 line of credit from Bank of America.

Beyond funding, he came in and wrote the business plan for Apple and talked his friend Mike Scott into coming on board as the president of Apple.

## Mike Scott as First CEO

Michael Scott was the first CEO, leading Apple from February 1977 to March 1981. He had previously worked with Markkula at Fairchild Semiconductor. Scott never actually went to the famous Apple garage, but he opened a bank account, supervised the manufacturing process, and structured the startup into a real company.

## The Early Hiring

Some of the earliest staff had been working at the Los Altos home of co-founder Steve Jobs' parents since the previous Apple Computer Company partnership was formed on April 1, 1976. Bill Fernandez was the first official employee to start at Apple, right after its founding, and met the Steves at Homestead High School in Cupertino, California and joined the company in 1976.
