   - the normal candidate deployment.

   Record both deployment IDs, the currently served deployment ID, and the exact Git SHA. Vercel supports staged Production deployments and later promotion without rebuilding ([Vercel staged deployments](https://vercel.com/docs/deployments/promoting-a-deployment), [CLI option](https://vercel.com/docs/cli/deploy)).

   - successful `cron.job_run_details`;
