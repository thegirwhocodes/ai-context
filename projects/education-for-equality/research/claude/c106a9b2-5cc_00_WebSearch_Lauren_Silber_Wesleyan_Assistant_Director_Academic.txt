Lauren Silber is the Assistant Director of Academic Writing at the Writers Room (The Office of Academic Writing), as well as an Associate Professor of the Practice in Academic Writing.

**Educational Background:**
She earned her PhD in English and American Studies from the University of Massachusetts Amherst and her BA in English from the University of Connecticut.

**Responsibilities:**
She specializes in academic writing program management and curriculum development, oversees the Wesleyan Writing Center, guiding a team of undergraduate tutors, and manages and assesses writing programs including one-on-one writing tutoring, semester-long writing mentorships, and thesis programming. **Research and Scholarly Interests:**
Her scholarship emerges at the intersections of migration studies, comparative race and ethnic studies, gender studies, and affect studies.
