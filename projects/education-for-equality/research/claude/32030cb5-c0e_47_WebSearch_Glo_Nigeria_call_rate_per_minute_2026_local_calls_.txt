## Glo Nigeria Local Call Rates (2026)

Globacom's voice tariff plans include Glo Berekete for new customers, Glo Super Talk and Glo Talkmasta 6for6 for voice-centred subscribers.

### Main Plans and Rates:

**Glo Super Talk Plan:**
Glo customers can call any network in Nigeria at a flat rate of 15.5k/sec (which equals approximately 9.3 naira per minute). This tariff offer comes with a daily access fee of N15 (excl. VAT), which is charged with the first call of the day.

**Glo 11kobo Plan:**
The call rate for the Glo 11kobo plan is 11kobo per second (11k/sec), or 6.6 Naira per minute to all Nigerian local networks. After paying an access fee of N7, which is levied together with the first call of the day, Glo members will be able to call all networks in Nigeria at 11 kobo per second only.

These rates are current and applicable to both Glo-to-Glo and calls to other Nigerian networks (Airtel, MTN, 9mobile).
