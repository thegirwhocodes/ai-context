The Bayit opens its doors to any member of the community wishing to use its kosher-style kitchen or library, hold a Jewish group meeting or event, or just hang out. The Bayit is Wesleyan's Jewish ethnocultural residential house, centrally located at 157 Church Street.

## Long Lane Farm

Long Lane Farm is Wesleyan's student-run and collectively organized organic farm, operating year-round at 243 Long Lane in Middletown, Connecticut. The student-run Long Lane Organic Farm uses ecological farming practices to produce food for the Wesleyan University Campus as well as the broader Middletown community.
