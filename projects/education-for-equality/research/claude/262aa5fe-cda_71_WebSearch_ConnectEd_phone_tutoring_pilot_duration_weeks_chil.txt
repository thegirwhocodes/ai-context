## Program Duration and Design

With ConnectEd, every week grade 3 to 5 students receive math problems delivered via text message along with a twenty-minute phone tutorial session. However, the exact duration of the pilot programs varied by location.

For example, in El Salvador, the intervention consisted of individual tutoring through personalized text messages and 20-minute phone calls during a period of 8 weeks for students allocated to the treatment group.

## Geographic Coverage and Research Partnership

A coalition of NGOs, governments, multi-laterals, and researchers in five other countries (India, Kenya, Nepal, Philippines, and Uganda) tested and adapted phone tutoring programming in their context, with J-PAL as a research partner and the evaluation funded by J-PAL's Innovation in Government Initiative.

## Effectiveness Results

On average, phone calls improved learning by 0.33 standard deviations, over a third larger than the median effectiveness of education interventions. Children who received phone tutoring learned more in every location, with the greatest gains in the Philippines and Uganda (0.45 and 0.89 standard deviations, respectively), where school closures were longer than in other countries.
