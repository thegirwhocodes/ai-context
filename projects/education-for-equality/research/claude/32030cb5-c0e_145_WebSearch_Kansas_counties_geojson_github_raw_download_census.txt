## Key Resources Found:

1. **get-tiger** - A workflow tool that uses make to quickly download Census geodata and prepare Shapefiles or GeoJSON for use in GIS. The Census publishes two sets of map data: Cartographic Boundary files and TIGER/Line.

2. **us-counties (GitHub)** - A GeoJSON object containing geometries of every county in the US, with data sourced from Census shapefiles.

3. **US Geojson Data (eric.clst.org)** - Provides different resolution options. The 500k files are the most detailed, the 20m files are the smallest with dramatic simplification, and the 5m files fall between them.

4. **us-atlas (TopJSON)** - A TopoJSON file containing geometry for counties, states, and nation that is quantized and simplified, derived from the Census Bureau's cartographic county boundaries.

5. **census2geojson** - A tool to convert TIGER/Line Shapefiles from the U.S. Census Bureau into GeoJSON.
