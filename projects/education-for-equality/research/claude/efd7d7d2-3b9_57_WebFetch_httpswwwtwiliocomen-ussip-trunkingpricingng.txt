## Additional Features

The service includes several features at no extra cost: unlimited call concurrency and secure trunking. Optional add-ons include call recording ($0.0025/min) and SIP Insights Advanced Features ($0.0024/min).

The pricing follows a pay-as-you-go model with no contracts or channel limits, and volume discounts are available for enterprise customers.
