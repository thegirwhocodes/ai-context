## About Ever Increasing Faith

Smith Wigglesworth's book "Ever Increasing Faith" was published in 1924 and contains 18 sermons that explore his beliefs and experiences. Some of his sermons were transcribed for Pentecostal magazines and collected into two books: Ever Increasing Faith and Faith that Prevails.

## Visions and Supernatural Experiences

Smith experienced his first vision when he poured oil on a sick woman's body, describing how "the Lord Jesus appeared" and gave him "one of those gentle smiles" that he "never lost."

In his teachings from "Ever Increasing Faith," Wigglesworth emphasized being filled with the Spirit of God and being taken on "to visions and revelations," wanting to move people "beyond all human plans and thoughts into the fullness of vision, into the full revelation of the Lord Jesus Christ."

## Wigglesworth's Ministry and Legacy

Smith Wigglesworth, often referred to as 'the Apostle of Faith,' was one of the pioneers of the Pentecostal revival that occurred a century ago. There were numerous claims of divine healing during Wigglesworth's ministry, and reports that people were raised from the dead.
