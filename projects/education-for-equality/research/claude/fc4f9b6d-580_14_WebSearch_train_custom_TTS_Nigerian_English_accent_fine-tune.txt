## African Speech Data and Research

XTTS (version 2, 750 M parameters) was trained on a dataset of over 16k hours comprising 16 languages including English, and the models were fine-tuned on training sets with only the GPT module fine-tuned.

Recent research demonstrates practical application of this approach: South Africans and West Africans rated generated utterances with regional accents, though generated accents do not always match the reference accent—for example, generated speech in Afrikaans accent may sound like Zulu, and Igbo generated accent may sound like Yoruba.
