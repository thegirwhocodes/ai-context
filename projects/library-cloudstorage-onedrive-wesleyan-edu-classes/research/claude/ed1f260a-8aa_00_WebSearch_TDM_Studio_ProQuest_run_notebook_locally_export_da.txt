## Running Notebooks Locally

When ready to use a dataset, you can turn on the Jupyter Notebook environment, which can take up to 10 minutes to complete. You can upload your own scripts to the Jupyter notebook environment for data processing and analysis.

## Export Data Restrictions

There are significant restrictions on what can be exported from TDM Studio:

1. **Export Limits**: The current export limit is 15 MB per week, and the export limit is shared by all users affiliated with the workbench.

2. **Copyright Restrictions**: Due to copyright restrictions, you cannot export full text or any consumptive information that could be used to reconstruct the full text. Export of full text is prohibited.

3. **What Can Be Exported**: You can export any derived data, as well as scripts, tables and visualizations. Datasets cannot be exported, only programs and resulting secondary analysis.

4. **Derived Data Requirements**: Derived data may include summaries of, and factual extracts from, the editorial content and metadata provided that the derived data cannot reasonably be considered a substitute for reading the full-text story.

These restrictions ensure compliance with copyright and licensing agreements while still allowing researchers to conduct analysis and share results.
