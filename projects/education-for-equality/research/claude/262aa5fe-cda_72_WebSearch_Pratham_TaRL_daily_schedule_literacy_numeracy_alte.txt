## Camp Duration and Daily Hours

In the direct model, Pratham instructors run Learning Camps consisting of 30-50 days of instruction (3 to 5 camps of 10 days each), for about 3 hours daily. In some implementations, roughly 120 days across 5 camps yields about 50 days of instruction at 2-3 hours a day.

## Literacy and Numeracy Structure

Regarding the daily schedule for literacy and numeracy activities, the TaRL approach groups children into levels for both literacy (Beginner + Letter Level, Word + Simple Paragraph level, and Story level) and numeracy (Beginner + One digit/two digit numbers, Addition + Subtraction, and Word Problem level). The search results indicate that groups are dynamic, with children moving from one group to another as they progress.

## Alternative Implementation Model

In the partnership model with government teachers, the approach is used over a longer school year (four to six months) with dedicated time for 1-2 hours a day.

However, the search results do not contain specific details about whether literacy and numeracy are taught on alternating days or on the same day within the camp structure.
