## About the Competition

DataFest is a data analysis competition where students are presented with a large, complex, surprise data set and work over the weekend to explore, analyze, and present their findings. Teams of 3-5 students work together and compete against other teams.

## 2026 Dates

DataFest 2026 is scheduled for Friday, April 17 – Sunday, April 19, 2026.

## How to Win / Judging Criteria

After two days of intense data wrangling, analysis, and presentation design, each team is allowed a few minutes and no more than two slides to impress a panel of judges. Prizes are awarded to the teams who impress the judges most.

## Award Categories

Based on past competitions, DataFest awards prizes in multiple categories, including Best Data Story, Best Data Insight, and Best Data Visualization, among others like best use of external data and best innovation.

For more detailed information about registration and specific 2026 competition details, you can visit the official DataFest blog at datafest.blogs.wesleyan.edu.
