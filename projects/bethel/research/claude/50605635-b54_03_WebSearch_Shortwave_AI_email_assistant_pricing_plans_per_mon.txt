## Shortwave Pricing Plans (2026)

Shortwave offers three main paid plans billed per user per month:

- **Business** — $24/month: 150–300 daily AI requests, 3 AI filters
- **Premier** — $36/month: ~600 daily requests, 10 AI filters
- **Max** — $100/month: ~900 daily requests, 50 AI filters

Additionally, Shortwave Business costs $9/user/month for full AI email features, team inboxes, and Google Workspace support (this appears to be an older team pricing structure).

The free plan is limited to personal Gmail only with capped AI features.

## Key Details

All plans offer a 14-day trial. The entry-level Business plan at $24/month covers all your connected Gmail accounts for one flat rate.

Note that Shortwave meters its AI — when you hit the limit, the assistant stops responding until the next day.
