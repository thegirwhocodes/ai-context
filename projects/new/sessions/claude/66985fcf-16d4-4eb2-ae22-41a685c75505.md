## Go — what it actually is

An **iOS commitment device**. Not a reminder app — it takes your money when you're late. Repo: `~/Class on Time`, ~4,200 lines TS, live at go-place.vercel.app, last commit **Jul 7** (a month cold, tree clean, synced to `origin/main`).

### The loop

**1. Ingest** — [calendar-sync](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/app/api/cron/calendar-sync/route.ts) pulls Google Calendar every 5 min. [extract-location.ts](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/lib/extract-location.ts) decides if an event has a *physical* place; [geocode.ts](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/lib/geocode.ts) turns the text into lat/lng. Fuzzy phrases ("gym", "church") get resolved once and remembered forever in `user_location_aliases` — your personal Significant Locations table.

**2. Confirm** — [morning-rollcall](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/app/api/cron/morning-rollcall/route.ts) pushes at 11:00 UTC. You open [rollcall.tsx](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/mobile/app/rollcall.tsx) and *talk* — Whisper transcribes, Claude Haiku classifies each event as confirmed or cancelled ([extract-commitments.ts](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/lib/extract-commitments.ts)). Cancelling late costs a fee. This is the "did you actually mean it" gate.

**3. Depart** — [departure-tick](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/app/api/cron/departure-tick/route.ts) runs every 5 min. For every pending event in the next 4 hours it hits Mapbox for a **walking** ETA from your phone's last GPS ping, sets `departureAt = requiredArrivalAt − eta`, and when that moment passes fires an Expo push: *"Time to head to X — 12 min walk."*

**4. Verify** — the phone reports arrival to [/api/arrivals](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/app/api/arrivals/route.ts) via a destination geofence. Writes `wasOnTime`.

**5. Charge** — [penalty-charges](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/app/api/cron/penalty-charges/route.ts) fires an **off-session Stripe charge** against your own saved card.

**6. Lock** — the anti-escape mechanism. You can't yank your card at 12:58 to dodge a 1:00 charge. Removal is a *request* that sits in `lockup_log` with `executesAt = +7 days`; [lockup-sweep](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/server/app/api/cron/lockup-sweep/route.ts) executes it nightly. If no active payment method exists, the whole app hard-locks ([index.tsx:40-49](../Class%20on%20Time/apps/mobile/app/index.tsx#L40-L49)). Signed `consents` rows ride along in Stripe metadata as chargeback defense.

### Data model
[schema.ts](../Class%20on%20Time/packages/db/src/schema.ts) — 9 tables on Neon/Drizzle: `users` (with live lat/lng), `oauth_tokens`, `events`, `arrivals`, `payment_methods`, `charges`, `user_location_aliases`, `consents`, `lockup_log`.

### Infra note worth knowing
Vercel Hobby only allows *daily* crons, so the three sub-daily jobs are poked by a **GitHub Actions cron every 5 min** ([.github/workflows/cron.yml](../Class%20on%20Time/.github/workflows/cron.yml)) hitting authed endpoints. Only rollcall + lockup-sweep run natively on Vercel. Config lives in [vercel.ts](../Class%20on%20Time/vercel.ts), not vercel.json.

That split works, and it's better than it first looks — but there's one framing trap to dodge.

**Why it works economically:** Go's current revenue is penalty income. That's unpredictable and it only pays you when your user *fails*. A prep-chain subscription converts that into recurring revenue that pays you when your user *succeeds*. You want to be moving off penalty revenue as you grow, not deeper into it.

**The trap:** "free tier fines you, paid tier stops the fines" reads as a protection racket if you say it plainly. Same mechanics, different framing:

- ❌ *Pay $8/mo and we'll stop charging you*
- ✅ *Go keeps you honest. Go Pro makes you early.*

Free = accountability. Paid = the coach. Don't let the copy imply you're selling relief from your own penalty.

**Cost logic also supports the line.** Free tier is cheap per user — a Mapbox call and a push. The prep chain is where the metered spend lives: LLM calls to generate and re-tune task chains, transport-mode logic, learned durations. Real marginal cost is the honest reason to charge.

**Architecture: one codebase, not two.** This is an entitlement flag, not a second app. Concretely — `users.tier`, a `prep_tasks` table hanging off `events`, and `departure-tick` branching: free path keeps `arrival − travel`, paid path walks the task graph backward. Everything else — calendar sync, geofence, Stripe, lockup — is shared.

That also gives you the ladder from your voice session: **Go** (free, punctuality) → **Go Pro** (paid, preparation) → **Cortex** (the life layer, later).
