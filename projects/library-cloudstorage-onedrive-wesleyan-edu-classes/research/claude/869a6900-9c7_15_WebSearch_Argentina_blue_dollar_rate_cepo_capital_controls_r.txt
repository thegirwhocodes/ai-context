## Argentina's Currency Control Framework

The term "CEPO" refers to a series of government-imposed restrictions on the purchase and sale of U.S. dollars in Argentina. For many years, individuals and businesses were unable to freely access dollars through official channels, leading to a parallel or black market where the "dólar blue"—a significantly higher unofficial exchange rate—became the norm.

## Recent Lifting of Capital Controls

As of April 14, 2025, the Argentine government has lifted most currency controls, locally known as the "cepo cambiario." Individuals and businesses are now permitted to purchase U.S. dollars without restrictions.

## Exchange Rate Convergence
