## Key Legal Issues

**Misrepresentation and Unfair Business Practices:**
When a former employer uses your image or bio to represent you as a current resource, this constitutes misrepresentation and could support claims of unfair business practice and unlawful advertising.

**How the Representation Matters:**
If a former company is representing that you still work for it through outdated bios or images, they are misleading the public. Such implied representation that someone still works there could be considered a violation of the Unfair Trade Practices Act.

**Potential Legal Claims:**
You may have a claim for unauthorized use of name and image, which can award actual damages, statutory damages of $750, attorney fees and punitive damages.
