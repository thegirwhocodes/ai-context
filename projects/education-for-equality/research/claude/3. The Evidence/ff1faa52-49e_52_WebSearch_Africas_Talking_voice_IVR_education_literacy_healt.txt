**Health and Literacy Case Studies in Kenya and Nigeria:**

1. **Malaria Literacy in Burkina Faso (3-2-1 Service):** The 3-2-1 Service is an IVR system that allows users to interact using a telephone keypad and a toll-free number, providing automated mobile hotline service that offers information in six languages on development topics including health and agriculture. Results showed a 30.33% increase in knowledge, 21.77% in attitude, and 35.38% in practice among the local language group.

2. **Fistula Screening in Nigeria and Uganda:** Implementation of the fistula screening hotline suggests that IVR-based interventions may be useful in expanding access to health services for stigmatized conditions, particularly in settings where literacy is limited.

3. **Health Information Literacy in Kenya and Nigeria:** A project aimed to enhance the capacity of public and community librarians in four African countries including Kenya and Nigeria, with 65 librarians from 34 public and community libraries trained on how to access and use consumer health information resources.
