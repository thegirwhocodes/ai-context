## Wesleyan Patricelli Center Funding Programs

The Patricelli Center New Venture Awards (NVA), formerly known as the Seed Grants, are three $6,000 awards that aim to recognize and support impactful student projects, organizations, and companies across sectors and communities. New Venture Awards payments will be made in two installments: $5000 in May, and $1000 in October pending satisfactory progress.

## Additional Grant Opportunities

The Patricelli Center Follow-On Fund will award up to $5,000 in additional funding per academic year to previous grantees with demonstrated and significant progress in their projects or ventures.

For conference attendance, up to $1000 is available to attend social impact or entrepreneurship conferences, gatherings, and other related events.

## Student Support Requirements

All applicants will receive mentoring from Patricelli Center staff and volunteers, 24/7 access to the PCSE Board Room, and priority involvement in PCSE programs. NVA recipients will be expected to submit regular reports and remain actively involved in Patricelli Center programs.
