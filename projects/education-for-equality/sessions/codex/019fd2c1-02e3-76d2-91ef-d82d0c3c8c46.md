However, Twilio/ElevenLabs phone calls are still 8 kHz μ-law—the same narrow telephone bandwidth. My expectation:

- U.S. caller → U.S. Twilio number: probably cleaner.
- Nigerian caller → U.S. Twilio number: possibly no better, or worse, because of the international phone route.
- Browser/WebRTC recording: likely substantially better because it can avoid 8 kHz telephone compression entirely.

The Oluremi clip does contain some line noise, but the bigger problem is that the phone channel has warped or discarded consonant information.
