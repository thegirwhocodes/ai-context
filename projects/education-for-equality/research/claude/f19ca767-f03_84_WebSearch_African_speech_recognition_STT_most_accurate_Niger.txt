## Key Findings for African Speech Recognition

### General African Language ASR Status

Automatic speech recognition (ASR) for African languages remains constrained by limited labeled data, and large pre-trained systems such as Whisper, XLS-R, MMS, and W2v-BERT have expanded access to ASR technology, but their comparative behavior in African low-resource contexts has not been studied in a unified and systematic way.

### Best Performing Models for African Languages

MMS and W2v-BERT are more data efficient in very low-resource regimes, XLS-R scales more effectively as additional data becomes available, and Whisper demonstrates advantages in mid-resource conditions.

### Nigerian Languages Specifically

For Nigerian languages, the NaijaVoices dataset is a 1,800-hour speech-text dataset with 5,000+ speakers that achieved 75.86% WER improvements for Whisper, 52.06% for MMS, and 42.33% for XLSR through finetuning experiments. This dataset focuses on Igbo, Hausa, and Yoruba languages.

### Whisper Alternatives

Top alternatives to Whisper mentioned for 2025-2026 include:

- Granite Speech 3.3 (IBM) – 8B parameters with WER ~ 5.85%, Apache 2.0 license
- Whale – Large-scale multilingual ASR achieving ~2.4% WER on Librispeech, outperforming Whisper large-v3
- OpenAI's Whisper Large V3 remains the gold standard for multilingual speech recognition with 1.55 billion parameters and support for 99+ languages
