Deep-research (pass 1) on "companies like Sage" — the AI-email landscape, run to inform Sage's launch/strategy. 114 agents, 154 claims extracted, 25 adversarially fact-checked. Full report is in the session conversation; the polished cited write-up is at `~/sage/COMPETITIVE_STRATEGY.md`. - 91c68191

**Verified high-confidence findings:**
- **Cora (Every)** is **draft-only** ("Cora will never send an email for you"), a hosted SaaS at **$15/mo** standalone / $20 with Every; model = auto-triage everything + a **twice-daily digest brief**, leaving only human email that needs a reply. Validates draft-only as a shipping *paid consumer* model — Sage's send-gate is a product stance, not a limitation. - 91c68191
- **Fyxer AI**: moat = a **500,000-hour exec-assistant time-tracking dataset** (agency run since 2016 specifically to harvest it); **$1M→$5M ARR in ~2.5 months**, $10M Series A Mar 2025 (20VC/Stebbings + Benioff, Claure), later $30M Series B; 40k+ inboxes; won via a **vertical beachhead** (recruiting, real estate). Lesson: the moat is proprietary data, not the LLM. - 91c68191
- **Superhuman**: raised **$114M+** (a16z/IVP/Tiger); **acquired by Grammarly July 2025**, terms undisclosed. The default exit for VC-backed email = absorption. REFUTED, do NOT repeat: "$35M ARR/$825M val/$125.93M raised", "19k→70k customers", "$30/$40 pricing", "12-specialist concierge → 10k users/yr" (all killed in verification). - 91c68191
- **SaneBox**: privacy-respecting **CLOUD** SaaS (email stays on the user's own server; processes headers/metadata; opt-in features do download content; subject lines are "semi-content"). Bootstrapped, profitable, ~12 people, 20–25% trial conversion, ~1.5% churn. Lost ~**half its customers the day Gmail shipped Priority Inbox** (Aug 2010); most returned because per-user personalization beat Gmail's global rules. Precedent: privacy-respecting *can* be a durable SaaS; Gmail platform-dependence is the defining risk. (REFUTED: specific "~50% margin / high-single-digit revenue".) - 91c68191
- **Inbox Zero (Elie Steinbock)**: the only open-source/self-hostable one; **open-core** — AGPL repo + hosted SaaS ($18/$28/$42 per user/mo). Distribution via open source ("almost a cheat code" for GitHub trending → #1 Product Hunt); ~11–12k stars, **8k–15k users**. Caveat the research insisted on: **stars ≠ users ≠ revenue**. - 91c68191
- **Privacy willingness-to-pay is empirically weak**: Caleb Fuller 2019 (n=6,083) — **86% of Google users won't pay anything for added privacy** (the privacy paradox). Medium confidence (2019, Google-specific, stated not revealed WTP). - 91c68191
- **Local-first framework**: Ink & Switch "local-first software" (2019) — its strongest argument is **longevity** (your software survives the company's death), the exact failure mode the email graveyard embodies. - 91c68191

**THE RECONCILIATION (critical — read before using pass-1's headline):**
- Pass-1's headline rec was "**lead with local-first / you-own-the-agent**." This is **IN TENSION** with the locked **hosted multi-tenant SaaS** decision in [[sage-business-build]]: you cannot run multi-tenant SaaS *and* have "no server / user owns the agent." The SaaS choice was made for sound business reasons (avoid the S-quadrant). **Architecture is NOT being relitigated.** - 91c68191
- **What survives into hosted SaaS:** (1) **draft-only** product (Cora proves it sells); (2) **privacy-respecting / data-minimal** handling as a *trust* differentiator (SaneBox precedent — privacy-respecting CLOUD SaaS); (3) **moat = accumulated per-user voice model + workflow data** (Fyxer lesson), not the model. - 91c68191
- **What does NOT survive:** "you own your agent / survives company death / no SaaS server" as the headline wedge — **drop it.** - 91c68191
- **Research CONFIRMS the locked pricing instinct #2:** price on the **OUTCOME** ("never deal with email again"), NOT on privacy (86% won't pay a privacy premium). - 91c68191
- **Distribution** is the hardest problem for all email tools; **open-core** (Inbox Zero model) is compatible with hosted SaaS and worth weighing as a channel. - 91c68191
- **Gmail platform-dependence + Google CASA restricted-scope review** is the defining structural risk — matches the gating constraint already noted in [[sage-business-build]]. - 91c68191

**Pass 2 (complete, session 91c68191 — 106 agents):** gap-fill on the graveyard + Shortwave + Notion Mail.
- **Graveyard = one cause of death, repeated: the acqui-hire.** Sparrow→Google (Jul 2012, ~$25M; ~5 eng into Gmail, "no new features" same day, pulled 2015). Mailbox→Dropbox (Mar 2013, ~$100M, 1M+ users; shut 2016, "couldn't integrate it"). Astro→Slack (Sep 2018, ~28 employees; apps shut 16 days later). All died from strategic misalignment, not tech failure — paying users abandoned. Sparrow backlash was about Google's "buy & abandon" pattern, NOT about charging money (the "paid model killed Sparrow" claim was REFUTED 0-3). Lesson: users punish *abandonment*; **bootstrapped/founder-owned removes the VC-exit pressure that killed them** (SaneBox's path). - 91c68191
- **Shortwave** (Andrew Lee, Firebase co-founder; started 2020 "bring back Google Inbox", $9M Series A Apr 2020, pivoted to AI late 2022): moat = a real **~dozen-LLM-call + cross-encoder + embeddings RAG pipeline** (not a wrapper) + **vendor-agnostic model freedom** (its answer to commoditization). Pricing $24/$36/$100 per seat/mo. - 91c68191
- **⚠️ AI-COMPUTE DEATH SPIRAL** (most important for hosted-SaaS Sage): Shortwave CEO says they'd "be bankrupt right now" without Anthropic **prompt caching** (~90% cheaper); full agent ~**100× a simple call**, iterates tokens ~30×; per-seat AI is metered to compute. **Because Sage is now hosted SaaS (not local-first), this risk APPLIES** — model per-user LLM cost on the always-on loop; prompt caching non-optional. $200/mo gives margin headroom vs Shortwave's $24–100, but must be modeled. - 91c68191
- **⚠️ DRAFT-ONLY vs the new "autonomous send" direction:** Shortwave — the most sophisticated player — **deliberately refuses to let its AI send/delete/archive**, citing **prompt injection** + "agent does something dumb." Even its aggressive sister product Tasklet only *queues* drafts. This independently VALIDATES Sage's original draft-only safety posture — and is in direct tension with [[sage-business-build]]'s shift to **"trusted autonomous send" as Sage Pro's $200/mo paid promise** (draft-only demoted to free ramp). Reconciliation: autonomous send is viable but must be **scoped/graduated** (allowlisted contacts, in-thread replies, dual-LLM quarantine, earned autonomy), not a flat capability. **Genuine product-safety decision flagged for Naomi — sync with Codex thread.** - 91c68191
