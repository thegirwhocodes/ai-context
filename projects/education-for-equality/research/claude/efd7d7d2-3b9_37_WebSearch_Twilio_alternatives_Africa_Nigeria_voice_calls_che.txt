## Africa-Specific Alternatives

**Africa's Talking** is a company that offers powerful mobile communication APIs for sending push notifications, SMS messaging and voice messages to Africa, founded by Nigerian developers who were looking for a way to send SMS messages to African countries at a lower cost than other companies. ## General Alternatives That Cover Africa/Nigeria

Several other platforms offer competitive pricing for African markets:

- **Plivo** is a developer-friendly CPaaS platform with APIs for SMS, voice, and WhatsApp, often chosen as a cost effective alternative to Twilio for core messaging and calling, frequently viewed as a cheaper alternative to Twilio thanks to aggressive pay as you go pricing in many regions. Pay as you go pricing starts at $0.0055 per message to send or receive SMS messages and goes up to $0.01 per minute to make voice calls.

- **Vonage** offers customizable APIs for SMS and voice services and is often cheaper than Twilio, especially for large volumes of SMS and calls.

- **Infobip** is a global enterprise CPaaS provider offering SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, email, voice, and more, with long-standing telco partnerships and local connectivity making it appealing for stronger regional support in emerging markets.

For operations specifically focused on Africa and Nigeria, **Africa's Talking** appears to be the most tailored solution given its founding purpose and local expertise.
