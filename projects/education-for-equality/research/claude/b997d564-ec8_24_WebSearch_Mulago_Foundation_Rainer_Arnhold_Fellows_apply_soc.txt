## Fellowship Overview

The Rainer Arnhold Fellowship was founded in 2003 and focuses on "scalable solutions to poverty." The program seeks founders who have a scalable solution to climate or poverty—and the chops to deliver it, in areas such as healthcare, education, livelihoods, nutrition, and a healthy environment.

## What Fellows Receive

The one-year fellowship is a mix of money, design thinking, coaching, and connections, with the main event being a week-long retreat. The fellowship comes with $100k and is the on-ramp to the foundation's portfolio—all fellows are considered for longer-term Mulago funding following completion of their fellowship.

## Selection Process

The foundation conducts an extensive search for fellows at the start of the year, depending heavily on their trusted network of doers and funders for referrals, and looks at thousands of organizations per year for approximately 20 spots. The process is competitive but kept low-hassle, starting with one short form and two independent reviewers who evaluate each application.

## Target Applicants

The foundation focuses on finding "social entrepreneurs" that are making an impact through proven solutions but are in their early stages of development as organizations.
