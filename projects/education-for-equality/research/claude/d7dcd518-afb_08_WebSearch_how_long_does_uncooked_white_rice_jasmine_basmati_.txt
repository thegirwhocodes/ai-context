## Shelf Life

White, jasmine and basmati rice all have an indefinite shelf life, when kept free from contaminants. More specifically, white rice (including jasmine and basmati) that has had its bran and germ layers removed stores virtually indefinitely under proper conditions, with the USDA and food storage experts estimating that properly sealed white rice remains safe and nutritious for 4 to 5 years at room temperature — and for up to 25 to 30 years in food-grade sealed containers with oxygen absorbers.

Some manufacturers like Mahatma Rice recommend that these rice products are best by two years (730 days) after the date of manufacture if stored properly and protected from infestation and extreme temperature fluctuations.

## Storage Guidelines

To maximize shelf life, follow these storage recommendations:

- Keep rice in securely sealed containers that keep out unwanted moisture and eliminate the risk of infestation, and once opened, store it in a tightly closed container that keeps out dust, moisture and other contaminants.
- Store uncooked rice in a dry and cool environment, and keep it far away from foods such as onions or garlic as rice will absorb strong aromas.
- For added protection against bugs, dust or other contaminants, uncooked jasmine rice may also be stored in the fridge or freezer in a sealed airtight container or resealable heavy-duty freezer bag.
