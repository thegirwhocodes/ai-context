## Document Content

FM 6-22, Developing Leaders provides a doctrinal framework covering methods for how leaders develop other leaders, improve their organizations, build teams, and develop themselves, with the title change reflecting emphasis on developing leaders at the direct and organizational levels and emphasizing the active process of teaching, training, coaching, mentoring, study, and learning.

The principal audience for FM 6-22 is all Army leaders, military and civilian, serving as a primary aid to develop individuals and cohesive teams from division down with focus on company to brigade levels.
