## Current Capabilities

Termii's voice call API enables you to send messages from your application through the voice channel to a phone number. However, only one-time-passwords (OTP) are allowed for now and these OTPs cannot be verified using their Verify Token API.

## About Termii

Termii is a Nigerian-founded communication platform as a service (CPaaS) company founded in 2017 by Gbolade Emmanuel and Ayomide Awe. The company serves 26,000 businesses that send over 1.9 billion messages and calls to end customers globally, with an omni-channel platform supporting messaging across SMS, voice, email, and USSD channels.
