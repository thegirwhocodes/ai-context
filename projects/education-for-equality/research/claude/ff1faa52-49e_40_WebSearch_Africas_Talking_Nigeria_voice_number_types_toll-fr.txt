## Voice Number Types Available

Africa's Talking offers several voice number types including Toll Free, Premium, Regular, SIP, and Virtualized Physical Lines.

### Specific Number Types:

**Toll-Free Numbers:** These are numbers where the user is not charged but you as a client get billed for incoming calls.

**Virtual Numbers:** In Nigeria, virtual numbers are of the form +23417006XXX.

**Premium Numbers:** Premium numbers are where the client is billed above normal rates and the client's account is billed for incoming calls.

**Regular Numbers:** Regular numbers charge the user normal call rates and the client's account is billed for incoming and outgoing calls.

## Billing and Functionality

Toll-Free (0800), Premium Numbers (0900), and virtualized numbers do not make outgoing calls, but the regular numbers attached to them (also known as hunting numbers) can make outgoing calls.

For virtual numbers specifically, when a person dials your virtual number, they are charged normal call rates unless you have a toll-free number, and your AT account balance is charged 1/- per minute once the call goes through.
