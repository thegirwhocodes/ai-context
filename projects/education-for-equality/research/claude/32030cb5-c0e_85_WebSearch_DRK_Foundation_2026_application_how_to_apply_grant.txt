## Application Timeline (Rolling Deadline)

DRK accepts applications year round from across their priority geographies and sectors, with an initial response expected within 8–10 weeks as part of their rolling application timeline, but formal diligence can take 2–4 months for selected candidates.

## Grant Amount

DRK provides up to $300,000 USD in either unrestricted grant funding or investment capital over a three-year period, usually in multiple tranches.

## How to Apply

Applications are accepted year-round through an online submission process, which typically takes 30 to 60 minutes to complete. If you don't have existing materials, you can create a simple two-to-three-page document responding to each of the prompts; please note that responding in bulletpoints is acceptable.

## Application Process Stages

The DRK application involves multiple stages:

1. Early interviews where you'll have exploratory conversations with a small group of DRK team members, with roughly 20% of applicants making it to this stage
2. Formal diligence, which typically takes 2-4 months and involves a series of conversations with you and those who understand your work, complemented by research, with additional materials including legal and financial documents requested
3. Meeting with additional DRK team members including investment committee members, followed by a closing process to finalize the deal

For questions about applying, you can contact info@drkfoundation.org.
