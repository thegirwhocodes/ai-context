## Founding and Leadership

OPay was officially launched in August 2018 by Yahui Zhou, the Chinese tech entrepreneur behind Opera Limited, after his team identified a clear gap in Nigeria's financial ecosystem: soaring mobile penetration versus limited, costly access to traditional banking.

## Early Strategic Approach

The founding team pursued a WeChat-inspired super-app model, debuting a zero-fee mobile wallet that rapidly disrupted fee-heavy incumbents. To overcome distrust in purely digital services and poor banking infrastructure, they recruited thousands of local agents as human ATMs - a hybrid distribution strategy that fueled viral peer-to-peer adoption during an early Lagos pilot and prompted a swift pivot from a browser payment gateway to a full-scale fintech ecosystem.

## Initial Funding and Early Employees

Zhou leveraged experience scaling global internet platforms and secured a $50 million Series A from Opera and Chinese VCs including Sequoia China and IDG Capital to build a mobile-first fintech. 

Dauda Gotring currently serves as the Managing Director/CEO of OPay Nigeria, leading the local team that oversees daily operations for over 50 million users.

## Rapid Expansion

After a successful Series A, OPay raised $120 million in Series B (late 2019) and $400 million in a 2021 Series C led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2, valuing the company at $2 billion. Its blend of wallet, transport, and utility services helped it amass 40 million users and a 600,000-agent network by early 2026.
