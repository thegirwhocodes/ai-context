## Key Principles for App Names

**Syllable Count & Structure**

The best app names are 2–3 syllables, easy to say, and fast to search in app stores. More specifically, 1-3 syllable names are generally best and easy to remember.

**Syllable Psychology**

Brand names with two syllables and a stressed-unstressed syllable pattern (called a trochee) – like Starbucks – sound more accented, urgent and lively. Meanwhile, Netflix and TicTac follow a spondaic format with two stressed syllables, giving the effect of forcefulness or intensity.

**Sound Quality (Euphony)**

A pleasing smoothness of sound (euphony) comes from using long vowels, liquid consonants (l, r), and semi-vowels (w, y), along with the avoidance of adjacent stresses.

**Memorability Factors**

Names that are easy to pronounce and spell have a 67% higher recall rate. Memorability is often tied to rhythm, simplicity, and mental imagery—shorter names generally win, but short isn't enough.

**Brandability vs. Description**

Overly literal names can limit brand expansion, so many tools offer "brandable" modes that create unique, flexible options.

The most successful approach combines short, smooth syllables with distinct pronunciation and semantic clarity for your specific category.
