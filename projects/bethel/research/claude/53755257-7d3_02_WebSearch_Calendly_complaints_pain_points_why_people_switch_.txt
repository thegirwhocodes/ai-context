## Pricing & Scalability Issues

Users cite Calendly's per-seat pricing model as the primary factor driving their switch, becoming expensive quickly as teams grow. Calendly's cost can quickly add up, especially when you have a large team and need to pay a separate fee for every user.

## Limited Customization

While Calendly covers the basics well, teams that need custom flows, advanced routing, conditional logic, or tailored booking rules often hit a ceiling quickly. Users needing more granular control over their scheduling workflows may find Calendly's customization options lacking, with branding capabilities like custom CSS, branded booking pages, or white-label solutions either limited or unavailable unless opting for the most expensive plans.

## Missing Business Features

Payment integration is a main reason people pick alternatives like Acuity over Calendly in practice. Teams move beyond Calendly because it lacks built-in meeting features, note taking, summaries, CRM workflows, complex routing, and real-time AI appointment scheduling.

## Apple Calendar Integration Removal

Calendly no longer supports iCloud Calendar integration for new users, which is problematic for people deeply invested in Apple who manage their schedule mainly through Apple Calendar.

## Customer Support Issues

Support concerns drive users to explore alternatives, as Calendly's growing user base has led some customers to report slow response times, generic troubleshooting, or difficulty resolving billing and technical issues, particularly for those on lower-tier plans.
