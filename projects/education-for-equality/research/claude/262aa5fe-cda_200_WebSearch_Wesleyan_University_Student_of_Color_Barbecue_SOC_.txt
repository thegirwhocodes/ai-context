## Overview

The SOC BBQ is an annual event open to all of campus to celebrate the presence of students of color at Wesleyan. Features include cultural performances and art, cuisine from a range of culinary traditions, information about student affinity organizations, and more.

## Purpose and Goals

The goal of the event is to build community amongst students of color on campus, with the hope that students can get connected to one another and be provided a space where their experiences and talents can be celebrated and uplifted. The event features SOC-run clubs and performers of color, allowing underrepresented students to come together and showcase their talents in a warm, welcoming environment, while also learning about and getting involved with student-led initiatives to foster community among minority groups on campus.

## History and Development

The SOC BBQ began in the '90s thanks to efforts from the Black Student Union and other SOC-run organizations to create a communal space, and was revamped in 2018 with support by the Resource Center to ensure its continuity.

## Recent Event Details

At the most recent event held on Friday, Sept. 19, Malcolm X House drew a crowd of approximately 500 students, with activities featuring student performances, catered food, an ice cream truck, student organization tables, and lawn games and face painting along High Street.
