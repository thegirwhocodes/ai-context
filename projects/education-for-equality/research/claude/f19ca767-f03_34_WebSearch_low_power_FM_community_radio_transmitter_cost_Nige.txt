## Nigeria Licensing

In Nigeria, the application form for a broadcast license costs ₦50,000.00 (Fifty Thousand Naira). For a Community Radio license, the approved transmitter power is 250 watts maximum with a mast height above ground level of 150ft maximum.

FM (Frequency Modulation) licenses are available for all comers including federal, state, local government, communities and private operators.
