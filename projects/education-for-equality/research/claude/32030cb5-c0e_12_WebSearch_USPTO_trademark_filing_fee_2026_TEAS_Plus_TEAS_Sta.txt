## Key Change: TEAS Plus and TEAS Standard Consolidated

The USPTO replaced the two types of application fees—Trademark Electronic Application System (TEAS) Plus and TEAS Standard—with one base application fee for applications under Trademark Act Sections 1 and 44. These fees became effective February 18, 2025.

## Current Base Filing Fee

The base application filing fee for each class of goods or services in a Section 1 or Section 44 application is $350 if the application meets the requirements.

## Additional Surcharge Fees

National applications now have a base application fee of $350 (per class) plus potentially three surcharge fees (per class) of: Insufficient information in an application ($100), Use of free-form identification of goods/services ($200), and Each additional group of 1,000 characters after the first 1,000 characters ($200).

However, the USPTO expects the majority of applicants will only pay the base fee and won't be subject to additional fees.

This new unified system replaces the previous distinction between TEAS Plus ($225-$250) and TEAS Standard ($275-$350) filing options.
