## Nigeria Public School Infrastructure Crisis: 2024-2025 Statistics

I've found comprehensive information about Nigeria's school infrastructure challenges. Here are the key findings:

### Overall Infrastructure Deficits

According to the Universal Basic Education Commission (UBEC), over 50% of public schools in Nigeria lack adequate classrooms, desks, and basic amenities such as water and sanitation facilities.

### Water and Sanitation Facilities

A 2022 National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) survey revealed that 62% of schools lack access to potable water, and 78% of schools do not have functional toilets.

### Classroom Conditions

In urban centers, overcrowded classrooms are the norm, with student-to-classroom ratios sometimes exceeding 100:1. Additionally, approximately 60 percent of schools need repairs, and over 4,000 public primary schools lack classrooms, forcing classes under trees.

### Other Learning Facilities

Only 35% of schools have a library, and fewer than 10% have science laboratories.

### Funding Challenges

In the 2025 fiscal year, the Nigerian government allocated N3.52 trillion to education, approximately 7.3% of the total national budget, which still falls short of the 15–20% benchmark recommended by UNESCO.

### Teacher-to-Student Ratios

The pupil-qualified teacher ratio is a staggering 58:1.

These statistics demonstrate the severe infrastructure crisis affecting Nigeria's public school system, with critical gaps in basic facilities essential for quality education.
