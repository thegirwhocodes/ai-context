## Top ASL Recognition Solutions

**SignAll SDK**
SignAll provides an SDK to integrate ASL recognition into your app or software, with fingerspelling recognition and sign language recognition based on a unique, manually annotated database. Unlike SignAll Chat, SignAll SDK is able to run on any device without additional hardware.

**Sign-Speak API/SDK**
Sign-Speak offers products including an API/SDK for AI sign language translation and captioning. You can embed AI-powered sign language recognition and translation directly into your product with just a few lines of code.

**SignStream API (Signapse)**
SignStream is a real-time AI translator for BSL and ASL that brings phrases to life in real time, perfect for tools and apps, and is lightweight and easy to integrate into your stack. Note that the API is entirely AI-generated with no human grammar checking, and the output may include grammatical inaccuracies, so it is not suitable for formal, legal, or official use.

**NVIDIA Signs Platform**
NVIDIA has developed Signs, an interactive web platform that includes an AI tool analyzing webcam footage to provide real-time feedback on signing, supporting ASL learning and accessible AI application development. NVIDIA is building a validated dataset of up to 400,000 video clips representing 1,000 signed words, slated to be available to the public for building accessible technologies.

**GenASL (AWS)**
GenASL is a generative AI-powered solution that translates speech or text into expressive ASL avatar animations.
