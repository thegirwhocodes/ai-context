## Madagascar RCT Results

A recent impact evaluation of TaRL in Madagascar found that for students in grade three, mathematics learning improved by 0.56 standard deviations of test scores.

Multiple high-quality studies have found that the TaRL approach is an effective way to improve learning outcomes, in most cases increasing test scores by 0.1 to 0.3 standard deviations per student, with impact estimates as high as 0.7 standard deviations in one evaluation. A recent randomized controlled trial (RCT) in Madagascar finds an average increase of 0.56 standard deviations per student.

## Additional Details on Madagascar Interventions

The Madagascar intervention was part of a broader package called PMAQ-TaRL. In Madagascar, the Ministry of Education received technical cooperation from the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) and experimented with a package of interventions called PMAQ-TaRL. The package is mainly composed of two parts: a component to strengthen the management capacity of the school management committee (SMC) and a component to improve the pedagogical skills of teachers and community volunteers in basic reading and in numbers and the four basic operations.

PMAQ-TaRL increased the proportion of students who could read a paragraph or story by 25.3 percent points for grade 3, 25.6 percent points for grade 4, and 14.4 percent points for grade 5.
