I found comprehensive information about RTI's IVR initiative in Malawi. Here are the key results and outcomes:

## Program Overview

RTI's MERIT program designed and launched a two-week remote training using IVR, SMS messages, introductory voice calls, content messages, small group discussions and review calls. This large-scale remote training approach allowed MERIT to safely reach over 15,000 instructional leaders, including section heads, head teachers and Primary Education Advisors, to refresh their knowledge on how best to support teachers in the classroom.

## Learning Outcomes

Participants reported positive content retention, and improved confidence with the content. More specifically, in Malawi, 83% of participants overall answered the content retention question correctly.

## Time Investment

The Malawi training was shorter and consisted mostly of IVR content with the opportunity to participate in WhatsApp discussion groups with a facilitator, with participants reporting spending on average 128 minutes on the content.

## Viability Assessment

Ongoing monitoring and real-time feedback allowed MERIT to adjust along the way and conclude that IVR is a viable method for refresher training, that should be considered even after students and teachers return to school.

## Implementation Lessons Learned

The program also documented several key lessons, including the importance of Primary Education Advisors using WhatsApp to communicate to teachers within their school cluster about participating in the program and the need to stagger message delivery by education divisions, with each of Malawi's six education divisions assigned a time window within which it would receive calls.
