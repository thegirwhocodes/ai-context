## WesNest Platform

WesNest is the main platform of information for and about student groups, as well as campus events. It's where you can re-register your student group, check your student group's finances, send out messages to people in your group, request funding from the Student Budget Committee, and submit contract requests.

## Food Sales and Fundraiser Registration

All fundraisers and events that will be selling or giving away food items must be registered, and have the space/table reserved through WesNest. This requirement would apply to activities like bake sales.

## Event Registration Requirements

All student-led/student-sponsored events must be registered with the Office of Student Involvement via WesNest. The student group hosting the event must submit and register the event through WesNest no less than 7 business days (14 business days, depending on the space) in advance.

## Financial Restrictions

It's worth noting that food is not approved for student club/organization purchasing (except for identity groups sponsoring cultural events), though this restriction applies to university-funded purchases rather than fundraising activities.
