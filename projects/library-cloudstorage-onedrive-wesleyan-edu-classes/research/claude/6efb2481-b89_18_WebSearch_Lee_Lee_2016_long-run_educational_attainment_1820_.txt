The Lee and Lee 2016 dataset accompanies the paper "Human Capital in the Long Run," published in the Journal of Development Economics, vol. 122, pp. 147-169.

**Dataset Coverage:**
The dataset includes full data sets on estimated school enrollment ratios from 1820 to 2010 and estimated educational attainment for the total, female and male populations from 1870 to 2010, with estimates available in five-year intervals for 111 countries.

**Download Location:**
You can download the list of variables and countries in this education dataset, and download full data sets on estimated school enrollment ratios from 1820 to 2010 and estimated educational attainment from the Barro-Lee dataset websites. The primary download pages are located at:
- https://barrolee.github.io/BarroLeeDataSet/LongTermData.html
- http://barrolee.com/?page_id=115

**Additional Features:**
Data sources of census/survey enrollment ratios and years of school establishment are also available, and figures for trend in school enrollments and total years of schooling, disaggregated by education level and gender, are available for individual countries.
