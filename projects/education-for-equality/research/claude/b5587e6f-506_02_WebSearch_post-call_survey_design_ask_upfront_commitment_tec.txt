## Key Techniques for Improving Post-Call Survey Completion

**Upfront Commitment and Transparency:**
Begin your survey with a brief introduction explaining its purpose and length (for example: "This will take less than 90 seconds"). This transparency increases participation rates and reduces mid-survey abandonment, as customers appreciate knowing exactly what they're committing to before they begin.

**Transfer to Survey Before Hangup:**
Encourage callers to stay on the line and complete a post call survey before transferring them, or follow up with a second call shortly after. If you wait too long, the client is unlikely to recall the type of service they received, which reduces their willingness to participate and the value of the data they supply.

**Survey Design for Higher Completion:**
- The key to a successful post-call survey is brevity. Customers are typically eager to finish their call, and they may not have the time or patience to answer long or complex questions. Aim to limit the survey to 3 questions, making sure each one is easy to answer and relevant to the customer's experience.

- Set up automatic triggers for the survey to be sent immediately after the call ends. Automation guarantees consistency and eliminates delays in data capture.

These techniques work together to create a streamlined experience that maximizes completion rates by reducing friction and making the commitment clear upfront.
