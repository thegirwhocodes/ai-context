## Sphereify - Relationship Management App

Sphereify is a relationship-management app designed to help you stay connected to the people who matter most by combining your existing phone contacts with customizable relationship types, origin tags, and AI-powered text prompts.

**Key Features:**

1. **Contact Management**: Sphereify integrates with your device's Contacts, automatically populating names, phone numbers, and email addresses, and you can edit each contact's Relationship type and Origin to track where or how you met.

2. **Notifications**: Each contact has a "Last Contacted" date, and Sphereify will notify you when someone is overdue for a check-in based on user-defined thresholds.

3. **AI Text Suggestions**: The app can craft short, friendly messages, considering the contact's name, your relationship, and how you first met to propose conversation starters.

4. **Statistics & Insights**: The Statistics screen tracks how many people are overdue and shows average, minimum, and maximum days since your last contact, with a distribution of your relationship types and contact origins.

The app helps you build and maintain meaningful connections by blending straightforward contact management with helpful AI-assisted conversation starters and a robust notifications system.
