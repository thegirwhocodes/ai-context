# Wesleyan University Food Sales & Preparation Guidelines

## Required Approvals & Timeline
All fundraisers and events selling or giving away food must be registered through WesNest with space/table reservations. "Requests must be made at least five business days prior to the planned event." The Director of Usdan University Center consults with Bon Appétit and Middletown Health Inspector to determine if activities may proceed. "Events may not proceed until all permissions are received."

## Required Documentation
Groups must provide:
- Complete ingredient lists
- Storage locations (before and after preparation)
- Transportation methods
- Sales/serving procedures
- Cost and revenue projections
- "Signed waiver indicating the Bon Appétit management company is not responsible for any portion of the food preparation or distribution"

## Prohibited Items
- Coffee or direct competition with campus dining
- Items containing alcohol
- "Events where food preparation would occur within the Usdan University Center"
- Potentially hazardous foods not meeting health guidelines
- Items already available through Campus Dining

## Where Food Can Be Prepared
Food preparation cannot occur inside Usdan. However, groups may coordinate co-sponsored activities with Bon Appétit where the food service provider handles preparation.

## Pre-Packaged vs. Homemade
"Pre-packaged items and bake sale items do not typically present a problem but will still be run through the same screening." Refrigerated or temperature-controlled items face stricter scrutiny and possible denial.

## No Liability/Insurance/Certifications Mentioned
The document contains no requirements for food handler certifications, liability insurance, or commercial kitchen access.
