# Prophetic Dream Interpretation Guide: Content Extraction

## Core Premise
"one-third of the Bible is God speaking through dreams, visions, and the prophetic" — establishing biblical foundation for dream significance.

## Dream Recording Methodology
The guide emphasizes five essential practices:
- Document immediately with emotional details
- Establish narrative perspective (participant vs. observer)
- Compare symbolic elements (why this object vs. another?)
- Note color significance and atmospheric conditions
- Identify individuals and their relational context

## Symbolic Categories

**Animals** (60+ entries): Spiritual representations ranging from positive (dove = Holy Spirit, lamb = Christ) to negative (snake = deception, fox = cunning). Each animal carries dual interpretations based on context.

**Body Parts** (15+ entries): Physical features represent spiritual conditions—eyes symbolize perception, feet indicate one's walk, nakedness suggests transparency or humiliation.

**Buildings/Places** (25+ entries): Structural contexts reveal life situations—hospitals indicate healing seasons, elevators suggest spiritual ascension/descent, houses represent personal ministry or family dynamics.

**Clothing** (10+ entries): Garments convey anointing status—wedding dress = covenant, tattered clothing = neglected anointing, swimwear = spiritual mobility.

**Colors** (12 entries): Spectrum meanings—gold (purity/idolatry), purple (royalty/false authority), white (holiness/legalism), green (growth/envy).

**Directional Symbolism** (6 entries): Cardinal and relational positioning—east = beginning, north = God's authority, left = rejection, right = God's favor.

**Food** (15+ entries): Nourishment symbolism—bread = Christ/provision, milk = elementary teaching, wine = Spirit's outpouring (or drunkenness).

**Insects** (9 entries): Generally negative—spiders = witchcraft, scorpions = evil attacks, butterflies = transformation (occasionally positive).

**Metals** (6 entries): Material symbolism—gold = divine nature, iron = bondage, silver = redemption.

**Nature/Weather** (15+ entries): Environmental indicators—fire = God's presence, floods = blessing or judgment, mountains = prayer places or obstacles, valleys = spiritual darkness.

## Additional Symbolic Elements
- **Miscellaneous** (25+ entries): Keys = authority, crowns = rulership, drowning = depression, pregnancy = promise in process
- **Numbers Reference**: Guide directs readers to separate resource "_Numbers That Prophesy_" for numerical interpretation

## Interpretation Principles

The methodology prioritizes personal context over generic meanings, encouraging dreamers to:
- Assess emotional responses within dreams
- Track recurring symbols across multiple dreams
- Examine color intensity and atmospheric quality
- Recognize personal vs. prophetic dreams through structural analysis
