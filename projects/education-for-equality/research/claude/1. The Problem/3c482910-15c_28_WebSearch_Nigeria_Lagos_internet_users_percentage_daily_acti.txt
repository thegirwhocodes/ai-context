## Overall Nigeria Internet Statistics

As of January 2025, there were 107 million internet users in Nigeria. The number of internet users increased by 2.0 million (+1.9 percent) between January 2024 and January 2025.

As of February 2025, Nigeria registered approximately half of its total population as internet users. Additionally, 128 million people in Nigeria did not use the internet at the beginning of 2025, suggesting that 54.6 percent of the population remained "offline" at the start of the year.

## Lagos Internet Subscribers

Lagos State maintained its position as Nigeria's digital capital with 17.59 million internet subscribers, accounting for about 12.4% of total subscriptions nationwide as of Q2 2025. However, subscriptions declined by 147,354 year-on-year from 17.74 million in Q2 2024.

The breakdown by operator in Lagos shows 8.22 million MTN, 5.87 million Airtel, 2.96 million Glo, and 0.50 million EMTS users.

## NCC 2024 Statistics

In Q1 2024, the total number of active internet subscribers stood at 164,368,292 from 157,551,104 reported in Q1 2023, showing an increase of 4.33%. Lagos state had the highest number of active internet subscribers in Q1 2024 with 18,841,943.
