Here's the straight answer, pulled from Lindy's own pricing page.

**Who Lindy is built for:** it's a **horizontal, self-serve, no-code AI-agent platform** — you describe what you want in plain English ("triage my inbox," "research leads and update my CRM") and it builds the agent. Their sweet spot is **entrepreneurs, sales teams, customer-success teams, and busy professionals** who are comfortable configuring their own automations. It's a DIY tool, not a done-for-you service.

**The tiers — same product, more usage capacity as you climb:**

| Tier | Price | Who it's for | Key limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plus** | **$49.99/mo** | *Individual professionals* wanting basic AI help | Standard usage, **2 inboxes**, iMessage/SMS chat, email drafting, scheduling, notes |
| Pro | $99.99/mo | Moderate workloads | 3× usage, 3 inboxes, computer-use, model choice |
| Max | $199.99/mo | Heavy users, demanding workflows | 7× usage, 5 inboxes |
| Enterprise | Custom | Orgs needing SSO, HIPAA, audit logs | Team + compliance |
| ~~Human Assistant (legacy)~~ | $8,000/mo | — | Real human, being phased out |

So **the $50 buyer is a single professional running their own inbox** — one or two email accounts, willing to set it up themselves. The $200 (Max) buyer is a power user juggling up to 5 inboxes.

**Why this matters for your play:** Lindy proves the price ladder is real, but it's **self-serve and generic**. A realtor doesn't want to *build an agent* — they want someone to hand them a thing that just catches their leads. That's your wedge: at $200 you're not competing with Lindy's $50 DIY tier, you're competing with their $200 tier **while offering what they don't — a done-for-you service, set up for you, tuned to real estate.** And Lindy's own $8,000/mo human-assistant line is the anchor that makes your "fraction of an assistant" framing land.
