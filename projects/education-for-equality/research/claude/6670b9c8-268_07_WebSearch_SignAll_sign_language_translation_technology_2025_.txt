## Overview of SignAll

SignAll is a developer of AI-based sign language translation solution for deaf and hard of hearing communities. SignAll is a seed company based in Budapest (Hungary), founded in 2016 by Zsolt Robotka and Janos Rovnyai.

## Technology and Products

The technology is built using AI and computer vision to recognize and translate sign language. It utilizes three webcams, a depth sensor, and a PC. The PC syncs up and processes the images in real-time, which are then transformed into grammatically correct, fully formed sentences by a natural language processing module.

SignAll 1.0 is the first product in the world to allow for real-time communication between deaf signers and hearing speakers through automated American Sign Language (ASL) translation technology.

## Recent Development Status (2025)

As of June 2025 they rate their current system at tier 2, which they describe as foundational but they have plans to reach tier 5 (advanced) in December.

## Products and Applications

SignAll Learn Lab is an interactive, AI-powered workstation to learn and practice American Sign Language. The SignAll system can be used in business and education settings. The technology increases accessibility for deaf employees in workplaces and allows companies to offer better customer service for deaf customers.

## Funding

SignAll has raised a total funding of $3.63M over 2 rounds from 5 investors.
