# DTIC Document Collection Timeline

Based on the Wikipedia article, here's what's documented about DTIC's collection history:

**Collection Start:**
DTIC traces its origins to June 1945 when the "Air Documents Research Center (ADRC)" was established as "a joint effort of the US Air Force, US Navy, and the United Kingdom's Royal Air Force." The center was "established to build a single collection of captured German aeronautical research."

**Early Collection Focus:**
The initial task involved sorting documents into three categories: those useful for the Pacific theater, those of immediate intelligence interest, and those valuable for future research.

**Collection Growth:**
After relocating to Wright Field in Ohio and being renamed the Air Documents Division, the organization eventually broadened its mission in 1948 "to include collecting, processing, and disseminating technical information." By the 1950s, ASTIA (the renamed organization) was developing "library catalog systems to organize its growing holdings."
