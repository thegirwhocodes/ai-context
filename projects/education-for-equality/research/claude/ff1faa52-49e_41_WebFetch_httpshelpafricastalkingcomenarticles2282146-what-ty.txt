# Voice Phone Number Types

Based on Africa's Talking documentation, here are the available voice number options:

## Number Types

**Toll Free (0800)**
"These are numbers where the user is not dialed but you as a client gets billed for incoming calls." Callers reach you at no cost to them.

**Premium (0900)**
"Numbers where the client is billed above normal rates and Client's account billed for incoming calls." Higher revenue-sharing option for inbound traffic.

**Regular**
"The user is billed normal call rates and the Client's account billed for incoming and outgoing calls." Standard bidirectional calling with normal pricing.

**SIP**
"Uses the internet Client's account is billed for incoming and outgoing calls." Internet-based calling solution.

**Virtualized Physical Lines**
"Billed normal call rates Client's account billed for incoming and outgoing calls." Traditional-style lines with standard pricing.

## Best for Free Inbound Calls

**Toll-free numbers are optimal** when you want users to call without charges. The client absorbs all costs, removing barriers to contact. However, toll-free lines cannot initiate outbound calls—you'd need attached regular "hunting numbers" for that functionality.

For pricing details, consult Africa's Talking' [voice pricing page](https://africastalking.com/voice#pricing).
