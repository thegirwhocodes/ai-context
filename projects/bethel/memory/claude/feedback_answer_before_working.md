When Naomi asks a question or presents options, explain and get confirmation before executing.

**Why:** Multiple times in the session, Naomi asked "answer before working" or "answer before going." She wants to understand what Claude will do and why before it starts doing it.

**Exceptions:**
- When she says "do it" -- proceed immediately
- When she says "yes" or "continue" -- proceed
- When she says "forget what I said, let's go with original plan" -- proceed with original

**How to apply:**
- Present options with tradeoffs
- Give cost/time/space estimates when relevant
- Wait for confirmation on architectural decisions
- Don't start building until the approach is agreed on
- But don't over-explain -- be concise
