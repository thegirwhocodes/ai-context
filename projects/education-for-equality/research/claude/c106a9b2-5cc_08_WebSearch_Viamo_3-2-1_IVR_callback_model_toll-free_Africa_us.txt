## Overview of the 3-2-1 Service

The Viamo 3-2-1 Service leverages Viamo's proprietary multi-channel mobile platform and uses Interactive Voice Response (IVR) technology that allows computers to interact with humans through voice tones and number inputs.

## Toll-Free Access and User Experience

Users can dial a toll-free number (often the shortcode "3-2-1") at a time and place of their choosing, confidentially and anonymously. Key messages are accessed through a series of listen-and-choose steps where the user navigates through IVR decision trees, and callers can navigate menus, play games, take quizzes, schedule call-backs, provide feedback, receive airtime payment, etc.

## User Engagement Metrics

The service has shown strong engagement across Africa. On average, 3-2-1 calls last almost 3 minutes and callers spend approximately 14 minutes per month on the Service. In some countries like Rwanda, 3-2-1 is reaching more unique users than Facebook, and in Mali, there are more than 2,000 people listening to the 3-2-1 content at any given moment for most of the day.

## Callback Features

Advanced campaign features allow listeners to repeat messages if needed, request a callback, and receive tailored content based on demographics such as gender, location, and age.
