## Overview of MIT Solve

MIT Solve has launched over 100 challenges and received over 26,800 applications from 185 countries, forging over 1,000 partnerships and mobilizing over $80 million in funding to 500 solutions poised to scale.

## Global Challenges and Application

Solve's Global Challenges are open for submission from September to November each year in five key areas: Climate, Learning, Health, Economic Prosperity, and Indigenous Communities, launching an open call for tech-based innovations. Selected solutions become Solvers, receiving funding, connections to the network, resources within MIT and beyond, and tailored support.

## Education/Learning Focus

Application clinics are workshops designed to support individuals or teams applying to Solve's open innovation challenges, providing expert guidance, feedback, and resources to help applicants refine their submissions.
