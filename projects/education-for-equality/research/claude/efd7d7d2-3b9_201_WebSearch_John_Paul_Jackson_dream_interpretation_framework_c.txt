## Background

John Paul Jackson (1950–2015) was an authority on biblical dream interpretation for over thirty years. He was the founder of Streams Ministries International, a Christian ministry which deals especially with reforming the modern practice of prophecy and biblical dream interpretation.

## Dream Interpretation Framework

Jackson has interpreted over 50,000 dreams and has taught dream interpretation around the world. His approach emphasizes that regardless of culture, there are universal symbols that communicate specific truths.

## Categories and Types

A key component of Jackson's framework is 20 different categories of dreams to help you interpret your own dreams. His training curriculum also includes comprehensive elements: Basic Types and Symbols, Intuitive Dream Recording, Basic Interpretation Skills, Dream Function, 20 Categories of Dreams, Spectrums of Light, Parabolic Principles in Dreams, Elements of Dreams, Colors in Dreams, Five Ways of Recording Dreams, How to Remember Your Dreams, and Developing Your Dream Vocabulary.

## Common Dream Symbols

Jackson documented interpretations for common dream symbols. For example, he identified meanings such as: Flying indicates a person has the ability to rise above problems and move in the spiritual realm, and Falling means some part of a person's life is out of control, or the person is losing control and is growing anxious.
