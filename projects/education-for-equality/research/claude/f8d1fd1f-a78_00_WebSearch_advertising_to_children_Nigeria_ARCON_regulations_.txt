## Key Regulations for Advertising to Children

The ARCON Act prohibits advertisements that exploit the inexperience or credulity of children and restricts the use of misleading or exaggerated claims that could misinform children.

## Restrictions on Specific Product Categories

For sensitive product categories, ARCON has implemented specific restrictions:

- **Alcoholic Beverages**: Alcoholic beverage advertising is regulated by NAFDAC and ARCON, restricting adverts during children's programmes and mandating "Drink Responsibly" and "18+" disclaimers, while prohibiting misleading health claims.

- **Tobacco Products**: Tobacco products are governed by the National Tobacco Control Act 2015, which prohibits all forms of tobacco advertising, promotion, and sponsorship, except at point-of-sale locations with strict restrictions.

## Protection of Children's Personal Information

Article 80(b)(ii) of the Advertising Code provides that identifiable personal information about children should be disclosed to third parties only after obtaining parental consent or where authorised by law.

## Enforcement Against Misleading Claims

ARCON has actively enforced protections against misleading advertising that targets vulnerable populations, including children. Advertisements often prey on vulnerable populations by claiming unrealistic results, and ARCON, in collaboration with NAFDAC, has cracked down on such advertisements (especially on TV and radio).
