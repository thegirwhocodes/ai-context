I've found search results related to Africa's Talking Voice API and phone number management. Here are the key findings:

## Voice Phone Numbers

Toll-Free (0800), Premium Numbers (0900) and virtualized numbers do not make outgoing calls but the regular numbers attached to them (also known as hunting numbers) can make the outgoing calls.

## Requesting Phone Numbers

To request a number, you will go to your app dashboard and under Voice->Request pick regular number.

## Callback Configuration

A callback URL is important if you want to know what is happening with your calls - this is where Africa's Talking POSTs details of a call. To use callback, you need to configure your callback, top up your account and proceed to make calls.

## Production Setup

You will be issued with a live number to use in the production account for a 2 weeks test period after which it will be removed.

## Nigeria-Specific Information

The search results reference Nigeria in the context of Africa's Talking services, and USSD push currently works in Nigeria only and requires you to have registered a callback URL in your account.

For more detailed technical assistance, you can reach out to voice@africastalking.com for technical assistance.
