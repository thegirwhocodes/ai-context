## Basic T-Bill Purchase Information

The minimum purchase for Treasury Bills is $100 with multiples sold in the same increment.

## Foreign National Eligibility and Requirements

Foreign nationals can buy Treasury bills through TreasuryDirect.gov. However, there are specific requirements: To open a TreasuryDirect account, an individual or entity account manager must have a valid social security number (SSN), be 18 years of age or over, and be legally competent.

Additionally, the account owner must have a United States address of record and have an account at a United States depository financial institution that will accept debits and credits using the Automated Clearing House method of payment.

## Tax Considerations for Non-Citizens

Non-U.S. citizens must fill out and include IRS Form W-8BEN when handling Treasury bonds, which relates to tax withholding on interest earned.

## Obstacles for Non-Residents

A key challenge for non-residents is the U.S. address requirement. The search results indicate that mechanisms that inhibit easy online access to TreasuryDirect include IP geolocation blocking or SMS verification that only works on U.S. mobiles, similar to difficulties faced by U.S. citizens living abroad.

The practical obstacles for non-resident foreign nationals mainly involve establishing a U.S. banking relationship, obtaining an SSN where applicable, and maintaining a U.S. address of record.
