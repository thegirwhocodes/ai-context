## CcHub–Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship 2026 (Cohort IV)

Applications are now open for the 2026 CcHub–Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship Programme (Cohort IV). This fellowship is co-created by Co-Creation Hub (CcHUB) and the Mastercard Foundation, supporting early-stage Nigerian EdTech startups that build inclusive, context-relevant learning solutions for underserved communities.

**Funding and Support:**
Selected startups will receive $100,000 in equity-free funding, alongside deep technical support, mentorship, and ecosystem access to scale their impact.

**Application Deadline:**
Interested and eligible startups should apply before April 10, 2026, via the official programme portal.

**Who Can Apply:**
This fellowship targets ventures innovating for learners who are often underserved by mainstream educational technology — including learners with disabilities, refugees, rural communities, and girls and women.

**Additional Resources:**
You can find more information on the Future of Learning website at https://futureoflearning.cchub.africa/ or apply through the official CcHub portal.
