## IVR Applications in Africa

The 3-2-1 Service is an IVR system that allows users to interact with a company's host system using a telephone keypad and a toll-free number, offering automated mobile hotline services providing information in six languages on development topics including health and agriculture.

IVR is a telephony system that interacts with callers, records information, and directs calls to an appropriate database of prerecorded information in voice form, and can accept telephone input through touch-tone keypad selection.

## DTMF Accuracy and Design Challenges

Telephone-based information services commonly rely on push-button (DTMF) input, primarily because high accuracy automatic speech recognition (ASR), essential for end-to-end spoken interaction, is not available for several languages in developing regions. High accuracy ASR alone is not sufficient for effective, inclusive speech interfaces, with existing services showing low task success rates despite using very high accuracy ASR.

## Low Literacy Design

In one Northern Uganda study, 74% of low-literacy rural women completed a long IVR survey. Participants preferred local language and voice interfaces, highlighting the potential of local language IVR to effectively boost health literacy in underserved areas.
