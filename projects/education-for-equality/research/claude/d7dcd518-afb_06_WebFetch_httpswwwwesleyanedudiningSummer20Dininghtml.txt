# Wesleyan Summer Dining 2026

**Start Date & Hours:**
Summer dining begins "Tuesday, 5/26/26 and runs through 8/25/26" with service "daily from 11am - 1pm and 5pm-7pm."

**Location:**
Dining is located in **Summerfields**, which is the only campus venue open during summer.

**Meal Plans:**
Undergraduate summer residents must apply for a plan regardless of financial aid status. Two options are available:
- 50 swipes for $500
- 150 swipes for $1,500

**Payment Methods:**
The dining operation accepts summer meal plan swipes, Middletown Cash, and credit cards. Walk-up prices are $15 for brunch and $18 for dinner (credit card), or $11 per meal with Middletown Cash (requires Wesleyan ID).
