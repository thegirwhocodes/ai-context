# IVR Post-Call Survey Best Practices for Maximizing Completion

Based on the article, here are the actionable tactics:

## Timing & Invitation Method

The article recommends **two approaches** to avoid caller abandonment:

1. **Ask permission during the call**: "Asking customers if they'd like to leave feedback at the end of the call. If they say yes, agents can direct them to the IVR survey"

2. **Pre-announcement strategy**: "Provide a recorded message at the beginning of the call letting customers know that if they stay on the line following the call, they will have an opportunity to leave feedback"

**Critical caveat**: Don't require upfront commitment. The piece warns against having "people press a button at the beginning of the call to do a survey at the end" since callers haven't yet experienced the interaction.

## Keeping Surveys Concise

**Ask fewer than 5 questions** to prevent fatigue and encourage completion. The author emphasizes that customers are "taking time out of their busy lives" and should receive "survey fatigue" avoidance through brevity.

## No Callback Approach

Avoid requesting callbacks: don't "ask for a customer's number to call back later—again, you want the feedback while it's fresh." This maintains response momentum and captures authentic sentiment immediately post-interaction.
