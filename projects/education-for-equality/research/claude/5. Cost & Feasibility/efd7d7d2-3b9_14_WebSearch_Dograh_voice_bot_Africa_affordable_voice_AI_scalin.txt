Building voice bots for places like Nigeria is very different - accents vary, languages are mixed, network quality is uneven, and user behavior is unpredictable. Specific challenges include:

- English blended with local languages, slang, Pidgin, and mixed cadence

- Many ASR systems struggle with Nigerian locations, terms, and the way we naturally blend words
