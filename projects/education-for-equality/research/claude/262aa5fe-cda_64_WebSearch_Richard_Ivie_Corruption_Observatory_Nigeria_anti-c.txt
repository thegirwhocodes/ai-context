## Richard Ivie and the Corruption Observatory

Richard Ivie is the Executive Director at Corruption Observatory and Public Awareness Initiative, and serves as an ISO Governance Expert and Anti-Corruption Practitioner.

## Organization's Mission and Objectives

The organization's main objectives are to promote good governance, raise awareness on the ills of corruption, develop and deploy critical tools for good governance and anti-corruption, organize outreach programs and develop standards on ethics, and good governance.

## Standards and Professional Involvement

The organization is a member of ISO TC 278 — ISO 37001 anti-bribery management systems standard; ISO TC 309 — ISO 31000 guidance for governance of organizations, ISO 31002 guidance for whistleblowing management system standard and Chair, verification and validation subcommittee NIS 901 — Nigeria good governance standard.

## Vision

The organization's goal is one national movement sharing one vision: a nation in which government, business, civil society and the daily lives of people are free of corruption.

Corruption Observatory and Public Awareness Initiative is a Non-profit Organization located in Lagos.
