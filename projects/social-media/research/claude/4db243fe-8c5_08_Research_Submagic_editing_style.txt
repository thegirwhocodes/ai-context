## 2. WHAT MAKES SUBMAGIC'S CAPTION STYLE "VIRAL" -- FULL TECHNICAL BREAKDOWN

### 2.1 Font Styles & Sizes

Submagic offers multiple creator-specific templates. The most popular fonts in the viral caption ecosystem:

| Font | Usage | Weight | Case |
|------|-------|--------|------|
| **Anton** (Google Font) | Hormozi "new style" | 400 (only weight) | UPPERCASE |
| **TheBoldFont** (free) | Hormozi "classic style" | Bold | UPPERCASE |
| **Komika** | MrBeast "Beast" template | Bold | UPPERCASE |
| **Montserrat** | Iman Gadzhi style | Light + Bold (200-700) | lowercase |
| **Bebas Neue** | Fitness/motivation | 400 | UPPERCASE |
| **Poppins** | Lifestyle content | 600+ | Mixed |

**Size specifications:**
- Optimal: **12-20% of screen height** (for 1080x1920 vertical video, that is roughly **230-384px**)
- Font size in MoviePy/programmatic terms: **fontsize=90** for basic, up to **150+** for emphasized keywords
- MrBeast style uses only **2 words per subtitle line** (very large text)
- General best practice: **4-6 words per two-line segment**

### 2.2 Word-by-Word Highlight Animation

This is the core "viral" mechanic. How it works:

- **Base state**: All words displayed in primary color (usually white `#FFFFFF`)
- **Active/narrated word**: Highlighted in accent color as it is spoken
- **Highlight colors available in Submagic**: Orange (`#FF5100` is Submagic's brand orange), white, black, plus custom hex
- **Popular highlight colors in the ecosystem**:
  - Yellow: `#F7C204` or `#FFC107` (warm yellow, most common)
  - Green: `#4ADE80` (MrBeast's keyword color)
  - Blue: `#60A5FA`
  - Red/Orange for urgency

The karaoke-style word highlight is achieved through **word-level timestamps from Whisper transcription** -- each word gets a precise `start` and `end` time, and the highlight color/effect is applied only during that word's time window.

### 2.3 Text Effects

**Shadow Settings (industry standard):**
- Opacity: 100%
- Blur: 70%
- Distance: 14%
- Angle: 10 degrees
- Multi-layer CSS approach: `text-shadow: 1px 1px 0 black, -1px -1px 0 black, 1px -1px 0 black, -1px 1px 0 black, 2px 2px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.7)`

**Stroke/Outline:**
- Width: **5-8px** black stroke for maximum contrast
- Hormozi new style: Yellow stroke specifically
- MrBeast: Black stroke, high weight

**Animation Effects:**
- **Pop-in / Scale**: Word scales from 0.7 to 1.0 over 0.3 seconds (with optional overshoot to ~1.05)
- **Bounce**: Spring-like easing with overshoot, word drops in from above or pops up from below
- **Slide-in**: Word slides from bottom to top (Hormozi style)
- **Fade**: Opacity 0 to 1 transition
- **Glow**: Outer glow effect using layered text-shadow with the highlight color, sizes S/M/L (MrBeast "Beast" template)
- **Light-to-Bold transition**: Text weight animates from light (200) to bold (700) as word is spoken (Iman Gadzhi style)

### 2.4 Positioning and Timing

**Position:**
- Vertical center or **lower-third** of the frame (most common for short-form)
- Horizontally centered
- For 1080x1920: text centered at approximately y=960 (middle) or y=1300-1400 (lower third)

**Timing:**
- Captions appear **0.1-0.3 seconds before** the corresponding audio (read-ahead)
- Word-by-word highlight duration: determined by Whisper word-level timestamps (typically 100-500ms per word)
- Animation duration per word: **0.2-0.3 seconds** for pop/scale effects
- Max **32-42 characters per line** (5-7 words)

### 2.5 Emoji Integration

Submagic auto-inserts contextual emojis alongside captions. Technical approach:
- Emojis placed adjacent to keywords (before or after the word)
- Animated in sync with the word they accompany
- AI selects emojis based on semantic content (e.g., money-related words get a money emoji)
- Density: roughly 1 emoji per 5-10 words (varies by style)

### 2.6 Color Schemes

**Standard viral palette:**
- Primary text: `#FFFFFF` (white)
- Stroke/outline: `#000000` (black)
- Keyword highlight: `#F7C204` (yellow) or `#FF5100` (orange)
- Background box (if used): `#000000` at 50-80% opacity

**MrBeast palette:** White + Black text, Green (`#4ADE80`) highlights, colored shadows
**Hormozi palette:** White text, yellow stroke, no background box
**Gadzhi palette:** White only, no highlights, minimalist

### Tier 1: Direct Submagic-style Replication

- GitHub: `github.com/francozanardi/pycaps`
- **Best open-source match for Submagic-style captions**

- Speaker diarization with automatic colors

### Tier 2: Word-by-Word Caption Generators (Python)

**Basic MoviePy + Whisper approach** (from angel1254.com tutorial):
```python
from moviepy.editor import VideoFileClip, TextClip, CompositeVideoClip
import whisper_timestamped as whisper

# Transcribe with word-level timestamps
audio = whisper.load_audio("video.mp4")
model = whisper.load_model("small", device="cpu")
results = whisper.transcribe(model, audio, language="en")

# Generate word-by-word text clips
for segment in results["segments"]:
    for word in segment["words"]:
        clip = TextClip(
            word["text"],
            fontsize=90,
            font="Arial-Bold",
            color="white",
            stroke_width=5,
            stroke_color="white",
            method='caption'
        ).set_start(word["start"]).set_end(word["end"]).set_position("center")
```

Key libraries: `moviepy`, `whisper-timestamped`, `Pillow`

### Timing Intervals

- Whisper "small" model: Best balance of speed/accuracy for word-level timestamps
- Word gap threshold: If gap between words > 500ms, start a new caption segment

- Caption pre-roll: 100-300ms before audio onset

## Summary of Recommended Approach

For **Python-based recreation**: Use `pycaps` (most feature-complete, CSS-based styling, templates, semantic tagging) or the `moviepy` + `whisper-timestamped` stack for maximum control.

For **DaVinci Resolve**: Use the built-in Word Highlight animated subtitle effect in Resolve 20+, or `auto-subs` plugin for AI-powered transcription directly in the Resolve workflow.

The key to the "viral" look is: **bold sans-serif font (Anton/TheBoldFont) in uppercase, white text with heavy black stroke (5-8px), word-by-word yellow highlight synced to speech via Whisper timestamps, and a pop/scale animation (0.3s, starting at 70% scale) on each highlighted word.**
