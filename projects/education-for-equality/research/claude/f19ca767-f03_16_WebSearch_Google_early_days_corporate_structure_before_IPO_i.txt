## Google's Incorporation History

Google Inc. was incorporated in California in September 1998 and re-incorporated in the State of Delaware in August 2003. This means Google began as a California corporation but later shifted to Delaware before its IPO.

## Delaware C-Corporation Structure

Google was incorporated in 1998 and went public after its 2004 IPO. By the time of its IPO, Google operated as a Delaware corporation, which is a C-corporation structure. Google is among many major companies, such as Wal-Mart, Coca-Cola, and Apple, that are incorporated in Delaware.

The SEC filings confirm this structure—Google's corporate documents show it was operating under Delaware corporate law, with a registered office in Wilmington, Delaware.

## Why Delaware?

Venture capitalists and angel investors often require Delaware C corp status before investing due to its predictability and favorable governance, and Delaware supports flexible stock issuance structures such as preferred shares, stock options, and convertible debt, essential for early-stage fundraising. This made Delaware an ideal choice for a company like Google that would need to raise venture capital and later go public.
