## Recent Sign Language Recognition Projects (2024-2025)

**SlowFastSign (ICASSP 2024)**
SlowFastSign is an official implementation for Continuous Sign Language Recognition with pretrained weights available, published at ICASSP 2024. The repository is available on GitHub at `kaistmm/SlowFastSign`.

**CorrNet (CVPR 2023, Updated 2025)**
CorrNet released an enhanced version called CorrNet+ in April 2024, and in January 2025 released a demo for continuous sign language recognition supporting multi-image and video inputs. The project is implemented in PyTorch (version ≥1.13 recommended).

**Other Notable Implementations**
One project uses a pretrained I3D model for extracting spatiotemporal features from video sequences, with 3D convolutions to model motion patterns in signing gestures. Various other projects exist using CNN+LSTM, 3D CNN, GCN, and Encoder-Decoder architectures.

**Comprehensive Resource**
Several recent papers on sign language recognition were published in 2024-2025, including works on continuous sign language recognition and translation, as well as lightweight models based on MediaPipe and knowledge distillation techniques.
