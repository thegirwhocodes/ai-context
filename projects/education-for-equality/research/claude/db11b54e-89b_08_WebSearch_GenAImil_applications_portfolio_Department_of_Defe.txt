## Overview of GenAI.mil

The War Department announced the launch of Google Cloud's Gemini for Government as the first of several frontier AI capabilities to be housed on GenAI.mil, the department's new bespoke AI platform. The platform is meant to make generative AI tools available to all three million military and civilian personnel.

## Platform Launch and Rollout

By the end of 2025, 3 million employees, warfighters, and contractors were given AI access on their desktop. The tech has since been adopted by the Army, Navy, Marines, Air Force and Space Force as their preferred enterprise system.

## Available Tools

GenAI launched with Google's Gemini, then added xAI's Grok, and is deploying ChatGPT. The Pentagon's Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO) currently has contracts with four frontier AI companies: Anthropic, xAI, OpenAI and Google.

## Use Cases for Soldiers and Personnel

The first wave of use cases includes summarizing lengthy policy documents, generating compliance checklists, drafting correspondence, analyzing imagery and video, and automating routine staff processes. Pentagon employees can use Gemini to conduct deep research, format documents and even analyze video or imagery in unprecedented speed.

## Agentic AI Development

Defense officials recently used the Pentagon's enterprise-wide generative artificial intelligence platform to create 100,000 agents amid a broader push by department leadership to speed up AI adoption.

## Data Security

All of the tools available on GenAI.mil are certified for Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) and Impact Level 5 (IL5) — meaning they're secure and approved for operational use by the department.
