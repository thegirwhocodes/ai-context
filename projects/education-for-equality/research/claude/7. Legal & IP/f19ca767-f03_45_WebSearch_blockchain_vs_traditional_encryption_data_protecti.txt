## Key Differences

**Blockchain Advantages:**
- Unlike traditional databases that are controlled by a central authority, blockchain operates on a peer-to-peer network
- Once data is recorded in a block, it cannot be changed or deleted without altering all subsequent blocks
- The decentralized nature of blockchain eliminates single points of failure, reducing the risk of data breaches

**Traditional Database Advantages:**
- They handle millions of records quickly
- Cost-effective: Already existing buildings do not need additional extravagant costs

## Cost Considerations

Initial deployment and operational costs can be high when establishing a blockchain network, though long-term benefits may make up for it. In contrast, businesses that are involved in large volumes of transactions at high speeds with structured data will benefit from traditional databases as they are faster and cheaper to use.

## Educational Applications

Educational institutions can benefit from blockchain technology by securing academic records and streamlining administrative processes. Blockchain provides a transparent and tamper-proof way to manage student data and credentials.

## When to Use Each

Blockchain technology can be used when data storage systems need to be secure, reliable, and affordable—for example, in financial institutions, medical institutions, and supply chains.
