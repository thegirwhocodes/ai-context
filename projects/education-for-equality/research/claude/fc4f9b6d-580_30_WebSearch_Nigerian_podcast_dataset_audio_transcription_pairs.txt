## Nigerian Datasets for TTS Training

The Nigerian Common Voice Dataset is a comprehensive dataset consisting of 158 hours of audio recordings and corresponding transcription, specifically curated to address the gap in speech and language datasets for African accents, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers working on Text-to-Speech (TTS), Speech-to-text (STT), and other speech systems. The dataset currently consists of 158 hours of audio recordings in 4 languages, but more voices and languages are always added.

Additionally, there is a crowdsourced high-quality Nigerian English speech dataset containing transcribed audio of Nigerian English sentences recorded by volunteers, with audio files paired with transcriptions in a TSV file.

The Nigerian Accent Speech Data is a comprehensive dataset of about 8 hours of audio recordings featuring speakers from various regions of Nigeria, capturing the rich diversity of Nigerian accents, and is curated for researchers and developers working on Text-to-Speech (TTS) and other speech systems.

## TTS Dataset Compilation Best Practices

For training a TTS model, you need a dataset with speech recordings and transcriptions, where the speech must be divided into audio clips and each clip needs transcription. It is important to use a lossless audio file format to prevent compression artifacts, with WAV file format being recommended.
