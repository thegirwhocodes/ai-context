## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR PRICING STRATEGY

1. **The unskippable premium is real and substantial.** Non-skippable formats command 2-3x higher CPMs than skippable equivalents. CTV ($35 avg CPM) vs mobile video ($20); rewarded video ($10-50) vs standard video ($5-15); cinema ($30-70) vs digital pre-roll ($15-35).

2. **Host-read/native integration commands the highest audio premiums.** Baked-in host-read podcast ads ($35-55 CPM) earn 2-3x more than programmatic spots ($5-15 CPM). Radio host endorsements cost 2-3x a standard spot.

3. **Product placement pricing is driven by integration depth**, not just screen time. A visual-only placement is $20K (Disney scale); an actor using the product is $60K; a storyline integration can reach millions.

4. **Education-specific advertising is constrained but valuable.** COPPA compliance reduces targeting capability (40-60% revenue impact), but contextual advertising in educational content reaches a captive, engaged audience. Duolingo monetizes ~$50M/year from ads to its free-tier users.
