## The Bayit

The Bayit is Wesleyan's Jewish ethnocultural residential house, centrally located at 157 Church Street. Home to 22 students, the Bayit opens its doors to any member of the community wishing to use its kosher-style kitchen or library, hold a Jewish group meeting or event, or just hang out.

## Friday Night Shabbat Dinners

Every Friday evening, a Shabbat program is held at the Bayit, filled with student-led prayer, discussion, song, dinner, and socializing, with services beginning at 5 p.m. Each week, there is fresh-baked challah followed by a meal cooked on Friday afternoon by fellow students. Shabbat Dinners are made with Kosher ingredients in the Bayit's kosher-style kitchen, can be vegetarian or meat meals, and always include options for those with dietary restrictions.

Services are always followed by a homemade, kosher (by ingredient) dinner (around 6:45 p.m.), which often ends with lively singing. The weekly Shabbat dinners are central to the WJC's close-knit community and allow everyone to bond over a shared love of food, regardless of religion, with tables set up in the Bayit's common room where guests are encouraged to sit together and mingle.

For more information, you can contact Rabbi David Leipziger Teva, the Jewish chaplain, at dleipziger@wesleyan.edu or 860-685-2278.
