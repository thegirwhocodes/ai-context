# Outsourcing Nigeria - Service Overview

## What They Do

Outsourcing Nigeria (also called Omni Channel) is a company based in Lagos that helps businesses with outsourced work. They're located at "35A Bode Thomas Street, Surulere, Lagos" and you can reach them at **08066938330** or **07001111444**.

## Main Services Offered

**Customer Support & Call Centers:**
- Customer service representatives
- Call center solutions
- Business phone systems (0700 and 0800 toll-free numbers)
- WhatsApp customer support solutions

**Sales & Recruitment:**
- Sales development representatives (inbound and outbound)
- Recruitment process outsourcing
- Sales training and recruiting/placement services
- Marketing support

**Business Operations:**
- Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) - handling routine business tasks
- Virtual assistants
- Co-working spaces and office support
- CRM software and ticketing systems

**Industry-Specific Staffing:**
They supply workers for agriculture, aviation, construction, food manufacturing, telecommunications, hospitality, logistics, manufacturing, retail, renewable energy, oil & gas, and waste management.

## How Their Process Works

They follow a five-step approach:

1. **Initial Contact** - Their sales team discusses your project needs and budget
2. **Analysis** - Experts propose a solution with scope and timeline
3. **Team Selection** - Best-fit workers or teams are chosen
4. **Contract Setup** - Agreement finalized with sprint planning
5. **Deployment** - Workers integrate with your team; they handle reviews and administration

## Pricing Models Explained

**Fixed Price:** You pay one set amount. Best when project details are clear and documented.

**Retainer:** Monthly or bi-weekly payments. Best when project scope is unclear or evolving.

**Hourly:** Pay for time spent. Best for rough ideas still being defined.
