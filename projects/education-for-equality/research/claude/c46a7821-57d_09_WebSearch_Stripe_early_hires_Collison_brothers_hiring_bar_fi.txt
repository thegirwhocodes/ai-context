Based on the search results, here's what I found about how the Collison brothers recruited their first employees and their hiring philosophy at Stripe:

## Hiring Timeline and Selectivity

Stripe had an incredibly high bar for hiring its initial team, taking almost 2 years to hire their first 5 employees. They had lots of conversations early on taking their time to get the right people.

## The Collisons' Personal Involvement

The company's reputation for an unusually high hiring bar was built in the early years, when Collison and his brother Patrick were making every call personally. Before candidates could get a job offer, most serious candidates were asked to meet with one of the two Collisons, as the twenty-something brothers liked having a say in who got hired because they said it maintained consistency. They would each spend as much as 15 hours a week on job interviews.

## Key Hiring Requirements

Early hires were experienced and were able to 'hit the ground running' - all had run their own businesses. For the first 10 hires, they knew what they wanted to build and simply had no time to train people.

## Recruiting Methods and Philosophy

They started working with people before hiring them. A trial week involved working with the team for at least a week before making an offer. Stripe was a small startup then and had no capacity to teach, so hires had to hit the ground running.

They focused on hiring people that were 'known to be good by others' i.e. so that they had the ability to attract other great individuals to Stripe.

## Equity as a Recruiting Tool

For their first 10 people at Stripe, they gave away more than 10% of the equity, with the guidance: "Be extremely generous with your first employees and less so with your investors."
