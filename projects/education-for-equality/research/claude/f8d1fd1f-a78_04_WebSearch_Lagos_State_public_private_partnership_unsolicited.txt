## Key Requirements for Unsolicited Proposals

Every unsolicited proposal submitted must include the proponent's name, address, Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registration details, valid tax clearance certificate, and contact details of its authorized representative.

## Process Overview

In select cases, the project could be initiated by the private sector as an unsolicited proposal under a transparent, competitive process which will also be managed by a Ministry, Department, or Agency (MDA).

However, it's important to note that Lagos State is less disposed to pursuing unsolicited proposals, as consideration of unsolicited proposals will be the exception rather than the rule, limited mainly to projects that demonstrate genuine innovation and/or use of proprietary technology.
