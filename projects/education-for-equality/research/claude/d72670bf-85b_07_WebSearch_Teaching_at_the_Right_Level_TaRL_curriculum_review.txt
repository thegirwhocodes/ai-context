## Overview

Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) is an approach developed by the Indian NGO Pratham that aims to build foundational skills in math and reading for all children before exiting primary school.

## Curriculum Review & Validation

Since its inception in India in the early 2000s, the TaRL approach has spread to more than 20 countries across the globe and has been adapted in more than 30 languages. Over two decades of research and practical implementation have established a strong body of evidence supporting the effectiveness of TaRL. Since 2001, six randomised controlled trials (RCTs) conducted in India have consistently shown that TaRL improves foundational literacy and numeracy.

## Organizations Involved

Backed by rigorous evaluation from the Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL), TaRL has been proven to accelerate learning and help children catch up within a short period, breaking the cycle of falling behind in school. Additionally, the Global Education Evidence Advisory Panel (GEEAP) identifies TaRL as one of the most cost-effective interventions for improving the learning of all children in low- and middle-income countries.

## Key Implementation Aspects

Children are first assessed using easy-to-administer reading & math assessment tools. This helps instructors group children by their current level, across grades or within the same class.
