## Ford Fellowship Overview

The Ford Fellow creates professional development programs for tutors throughout the semester which may include the Ford Seminar (.5 credit tutor training course). All Tutors (including Writing Mentors and Course Tutors) receive extensive training through the Ford Teaching Seminar.

## Ford Fellow Responsibilities

The Ford Fellow develops new programs and collaborations to expand the Writers Room resources, works closely with faculty and staff in the Office of Academic Writing, and coordinates the schedules of a staff of 30 undergraduate tutors and mentors. The current Ford Fellow helps run the Writers Room programs and supervises the team of peer tutors and mentors.

## Philosophy and Peer Tutoring

Several peer tutors with philosophy interests are involved in the program. One peer tutor doubles majors in philosophy and CEAS, dreaming to be a more poetic and creative writer while becoming accustomed to argumentative writing style throughout their years at Wesleyan. Another peer tutor is a sophomore intending to double major in Philosophy and STS and is fascinated by how words can connect people across differences.
