## Hook & Retention Strategy

Your first three seconds determine everything: whether the algorithm pushes your content, whether viewers stay, and whether you get any meaningful engagement. Up to 50% of viewers drop off in the first three seconds, and the Instagram algorithm interprets early drop-off as a signal that your content isn't worth promoting.

Effective hooks exploit low-attention scrolling by creating pattern interrupts: unexpected visuals, contrarian statements, or curiosity gaps that force the brain to shift from passive to active attention mode.

## Scripting & Content Framework

The most successful short-form scripts in 2026 follow a four-part framework that's been battle-tested across 118,584+ viral videos: Hook → Problem → Solution → CTA. Aim for 15-30 seconds for maximum virality, which is about 75 words for 30 seconds at natural pace.

## Editing Process & Pacing

Aim for a cut or visual change every 1.5 to 3 seconds to maintain energy, and static shots longer than 4 seconds become danger zones where viewers are most likely to scroll away. Use jump cuts to remove "dead air" so every second serves a purpose and remove pauses to speed up clips when needed so silence doesn't kill momentum.

## Batching & Workflow Efficiency

Most agencies start with 3–4 Reels per client per week, then increase volume only after the workflow (batching, approvals, scheduling) is stable. Successful founders and lean marketing teams treat reels as a manufacturing process: a repeatable series of technical and creative steps that guarantee a baseline of quality every time you hit "Share."

## Key Performance Metrics

Prioritize a clear hook in the first seconds, 9:16 formatting, captions, original (non-watermarked) content, and tracking retention, saves, and shares. Reels with strong 3-second hold rates (above 60%) outperform those with weak holds (below 40%) by 5-10× in total reach.
