# Barro-Lee Long-Term Educational Data Summary

**Time Coverage & Countries:**
The primary Lee and Lee (2016) dataset covers "estimated school enrollment ratios from 1820 to 2010 and estimated educational attainment...from 1870 to 2010" across 111 nations. An earlier version (Barro & Lee, 2015) provides attainment data from 1870-2010 for 89 countries, with projections extending to 2040 for 146 countries.

**Variables Available:**
- School enrollment ratios (from 1820)
- Educational attainment by gender (total, female, male populations)
- Years of schooling
- Two human capital stock measures based on different labor substitution assumptions
