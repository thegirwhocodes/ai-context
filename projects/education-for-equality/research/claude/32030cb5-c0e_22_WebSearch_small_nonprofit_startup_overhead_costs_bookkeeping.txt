## Filing Fees

State incorporation fees typically range from $50 to $400, with expedited filing available for additional fees. For federal tax-exempt status, applying for tax-exempt status with the IRS costs $275 to $600, depending on the form used (Form 1023-EZ or Form 1023). On average in the U.S., it costs about $859 in state and federal fees to start a nonprofit organization.

## Bookkeeping Costs

Bookkeeping costs vary based on organization size, lifecycle stage, transaction volume, number of employees, payroll processing, expense accounts, and how accounting systems and policies are set up. For outsourced services, bookkeepers charge hourly rates between $30 and $60, but most nonprofits prefer flat monthly pricing for predictable budgeting. Additionally, outsourced bookkeeping services range from $300 to $2,000 per month, depending on the size of your business, the number of monthly transactions, and the services you need.

## Annual Report Requirements

Nonprofits need to file a Form 990 every year to keep their nonprofit status, and when undertaking 990 reporting internally, you'll want to factor in costs for a bookkeeper, accountant, or fundraising manager for tracking expenses and revenue.

## Overall Overhead Considerations

Initial investments in fundraising tools and compliance with state regulations (e.g., annual reports, permits) are essential, adding to the overall startup expenses.
