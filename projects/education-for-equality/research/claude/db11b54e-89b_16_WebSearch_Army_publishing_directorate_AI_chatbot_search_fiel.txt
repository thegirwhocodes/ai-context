## AI Chatbot Development

The U.S. Army has built its first AI chatbot for soldiers, called Victor, which was unveiled by the Army's chief technology officer Alex Miller. The platform pairs a Reddit-style peer forum with an AI chatbot called VictorBot, designed to help troops retrieve mission-specific knowledge without having to file formal requests through the military chain of command. The data pool feeding the system already spans more than 500 repositories, drawing from active theatres including the Ukraine-Russia war and Operation Epic Fury, the U.S. air campaign against Iran.

## Field Manual Updates Using AI

Doctrine writers are now using artificial intelligence to update field manuals, with leaders at the Combined Army Doctrine Directorate training authors on generative AI tools to speed up research and drafting. Soldiers write the manuals and doctrines for the Combined Arms Doctrine Directorate at Fort Leavenworth, Kansas.
