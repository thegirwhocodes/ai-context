# Milnerva Overview

**What it does:** Milnerva is an AI-powered writing assistant designed to help military personnel create professional documents quickly. The platform uses artificial intelligence to transform user input into regulation-compliant content.

**Products covered:** The tool handles NCOERs (Noncommissioned Officer Evaluation Reports), OERs (Officer Evaluation Reports), counseling statements, awards, memorandums, and citations.

**Input methods:** The platform accepts text input. Users can "write naturally" and the AI converts their words into Army-approved English. There's no mention of voice input capabilities on this page.

**Pricing:** A free tier exists with limitations. Paid plans cost $10/month to cover AI usage costs. New users receive a 7-day trial of the paid version.

**Creator:** Milnerva was built by a former Soldier. The name combines "military" and "Minerva" (Roman goddess of wisdom and warfare).

**Additional details:** The service has 20,000+ active users and has generated over 24,000 documents. It uses OpenAI's models, primarily GPT-4.1, with more complex tasks handled by o3 models. An iOS mobile app is available; Android is in development.
