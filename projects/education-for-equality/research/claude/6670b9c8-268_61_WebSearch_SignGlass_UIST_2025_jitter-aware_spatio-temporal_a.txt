## SignGlass: UIST 2025 Paper Overview

SignGlass is titled "First-Person View Comprehensive and Generalizable ASL Translation Using Wearable Glass" and is authored by Yongxiang Cai (Binghamton University), Taiting Lu (The Pennsylvania State University), Zhenghao Li (Pennsylvania State University), Hao Zhou (The Pennsylvania State University), Kenneth DeHaan (Gallaudet University), Xuhai "Orson" Xu (Columbia University), Mahanth Gowda (Pennsylvania State University), and Yincheng Jin (Binghamton University).

### Architecture Details

SignGlass integrates a jitter-aware spatio-temporal attention mechanism for robust recognition of hand movement patterns. Complementing this, a dual-camera, temporally-aware facial module captures the subtle facial expressions essential for ASL comprehension.

The system uses three wearable cameras. The other two cameras are positioned on the sides of the glasses' legs to focus on non-manual markers, such as facial expressions.

### Processing Pipeline

The processing mechanism of SignGlass consists of the ASL-to-Gloss recognition module and the Gloss-to-English translation module. A cascaded data augmentation strategy is used to improve model generalization.

### Performance Metrics

The system achieved a WER of 16.98% and a BLEU-1 score of 83% in leave-one-user-out testing, showing strong generalizability.
