I found relevant information about Twilio's gross margin challenges related to A2P fees. Here's what the search results show:

## The A2P Fee Pass-Through Problem

Twilio's gross margins faced pressure from carrier pass-through fees, declining 280 basis points year-over-year to 50.1%, primarily due to a $20 million increase in Verizon A2P fees in Q3 2025. The company incurred carrier pass-through fees of $23 million associated with increased Verizon A2P fees in Q4 2025, which primarily drove the sequential decline in gross margin.

## The Margin Headwind Without Profit Impact

Interestingly, the company said recent A2P messaging fee increases from major U.S. mobile carriers are expected to create a modest margin headwind, even though the fees are passed through to customers at cost and do not affect gross profit dollars. Management noted these fees are neutral to dollar profits but reduce reported non-GAAP gross margin by about 170 basis points and operating margin by 60–70 basis points in FY 2026, all else equal.

## Forward-Looking Concerns

Since middle of 2025, all major U.S. carriers have announced A2P increases, including AT&T, whose rate increases will go into effect on April 1, and Twilio's full year revenue guidance assumes approximately $190 million in incremental pass-through revenue from these fees.

This situation illustrates how passing through low-margin fees directly to customers can create a "margin dilution" effect even when gross profit dollars remain unaffected, making reported profitability metrics look worse than the underlying business performance.
