## Typical Attention Span for 8-Year-Olds

For children ages 5 to 8 years old, the typical attention span is approximately between 12 and 24 minutes. This means an 8-year-old should be able to concentrate on a single activity for roughly 12-24 minutes on average.

## Factors Affecting Concentration

However, these are just averages, and individual children may vary. Kids who are doing an activity they're interested in will likely focus longer and be more likely to struggle to stay focused on things they find boring or challenging.

Several factors can influence your child's ability to maintain attention:
- Attention spans often get longer when your child is super interested in the task at hand—and then shrink back down when they're less into the task
- Research shows that children who are hungry are simply unable to focus, resulting in a short attention span
- Being in a space with lots of distractions, like loud noises and flashing lights is very distracting, which chips away at attention
