## USSD Push/Network-Initiated Overview

Network-initiated (NI), also known as USSD Push or Mobile Terminating USSD, occurs when the application starts the USSD session on the handset. USSD Push enables various use cases like subscriber surveys, payment verification, and marketing messages.
