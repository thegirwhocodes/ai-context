## Key Findings:

**Nigerian Pidgin as a Lingua Franca:**

Nigerian Pidgin is an English-based creole language spoken as a lingua franca or vehicular language across Nigeria. Between three and five million Nigerians use it as their first language, while a further 75 million have it as their second language.

**Educational Comprehension and Effectiveness:**

Using Nigerian Pidgin as a medium of instruction could enhance understanding and communication, especially for students whose first language is not English. This approach can alleviate the challenges learners face in understanding the standard version of English imposed by colonial rule in formal education.

However, there are challenges: Students who come from homes where Pidgin English is their lingua franca struggle to cope with their English lessons and it takes a long time for them to internalize English lexical items and vocabulary.

**Educational Barriers:**

Teachers would need specialized training to effectively use Nigerian Pidgin as a medium of instruction, ensuring quality education is maintained. Additionally, some may perceive Nigerian Pidgin as a "lesser" language, potentially leading to negative attitudes towards its use in education.

**Recognition and Potential Impact:**

If Nigerian Pidgin were recognized as a co-official language with English, it would enhance the participation of all citizens in the economic, social and political development of the country as well as equalizing educational opportunities.
