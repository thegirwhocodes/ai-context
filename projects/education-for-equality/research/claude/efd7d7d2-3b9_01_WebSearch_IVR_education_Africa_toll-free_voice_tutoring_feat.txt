## IVR Technology for Education in Africa

IVR has proven to be the most effective technology for distance learning in rural Africa. IVR is an automated system that utilizes any type of phone to deliver information to users by voice through audio recordings and allows them to give feedback by pressing a key that corresponds to their choice. IVR has become the go-to technology for organizations that wish to offer effective training to groups of people in rural communities, such as farmers, women, youth, health workers, etc.

## Key Features for Education

Organizations can create and manage audio files for training material, either by creating the training material audio from text, recording it on the web dashboard, or uploading pre-recorded audio files. Participants can dial in when they want to listen to a training recording, or organizations can create an automated call broadcast to reach them at a specific time.

## Toll-Free and Phone-Based Advantages

By using toll-free numbers, organizations can provide customers with a free communication channel. Customers can make free phone calls by dialing a toll-free number. Instead of the caller, the number owner will be responsible for all incoming and outgoing call charges. By allowing low-literacy populations to interact with technology, IVR systems form an avenue to build technological skills in developing countries. Developing countries have a prevalence of mobile phones even in rural areas, which allows room for IVR technology to support social good projects.
