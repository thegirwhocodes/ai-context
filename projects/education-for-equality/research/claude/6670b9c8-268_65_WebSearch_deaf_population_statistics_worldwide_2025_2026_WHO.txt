## Global Statistics

More than 1.5 billion people worldwide, nearly 20% of the global population, have some degree of hearing loss. Among these, approximately 430 million people worldwide have disabling hearing loss (greater than 35 decibels) that requires rehabilitation services.

## Children with Deafness

Globally, there are approximately 34 million deaf children. Additionally, it is estimated that up to five out of every 1000 babies are born with hearing loss or acquire it soon after birth.

## Age-Related Trends

The prevalence of hearing loss increases with age, among those older than 60 years, over 25% are affected by disabling hearing loss. 65.3% of people aged 71 and older have hearing loss, representing 21.5 million Americans.

## Socioeconomic Disparities

Nearly 80% of people with disabling hearing loss live in low- and middle-income countries.

## Future Projections

Globally, 1.5 billion people have hearing loss, and this number is expected to reach 2.5 billion by 2050. This increase would mean that over 700 million people globally would need hearing rehabilitation at that time.

These statistics underscore hearing loss as a major global public health challenge affecting diverse populations across all age groups.
