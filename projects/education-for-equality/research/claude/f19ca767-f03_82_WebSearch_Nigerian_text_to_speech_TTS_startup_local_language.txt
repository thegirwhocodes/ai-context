**Abena AI** - Generate natural-sounding audio in Twi, Ghana Pidgin, Yoruba, Swahili, Hausa, Ewe and more with Abena AI.

## Community Projects

**TTSYoruba** - This is an experiment in finding out whether speech synthesis is possible in an African language. Yorùbá is the natural first choice because the TTS machine was created to support a Yorùbá Names Dictionary at YorubaName.com.

**LangEasy** - LangEasy allows users to record audio samples by translating English sentences into Nigerian languages such as Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Pidgin, and Ibibio.
