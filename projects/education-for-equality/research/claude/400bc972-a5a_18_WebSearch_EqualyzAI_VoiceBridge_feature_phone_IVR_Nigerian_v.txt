## EqualyzAI VoiceBridge Overview

VoiceBridge is an IVR-based system that lets users contribute speech over feature phones with no internet required. It's designed for collecting new voice datasets, and serves as the foundation that makes their other products possible, and is available to researchers and NGOs building their own African language datasets.

## Code-Switching Approach

EqualyzAI has built their voice AI stack specifically to handle code-switching as a core feature. They built a voice AI stack for the African linguistic and infrastructure reality: low-resource languages, code-switching as default, bandwidth constraints, and populations who access voice services through feature phones, and not just smartphones.

Their models are trained on naturally mixed speech, English-Pidgin, Yoruba-English, the way people actually talk. The team started building the solutions from the ground up, engaging the everyday speakers of local languages to collect data that is truly reflective of how they use the language, how they communicate, the code-mixing they use, the augmentation, the code-switching that they use, etc.

VoiceMaker, their text-to-speech and transcription product, turns text into natural speech in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, and Pidgin, and transcribes multilingual audio with code-switching support.
