## Response Rates and Call Pickup

In Uganda, an IVR survey achieved a 1.9% response rate through initial random digit dialing, while human call center follow-ups (CATI) achieved 11.8%, and post-CATI IVR surveys achieved 44.9% response rates. IVR completion rates of 1.9% are comparable to other low and middle-income countries where IVR response ranges between 0.5% and 3.3%.

Response rates for IVR surveys in Mozambique and Zimbabwe were 9% and 8%, respectively, while a more recent random digit dialing survey in Ghana had a response rate of 21%.

## Impact of Human Callback Interventions

Those who spoke with a call center agent were significantly more likely to complete follow-up IVR surveys, with post-CATI IVR samples showing marked improvement in contact and response rates compared to initial IVR.

## Key Health Interventions Using IVR

Research in Sub-Saharan Africa has deployed IVR for multiple health purposes:

- An assessment of the 3-2-1 IVR Service in Burkina Faso evaluated its effectiveness for improving health literacy and promoting effective disease management among internally displaced persons as part of a comprehensive initiative for underserved communities.

- The "Dial-COVID" platform used interactive voice response in ten languages to track COVID-19 symptoms and disseminate health information in refugee settlements in Uganda.

- Digital MindSKILLZ, an IVR-based intervention, demonstrated reach in delivering mental health information to over 425,000 users across six sub-Saharan African countries, with most users under 25 and from rural areas.

## General Benefits of IVR

Typical automated call surveys achieve 25-75% response rates, while SMS surveys typically achieve only 5-15% response rates.
